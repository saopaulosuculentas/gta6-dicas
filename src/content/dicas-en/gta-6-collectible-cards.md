GTA 6 collectible cards are tucked away in **safes and stashes** around the map, and you can sell them at a **pawn shop**. The easiest one sits in a warehouse safe near Jason's house and goes for **about $6,000** at **Hamlet Pawn**. A full card map **hasn't been published yet**.

## Confirmed locations

| Card | Where | Shown by |
|---|---|---|
| **Vice City Narcos** set | Office safe in the [abandoned warehouse](../gta-6-early-money-warehouse/) south of Vice-Dale County, near Jason's house | DooDooPoo, GrandTheftIntel and Musty Sky |
| **The Legends of Ratface** | Hidden stash in the garage of the [UFO house](../gta-6-ufo-house-easter-egg/) in Bellville, Leonard County | ZacCoxTV |

The warehouse safe has **no code**: spin the right stick until the lock gives. You can get there right after the prologue.

## Found, but no exact spot yet

- **Sports card in an RV:** WiLLiSGaming says some **RVs out in the wilderness** have a lockbox, and the code is usually written on the wall. One of them held a collectible sports card. The spot is only shown on the map in his video.
- **Other sets:** T5G found cards from an X-Men parody, dancers from the **919 Club** and American wrestlers. He didn't give locations.

Everything else is **not mapped yet**. We'll add locations here as they get confirmed.

## Where to sell and what they're worth

1. Head to a **pawn shop**. The first one is in **Hamlet**, marked with a **green icon** on the map, per Musty Sky.
2. Talk to the clerk and pick the option to sell collectibles. DooDooPoo pressed **Y** on Xbox.
3. The menu has two tabs: **Collectible Cards** and **Collectible Items**.
4. Pick a set and use **Sell All** to sell every card you have from it.

The warehouse card went for **$6,000** in videos from DooDooPoo, GrandTheftIntel and Musty Sky. No one has posted prices for the others yet.

In DooDooPoo's video, the Collectible Cards list has **18 rows**, and only Vice City Narcos was unlocked. The rest show as Undiscovered. T5G says the cards you collect get stored in a **box in Jason's house**. GamesRadar+ notes that the **Exploration** tab of the map index lists pawn shop collectors.

## Sell or keep?

There's a case for both:

- **Sell:** the **Pixel Art** cheat asks you to make **$250,000 selling collectibles**. And $6,000 early in the game goes a long way, as Musty Sky points out.
- **Keep:** the **Don't Touch My Deck** trophy wants **3 complete card sets**. The **Bubblegum** cheat asks you to find all **24 cards in the Vice City Flamingos set**.

No source has tested whether a sold card still counts toward its set. If the trophy matters to you, hold on to your cards until that's cleared up. Cheats only work after you finish the story; see the [full cheat list](../all-gta-6-cheats-list/).

## How many cards are there

The total **hasn't been revealed**. Here's what is known:

- The **Vice City Flamingos** set has **24 cards**, per PC Gamer and GamesRadar+.
- The trophy asks for **3 complete sets**, so there are at least three.
- The pawn shop list shows 18 rows in DooDooPoo's video, but nobody has confirmed that each row is a set.
