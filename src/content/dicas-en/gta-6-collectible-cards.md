GTA 6 collectible cards are tucked away in **safes and stashes** around the map, and you can sell them at a **pawn shop**. The easiest one sits in a warehouse safe near Jason's house and goes for **about $6,000** at **Hamlet Pawn**. A full card map **hasn't been published yet**.

## Confirmed locations

| Card | Where | Shown by |
|---|---|---|
| **Leroy Rook (Silver)**, from the **Vice City Narcos** set | Office safe in the [abandoned warehouse](../gta-6-early-money-warehouse/) south of Vice-Dale County, near Jason's house | DooDooPoo, GrandTheftIntel, Musty Sky, Arekkz Gaming, FuriousFade, Rodwenn and BennyCentral |
| **The Legends of Ratface** | Hidden stash in the garage of the [UFO house](../gta-6-ufo-house-easter-egg/) in Bellville, Leonard County | ZacCoxTV |
| Two cards | The **Key Lento** radio tower, about 0.36 miles from Jason's house: one in the room near the top, one inside the phone box | Millzaa, Arekkz Gaming, Rodwenn and BennyCentral |
| Trailer safe card | A trailer on the west side of a small trailer park in the swamp west of the Keys | RageGamingVideos |
| Cards on a garage table | The garage of a mansion on **Vespucci Island**, Vice City, the same island as [Paul Walker's car](../gta-6-paul-walker-eclipse-car/) | Imack Studios |

The warehouse safe has **no code**: spin the right stick until the lock gives. You can get there right after the prologue. Its card is **Leroy Rook (Silver)**: FuriousFade read the name out as he opened the safe, and Arekkz's screen shows a "Vice City Narcos 1/24" counter.

### Key Lento radio tower

Climb the tower using the stairs inside and the ladders outside. In the room near the top there's a card on a table next to a magazine. Millzaa pulled **Thelma**, who he says is one of 18 Delights dancers. A second card sits inside the phone box out on the balcony: his came from the backyard wrestling set, which he says has 12. Rodwenn warns that the top card isn't worth much, and Millzaa confirms it: **$20** at the Hamlet pawn shop. The same climb has a sniper and a parachute; see [GTA 6 secret early items](../gta-6-secret-early-items/).

### Swamp trailer

According to RageGamingVideos, in the swamp west of the Keys, where the game starts, there's a small trailer park next to a story mission marker. The trailer on its west side has a **safe**, and the code is on the **photos** inside the trailer, which you can zoom in on. The card inside sold for **$1,350** at a pawn shop. It's on the way to the Kingfishers' fishing shacks.

### Vespucci Island garage

Imack Studios opened the garage of a mansion full of expensive cars on Vespucci Island (press Triangle, or Y on Xbox, and play the lock minigame). Inside, there are **collectible cards** on a table to grab and sell.

## Found, but no exact spot yet

- **Sports card in an RV:** WiLLiSGaming says some **RVs out in the wilderness** have a lockbox, and the code is usually written on the wall. One of them held a collectible sports card. The spot is only shown on the map in his video. The swamp trailer RageGamingVideos showed above works the same way.
- **Other sets:** T5G found cards from an X-Men parody, dancers from the **919 Club** and American wrestlers. He didn't give locations.

Everything else is **not mapped yet**. We'll add locations here as they get confirmed.

## Where to sell and what they're worth

1. Head to a **pawn shop**. The first one is in **Hamlet**, marked with a **green icon** on the map, per Musty Sky.
2. Talk to the clerk and pick the option to sell collectibles. DooDooPoo pressed **Y** on Xbox.
3. The menu has two tabs: **Collectible Cards** and **Collectible Items**.
4. Pick a set and use **Sell All** to sell every card you have from it.

The warehouse card went for **$6,000** in videos from DooDooPoo, GrandTheftIntel and Musty Sky. This page used to say no one had posted prices for the others. Now there are two: the swamp trailer card sold for **$1,350** (RageGamingVideos), and the table card on the radio tower is worth just **$20** (Millzaa). RaidAway took two cards to a pawn shop: a **backyard wrestling** card was worth **$150**, and a **comic** card **$50**. Prices vary a lot.

**Check the value before you go.** Millzaa shows you can see what your cards are worth straight from the map: hover over the Hamlet pawn shop. In Rodwenn's video, the shop's map card lists $6,000 in collectibles to sell while he's carrying the warehouse card. The same card says the shop buys cards, doubloons and meteorites.

In DooDooPoo's video, the Collectible Cards list has **18 rows**, and only Vice City Narcos was unlocked. The rest show as Undiscovered. T5G says the cards you collect get stored in a **box in Jason's house**. GamesRadar+ notes that the **Exploration** tab of the map index lists pawn shop collectors.

## Sell or keep?

There's a case for both:

- **Sell:** the **Pixel Art** cheat asks you to make **$250,000 selling collectibles**. And $6,000 early in the game goes a long way, as Musty Sky points out.
- **Keep:** the **Don't Touch My Deck** trophy wants **3 complete card sets**. The **Bubblegum** cheat asks you to find all **24 cards in the Vice City Flamingos set**.

No source has tested whether a sold card still counts toward its set. If the trophy matters to you, hold on to your cards until that's cleared up. Cheats only work after you finish the story; see the [full cheat list](../all-gta-6-cheats-list/).

## How many cards are there

The total **hasn't been revealed**. Here's what is known:

- The **Vice City Flamingos** set has **24 cards**, per PC Gamer and GamesRadar+.
- The **Vice City Narcos** set, home of the warehouse card, also has **24**: the "1/24" counter shows on Arekkz Gaming's screen.
- According to Millzaa, the **Delights dancers** set has 18 cards and the **backyard wrestling** set has 12.
- The trophy asks for **3 complete sets**, so there are at least three.
- The pawn shop list shows 18 rows in DooDooPoo's video, but nobody has confirmed that each row is a set.
