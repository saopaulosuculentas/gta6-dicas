No, GTA 6 doesn't have an online mode or a 2 player mode. It launched on November 19 as a **single-player game**. PlayStation's FAQ and Xbox's official page use the exact same words: GTA 6 is a single-player experience.

## What's confirmed

- **Single player only.** The PlayStation Store lists the game as **1 player**, so there's no split screen, no local co-op and no online co-op.
- **Offline play works.** PlayStation lists offline play as enabled. You need the internet to download the game, not to play it.
- **No online date.** Rockstar hasn't announced a multiplayer mode for GTA 6, or when one might arrive.

## What about GTA Online?

On launch day, Kotaku's live coverage noted that players who spent hundreds of hours in GTA Online were still desperate to know what Rockstar has planned for multiplayer in GTA 6. Windows Central's review lists the player count as "single player (for now?)", with a question mark. Until Rockstar says something, any date you see for a GTA 6 online mode is guesswork.

## Remote Play isn't multiplayer

Rockstar says GTA 6 supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox. That only streams your own game from your own console to another screen. It doesn't let a second person join in.

## What about the GTA+ month?

Digital purchases of either edition made before November 20, 2026 include **one month of GTA+**, Rockstar's membership. Rockstar lists it as part of the digital bonuses, and PlayStation notes the month rolls into a paid monthly subscription unless you cancel it in your account settings before the next payment date.

- **What it costs afterward:** GamesRadar+ puts GTA+ at **$7.99 / €7.99 / £6.99 a month** on PS5 and Xbox Series X. PC Gamer quotes the same $7.99.
- **Where to claim it:** GamesRadar+ says to open the pause menu and go to the **Store** tab. PC Gamer found it on the "store" page of the main menu.
- **What it does in GTA 6:** even without a multiplayer mode, the membership adds a few story-mode perks, including an exclusive car with its own garage, free or cheaper rides, 10% more for cars you sell and some shop discounts. PC Gamer isn't impressed, calling it thin for a monthly bill. The full list is in [every GTA+ reward in GTA 6](../gta-6-gta-plus-rewards/).
