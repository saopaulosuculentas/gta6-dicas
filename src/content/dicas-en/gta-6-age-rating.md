GTA 6 is rated **M for Mature 17+** by the ESRB in North America and **PEGI 18** in Europe. Both boards flag the same core reasons: graphic violence, drug use, strong sexual content and harsh language. PlayStation's official page puts it plainly: the game is intended for adult players.

## The ratings side by side

| Board | Rating | Reasons listed |
|---|---|---|
| ESRB (US and Canada) | M (Mature 17+) | Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, Use of Drugs and Alcohol; In-Game Purchases |
| PEGI (Europe) | 18 | Strong graphic violence, strong sexual themes and use of illegal drugs; in-game purchases |

Ratings differ from country to country, and PlayStation recommends checking your local rating before you buy.

## What the ESRB says

The ESRB's summary explains why it landed on M. In short:

- missions are built around crime, like stealing cars, pulling heists and dealing drugs, using guns, explosives and melee weapons;
- you can attack civilians and police, which raises your wanted level;
- there's frequent blood, and some weapons can dismember;
- sex scenes are implied and fade to black, and nudity appears in some situations;
- characters can use drugs and alcohol, which distorts the screen and affects the controls;
- there's crude humor and strong language, including racial slurs.

## What PEGI highlights

PEGI gave it an 18 for **strong graphic violence, strong sexual themes and illegal drug use**. Its write-up says violence is frequent, bloody and highly realistic, and that you can hurt anyone in the open world, from bystanders to cops. Drug use is described as frequent and prominent. It also mentions strong swearing and discriminatory language on the in-game TV shows and radio stations. Pets can be harmed, though without the same level of injury detail as people. In-game purchases are optional, and PEGI notes you can play without buying anything extra.

## A note for parents

PlayStation's GTA 6 page warns that Grand Theft Auto games regularly feature profanity, violence, sexual themes and illegal acts, and are generally **not recommended for children**. PlayStation accounts can be age restricted through the **PlayStation Family app**. On Xbox, the free month of GTA+ that came with pre-orders can't be redeemed by child accounts with restrictions on age-restricted content, according to Xbox Wire.
