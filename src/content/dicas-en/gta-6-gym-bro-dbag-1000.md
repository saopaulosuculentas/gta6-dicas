The GTA 6 gym bro is a street encounter worth **$1,000** right at the start. His name is **D-Bag**, he trains on a grassy strip on **Shore Drive** across from the **Hotel Dixon**, and he bets you can't make him tap to a **rear naked choke**. MrBossFTW showed how it plays out.

## Where to find him

- **Street:** Shore Drive, basically across from the **Hotel Dixon**.
- **The spot:** the **grassy area** between the beach and the road.
- **How to spot him:** a buff guy **shadowboxing**.

MrBossFTW says you can do it very early in the game.

## How to win the $1,000

1. Talk to him. D-Bag brags about his neck and challenges you to choke him out: **$1,000** if he taps.
2. **Accept.** He walks you through the hold and you lock it in.
3. **Mash Circle (B on Xbox)** as fast as you can.
4. Hold on long enough and he **taps**, then pays up.

MrBossFTW lost his first attempt because he underestimated how much mashing it takes. He came back and won the second time.

## Tips so you don't lose

- **Sleep first.** MrBossFTW went in with **full energy and stamina** after sleeping, which helped him last longer.
- **Don't decline.** If you say no, he **won't offer the bet again**.

## If it goes wrong

You can just fight him. He puts up a real fight, but knocked out he drops some cash and Zombix. If he dies, the $1,000 is on his body, though MrBossFTW says that's far more trouble than winning the bet.
