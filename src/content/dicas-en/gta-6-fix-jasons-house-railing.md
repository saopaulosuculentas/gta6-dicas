To fix the railing in GTA 6, go out the back of Jason's house, head down to the **lower deck with the gym equipment**, **hold Triangle (Y on Xbox)** at the gap in the railing and press **R2 (RT)** to nail the new panels in. The railing is one of several repairs around the house. They're all optional, each one helps Jason's relationship with **Lucia**, and there's a catch: **you can only do them before you pick Lucia up from prison**.

The repair objective shows up in Chapter 1, while Lucia is about to get out. GamesRadar+ calls it the **Repair The House** side mission and files it under **Gear Up**. GameSpot explains the backstory: the waterfront house in Key Lento belongs to Jason's boss, **Brian Heder**, who lets him stay there rent-free. It costs Jason nothing, but it's half finished, with a missing wall and a leaky roof.

## Don't miss the deadline

GameSpot warns that the projects **lock as soon as you decide to go and get Lucia**. Once she moves in, anything you left undone can't be finished anymore. So knock them all out before you drive to the prison.

Even if you don't care about the romance, GameSpot points out that the house simply ends up nicer.

## Is it 4 repairs or 5?

It depends who's counting. GamesRadar+ and Arekkz Gaming list the wall and the paint job separately, for **5 repairs**. GameSpot rolls both into a single **Fix Wall** project and says there are **4**. The actual work is the same.

## Every repair and what it unlocks

Buttons are listed as **PS5 / Xbox**:

| Repair | Where | What to do | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Railing | Lower back deck with the gym gear and the BBQ, at the gap next to an aluminum ladder | Hold **Triangle / Y** to fit new panels, then **R2 / RT** to nail them | A boost with Lucia |
| Steps | Bottom of the outside staircase up to the front door, the one closest to the mailbox and driveway. The planks sit on a blue tarp | Hold **Triangle / Y**. Hold **L2 / LT** and **R2 / RT** to rip out the old steps, then press **X** to put in new ones | A boost with Lucia |
| Leaky roof | Front of the house, up the yellow ladder beside the door | Walk into the ladder to climb, go to the exposed patch and nail tiles with **R2 / RT** | A boost with Lucia |
| Bedroom wall | Jason's bedroom. The plasterboard is stacked at the foot of the bed, and the open wall frame is across from the bed | Hold **Triangle / Y** and screw the panels in with **R2 / RT** | A boost with Lucia, and it unlocks the paint job |
| Paint | Paint cans next to the dresser | Pick Tropical Green, Stormy Blue or Peach Pastel | A boost with Lucia. Afterwards Jason stores the cans in the storage room outside, where the punching bag is |

For the steps, GamesRadar+ only lists **X** for installing the new boards and doesn't give the Xbox button. GameSpot rates the roof as the easiest repair to find and the railing as one of the trickiest.

## Finding and fixing the railing

One thing trips people up here: you can see the gap from the upper deck, but you can't repair it from that side. Here's the route GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer describe:

1. Inside the house, open the **glass sliding door** near the TV and step out onto the back deck.
2. Straight ahead, opposite the door and near the deck chairs, you'll see the railing with panels missing. **You can't fix it from here.**
3. Turn left and take the stairs on your right down to the lower deck, where the barbell, weights and BBQ are.
4. Face the house and look for the gap in the railing beside an **aluminum ladder**. GamesRadar+ puts it to the left of the ladder; PC Gamer says it's between the ladder and the weights. In Arekkz Gaming's video there are loose boards and paint cans leaning underneath.
5. When the **paintbrush icon** appears, walk up and **hold Triangle (Y)**. Jason starts fitting the new panels.
6. Press **R2 (RT)** every time the game prompts you to nail a panel in. A **Nail** prompt with R2 shows up on screen.

## The bedroom wall and paint

The wall comes first. GameSpot describes the bedroom wall as only half built. GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer both say the panels are stacked at the foot of Jason's bed, while GameSpot tells you to interact with the wooden frame on the wall across from the bed. Screw the plasterboard in with Triangle (Y) and R2 (RT), then walk over to the paint cans by the dresser and choose a color. Arekkz points out that the color is entirely up to you.

## What to buy and do before Lucia gets home

According to GameSpot, once the house projects are done there are a few more time-sensitive jobs before Lucia arrives, and she appreciates all of them:

- pick up the **groceries**;
- get her a **custom necklace** (our [beginner tips](../gta-6-beginner-tips/) mention a store and a price);
- collect **her friend's clothes** for her;
- buy **flowers**;
- get a **haircut** (see the [barber and clothing stores](../gta-6-mask-clothes-tattoo-barber-shops/)).

## What you get out of it

- **A better relationship with Lucia.** Arekkz calls fixing the house one of the easiest ways to raise it, and says **each job** gives the relationship a bump. GamesRadar+ says the payoff shows up later on, and GameSpot notes that the game itself tells you the projects make Lucia happier.
- **A punching bag.** Once the wall is painted, Jason stores the paint cans in a storage room outside. The punching bag is at the back of that room: hold **Triangle (Y)** to hang it and press **Square (X on Xbox)** to train your stamina.

MrDalekJD lists the same jobs (drywall, roof leaks, fresh paint) and also frames them as a way to impress Lucia before she moves in.

## When to do it

You can start as soon as Gear Up begins. KultZ suggests the day Lucia calls with errands before you pick her up: you have to carry the groceries into the house anyway, so knock out the **bedroom wall** and the **steps** while you're there.

The hard limit is the prison run. Per GameSpot, once you commit to picking Lucia up, there's no going back to the projects.
