To fix the railing in GTA 6, head to the back of Jason's house by the gym equipment, **hold Triangle (Y on Xbox)** at the broken section and press **R2 (RT)** to nail the new panels in. The railing is one of **5 repairs** around the house. They're all optional, and each one helps Jason's relationship with **Lucia**.

The repair objective shows up in Chapter 1, while Lucia is about to get out of prison. GamesRadar+ files Jason's house under the **Gear Up** mission.

## All 5 repairs

Buttons are listed as **PS5 / Xbox**:

| Repair | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Railing | Back of the house, near the workout gear | Hold **Triangle / Y** to fit new panels, then **R2 / RT** to nail them |
| Steps | Bottom of the outside staircase, by the stack of planks | Hold **Triangle / Y**. Hold **L2 / LT** and **R2 / RT** to rip out the old steps, then press **X** to put in new ones |
| Leaky roof | Front of the house, up the ladder beside the door | Walk into the ladder to climb, go to the exposed patch and nail tiles with **R2 / RT** |
| Bedroom wall | Jason's bedroom, at the plasterboard | Hold **Triangle / Y** and screw the panels in with **R2 / RT** |
| Paint | Paint cans next to the dresser | Pick Tropical Green, Stormy Blue or Peach Pastel |

For the steps, GamesRadar+ only lists **X** for installing the new boards and doesn't give the Xbox button.

## Fixing the railing, step by step

1. Go out the back to the deck where the barbell and weights are.
2. Look for the stretch of railing with missing slats. In Arekkz Gaming's video it sits next to an aluminum ladder, with loose boards and paint cans leaning underneath.
3. Walk up to it and **hold Triangle (Y)**. Jason starts fitting the new panels.
4. Press **R2 (RT)** to nail each one. A **Nail** prompt with R2 shows up on screen.

## The bedroom wall and paint

The wall comes first. Screw the plasterboard in with Triangle (Y) and R2 (RT), then walk over to the paint cans by the dresser and choose a color. Arekkz points out that the color is entirely up to you.

## What you get out of it

- **A better relationship with Lucia.** Arekkz calls fixing the house one of the easiest ways to raise it, and says **each of the 5 jobs** gives the relationship a bump. GamesRadar+ says the payoff shows up later on.
- **A punching bag.** Once the wall is painted, Jason stores the paint cans in a storage room outside. The punching bag is at the back of that room: hold **Triangle (Y)** to hang it and press **Square (X on Xbox)** to train your stamina.

MrDalekJD lists the same jobs (drywall, roof leaks, fresh paint) and also frames them as a way to impress Lucia before she moves in.

## When to do it

You can start as soon as Gear Up begins. KultZ suggests the day Lucia calls with errands before you pick her up: you have to carry the groceries into the house anyway, so knock out the **bedroom wall** and the **steps** while you're there.
