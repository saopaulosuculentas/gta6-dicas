You can't play GTA 6 on PC or PS4, and it isn't on Game Pass. The game runs on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** only. Xbox One owners are the one exception: they can **stream** it through Xbox Cloud Gaming, but they still have to buy it.

## Where GTA 6 runs

| Device | Can it play GTA 6? |
|---|---|
| PS5 | Yes |
| PS5 Pro | Yes, with enhanced visuals |
| Xbox Series X | Yes |
| Xbox Series S | Yes |
| PS4 | No |
| Xbox One | Cloud streaming only |
| PC | No version announced (the leaked build isn't playable) |
| Game Pass | Not included |

## PS4: no

PlayStation's official page is blunt about it. GTA 6 is only playable on PS5 consoles and will not be available on PS4. If you're on PS5 Pro, you get extra clarity from PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) plus ray-traced shadows, on a compatible display.

## Xbox One: cloud only

The game **doesn't install or run natively on Xbox One**. According to Xbox, older consoles can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming, which started serving GTA 6 at 1 a.m. ET on November 19. To do that you need two things:

- **Your own copy of the game.** Cloud access doesn't include the game itself.
- **Cloud playtime.** Game Pass members can use their plan's monthly cloud hours. Without Game Pass, you can buy cloud playtime in the Xbox Store or play with ads, in select regions.

Rockstar says the streamed version uses **Xbox Series S graphics settings**, and that your connection speed can affect how it plays. Its support page lists cloud play only on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles.

## Game Pass: not included

The official Xbox FAQ answers this one directly: GTA 6 isn't available with Game Pass, and players must purchase it. Windows Central's Xbox review lists Game Pass as "No" too. Nothing has been announced about it joining the service later.

## PC: not announced

Rockstar's support page only lists PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. On launch day, Kotaku still described a PC port as only a rumor, so there's no official version or date. Rockstar does say the game supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox, which streams from a console you already own. According to GameSpot, Microsoft has now confirmed Xbox Remote Play support.

**What about the videos of GTA 6 running on PC?** On November 23, the leaker Cyberleek released the executable for the leaked Xbox build, and fan groups are trying to get it running. So far it only boots to a black screen or shows the menus. It isn't playable, and every "GTA 6 PC" download is fake or unfinished.
