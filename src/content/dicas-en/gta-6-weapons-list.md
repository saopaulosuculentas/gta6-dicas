PC Gamer's list of confirmed GTA 6 weapons has **five handguns, one shotgun and one rifle**, plus a hammer, a baseball bat, the Molotov cocktail and a grenade launcher. These are the guns whose names showed up in official videos, in Rockstar's own messaging or in the Ultimate Edition details.

Keep in mind the list was built **before launch**, from the trailers and the gameplay reveal, so the full game almost certainly has a lot more. One change from GTA 5 stands out: instead of generic names, nearly every gun now carries a parody manufacturer brand.

## Guns seen only in trailers

None of these had an official name when PC Gamer wrote the list. They were picked out from screenshots and footage:

- **Capo pistol:** Jason holds one at the end of the liquor store robbery in the first trailer, and Raul Bautista carries one later. It looks like a Colt M1911.
- **MP5-style SMG:** Jason and Lucia use it in the bank robbery from the second trailer. Jason's had a red dot sight, likely an upgrade you buy in the game.
- **Hunting rifles:** a Duke-branded sniper rifle, a bolt action that resembles the Remington 700, and one that resembles the M1A, all carried by hunters out in the woods.
- **MAC-10-style SMG:** Jason has one wearing the paint job from the Vintage Vice City pre-order pack.
- **Double-barreled shotgun:** a hunter aims one at an alligator.
- **M249-style light machine gun:** the owner of Phil's Ammu-Nation shows one off in the second trailer.
- **On the wall at Phil's Ammu-Nation:** AK-47-style rifles, Uzi-style SMGs and a shotgun close to the Benelli M4.

PC Gamer also jokes about the stiletto heel Lucia uses to hit a man in the gameplay reveal, though it suspects that moment is a cutscene only.

## Where to get weapons

PC Gamer's list **doesn't say where to find each gun**. Here's what we do know:

- **Ammu-Nation:** the gun store appears in the second trailer, stocked with rifles and SMGs. To store and recover what you buy, see [how to store weapons in your trunk](../gta-6-store-weapons-trunk/).
- **Police cars:** in a separate guide, PC Gamer shows cop car trunks holding assault rifles, shotguns and even body armor. The steps are in [free guns from cop cars](../gta-6-free-guns-cop-cars/).
- **Early game:** you can grab a rifle early at the [early money warehouse](../gta-6-early-money-warehouse/), and a sniper rifle from the [Skunk Ape mission](../gta-6-skunk-ape-mission-sniper-rifle/).
