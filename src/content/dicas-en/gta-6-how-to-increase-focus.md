The fastest way to increase focus in GTA 6 is **fishing**: Arekkz Gaming got about **5% focus per fish**, against 1% per basket on the basketball hoop. You can also raise it by feeding Jason's snake, petting dogs and cats, eating before you head out, playing pool or golf, using the shooting range and doing side jobs like Shirley's rum run. This guide pulls together tips from eight English-speaking YouTube channels, Kotaku and GamesRadar+.

Focus is the ability you turn on by clicking **both sticks** (L3 + R3 on PS5, LS + RS on Xbox). It slows time in gunfights, shows enemy weak points and highlights loot. The higher your focus level, the longer you can keep it running, and BennyCentral calls it the stat you want to train the most.

## Fishing: the best method

BennyCentral, Arekkz Gaming, KhrazeGaming and GamingBolt all point to fishing. There are **fishing rods behind Jason's house**, so you don't have to travel. Grab one and walk down to the water.

1. Press **L3 + R3** to turn on focus. Fish get highlighted, so you know where to cast.
2. Cast your line, then **reel in slowly** to lure a fish.
3. When you feel a nibble, press **R2** right away to hook it.
4. Don't reel in yet. Let the fish fight until it wears itself out. If it swims one way, pull the rod the **opposite way**.
5. Once it calms down, reel in as fast as you can. If it starts fighting again, repeat step 4.

Step 1 is from Arekkz Gaming, and steps 2 to 5 are KhrazeGaming's technique. KhrazeGaming adds that fishing, like other minigames that raise your stats, also improves focus. BennyCentral found it the most effective way to train it: every fish you land moves your focus level up. He repeats it in a later tips video, where he calls fishing the quickest way he's found to grow the bar and says a few minutes on the water make a real difference. In JorRaptor's video, a **Focus +10%** pop-up appears while Jason walks down a dock with his rod during the fishing segment, though the clip doesn't make clear exactly what triggered it.

## Basketball and the snake at Jason's house

| Activity | What the channels found |
|---|---|
| Fishing | About **5% per fish** (Arekkz Gaming). |
| Basketball hoop, right outside | **1% per basket** (Arekkz Gaming). RageGamingVideos says it raises focus by a certain amount per day, which points to a daily limit. |
| Feeding the snake | Raises focus. RageGamingVideos says once per day; Arekkz Gaming says to feed it whenever you're back home. |
| Checking on the snake | Just stopping to look at the snake in the living room gives **3%**, according to BennyCentral. |

RageGamingVideos treats it as a daily routine. Stat training runs on timers, so he does a strength workout, a cardio workout and a focus workout once per timer reset, **for each character**, because Jason and Lucia are tracked separately. MrDalekJD adds that working out every day for three days in a row builds up both strength and focus.

## Permanent focus boosts

GamingBolt lists four ways to **permanently** raise focus, all without violence: **fishing, pool, golf and the shooting range**. The shooting range also helps you get used to aiming and test different settings.

Your **criminal profile** counts too. According to GamingBolt, the Professional and Hardened profiles get a higher max focus, while Violent and Psycho refill focus with headshots. The details are in [criminal profile explained](../gta-6-criminal-profile-explained/).

## Shirley's rum run

Early in the story, Shirley, the neighbor on the corner by Jason's house, yells at you as you drive past. Stop and talk to her and she asks you to pick up some rum. RageGamingVideos says that bringing it back gives a **permanent upgrade to focus**. MrDalekJD found this favor in the map's **Exploration** tab, which lists jobs for your neighbors, and a later favor for Shirley lets you use her car.

BennyCentral files these under what the game calls **thoughtful actions**, and says doing Shirley's favor is worth the same 3% as checking on the snake. Before you head off for the rum, pet **Argula**, Shirley's dog. GamesRadar+ says it counts as a thoughtful action that can push your focus bar up, depending on your criminal profile.

## Pets, garbage and food

These come from Kotaku's launch tips:

- **Pet dogs and cats.** Hold the left trigger (L2 or LT) on one to zoom in, then praise or pet it. It buffs focus and earns good behavior points for your criminal profile. The Pet option doesn't show up right away, though. In icePlaysGTA's video, aiming L2 at a labrador only brought up **Praise** (X), **Scold** (Square) and **Study** (R1). Once he praised the dog, Praise dropped off the list and **Pet** appeared on **Triangle**. So if you can't find it, praise first. The full walkthrough is in [how to pet animals](../gta-6-how-to-pet-animals/).
- **Throw away your garbage.** Not littering boosts focus and also earns good behavior points.
- **Eat and drink before heading out.** Food and drinks buff health, stamina and focus. MrDalekJD gives the same tip: eat or drink at home before you leave, especially before a story mission. Pick carefully at the fridge: BennyCentral says **water** buffs stamina and focus, while the **protein shake** buffs health and stamina instead.

DPJ also lists helping people, feeding pets and feeding your own snake as ways to earn focus. None of these sources says how much each one gives, or how long the buff lasts.
