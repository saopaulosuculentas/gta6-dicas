GTA 6's giant crab mystery can be solved. It's in a hidden lab in **Mariana County** where someone was experimenting on crabs to make them bigger. The computer password is **CLEAVES**, the safe code is **00072**, the boathouse door code is **0212**, and at the end you walk away with a stash bag and the **Crabshack VF5** SMG. Gator Keys solved it on stream, and YOUNG DIRK, Arekkz Gaming, Millzaa, RageGamingVideos and BennyCentral all ran through the same codes in their own videos.

## Where it is

- **Area:** Mariana County, in southern Leonida. YOUNG DIRK calls the area **Pelican Valley**, and so do Arekkz Gaming, Millzaa, RageGamingVideos and BennyCentral. It's most likely the name of the stretch of swamp the island sits in.
- **The spot:** a cluster of strange buildings near the water, with a **boathouse** next to them.
- **The clue:** a **blood trail** runs from the main building to the boathouse door, which has a **keypad**.
- **Getting there:** YOUNG DIRK flew in by **plane** so he wouldn't need a boat. Try to land right at the site, because the water around it is full of **alligators**.
- **Or by boat:** Arekkz Gaming and RageGamingVideos took **Jason's boat**, which is moored at the dock behind his house. Arekkz tied up at the island's little dock and walked in through the gate. It's much quicker than driving or wading.

The Skunk Ape lives in the same county; see the [Skunk Ape mission](../gta-6-skunk-ape-mission-sniper-rifle/).

## Step by step

1. **Turn on the power.** The switch is at the back of the main house. YOUNG DIRK does this before going inside.
2. **Check the back bedroom.** There are **words carved into the wood**. Look at them and use the on-screen zoom: each one becomes a password option on the computer.
3. **Use the computer** and pick **CLEAVES**. YOUNG DIRK cycles through the options with left on the D-pad.
4. **Read the journal.** It logs crab experiments. Most crabs died. One was a breakthrough: **specimen 00036**.
5. **Jump scare.** When you back out of the computer, the **giant crab attacks**. Jason kicks it off and it runs. You can't chase it.
6. **Find where it came from.** The tank it broke out of has more notes: the crabs were being raised **to eat and sell**.
7. **Go back to the computer.** A note says **safe code = double success**. The success was number 36, so the code is **36 × 2 = 00072**.
8. **Open the safe.** It holds some cash ($1,000 in YOUNG DIRK's video) and the **emergency protocol** note. Zoom in on it (the prompt in YOUNG DIRK's video is RT on Xbox, R2 on PS5) or press **Triangle (Y on Xbox)** to show the text: the number is **0212**.
9. **Enter the boathouse.** Follow the blood trail and punch **0212** into the keypad. The **Crabshack VF5** is lying on the floor right by the entrance. Further in, the person behind the experiments made it there and died. On them are a few dollars and a **stash bag for Brian**.

## The boathouse code

Gator Keys didn't read the emergency protocol number out loud. In YOUNG DIRK's video the note is on screen with **0212** (2:02), and he types that number into the door keypad (2:40). If the door won't open in your game, double-check the note from the safe.

## Reward

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Stash bag | Between $8,000 and about $10,000, depending on the video. Fence it at Brian's lockup or Donnie's ([fence locations](../gta-6-fence-locations/)) |
| Crabshack VF5 | Unique SMG with a scope and flashlight already on it. YOUNG DIRK says it goes into your weapon locker for good |
| Safe | Cash ($1,000 to almost $1,100, depending on the video) |
| Jet ski | In the boathouse garage, for your getaway |

The payout isn't the same in every video. Gator Keys puts the bag at about $10,000. YOUNG DIRK got **$8,000** from Brian for it, plus **$1,000** from the safe and $12 off the body, so roughly $9,000 in total.

The newer videos lean toward $10,000. Millzaa's bag held **$10,000 in marked bills** and Brian paid him the full amount. Arekkz Gaming says the same bag is worth $10,000 at Donnie's, and the safe gave him **$1,093** and Millzaa almost $1,100. RageGamingVideos, on the other hand, reports $8,000 in the bag and $1,000 in the safe, like YOUNG DIRK.

## A closer look at the Crabshack VF5

According to RageGamingVideos, the SMG comes with a **tactical flashlight** and **sights** already fitted, a **biohazard** wrap and a little crab sticker. Millzaa shows the same sight and the flashlight on the right side. Arekkz found it great up close: it fires very fast but isn't accurate at range. Since it's a unique variant, you can't change the attachments.

## Getting out: the jet ski

There's a **jet ski** in the boathouse garage. Millzaa and Arekkz Gaming both show it: roll up the garage shutter, get on and hotwire it to start the engine. That way you leave the island without wading back through the alligator river. Arekkz says it's faster than Jason's boat and handy for hitting other spots in the area on the same trip, like some of the [unique weapons](../gta-6-unique-weapons-locations/).

There's also a locked storage crate near the dock. According to Arekkz, it only opens with bolt cutters, which you don't get until later in the game.

## The reference

Gator Keys points to **Island Claws**, a 1980s horror movie where a marine biologist uses growth hormones to breed bigger crabs for food and a giant one breaks loose in the Florida Keys. That's the exact region GTA 6 recreates.
