Paul Walker's car is in GTA 6. It's a nod to the **green Eclipse** from *The Fast and the Furious*, decals included, and it sits in a **double garage on the Rialto Islands in Vice Beach**. DPJ found it and showed how to keep it for good.

## Where it is

- **Area:** Vice Beach, on the **Rialto Islands**, the bridge-linked islands between Vice City and the beach.
- **The spot:** a house's **double garage**, with the car inside.

DPJ shows the exact map marker in his video at 1:52.

## Wait for Moses first

You can grab it right at the start, but DPJ advises against it:

- He believes cars like this **don't respawn**. If you don't **register** it, it may be gone when you reload your save.
- Registering unlocks with **Moses** in Port Gellhorn, in **Chapter 2**.
- Registering this car cost him about **$6,750**, so save up first.

For how registering works, see [how to steal cars in GTA 6](../gta-6-how-to-steal-cars/).

## How to get it

1. Head to the garage on the Rialto Islands.
2. Break into the garage. **The alarm goes off**, but nobody showed up in the video: DPJ just waited for it to stop.
3. Before you leave, **open the trunk** and clear it out.
4. Open the shutters and drive off. You don't need the slim jim.
5. Take it straight to **Moses** and register it. Registering swaps the plates (the original has a line from the movie).

## Is it worth it?

DPJ says it's **super fast** and one of the cars most worth keeping. For more rare cars with locations and times, see [Randy's wish list cars](../gta-6-rare-car-locations-randy/).
