Beating GTA 6's story takes **roughly 45 to 90 hours**, depending on how often you wander off. If you clear every optional mission as well, plan on **80 to 100 hours**. Finding every collectible and finishing every activity pushes it **past 200 hours**. Those numbers come from critics who finished the game for review and from the GamesRadar+ estimate.

## How long reviewers took

| Who | Hours | What they did |
|---|---|---|
| Jeff Gerstmann (cited by Kotaku) | About 45, maybe 50 counting reloads | Beat the story |
| Kotaku | About 90 to the credits | Story plus plenty of side content. Now past 150 hours and still finding new things |
| PC Gamer | About 100 | Finished the main story |
| GamesRadar+ | Over 100 | Story, every optional mission and a good chunk of the activities |
| IGN (cited by PC Gamer) | 120 | Beat the game with big parts of the map still unvisited |

Kotaku reckons a faster player could wrap up the story in around **60 hours**, and doesn't think you can realistically finish it much quicker than Gerstmann's 45 to 50.

## The GamesRadar+ estimate

| Playstyle | Hours |
|---|---|
| Main story only | 60 to 80 |
| Every main and optional mission | 80 to 100 |
| Every collectible and activity | 200+ |

## Why the range is so wide

The map is huge, and it's packed with distractions. The GamesRadar+ reviewer didn't reach Lummox County until the **80-hour** mark. PC Gamer admits to losing hours just picking a point on the horizon and getting sidetracked on the way there.

In practice, sticking to the story keeps you near the 45 to 60 hour range. Stop for every store, robbery and side activity and you'll sail past 90.

## How big the story is

According to PC Gamer's list, the story has **91 missions across 5 chapters**. Every name is in our [GTA 6 mission list](../gta-6-mission-list/).

For a sense of pacing, MrDalekJD says **Chapter 1** wraps up, with the mission where you finally play as Lucia, around **6 to 10 hours** in, depending on how much you explore. The [Chapter 1 walkthrough](../gta-6-chapter-1-walkthrough/) covers it step by step.

## Compared to GTA 5

Kotaku points out that GTA 5 takes **32 hours** to rush through the story, per HowLongToBeat, and over **50** with side content. Going by the numbers above, GTA 6 asks for a lot more of your time.

## After the credits

Finishing the story doesn't end the game. You can [keep playing after the ending](../gta-6-keep-playing-after-ending/), and anyone chasing the platinum should check the [trophy and achievement list](../gta-6-trophies-achievements-list/).
