Gun stores in GTA 6 carry **one or two unique weapon variants** that only that store sells, along with up to **three bundles** of guns that come with mods already fitted. Arekkz Gaming went through three stores and the names and prices are right there on screen. He's the only channel to show them so far, so treat this as unverified for now.

## How to spot a store's unique weapon

1. Walk into any gun store and check the minimap: unique weapons show up as a **gold gun icon**.
2. Get close to the display and press **Triangle (Y on Xbox)** when **View** pops up.
3. The card shows an **Exclusive** tag, the price and a line naming the only store that sells it.

Two things the screen tells you before you buy:

- **You can't modify them.** Every one carries the "Variants can't be modified" warning. What you see is what you get.
- **You pay from BuckMe.** The price uses the banking app's icon. At Phil's, Arekkz had $6,905 in his wallet and $1,774 in BuckMe, and a $3,780 purchase was blocked for low BuckMe funds. Hit an [ATM](../gta-6-bank-atm-deposit/) first.

## Unique weapons by store

| Store | Location | Unique weapon | Type | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chuck's Tackle & Shot | Key Lento, the starter store | Mahi Nipper .38 | Compact pistol | $1,160 |
| Big Shots | Hamlet | The Silver 1922 | Heavy pistol | $2,210 |
| Big Shots | Hamlet | Sand Storm ES9 | Military pistol | $1,470 |
| Phil's Ammu-Nation | Next to Peacock Estates | Honky Tonk Mustang .357 | Magnum revolver with a red dot | $3,780 |
| Phil's Ammu-Nation | Next to Peacock Estates | Star Spangled 556 | Carbine rifle in a US flag finish | $3,050 |

The map lists Chuck's inventory as **Limited** and the other two as **Expanded**, so the bigger stores simply stock more. Arekkz rates The Silver 1922 as one of the harder-hitting pistols; its card shows 5.5 damage against 4.2 for the Sand Storm ES9.

## Bundles

Talk to the clerk (**L2** when you're close) and open **Weapons and Mods**. The first tab is **Bundles**, with up to three per store.

| Store | Bundle | Price | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chuck's Tackle & Shot | Fortune & Son Moreland 850 (shotgun) | $2,445 | Weapon and mods |
| Chuck's Tackle & Shot | Duke 556 | $1,920 | Mods only, for a rifle you already own |
| Big Shots | Kozlov KZ-59 (assault rifle) | $3,620 | Locked: buy 1 more gun |
| Big Shots | ADB FRA (battle rifle) | $6,165 | Locked: buy 1 more gun |
| Phil's Ammu-Nation | Girardi Super400 (shotgun) | $7,445 | Sold out |
| Phil's Ammu-Nation | ADB M350 (machine gun) | $13,455 | Sold out |

Watch the small print: some bundles only unlock after you **buy more guns** at that store, and others read **"Currently sold out"**. The video doesn't show when stock comes back. The Duke 556 bundle at Chuck's is mods only, and you can grab the rifle itself for free at the [early money warehouse](../gta-6-early-money-warehouse/).

## Worth it?

If you only care about performance, the free unique guns around the map cover the early game; see [GTA 6 unique weapon locations](../gta-6-unique-weapons-locations/). The store variants are for the look and for collectors. Once bought, they come back from the gun store locker if you lose them.
