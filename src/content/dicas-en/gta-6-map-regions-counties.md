The GTA 6 map of Leonida is split into **5 counties**: **Vice-Dale, Leonard, Kelly, Lummox and Mariana**. Each one has at least one landmark worth knowing, from the packed streets of Vice City down to the Key Lento islands where the story opens.

It's a lot of ground. Rockstar called the map twice the size of GTA 5's, and PC Gamer says it feels even bigger than that: crossing it takes ages, even in a plane. On November 19, 2026, Rockstar put the **full map up for download** on its website, in a higher resolution.

## All 5 counties at a glance

| County | Main locations |
|---|---|
| Vice-Dale County | Vice City, Vice Beach, VCIA Airport |
| Leonard County | Ambrosia, Lake Leonida |
| Kelly County | Port Gellhorn, Gellhorn Raceway, Capri |
| Lummox County | Mount Kalaga National Park |
| Mariana County | Mile Zero Air Station, Key Lento |

## Vice-Dale County

- **Vice City:** the centerpiece, packed with jewelry, cars and drugs to steal. It's by far the densest part of the map.
- **Vice Beach:** a long run of beachfront east of downtown.
- **VCIA Airport:** where the planes sit until somebody takes one. Our [airport guide](../gta-6-airport-steal-plane/) covers how to get in.

## Leonard County

- **Ambrosia:** the name sounds pleasant, the place less so. It's home to a biker gang and a sugar refinery.
- **Lake Leonida:** the largest inland lake on the map, right next to Ambrosia.

## Kelly County

- **Port Gellhorn:** a former tourist spot gone to seed, with dead malls and grimy hotels. These days the local money comes from drugs and drinks. The town also shows up in our [fence locations](../gta-6-fence-locations/) guide.
- **Gellhorn Raceway:** the place for track racing and drag racing.
- **Capri:** high-end shopping, expensive homes and police with zero patience for street performers.

## Lummox County

- **Mount Kalaga National Park:** the big draw in northern Leonida, with room to hunt, fish and go off-road.

## Mariana County

- **Mile Zero Air Station:** the military base in the southern keys and the easiest place to grab a tank. Walking in uninvited earns you a steep Wanted level. See [how to get into the base](../gta-6-military-base-location/).
- **Key Lento:** a strip of outlying islands. This is where you start the game as Jason.
