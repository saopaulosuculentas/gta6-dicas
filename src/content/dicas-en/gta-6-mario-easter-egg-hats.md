**GTA 6 has a Mario easter egg: a floating, half-invisible block that drops two caps.** One has a **J** for Jason, the other an **L** for Lucia, a nod to Mario and Luigi. The block sits in **crumbling brick and stone ruins in the woods southwest of Moreau, in Lummox County**, the region north of Vice City. Kotaku credits player xGarbett with finding it.

## Location

- Head to **Lummox County**, north of Vice City.
- Go into the **woods on the southwest side of Moreau**.
- Look for the **ruins of an old brick and stone building** (Kotaku).
- TheGamingRevolution opens its video by marking the spot near the **MacAlister Gold Mine** on the map, then walks into a campsite at the ruins.

## Making the block show up

The block is see-through and hangs in the air **near the top of one of the tallest parts of the ruin**. Kotaku's writer couldn't spot it from the ground. It only showed up once he climbed the ruins the same way xGarbett did.

1. Get to the ruins.
2. Climb to the top of the walls.
3. Look for a faint cube floating above you.

## Breaking it

- **Jump into it, Mario style.** That's how xGarbett and TheGamingRevolution do it (1:15 in the video).
- **Shoot it.** Kotaku's writer couldn't get Jason to break it with a jump, so he fired at it and the hats popped right out.

If jumping doesn't work after a couple of tries, just shoot it.

## The reward

You get **two baseball caps**: the **J** cap for Jason and the **L** cap for Lucia. TheGamingRevolution shows Jason wearing his at 1:33. Kotaku's writer grabbed both, called Lucia and headed to a clothing store so the pair could dress up together.

IGN's video jokes that wearing your initial on your head while committing crimes isn't the smartest move. Still, the hats are yours.
