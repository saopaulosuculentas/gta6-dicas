GTA 6 marks **illegal operations** on the map that you can raid, and a few of them hold far more than cash: a **legendary assault rifle**, safes, luxury watches and even a supercar with **no tracker**. Here are two that TikTok creators showed on Nov 21 and 22, step by step.

Each spot comes from one creator so far, so this page is **unverified**. Cash amounts are what showed up in the videos and may differ in your game.

## Gated house just north of Little Cuba (Guardian Brothers AR)

Shown by **1deflay**, who says the whole run takes about two minutes and adds up to roughly $50,000 counting the car.

1. Set a waypoint just **above Little Cuba** in Vice City. A brown van pulls into a gated yard when you arrive.
2. Head to the back and climb the **wall with no spikes** on top. Nobody guards that side.
3. Parked there is a **nitrous Mercedes AMG**, unlocked and tracker free. The creator says it sells for about $8,500.
4. Use the AC units outside to get onto the **roof**, then drop through the hole. There's a watch and other loot on the table.
5. Go down one level. The room on the left has cash. Someone is standing at the safe: take them out silently for the stealth bonus. The safe gave the creator about $2,700 and $6,300.
6. On the same floor, drop down through another room to find **three enemies**. With a suppressed gun, nobody raises the alarm.
7. In the back you'll find **body armor** and the legendary **Guardian Brothers AR**. The center table holds the **stash bags**: two of them paid **$12,600 each** in the video.
8. Leave through the red door with the exit sign. The Mercedes is on your left.

## Vespucci Island mansion (legendary rifle and a free Emerus)

Shown by **mrgunsenburner**, who says you can do it at any point in the story.

1. Set a waypoint to **Vespucci Island**, just east of Vice City.
2. Hop the wall on the **left** and head upstairs. A suppressed pistol makes this much easier.
3. Take out the **two guards** up top and go through the door.
4. Make your way to the **office in the back right**. The **legendary assault rifle** is there, and it stays with you even in Chapter 1. **Body armor** hangs on the wall next to it.
5. There are **two safes**, one on each side of the room, both with cash.
6. Back in the garage, the red **Emerus** is unlocked with no tracker. Open the gate and drive off.

Vespucci Island is also where you'll find [Paul Walker's car](../gta-6-paul-walker-eclipse-car/).
