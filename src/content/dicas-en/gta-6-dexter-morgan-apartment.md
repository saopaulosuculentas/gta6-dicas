**GTA 6 has Dexter Morgan's apartment building from the Dexter TV series in Vice City.** It's on **Washington Island**, in the east of the city. His door is **locked**, but the outdoor hallway matches the end of the show's opening sequence. GameSpot and IGN both show it.

## Where it is

1. On the map, go to **Washington Island**, in eastern Vice City, east by northeast of Penmarch Isle.
2. Find the **Pay 'n' Spray** and **Vice Beach Top Fun Drones** icons.
3. Between them is a **small light blue building** with a **cylinder** on its south side, between a bigger building with a rectangle on its roof and a smaller apartment complex.
4. The cylinder is a **staircase**. Take it to the **second floor** and head **west** along the outdoor hallway, with apartments on your left.
5. Dexter's is the **last one on the row**, a corner unit with a water view.

## Other nods to the show

According to GameSpot:

- The building is called **Trinity Apartments**, a nod to the **Trinity Killer** from Season 4.
- On the **roof** you'll find Dexter's **tools**: a knife, tape, a syringe, a plastic bag and rope, according to IGN.
- A **boat** docked out front looks a lot like Dexter's **Slice of Life**. You can take it.

## The hidden kill room

IGN found one more nod, away from the apartment: a **plastic-wrapped room** like Dexter's, in the **garage of a house on the Key Lento coast**.

1. Take a boat to the coastal house IGN shows.
2. You can go inside, but an **armed man** doesn't like visitors. Take him down.
3. The house has weapons: a **sniper rifle**, body armor and a **rocket launcher** in the bathtub.
4. If you were loud, leave and wait for the cops to go. Then come back and **break into the garage**: the plastic room is inside, with tools and bags.
