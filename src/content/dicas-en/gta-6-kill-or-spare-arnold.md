**Spare Arnold.** In the Chapter 1 mission **Hey Arnold!**, which GamesRadar+ calls the story's first major moral choice, letting him go is easier on your criminal profile, and killing him doesn't earn you anything. The scene that follows is almost identical either way. What really changes is your profile, plus a possible Arnold cameo later on.

## How the mission gets you there

Hey Arnold! comes after Pipework in the [mission list](../gta-6-mission-list/). Arnold is Brian's business partner and he's gone quiet. According to GamesRadar+, he's unhappy with how Brian runs the drug side of things.

1. Jason, Brian and Cal track Arnold down at a **radar station near the Aerostat**.
2. After a heated exchange, Arnold and his crew bolt in a **pickup truck**. A car chase with gunfire follows.
3. The chase ends at the **Key Lento marina**. Arnold ditches the truck and runs. Brian tells Jason to shut him up, however he sees fit.
4. Chase him on foot. Either you tackle him on the way, or he runs out of room at the **end of the pier**. Then he starts begging.

## Every choice and what it does

| Choice | How | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Spare | Hold **L2** (LT) to interact, then press **X** (A) on **Spare** | Jason threatens Arnold and lets him go. Counts toward a more restrained profile |
| Kill on the pier | Shoot him while he's begging | A warning says indiscriminate violence leads to a violent profile |
| Kill during the chase | Shoot Arnold or his truck before the pier | Faster, but counts toward a ruthless profile |
| Do nothing | Wait on the pier | Arnold leaves. PC Gamer says this counts the same as sparing him |

The guides don't agree on the labels. GamesRadar+ calls it a **Restrained** contribution for sparing and **Ruthless** for the chase kill. PC Gamer says killing him gives you a **Hardened** rating. None of those match the five tiers in the [criminal profile](../gta-6-criminal-profile-explained/) exactly, so the simple read is this: sparing leans clean, killing leans violent.

## Does the next scene change?

Barely. GamesRadar+ says the cutscene after your choice is the same. Gamerpillar and Red Dead Family recorded both versions, and only one line is different. At Brian's party he asks if the job is handled:

- **If you killed him:** Jason says it's taken care of.
- **If you spared him:** Jason says Arnold will keep his mouth shut, and Brian likes hearing that.

According to GamesRadar+, when you spare him Arnold slips away and Brian never finds out he was let go.

At the same party Brian introduces **Kenton**, who opens up the diving jobs, as Syed Abbas Sheerazi's video shows. See [sunken treasure and shipwrecks](../gta-6-sunken-treasure-shipwrecks/).

## The long-term consequence

PC Gamer writes that sparing him leads to an appearance in **a later mission**. The wording is muddled: it says **Brian** shows up later if you let "him" live. Brian is in the story either way, so it most likely means Arnold. It doesn't say which mission or what changes there. No guide or video has shown that return yet, so the long-term payoff **hasn't been shown so far**. PC Gamer adds that there's no downside to sparing him and no bonus for killing him.
