**No story spoilers:** this page only covers how many endings exist and which game systems drive them. Nothing about what actually happens.

GTA 6 has **at least 4 endings**, based on reviewers and players who have already finished the story, as GameSpot reports. Rockstar itself confirmed to GameSpot that there is **more than one ending**. The two things that matter most are **Jason and Lucia's relationship** and **how much money sits in the couple's savings account**.

## How many endings people have seen

- **Rockstar:** confirmed to GameSpot that the game has multiple endings, without giving a number.
- **GameSpot:** its team finished the game and saw **2 endings**. Adding what other players have reported, the site counts **at least 4**.
- **Kotaku:** its reviewer also saw **2 endings** and says they play out in very different ways.

That count could go up as more people finish the game in different ways.

## What decides your ending

GameSpot points to **two late-game systems**:

1. **The couple's relationship.** The closer Jason and Lucia are, the better.
2. **The shared savings account.** It tops out at **$2 million**.

| Ending | What you need |
|---|---|
| The best one | Maxed-out relationship and full savings ($2 million) |
| A good one | Maxed-out relationship without filling the savings |
| The rest | Still being tested. GameSpot expects one of them comes from hitting neither goal |

To raise the relationship, see [Jason and Lucia's romance guide](../gta-6-romance-jason-lucia/). To build up that $2 million, see [how to make money in GTA 6](../gta-6-how-to-make-money/), and remember to bank what you earn.

## What isn't confirmed yet

- **Criminal profile.** Some players think each character's [criminal profile](../gta-6-criminal-profile-explained/) counts too, since it shifts depending on whether you kill or spare people. Nobody knows yet whether it touches the ending.
- **Mission choices.** GameSpot says some story decisions could matter at the end, such as [whether you give the statue to Benedict Halifax](../gta-6-benedict-halifax-statue/). That hasn't been confirmed either.

## Scenes change along the way, too

It's not just the finale. Rockstar senior design director Luke Howard told Game Informer that several story scenes were captured in **different versions** depending on the couple's relationship and criminal profile, ranging from subtle to more significant changes, as GamesRadar+ reports.

His example: after Lucia pulls off a tense armored truck robbery, Jason pulls her aside to check on her. That moment has several versions, with the couple closer or more distant. If the romance fizzles, they stay partners in crime and the story doesn't lose anything for it.

## You can see another ending

You can. According to Kotaku, once the credits roll the game **takes you back to just before the final mission**, and its **marker stays on the map**. You can fix whatever you missed and play the finale again. How that works is covered in [keep playing after the ending](../gta-6-keep-playing-after-ending/).
