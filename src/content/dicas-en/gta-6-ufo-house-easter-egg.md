The UFO house is in GTA 6. Back in 2021, Rockstar slipped an image of a house with a flying saucer over it into the GTA San Andreas remaster, and fans bet it was a hint at the next game. ZacCoxTV found that same house in **Bellville, Leonard County**, and Milo got inside too, holding it up against the 2021 image. InkTV is the third channel to show the place inside and out. The front door is fake, but the **garage opens**, and whoever lives there is deep into conspiracy theories.

## Where it is

- **Region:** Leonard County, north of Vice City. InkTV puts it in the southern part of Leonard County, just above Vice-Dale County.
- **Town:** Bellville, a small neighborhood of houses with white picket fences.
- **The house:** yellow, with a **double garage** and a **for-sale sign** in the yard. There's usually a car parked out front.

The map is huge, so ZacCoxTV recommends flying in by helicopter. From the air you can also spot a rude joke a neighbor mowed into his lawn.

## How to get in

1. Head to the house in Bellville.
2. The front door and windows don't open. Go to the **garage**.
3. Get close and the game shows a **break in** prompt. Break into the garage and walk in.

Breaking in calls the cops. ZacCoxTV got a wanted level right away and was busted on his first try. There's also a **dog in the yard** and a **snake** in the garage that attacked him. Save before you go.

## The car out front

Milo found one more scare: pop the **trunk of the car parked at the house** and a **huge snake** comes out. That was all he found in there.

InkTV opened the same trunk and got something else entirely: an **H&L .38 revolver** and nothing more. So the trunk contents seem to change between playthroughs. Either way, open it carefully.

## What's inside

The garage is a paranoid neighbor's hideout:

- **Conspiracy boards:** clippings about a woman who walks through walls, "America's most abducted man" and the Skunk Ape. Milo also found a newspaper story about that woman.
- **A Skunk Ape magazine:** Milo found it lying on the bed. The cover InkTV shows has the Skunk Ape standing on top of pyramids, with more mystery stories inside. It's about the creature that has its own mission in the game (see the [Skunk Ape mission](../gta-6-skunk-ape-mission-sniper-rifle/)).
- **More boards:** InkTV also points out a drawing of a UFO lifting a car and the all-seeing eye symbol that GTA V players will recognize.
- **Radio and food:** a radio and snacks you can pick up, including a meteorite-themed candy bar.
- **A piece of meteorite:** you can grab it and sell it. In the game, a buyer on the Lifeinvader marketplace says they'll pay for what you find.
- **A collectible card:** a card from **The Legends of Ratface** set, which counts toward the game's card collection.

ZacCoxTV didn't find anything else on his first visit. The clippings could be hinting at other secrets, but so far nobody has shown an actual UFO connection. Another UFO hunter's hideout sits in the watchtower shed on Mount Kalaga, behind a keypad; see the [easter eggs list](../gta-6-easter-eggs-list/). For an actual flying saucer, see the [UFO abduction in Kelly County](../gta-6-ufo-abduction-truth-is-out-there/).

## The owner worked out they're in a game

PC Gamer walked into the garage by day and found a huge board with **"IT'S A SIMULATION"** in red. The notes ask why the owner can't leave Leonida, count **140 identical people** and puzzle over the number 6 and who "L" and "J" are. Boat brochures lie around, so the joke is that they plan to sail for the edge of the map.

## Why it's an easter egg

The UFO house image showed up in the San Andreas remaster in 2021. Finding that exact house in GTA 6, covered in abduction clippings, is Rockstar landing the joke five years later.
