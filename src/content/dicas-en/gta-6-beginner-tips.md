GTA 6 changes a lot compared with GTA 5, and some of those changes cost you if you don't know about them. Here are the beginner tips DPJ and WiLLiSGaming showed in gameplay, plus extra ones from MrDalekJD, KultZ, Arekkz Gaming and other channels.

## Before you play: settings

1. **Map on the View button.** DPJ's trick: in Settings, set the View button to camera, zoom the camera all the way out, then set the button back to **map**. You keep the wider camera and the map is one press away.
2. **Run by holding the stick.** In the movement options, set running to **holding the left stick forward** and movement speed to "walk fast". It helps when you need to escape. BennyCentral likes a different speed, **jog**: a slight push on the stick walks, and pushing it all the way jogs, while clicking the left stick still sprints. It comes down to taste, so try both.
3. **Drive-by aim assist** on: aiming manually while driving is hard at first.
4. **Sensitivity:** if aiming feels sluggish, raise look sensitivity and aim acceleration a little (DPJ uses 9).
5. **Vehicle camera:** if it lags behind on turns, set the follow speed to around 5 or 6.

## Money

6. **Stash your cash.** If you die or get busted, you lose the money on you. Keep it under the floorboards at home and carry only what you'll spend. Once the bank unlocks through the story, MrDalekJD's habit is to stop at an ATM before anything risky. See [do you lose money when you die](../gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die/) and [how the bank and ATMs work](../gta-6-bank-atm-deposit/).
7. **The $5,000 goal.** Early on, Lucia wants a bank account but Jason has bad credit, so the bank gives him the **BuckMe** app. You need to save **$5,000** in it before the story moves on, according to WiLLiSGaming.
8. **Chapter 1 money resets.** WiLLiSGaming warns that your cash goes back to zero when chapter 2 starts. Only the family savings in BuckMe stay. Don't grind a fortune in chapter 1. See [how to make money fast](../gta-6-how-to-make-money/).
9. **Check the mailbox.** After main missions there's often cash in the mailbox at Jason's house, usually with a text from Donnie.
10. **Loot the bodies.** MrDalekJD's advice: don't drive off the second a shootout ends. Downed enemies can drop cash, ammo and weapons, and when there are several of them it adds up fast, which matters early on when money and ammo are tight. Just remember that loose cash is only safe once you've stashed or banked it.

## Push the story

11. **A lot is locked behind progress.** Better cars, weapons, phone apps and faster money all depend on the story, DPJ says. You don't need to rush, but move on until you get the tools, like the slim jim and key cloner in [how to steal cars](../gta-6-how-to-steal-cars/). Other channels agree and give examples:
    - **MrDalekJD:** the bank and the slim jim both come through the story.
    - **Arekkz Gaming:** car customization, your bank account and the places where you sell valuables.
    - **KhrazeGaming:** key cloners, extra garages to register more cars and access to pricier places to break into. He says you'll have most of it by the middle of the story.

## Your phone is your quest log

12. **Pending jobs live on your phone.** There's no reliable journal. Press Up on the D-pad: messages from characters list suggested jobs and past ones, and remind you of money waiting for you.
13. **Apps worth knowing:** Waink It (scans cars), Snapmatic (the in-game social network, with camera and gallery), weather, contacts and the fitness app, which tracks your workout window and Focus. For controls the game never explains (flipping cars, sitting, RydeMe fast travel), see [GTA 6 hidden features](../gta-6-hidden-features/), and to find missions and shops on the map, see the [index guide](../gta-6-index-find-missions/).

## Police and time

14. **The cops remember you.** They track your car, weapon and outfit. Change clothes fast: hold **L1 (LB on Xbox) and press R1 (RB)** to take off pieces, like your hat. Keep outfits in your car's trunk to change away from home. More in [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).
15. **Skip time at your bed.** Walk up to your bed at home to skip hours, handy for timed missions or to skip the night. MrDalekJD points out that you get to choose the time you wake up, which beats waiting around.

## Relationship with Lucia

It's optional: she's your partner in crime either way. If you want to build the relationship, answer her calls, fix up the house (steps, patio fence, balcony, bedroom paint) and bring some cash when you pick her up so you can buy her a beer.

Arekkz Gaming counts **five repair jobs** around the house, and each one gives the relationship a bump:

- patch the bedroom wall;
- paint the bedroom (you pick the color once the wall is fixed);
- fit new planks on the outside stairs;
- fix the roof, reached by the ladder next to the front door;
- repair the railing at the back, near the gym equipment.

More in [Jason and Lucia's relationship](../gta-6-romance-jason-lucia/).

### Lucia's optional errands

At one point in chapter 1, Lucia calls with **five optional errands**. KultZ went through all of them:

- **Groceries:** carry the bags all the way into the house. Leaving them in the trunk doesn't count.
- **Flowers** in South Key Lento.
- **A haircut** for Jason.
- **Lucia's necklace** at the Siren's Chest store. Budget around $500. He says the gift comes up again later in the story.
- **Her clothes:** pick up the call from an unknown number. A friend of Lucia's has her clothes at the Manatee's Rest bar. Put the bag in the trunk and drive that same car to the prison to pick her up.

KultZ suggests keeping enough cash on you for these errands and stashing the rest.

## Ultimate Edition

If you bought the Ultimate Edition, the extras are in the pause menu under Store. Many unlock in chapter 1: car skins show up at any mod shop, and weapon skins in the locker at Ammu-Nation.

**Don't forget the car.** Arekkz Gaming reminds Ultimate owners about the free upgrade for Jason's car. It unlocks after the car repair mission early in the story: that opens customization, and there you can pick the Ultimate Edition version, which he says comes with better stats and its own paint job. The full list of extras is in [how to claim the Ultimate Edition bonuses](../gta-6-ultimate-edition-bonuses/).
