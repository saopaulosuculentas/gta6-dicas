GTA 6 changes a lot compared with GTA 5, and some of those changes cost you if you don't know about them. Here are the beginner tips DPJ and WiLLiSGaming showed in gameplay.

## Before you play: settings

1. **Map on the View button.** DPJ's trick: in Settings, set the View button to camera, zoom the camera all the way out, then set the button back to **map**. You keep the wider camera and the map is one press away.
2. **Run by holding the stick.** In the movement options, set running to **holding the left stick forward** and movement speed to "walk fast". It helps when you need to escape.
3. **Drive-by aim assist** on: aiming manually while driving is hard at first.
4. **Sensitivity:** if aiming feels sluggish, raise look sensitivity and aim acceleration a little (DPJ uses 9).
5. **Vehicle camera:** if it lags behind on turns, set the follow speed to around 5 or 6.

## Money

6. **Stash your cash.** If you die or get busted, you lose the money on you. Keep it under the floorboards at home and carry only what you'll spend. See [do you lose money when you die](../gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die/).
7. **The $5,000 goal.** Early on, Lucia wants a bank account but Jason has bad credit, so the bank gives him the **BuckMe** app. You need to save **$5,000** in it before the story moves on, according to WiLLiSGaming.
8. **Chapter 1 money resets.** WiLLiSGaming warns that your cash goes back to zero when chapter 2 starts. Only the family savings in BuckMe stay. Don't grind a fortune in chapter 1. See [how to make money fast](../gta-6-how-to-make-money/).
9. **Check the mailbox.** After main missions there's often cash in the mailbox at Jason's house, usually with a text from Donnie.

## Push the story

10. **A lot is locked behind progress.** Better cars, weapons, phone apps and faster money all depend on the story, DPJ says. You don't need to rush, but move on until you get the tools, like the slim jim and key cloner in [how to steal cars](../gta-6-how-to-steal-cars/).

## Your phone is your quest log

11. **Pending jobs live on your phone.** There's no reliable journal. Press Up on the D-pad: messages from characters list suggested jobs and past ones, and remind you of money waiting for you.
12. **Apps worth knowing:** Waink It (scans cars), Snapmatic (the in-game social network, with camera and gallery), weather, contacts and the fitness app, which tracks your workout window and Focus. For controls the game never explains (flipping cars, sitting, RydeMe fast travel), see [GTA 6 hidden features](../gta-6-hidden-features/), and to find missions and shops on the map, see the [index guide](../gta-6-index-find-missions/).

## Police and time

13. **The cops remember you.** They track your car, weapon and outfit. Change clothes fast: hold **L1 (LB on Xbox) and press R1 (RB)** to take off pieces, like your hat. Keep outfits in your car's trunk to change away from home. More in [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).
14. **Skip time at your bed.** Walk up to your bed at home to skip hours, handy for timed missions or to skip the night.

## Relationship with Lucia

It's optional: she's your partner in crime either way. If you want to build the relationship, answer her calls, fix up the house (steps, patio fence, balcony, bedroom paint) and bring some cash when you pick her up so you can buy her a beer.

## Ultimate Edition

If you bought the Ultimate Edition, the extras are in the pause menu under Store. Many unlock in chapter 1: car skins show up at any mod shop, and weapon skins in the locker at Ammu-Nation.
