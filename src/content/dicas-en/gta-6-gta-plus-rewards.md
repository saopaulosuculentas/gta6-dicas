A GTA+ membership gives you a handful of story mode extras in GTA 6: an **exclusive '90s Annis Venturas Blvd Elegy**, a **single-car garage in Crosstown**, free transit and rentals, **10% more** when you sell your own cars and **10% off** at a bunch of shops. None of it is required to finish the game. GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer both published the full list, and their breakdowns match.

## Every GTA+ perk in GTA 6

| Perk | What you get |
|---|---|
| Annis Venturas Blvd Elegy | A '90s sports coupe with top-tier tuning and an exclusive widebody look |
| Crosstown garage | One parking spot, for the Elegy or any other car you own |
| Public transit | Free rides |
| Rentals | Free bike, Seashark (jet ski) and scooter rentals |
| RydeMe | Cheaper fares in the rideshare app |
| Scooter Bros | Two extra car delivery credits per day |
| Car sales | 10% more for owned vehicles sold at Pay 'n' Spray |
| Stores | 10% off weapons, clothes, haircuts, tattoos and vehicle mods at participating shops |

## Things to know before you subscribe

- **Some perks unlock later.** GamesRadar+ says the RydeMe discount kicks in once you get the app in Chapter 1, and the Scooter Bros upgrades arrive from Chapter 3 on.
- **You need to be online.** Claiming the perks takes an active membership and an internet connection.
- **Car sales:** the 10% bonus covers owned cars sold at Pay 'n' Spray. Ultimate Edition vehicles can't be sold at all. More in [how to sell cars in GTA 6](../gta-6-how-to-sell-cars/).
- **Store discounts:** not every shop takes part. In the in-game index, eligible businesses have a **small yellow icon** above their name. See how the [index](../gta-6-index-find-missions/) works.
- **The Elegy:** GamesRadar+ calls it a solid first car for street races once you reach Vice City. The Crosstown garage is GTA+ only; the rest are in [GTA 6 garage locations and prices](../gta-6-garage-locations-prices/).

## Price and the free month

GTA+ costs **$7.99**, **€7.99** or **£6.99** a month on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It renews every month and can be canceled at any time. You can sign up through the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store or straight from the GTA 6 menu.

If you pre-ordered, you already have **one free month** waiting: open the menu, go to the **Store** tab and redeem it. After that it **renews automatically at the regular price** unless you cancel. The details of the pre-order month are covered in [does GTA 6 have online multiplayer](../gta-6-online-multiplayer-2-player/).

## The GTA Online side

PC Gamer points out that subscribing through GTA 6 also gets you the GTA Online version of GTA+, which includes **$500,000 in-game cash every month**, exclusive cars and discounts there.

## Is it worth it?

Both outlets treat it as a bonus rather than a must. GamesRadar+ calls the perks nice to have but not essential. PC Gamer is blunter: the game already has more than 500 vehicles, and by midgame nobody is sweating rideshare fares or haircut prices. Its take is that GTA+ only really adds up if you also want the GTA Online perks. If you don't, cancel before the free month runs out.
