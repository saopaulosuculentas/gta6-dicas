**Randy**, the high-end car buyer in Balsero (unlocked in Chapter 3), keeps a **wish list** of rare cars. Each one sits in a set spot, sometimes only at a certain time. How to Everything's video goes through 10 of them. Most open with the **key cloner**; see [how to steal cars](../gta-6-how-to-steal-cars/) for when you get it.

You don't have to hand them over: you can keep the car instead. For where Randy is, see [fence locations](../gta-6-fence-locations/).

## All 10 cars and where to find them

| Car | Where | When |
|---|---|---|
| Bravado Banshee | Outside the Sweet Bay Inn, East Buckley Drive, Capri | Around 3 a.m. |
| Pegassi Tempesta | NE 3rd Street, Vice City (Vice-Dale County), during a tow truck event | Around 1 p.m. |
| Pegassi Infernus | 7th floor of the Crossroads Town police station garage | Any time |
| Pegassi Vacca | Villa on Flamingo Peak Way, Waning Sands, east of Bellville (Leonard County) | Any time |
| Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire | M7 junkyard east of Boone Speedway, off Galena St, Hamlet | Any time |
| Mamba GT | Mansion in Leaf Links, Vice Beach | Added to the list after a mission |
| Ascot Paragon | Outside the Banana Hole bar, Shore Drive, Ocean Beach | Around 6 p.m. |
| Pegassi Furia | Mansion on Palomino Lane, Peacock Estates, Peacock Bay | Any time |
| Pegassi Toros | Bayside Drive, Costa Retreat (west of Gloriana Key) | Around 6:30 p.m. |
| Pfister Growler | Multi-story car park on Fig Street, Ocean Beach | Around 5 p.m. |

## How to get each one

### Banshee, Paragon, Toros and Growler: parked
Show up at the right time, open the car with the **key cloner** and drive off. The **Paragon has a tracker**, so keep your speed up so the cops can't trace you. The Toros has some security around, so be quick.

### Tempesta: the tow truck
It's a **random event**: a repo man is towing the owner's car. Threaten the owner to get him out of the picture, release the Tempesta from the tow truck and leave. The Waink app shows its sale value.

### Infernus: inside the police station
1. Go to the **Crossroads Town police station** and wait for a cop to open the **gate**.
2. Sneak in without drawing attention and head up to the **7th floor**.
3. Open the Infernus with the key cloner and drive up to the **top floor**.
4. There's a **stunt jump** up there: use it to escape.
5. The car has a **tracker**, so stay fast.

### Vacca: villa garage
Hop the fence. If you want a quick getaway, plant explosives on the gate first without setting them off. The Vacca is in the garage **closest to the house, on the right** as you face the entrance. Clone the key, blow the gate and go.

### Gauntlet Hellfire: junkyard
You can take it without anyone noticing. **Open the entrance gates first**, sneak around the **right side** of the junkyard and grab the car at the back.

### Mamba GT: you need the key
The Leaf Links mansion has **full security**. Take the path on the right and use cover. The car is behind the building on the right, but you need its **key**: get into the house with the **snap gun** on the doors. The key is in a **bowl on the table behind the sofa**.

### Furia: over the hedge
Skip the gate. Go left and hop the **hedge**. You'll end up between the water and a garage. The Furia is inside: clone the key, open the shutters and drive out.

## Keeping the car

DPJ explains that to keep a rare car for good, you have to **register** it with **Moses** in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2). Registering swaps the plates and isn't cheap: he paid about **$6,750** for a rare car. If you grab one before you have Moses, it may be gone when you reload your game. See also [Paul Walker's car](../gta-6-paul-walker-eclipse-car/) and the [GTA 6 cars list](../gta-6-cars-list/).

The locations and times come from a single channel, so this page is unverified for now.
