To raise Jason and Lucia's relationship in GTA 6, act like a couple: hold hands, talk, go out together, answer texts and pull off crimes as a team. Every action that counts plays a chime and puts a **+♥** on the right side of the screen, according to PC Gamer. There are **4 levels**. The couple starts Chapter 1 on good terms, but reaching the top still takes a while.

## The 4 levels

| Level | Name |
|---|---|
| 1 of 4 | Running on empty |
| 2 of 4 | Partners in Crime |
| 3 of 4 | Crazy in Love |
| 4 of 4 | Hot Together |

Your level moves with how you play and what you choose, in story missions, in random crimes and in everyday life at home.

## What adds hearts

PC Gamer lists these, and says there are plenty more:

- **Affection:** holding hands, a hug or a kiss (look for the prompt with a heart), greeting your partner when you meet up, and complimenting new clothes or a new haircut.
- **At home:** going to bed at the same time, hanging out on the couch, starting a conversation, and picking **Go Out** when you're both home.
- **Dates:** getting your hair done together, minigolf, scuba diving, dancing at a club, and jumping out of a plane together with a [parachute](../gta-6-parachute-location/).
- **Gifts:** buying food or drinks and handing them over, or buying clothing accessories for each other. Both of you have to be at the store.
- **Phone:** replying to texts, checking in by text, sending selfies, and texting goodnight when you're away from home.
- **Manners:** opening the car door for each other.
- **Money and crime:** successful joint crimes and deposits into the **Buck Me** savings account.

For more early game advice, including fixing up the house, see our [beginner tips](../gta-6-beginner-tips/). Shopping together also comes up in [hidden features](../gta-6-hidden-features/).

## What costs hearts

You can lose points too. It didn't happen often in PC Gamer's run, and these were the causes:

- failing a joint mission;
- ignoring your partner for a while;
- some mission choices your partner doesn't agree with;
- hitting them with your car.

## Play as both of them

Rockstar's advice is that a strong relationship needs both sides. Be good to Lucia while you play as Jason, and be good to Jason while you play as Lucia. PC Gamer's writer finished the story at 4/4 after spending almost the same amount of time with each character.

They each have solo missions, storylines and activities, and some of those are required. You can spend long stretches with one character. Just check in with your partner now and then, answer their texts, and switch over every so often.

## What you get out of it

- **Crimes go better as a pair.** A second person can hold people at gunpoint, handle crowd control during a robbery, shoot out a cop's tires while you drive, and carry off more loot than one person could.
- **Intimate couple scenes.** You can't trigger them on demand. The game offers them at certain moments, shown by double hearts over the home icon on the map or by a short timed prompt. In PC Gamer's playthrough, every one happened at 4/4, including one after a big savings milestone and one after a large heist.
- **What changes between 4/4 and 1/4 is still unknown.** PC Gamer has played the story once and kept the relationship high almost the whole time, so it can't say what a weak relationship does yet. We'll update this page when someone tests it.
