The bulldozer cheat (hvy bulldozer) in GTA 6 is **Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, Right, Right, Down**. What it does: spawns an HVY Bulldozer. It is one of 21 vehicle cheats and one of the few with a published sequence.

## How to enter it

1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, Right, Right, Down.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to **find the HVY Bulldozer in the world so it is logged in the Progress menu**. Progress you made during the story counts.

## What you need to know

- No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
- Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
- The Maibatsu Boomazoom cheat is Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Left. The MTL Pumper ends in Up instead of Down. All of them are in the [full cheats list](../all-gta-6-cheats-list/).
- The sequence matches in Bubs, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer, so the badge is "Confirmed".
