Hair and beards **don't grow over time** in GTA 6. The GamesRadar+ review spells it out: unlike Arthur Morgan in Red Dead Redemption 2, Jason and Lucia keep the same look until you decide to change it. Their **bodies do change**, though. Food and exercise shift weight and muscle, just less dramatically than you might expect.

## Hair and beards only change when you change them

Since nothing grows back by itself, you have two ways to adjust your look.

**1. The bathroom mirror at home.** Kotaku says you can shave your beard and your head there, but you can't choose a new style. MrBossFTW's footage backs that up: the mirror's Hairstyle menu offered a single option, **Buzz Cut**, and a separate Body Hair menu let him shave Jason's chest.

MrBossFTW sums it up this way: at the mirror, Jason can switch between a few cuts and his facial hair, and Lucia can change her makeup and hair. For a style that's different from your current one, you need a salon.

**2. A salon.** It's the only way to get long hair back. Kotaku jokes that only Vice City's barbers have the power to make hair regrow instantly. Like every store in the game, salons are open 24 hours a day. Our guide to [mask, clothing, tattoo and barber shops](../gta-6-mask-clothes-tattoo-barber-shops/) shows where to find them.

The practical takeaway: **think twice before you shave it all off**. Going back costs you a salon visit.

## Weight changes, but only a little

In its review, GamesRadar+ says eating and exercising affect how defined Jason's and Lucia's bodies look, and resting helps offset the wear and tear of a criminal lifestyle. But weight and health swings are **milder** than in Red Dead Redemption 2, and you're never at the mercy of the system.

To keep an eye on your progress, open the **fitness app** on your phone. GamingBolt points out that it shows your strength, cardio and focus, so you can see which one needs work.

## Can you make Jason or Lucia fat?

Yes, but not very fat. Within hours of launch, players hit the **maximum weight** for both characters, and GamesRadar+ shared the results: neither looks as big as Arthur Morgan at his heaviest.

The player behind "True Fat Jason" posted the steps:

1. Eat at least **four burgers or tacos** from the fast food chains.
2. Go to sleep.
3. Eat more fast food.
4. Damage your car until the game prompts a repair. According to the player, the repair's loading screen gives the calories time to sink in.
5. **Skip workouts** with both characters.

This is a player's method passed along by GamesRadar+, not an official rule from Rockstar. The same player also showed an "Overweight Strong" Jason with plenty of both fat and muscle, built from heavy workouts plus a fast food diet.

## Building muscle instead

Going the other way means training. MrDalekJD explains that weights build strength and health, while the treadmill, running and the punching bag build stamina. Want an extra reason to hit the gym? Try the [Gym Bro challenge](../gta-6-gym-bro-dbag-1000/) for $1,000, and check the [beginner tips](../gta-6-beginner-tips/) for more early-game advice.

## Still unknown

- How long it takes for body changes to become clearly visible.
- Whether muscle fades if you stop training. Nobody has shown that in the released game yet.
- Whether weight affects running or fighting.
