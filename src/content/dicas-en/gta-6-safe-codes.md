**There's no single safe code in GTA 6.** In story missions the game gives you the combination. In **robberies**, you find it on the spot or through a clue that may be in another hideout. Many are **4 digits**. PC Gamer explains the two ways, and MrDalekJD and Arekkz Gaming both cover them in their beginner videos.

## Option 1: find it written down

The code is often written somewhere inside the store or bank:

- on a note on a **board in the back office**;
- carried by a **guard** you knocked out.

Turn on **Focus mode** (press **L3 and R3** together) to highlight important objects. MrDalekJD points out that Focus lights up notes with safe codes on them, along with enemies behind walls and the source of nearby noises. His habit: switch it on every time you walk into a new place, before you start digging through the room.

## Option 2: threaten an employee

Aim your weapon at an employee. You get **Threaten** and **Safe Code?** prompts. Pick the safe code and they'll hand it over. In some robberies this also makes the cashier empty the register, like in older GTA games.

According to Arekkz Gaming, some clerks will even let you know the store has a safe and give up the combination once you pull a gun on them. In the staff area at the back of a shop, Focus also highlights the **safe itself** and anything else you can interact with.

## Some codes are fixed

This page used to say every robbery code was randomized. **That was wrong.** On November 21, PC Gamer reported that the bowling alley code, like many other robbery codes, **isn't randomized**.

- **Kingpin Bowling: 0300**, the same number at PC Gamer and GamesRadar+. See the [bowling alley safe code](../gta-6-bowling-alley-safe-code/).

That doesn't mean they all are. If a number from a video fails, look for the code in your own game.

## Opening the safe

Once you have the code, it appears in the **lower right corner** when you interact with the safe. Enter the 4 digits and it opens.

Be quick: the sooner it's open, the sooner your bag is full and you're gone.

## Pick your target on the map

Select a store or bank on the map before you go. It shows whether it **has a safe**, its **security level** (low to high) and the **approximate value** of what's inside, so you can choose a target worth the risk.

## Tip: go in the back

In chapter 3 you get a tool to pick locks, and from then on back doors are the way to go: subdue the staff in the back, kill the alarm and destroy the security footage before opening the safe. MrDalekJD adds that Focus marks doors too: a white door can be opened, while a red one is alarmed, so look for another way in. More in [how to make money fast in GTA 6](../gta-6-how-to-make-money/).
