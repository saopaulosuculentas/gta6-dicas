**There's no single safe code in GTA 6.** In story missions the game gives you the combination, but in **robberies** it's **randomized** every time. To open the safe you need to find the **4-digit code** on the spot. PC Gamer explains the two ways.

## Option 1: find it written down

The code is often written somewhere inside the store or bank:

- on a note on a **board in the back office**;
- carried by a **guard** you knocked out.

Turn on **Focus mode** (press **L3 and R3** together) to highlight important objects.

## Option 2: threaten an employee

Aim your weapon at an employee. You get **Threaten** and **Safe Code?** prompts. Pick the safe code and they'll hand it over. In some robberies this also makes the cashier empty the register, like in older GTA games.

## Opening the safe

Once you have the code, it appears in the **lower right corner** when you interact with the safe. Enter the 4 digits and it opens.

Be quick: the sooner it's open, the sooner your bag is full and you're gone.

## Pick your target on the map

Select a store or bank on the map before you go. It shows whether it **has a safe**, its **security level** (low to high) and the **approximate value** of what's inside, so you can choose a target worth the risk.

## Tip: go in the back

In chapter 3 you get a tool to pick locks, and from then on back doors are the way to go: subdue the staff in the back, kill the alarm and destroy the security footage before opening the safe. More in [how to make money fast in GTA 6](../gta-6-how-to-make-money/).
