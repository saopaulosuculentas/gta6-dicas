**GTA 6 has 12 pieces of equipment, and they all unlock through the story.** They're **always on you**, so there's nothing to carry: you pick some from the **item wheel**, next to your weapons, and others kick in on their own when you interact with something, like the Key Cloner when you steal a sports car. The list is under **Progress > Equipment**.

The list comes from PowerPyx. GameSpot confirms the missions for the main ones.

## All 12 items

| Equipment | When it unlocks | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Binoculars | Chapter 1, Gone Fishing | See far away and **study animals** (counts toward 100%) |
| Flashlight | Chapter 1, Gone Fishing | Light up dark places |
| Duffel Bag | Chapter 1, Lab Fees | Carry stolen goods. Used automatically |
| Headphones | Buy them at an electronics store | Listen to music on your phone |
| Crowbar | Chapter 3, Meet the Heders | Open crates. Used automatically |
| Masks | In some story robberies; others at Ammu-Nation | Hide your face |
| Metal Detector | Chapter 1, Island Dreams (Kenton) | Find **treasure** on the seabed |
| Slim Jim | Chapter 2, Moses's mission | Open cars **without setting off the alarm** |
| **Snap Gun** | Chapter 3, Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs | Open locked doors **without the alarm** |
| Cut-off Tool | Chapter 3, Meet the Heders | Cut padlocks and open ATMs |
| **Key Cloner** | Chapter 3, Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS | Steal sports cars with an **advanced tracker** |
| **Dye Pack Jammer** | Chapter 3, Credit Check | Stop **dye packs** from ruining stolen cash |

## The ones that change the game most

- **Slim Jim:** Moses gives it to you in Chapter 2. It opens older cars without the alarm. See [how to sell and register cars](../gta-6-how-to-sell-cars/) and [the Slim Jim and Snap Gun](../gta-6-slim-jim-snap-gun/).
- **Snap Gun:** gets you in through the **back** of stores without the alarm. According to GameSpot, it's the missing piece for a clean robbery. See [Yasmani's jewelry store safe codes](../gta-6-yasmani-jewelry-safe-codes/).
- **Key Cloner:** opens up **Randy's list** and the most expensive cars. See [Randy's rare cars](../gta-6-rare-car-locations-randy/).
- **Dye Pack Jammer:** without it, cash from **banks, Payday Loans and ATMs** can blow up in pink dye and become worthless. After Credit Check, it stays active for good. See [how to steal ATMs](../gta-6-steal-atm-tow-truck-winch/).
- **Cut-off Tool:** opens the padlocks that need it, like the container holding one of the parts for [Wyman's classic cars](../gta-6-wyman-classic-car-locations/).
