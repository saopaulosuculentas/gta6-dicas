GTA 6 has manual saving, so you choose when to create a checkpoint. The quickest way is the **main menu**: choose **Game** and then **Save Game**. There are **15 manual save slots**, according to PC Gamer.

## How to save

1. Open the main menu.
2. Select **Game**.
3. Choose **Save Game** and pick one of the 15 slots.

You load an older save from the same menu.

## Tip: one save per chapter

PC Gamer suggests keeping **a save at the start of each chapter**. With 15 slots you have room to go back to any point in the story and try a mission a different way.

Saving often also protects your money: [the cash on you is lost if you die](../gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die/), so stash it and save.

## How to quit GTA 6

There's **no quit option** in the game. GamesRadar+ says you have to go back to the **PS5 or Xbox home screen** and close the app from there. **Save first**, or you'll load back in from the last autosave.

## The autosave slot

On top of the 15 manual slots there's a **16th slot** reserved for the autosave. It saves after missions, when you get wasted or busted, and after some big open world moments. It happens less often than you'd think: see [GTA 6 autosave explained](../gta-6-autosave-explained/).

You can't save from the menu **during a mission**. From the Start menu you can **restart the mission** from the beginning or from the last checkpoint.

## When you can't save

**After using a cheat**, saving is disabled until you quit the game. To save again, quit without saving and load back in. That's why it's worth saving before you try any [cheat](../how-to-use-cheats-gta-6/). Since cheats only unlock after the story, you don't need to worry about it early on.
