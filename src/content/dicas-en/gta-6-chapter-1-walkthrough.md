GTA 6's Chapter 1 opens with Lucia behind bars, then hands you Jason in the Leonida Keys and starts stacking up missions fast. Below is the goal of each of the **first 7 missions**, from Prologue to Unwanted Attention, plus the controls the game teaches and what to watch out for. It's built on GamesRadar+'s walkthrough, which is still being written and currently ends at mission 7. The Lab Fees codes match what Bombastic and Musty Sky showed in their own playthroughs.

Controls are listed as **PS5 / Xbox**.

## 1. Prologue

**Goal:** learn the basics as Lucia, still locked up in Leonida Penitentiary.

- Talk to your cellmates: hold **L2 / LT** to target someone and press **X / A** to talk.
- Follow the guard to area A102 and wait. You can chat with other inmates in the meantime.
- In Counselor Gates' office, a flashback to a bank robbery kicks in. Hold **L2 / LT** to point the gun and press **Square / X** to threaten. Lean on the teller and grab the cash bundles with **Triangle / Y**.
- A customer will grab Lucia. Tap **Square / X** quickly to break free.
- Run across the rooftops: **Square / X** jumps and climbs, **L3** runs, and **L3** again sprints.

**Don't stress:** with 2 stars there's no real way out. Lucia always gets caught at the end, because the story needs her to.

## 2. Gone Fishing

**Goal:** scoop up the packages dropped into the sea, then pull Donnie out of the Kingfisher camp.

- **R2 / RT** speeds up the boat and **L2 / LT** slows it down. Follow the pink circle on the minimap.
- Near a package, press **L3 + R3** together for **Focus**: rings pulse around whatever you're hunting for. Get close and Cal hooks it on his own.
- There are **3 packages**. If the minimap fades, press **down** on the D-pad to bring the objective back. **X / A** does tight turns at speed.
- Donnie changes the plan over the radio: head to a small dock about 0.5 miles away. That's where you try the **binoculars**: hold **L1 / LB**, pick them on the wheel with the right stick, let go, then use them with **L2 / LT**.
- At the camp (Sunrise RV Park), **stealth** is the way to go. **Circle / B** crouches. There are no vision cones: watch the **yellow bar** over enemies' heads and yellow flashes at the screen edges instead. Stick to the **left side** of the camp and stay off the main road.
- When Donnie mentions a turbine, hold **R3** to spot it on the west side. He's in the shack next to it. Expect a fistfight inside: **Square / X** punches and **X / A** right before a hit lands counters it.
- Low on health? There's a **Zombix** bottle on top of the terrarium in the middle of the shack.
- A big gunfight follows on the way out. **R1 / RB** moves you in and out of cover. You can hold up to **2 pistols and 2 long guns**.
- **Optional:** a green dollar sign marks **Brian's cash** inside a white and red camper. Someone jumps you once you're inside.
- Escape in the **Dodo** seaplane: **R2 / RT** throttles up, the left stick handles pitch, and **Square / X** or **Circle / B** work the rudder. To land at Brian's marina to the north, hold **L2 / LT** to slow down and ease the nose down.
- Finally, drop Cal off at home in Jason's Ganado and head back to Jason's place. Lucia calls.

## Before Gear Up: Jason's house

GamesRadar+ counts the house as part of Gear Up. None of it is required, but it pays off:

- **Repairs:** 5 jobs (railing, steps, roof, bedroom wall and painting that wall). According to the guide, they **help your relationship with Lucia** later on.
- **Stash:** Jason can only carry **$2,000** in pocket cash. The loose floorboard next to the bed holds up to **$20,000**.
- **Collectibles:** 4 reads inside the house: Ashes of Glory (bedroom dresser), Lethal by Design (dining table), Orca (bookcase with the radio) and Off the Hook (counter in the downstairs storage room).

## 3. Gear Up

**Goal:** buy a longarm at Ammu-Nation (Chuck's Gun Shop).

- Before leaving, check the **mailbox** outside: that's where job payments land. If you stashed all your money, take some back out.
- On PS5, the **touchpad** opens the in-game index with missions and destinations. Holding **down** on the D-pad shows nearby places.
- Talk to Chuck at the counter. GamesRadar+ recommends the **Fortune & Son Moreland** shotgun for **$350**. The **Duke 556** assault rifle costs **$1,450** and could eat most of your cash.
- Carrying a gun in the open draws attention. Leave it in the locker at the back of the store or in your trunk.
- A fight breaks out as you leave. Settle it **with your fists** so the cops stay out of it: **Square / X** punches, holding **X / A** blocks and tapping **X / A** dodges.

## 4. Just a Scratch

**Goal:** get the Ganado fixed at Joey's shop (Coastal Auto Care), at the bottom end of Key Lento.

- In the index, go to the diary tab and set **Fix Your Car** as your destination. Leaving Ammu-Nation, turn left.
- Stop at the green garage door, hold **up** on the D-pad to talk to Joey, then drive inside.
- Under **Full Engine**, pick **Stock Engine** for **$100**. Switch tabs with **L1 / LB** and **R1 / RB**. Under **Cosmetics**, the **Respray** is free.
- **Ultimate Edition** owners will find the free **Retro Build** under **Pro Builds > Full Kits**, which paints the car and bumps its speed and acceleration.
- Lucia will appreciate the repair too. Afterwards you can call Joey from your contacts to fix or deliver the Ganado.

## 5. Lab Fees

**Goal:** collect overdue rent at the Moonstone Boutique, in the Key Lento art market.

- Before going in, call Brian (**up** on the D-pad, then Contacts). He explains the shop is a front for a drug lab.
- Want a more professional Criminal Profile? **Don't storm in shooting.** Target the clerk with **L2 / LT** and pick **X / A** to defuse or **Square / X** to threaten.
- When he pulls a baseball bat, take it with **Triangle / Y**. Leave his face alone and he hands over the code before running off.
- The safe is in the back: code **0420**. Use Focus (**L3 + R3**) to find items, and empty the register on your way out.
- Brian wants the goods in his lockup. The door code is **7700**. Drop everything in the box and collect your pay from Jason's mailbox later.

**How it went for Bombastic:** he tried talking first. The clerk claimed he had no rent money, and only after Jason got aggressive did he offer the acid instead and give up the code **0420**. The safe was in the back room, and the register only had a few dollars in it. Musty Sky uses the same **7700** to open Brian's lockup when he drops off stolen goods.

More codes are on our [GTA 6 safe codes](../gta-6-safe-codes/) page, and selling stolen goods is covered in [fence locations](../gta-6-fence-locations/).

## 6. Pipework

**Goal:** collect rent from Mandy, who only pays if Jason fixes something first.

- Brian calls: answer with **up** on the D-pad.
- Mandy's place is close to Jason's house. Look for the **green car in the driveway** and the **American flag**.
- Inside, target her with **L2 / LT** and ask for a plunger with **Square / X**.
- The plunger sits next to the toilet. Grab it with **Triangle / Y** and go to the kitchen sink.
- Tap **R3** over and over to plunge. Then Jason crawls under the sink: hold **R2** to turn the wrench, five times. What comes out is gross. Then you're free to leave.

## 7. Unwanted Attention

**Goal:** get a Phoenix out of the police impound lot in Hamlet and hand it to Brian.

- Meet Cal under the underpass and drive his **95 Bison 900** to Hamlet, 1.6 miles away (around 5 minutes). Hold **left** on the D-pad to switch radio stations.
- Cal installs the **Cop Scanner** on your phone, so you can listen in on police radio.
- The easiest way through, with the fewest stars, is **stealth**. Leaving the cops unharmed also helps your Criminal Profile.
- **Don't force the gate** with **Square / X**, it's loud. Hop the fence with **X / A** and crouch right away with **Circle / B**.
- Head straight to the back of the lot. In front of a white van there's a **stack of boxes**: climb them and get over the next fence.
- There are **3 cops**: one watching a boat on the left, one looking at a car in the garage ahead, and one in the guard station on the right. **Throw a bottle** with **R2** near the guard station, then crouch-run in and open the gate at the controls.
- The Phoenix is hoisted up in the middle of the lot (the **WAiNK** app on your phone helps you spot it). To hotwire it, line up both sticks with the glowing spots on the bars.
- Driving off gives you **2 stars** straight away. Get out of the red circle on the minimap fast.
- Brian sends you to the **Pay 'n' Spray** behind Boone Speedway. Do both: **repair** it (better condition means a bigger reward) and **respray** it so the cops stop recognizing the car.
- Drop it at Brian's garage and grab your pay from the back office.
- To lower your recognition, take off the hat and sunglasses from the menu (**L1 / LB**, items tab with **R1 / RB**) or buy a new outfit off the mannequins at the nearby Ammu-Nation (**$59 to $96**).
- To get home, call Joey to bring your car or ring **Paradise Taxi**. Once there, eat, shower and sleep.
