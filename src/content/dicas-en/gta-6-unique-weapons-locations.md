GTA 6 has **unique weapons**: special variants with their own wraps and attachments that you couldn't afford to build early on. BennyCentral says there are **130** spread across Leonida, while WiLLiSGaming talks about "hundreds". Some are close to the start of the game.

When you pick one up, the game tells you it's a **unique weapon variant** and that, if lost, it can be **recovered at the weapon locker** in Ammu-Nation. WiLLiSGaming and YOUNG DIRK back this up: unique guns survive death and come back from the locker or your car's trunk. See [how to store weapons in your trunk](../gta-6-store-weapons-trunk/).

Not every unique gun is hidden: some are **sold in gun stores** (see the section near the end). And if you want an early-game route that grabs guns and cash in one trip, see [GTA 6 secret early items](../gta-6-secret-early-items/).

## How to spot a unique weapon

- **Yellow icon on the map.** According to WiLLiSGaming, a small **yellow gun icon** shows up on the map when you're near a unique weapon. Arekkz Gaming calls it a golden gun icon, and inside gun stores it shows on the minimap.
- **No modding.** OutlawGarry thought a **blue** weapon is yours and can be customized while a **yellow** one is unique and can't be, but he wasn't sure. Arekkz Gaming and BennyCentral now say the same thing: weapon variants **can't be modified**. What you find is what you get, which is why the ones that come with a scope and suppressor are so handy early on.
- **Not everything is unique.** Grenade launchers and rocket launchers you find lying around are regular weapons: die with them and they're gone (WiLLiSGaming). The SM-69 sniper on the Key Lento radio tower and the Duke 5.56 rifles in hideouts are regular too, according to Arekkz Gaming, Rodwenn and RageGamingVideos. Keep those in your trunk.

Several spots have now been shown by more than one channel: the Kingfishers W-22, the tiger motel sniper, the swamp cabin guns, the Conspiracist Type 9, the glider pistol and the Crabshack VF5. When only one channel has shown a spot, the text says who.

## Hunter's Laird 350 sniper (random event)

KhrazeGaming calls it your first "yellow" (rare) sniper: **top-tier accuracy and range** with a long-range scope already on it.

1. Head to the west side of the highway into **Hamlet**, near **Grassrivers**, at a junction.
2. You'll hear a **gunshot** nearby. If you can't place it, press **L3 + R3** (focus) to reveal where the sound comes from.
3. You'll find **two guys** (one is named Todd) with the sniper lying on the ground.
4. Play along: grab the truck down the road and drive them to the **hospital**.
5. At the hospital, one of them **hands you the rifle** for good.

## Kingfishers W-22 Small Game Rifle (near silent)

A small game rifle with a scope and a suppressor that barely makes a sound. Headshots drop enemies instantly, which makes it great for stealth. KhrazeGaming and WiLLiSGaming both picked up this rifle.

1. Early on, **Donnie** tells you about a robbery opportunity at a **Kingfishers compound** in Grassrivers.
2. Bring firepower: WiLLiSGaming counts **20 or more enemies** there. KhrazeGaming recommends coming **by boat** and picking off enemies on the pier from range.
3. Go into the building **closest to the water**, one of the gang's fishing shacks.
4. A **weapons crate** on a desk holds the rifle.
5. To get away, WiLLiSGaming takes one of the boats out back and leaves along the river.

Arekkz Gaming, BennyCentral and RageGamingVideos went after the same rifle. Arekkz and Benny call the spot **Wetman Bay**: a small island with a little dock and the gang's fishing shacks. Come in by the dock and the rifle is in a case inside the **main cabin**; Benny says it sits on the **kitchen counter**. Arekkz warns that body shots do very little damage, so aim for the head.

There's a bonus too. According to RageGamingVideos, behind the **little red shed on the docks** a scoped **Duke 5.56** rifle is leaning against the wall. It isn't unique, but it's free. Put it in your trunk and it stays with you.

Clear the rest of the compound too: the drug bricks there go for about $350 each at [Brian's lockup](../gta-6-fence-locations/).

## Black Eagle Import M306 rifle (Lucia only)

This is WiLLiSGaming's favorite find: a marksman rifle with a scope and a suppressor. It sits in a **Black Eagle warehouse**, part of one of the game's gun store chains. He doesn't show where the warehouse is on the map.

1. **Save first.** The staff are unarmed, but they will call the cops. Once the police surround the building, the gun is out of reach. He needed the warehouse to himself for roughly 10 to 20 minutes.
2. Play as **Lucia**. The rifle is in a room you can only reach through a **narrow gap**, and Jason is too big for it.
3. As you walk into the warehouse, head **left**. The gap is behind a small opening in the wood. He used **cover (R1 on PS5)** to squeeze through.
4. The rifle is in a **case** inside. Leave through the same gap.

It took WiLLiSGaming 5 to 10 minutes to get in, and he says he doesn't know if there's another way. The same warehouse has a **rocket launcher**, and a safe in the office to the right of the entrance holds a Black Eagle **Desert Eagle**. Nobody has a working code for that safe yet: the one going around online didn't work for him.

## Swamp Bait revolver and Cruel Huntsman SM69 sniper (swamp cabin)

Two unique guns in one trip, according to WiLLiSGaming. They're in an **abandoned cabin in the middle of the Pelican Valley swamp**, near the Keys. Just walk in: the **Swamp Bait** revolver and the **Cruel Huntsman SM69** sniper are both there.

Don't go at night. If an **alligator** grabs you in the water, it kills you in one go.

Millzaa visited the same cabin. He places the little house, which has no name on the map, **just south of Hamlet**, and you have to wade through a bit of river to reach it. Inside you'll find butchered alligators, a cleaver on the table and a body. The sniper is **on the floor** with a scope already fitted. The **Swamp Bait** revolver isn't out in the open: Millzaa spotted it by zooming in with **Focus**. There's also some footage on the floor showing what happened to the cabin's owner.

## Cockroach Extreme carbine and Dvatek GL40 grenade launcher

WiLLiSGaming found both in Vice City, in the Vice-Dale area (he calls it "Dale County"), in the **back storeroom of a store that anyone can walk into**.

1. Go into the store and head for the storeroom.
2. Knock down the **wall of toilet paper**.
3. The **Cockroach Extreme** carbine and the **Dvatek GL40** grenade launcher are behind it.

The carbine is unique. The grenade launcher isn't, so you lose it if you die.

## Conspiracist Type 9 SMG (junkyard)

WiLLiSGaming found the **Conspiracist Type 9**, a unique SMG, in a **junkyard west of Vice City**, on SR-120 near Omega Pkwy, with Buckskin Swamp to the west. He says "Mariana County" out loud, but the map in his video puts the junkyard between Kelly County and Vice-Dale County. The weapon's name shows on screen. The same junkyard has a [rocket launcher](../gta-6-rocket-launcher-location/). He suggests waiting until **Chapter 2**, when the story takes you to that area anyway.

1. Jump the junkyard fence.
2. Look for an **RV covered in antennas**.
3. Round the back there's a **bunker door**. Inside you'll find a dead man and a weapons container with the SMG.
4. There are also **grenades** and a **rocket launcher** down there. Save the rockets for when you really need them: the launcher isn't unique and you lose it when you die.

Millzaa, Rodwenn and BennyCentral found the same SMG and give an easier landmark. The junkyard is a **trailer park right across the street from the Griffin Shooting Range**, a gun range in Balsero. On the map, Millzaa describes it as sitting "between the D and the A" of the word LEONIDA.

1. Stop in front of the shooting range, cross the street and climb the fence.
2. Go into the trailer. WiLLiSGaming mentions a bunker door at the back; Rodwenn and Millzaa show a **hatch in the floor** that drops into a cellar.
3. Down there, the Conspiracist Type 9 is in a gun case next to the rocket launcher, grenades and healing items.

According to BennyCentral, it's a machine pistol and goes into your pistol slot.

## Donnie's H&L 38 revolver (missable)

DPJ warns this one is easy to miss for good.

1. Near the end of Chapter 1, after the mission **Swamp** (with Brian), you have to pack Donnie's things at his trailer.
2. You load them into his car and drive it somewhere remote to **destroy** it.
3. **Before you destroy it,** open the **trunk**: the revolver is inside.

Destroy the car first and the weapon goes with it.

## Sniper at the tiger motel (Manatee Motel)

Four channels have now shown this motel, with a **tiger** on the loose and a sniper as the prize.

- **BennyCentral** describes a sniper with a custom wrap, taken from the **Kingfishers**, at an **abandoned motel in Pelican Valley**. It comes fully kitted out.
- **OutlawGarry** calls the place the **Manatee Motel** and says it's just up the road from **Key Lento**, where the game starts. In his video, a group of hunters had a tiger locked up, it got loose and mauled everyone.
- **Millzaa** found the motel marked on the map as an event. He puts it **just south of Hamlet**, about a mile from Jason's house. On the map in his video, that's the northern edge of Pelican Valley, across the water.
- **Rodwenn** went to the same spot and took the sniper from the next room on the left.

This page used to say the area depended on who you asked (Pelican Valley or near Key Lento). Millzaa's map settles it: the motel sits in northern Pelican Valley, right below Hamlet, and the road from Key Lento gets you there.

1. When you get there, the **tiger** is on the attack. Wait for it and the wounded man to go down, or shoot it yourself.
2. Loot the rooms and the bodies for cash. OutlawGarry found a **safe with $1,000** and left with about **$3,300** in total. The safe at the back gave Millzaa **$917** and Rodwenn just over $1,000. There's also a flare gun and some healing items.
3. Grab the sniper from the weapons case in the **room next door**. OutlawGarry found **two snipers**, one of them yellow (unique). Millzaa also picked up **$640** worth of drugs in that room.
4. Leave quickly or be ready to fight: in OutlawGarry's video someone called the police, and in Millzaa's an armed group showed up right after.

Earlier, neither video made the weapon's name clear. Now it's settled: Millzaa's screen shows **Kingfishers Laird 350** along with the unique variant notice, and Rodwenn and BennyCentral say the same name. Rodwenn adds that it comes with its own wrap, a sight and a stock upgrade. Don't mix it up with the Hunter's Laird 350 from the random event.

## Sniper on top of the radio tower

Arekkz Gaming found a scoped sniper at the top of a **radio tower in the starting area**, close to the clothing store.

1. Take the stairs to the **top platform**.
2. Go through the door at the top and climb the **ladder**: the sniper is there.
3. Further up there's a **parachute** to jump off with.

Now we know which tower it is. It's the one in **Key Lento**, about 0.36 miles from Jason's house, according to Millzaa. Arekkz says it's near Donnie's trailer and counts as one of the game's 31 landmarks, so scan it with your phone before you climb. Millzaa, Arekkz and BennyCentral all call the gun the **SM-69**.

Heads up: this sniper is a **regular** gun, not a unique variant. Arekkz and Rodwenn both point out that you lose it if you die. On the same climb you'll find **2 collectible cards** and the parachute at the top, which only works once. See [collectible cards](../gta-6-collectible-cards/) and [parachutes](../gta-6-parachute-location/).

The first video didn't make clear whether this is the same tower as the [radio tower easter egg](../gta-6-radio-tower-easter-egg/). Going by the locations, it isn't: the egg tower is near Hamlet, while this one is in Key Lento.

## The crashed glider pistol (Turnt Nipper .38)

Milo found a **propeller glider stuck in a tree** on a small island in the south. **Shoot the strings** holding it up and it drops, with a **unique pistol** inside. The video places the island "just north of Mariana County" but doesn't show the exact spot.

Arekkz Gaming and BennyCentral went to the same glider and fill in the gaps. On Arekkz's screen the gun shows up as the **Turnt Nipper .38 Pistol**, with its own decal and a red dot sight already fitted. Benny describes the wrap as energy drink branding.

1. Head to **Bobcat Bight**, at the far end of the sandy strip. The glider is on a small island, so bring a boat or a jet ski.
2. Walk up from the beach to the **pine trees**. The wrecked glider is hanging in the branches with a bunch of ropes.
3. Shoot every rope. Arekkz used a rifle with a big magazine, since the last rope swings around and takes a few tries.
4. Once the glider drops, the pistol is inside it or on the ground right next to it.

## Crabshack VF5 SMG

This is the reward for the giant crab mystery in Mariana County. YOUNG DIRK shows it **lying on the floor right inside the boathouse**, scope already fitted, and it goes into your weapon locker. RageGamingVideos and Millzaa add that it also has a **tactical flashlight** and a biohazard wrap. The full solution, with all three codes, is in [the giant crab mystery](../gta-6-giant-crab-mystery-codes/).

## Marabunta Grande KZ59 rifle (Nova Auto junkyard, Hamlet)

Only BennyCentral has shown this one so far, and the name comes from his voiceover. It's a gold assault rifle engraved with M7 gang designs.

1. Head to the **Nova Auto** junkyard in **Hamlet**.
2. Climb the wall from the **dumpster** and take out the M7 gang in the yard.
3. The rifle is in a case on the **workbench under the shed**.
4. In the garage, the safe in the back room opens with **777**, which is written right above it.
5. A button on the wall opens the garage shutter. Drive out in the **lowrider** inside: Benny says it has no tracker.

## Gold KZ59 at the Palmer Fishing Warehouse (Bobcat Key)

Also from BennyCentral alone. It's another gold KZ59 with fine engravings; the name he says isn't clear, so we're not printing it.

1. Go to the **Palmer Fishing Warehouse** in **Bobcat Key**.
2. Fight your way through the gang and head up to the **office**: the rifle is in a case.
3. The computer downstairs gives you the code for the office safe. Benny reads it out as **18886**; check it on the computer before you punch it in.

## Gun nut's sniper (Key Lento)

According to BennyCentral, a stilt house in Key Lento that looks a lot like Jason's belongs to a firearms enthusiast. Climb the back stairs and break the door open. The owner is home and comes at you. The unique sniper, with a patriotic wrap, is in a case on the kitchen counter, and the bedrooms hold body armor, a machine gun and an assault rifle. Benny marks the house on his map but doesn't give the street name.

## Marabunta Grand Hobo shotgun (behind the Hamlet pawn shop)

RageGamingVideos found this engraved legendary double-barrel shotgun while visiting the **Hamlet pawn shop** at night. Behind the shop was a car with its trunk open, blood on the ground and screaming from the building next door. Follow the blood trail through a door into a warehouse: the shotgun sits in a gun case out in the open, with some cash. Whether you deal with what's in the next room is up to you. He isn't sure if it's a random event or if it always happens there.

## Unique weapons you can buy

Every gun store sells one or two exclusive variants, marked with the golden gun icon. Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento has the **Mahi Nipper .38** for **$1,160**, and Big Shots in Hamlet has **The Silver 1922** for **$2,210**: Arekkz Gaming and BennyCentral show the same prices. The full list, with bundles, is in [GTA 6 gun store unique weapons](../gta-6-gun-store-unique-weapons/).

## Tip

According to KhrazeGaming, **enemy hideouts refresh** every few in-game days and restock goods and weapons. For buying and customizing everything else, see the [GTA 6 weapons list](../gta-6-weapons-list/).
