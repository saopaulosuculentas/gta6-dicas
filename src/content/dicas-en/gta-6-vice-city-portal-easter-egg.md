**GTA 6 has a hidden portal that drops Jason into the 2002, PS2-era version of Vice City.** You land in the original Malibu Club, blocky graphics and all, and come home with **Tommy Vercetti's blue Hawaiian shirt** and a **chopper motorcycle**. Kotaku credits creator Memeio with figuring it out.

Fair warning: it's a long one. You power up a tower in the south, hit a switch at **12 electrical substations** in a set order, then head to a power plant **between 7 p.m. and 6 a.m.** Kotaku's writer needed about 30 minutes of flying with Memeio's guide. Dan Allen Gaming says to set aside an hour.

## Before you start

- **Save first.** Dan Allen Gaming saves right before the tower.
- **Grab a helicopter.** By car, every leg takes several minutes. Dan Allen thinks there are a couple of helis in the bottom left of the map.
- **No fast travel.** The 12 substations have to be done in one sitting.
- **Don't die.** Dan Allen stresses this near the end, and Misleading has a whole section on dying and fast travel (5:12).

## Rewards

- **Tommy's blue shirt** from Vice City (Kotaku).
- A **chopper** modeled on the American-flag bike Tommy gets early in the 2002 game (Kotaku). TmarTn2 lists it as the Malibu Zombie Bobber.
- A **phone call from Kent Paul** afterward, according to the TmarTn2 (17:42) and Misleading (7:54) chapters.

## Step 1: the tower in the south

It starts at a tower in the **southern part of the map**. Misleading calls it the radio tower.

1. Check the poster. It references **"Life Is Passing Me By"**, the PC cheat that sped up the clock in the original game.
2. Pull the **lever**, then press the **button**. A short sound from Vice City plays.
3. When the TV shows the **Malibu**, the easter egg is live (Dan Allen Gaming).

## Step 2: the 12 substations

Each substation has an **LPL** sign and is often fenced in. Letters are painted on each one, and together they spell **LIFE IS PASSING ME BY**. Visit them in the order that spells the phrase and hit **one** switch on the panel at each.

The panels are marked **W, A, D** and a **dot**. The right one follows the old PS2 cheat: Dan Allen points out that D stands for Circle, and the cheat opened with Circle, Circle. **Hit the wrong switch or go out of order and you have to start over**, he warns.

## Step 3: the text and Reactor 2

Hit the 12th switch and a **text from "Unknown"** arrives: **"Reactor 2 1906."** Dan Allen reads it as **Reactor 2 at the power plant, from 7 p.m. to 6 a.m.** The portal won't open during the day. If the text came through, you've done it: you can save and go whenever you like.

1. Wait for night. Dan Allen showed up around 10 p.m.
2. Fly in by **helicopter** and **parachute** down onto the plant so you don't get spotted. That was the easiest way he found.
3. Behind a **metal door** you'll find a huge **pink portal**. Step through and a cutscene takes over.

## Inside 2002 Vice City

Jason ends up in the **Malibu Club** from the original game, with PS2-style animation.

- You can dance on the floor.
- You can try to order a drink. Kotaku found Jason can't hold the glass, since PS2-era hands had no fingers, so he spills it.
- Head up the **back stairs** to find **Kent Paul**. Before you can chat, a new portal pulls Jason back to 2026.

Back in the present, the shirt and the bike are yours (TmarTn2 16:53, Misleading 7:27).

Exact addresses for the substations haven't been published in writing, which is why the table points you to the video timestamps. The switch for the 10th stop isn't spoken in Dan Allen's guide. We'll update this page when a source we can check spells those out.
