Mandy's house is a **short drive from Jason's place**. GamesRadar+ gives two landmarks: a **green car in the driveway** and an **American flag** flying outside. None of our sources name the street, so the quickest way there is to set the mission as your destination from the map index.

Mandy is the tenant in **Pipework**, a Chapter 1 mission where Brian sends Jason to collect her overdue rent.

## When the mission shows up

According to GamesRadar+, Brian calls a couple of minutes after you drop the Moonstone Boutique stash at his lockup at the end of **Lab Fees**. Answer with **D-pad Up** when your phone rings. He's got another tenant behind on rent and wants Jason to go collect.

The sources don't agree on the order. GamesRadar+ puts Pipework right after Lab Fees, while PC Gamer's list has it as **mission 7 of Chapter 1**, after Unwanted Attention, summed up as Jason doing some DIY. If Brian hasn't called yet, check the [mission list](../gta-6-mission-list/) to see what comes first.

## Finding the house with the index

1. Open the map (touchpad on PS5).
2. Press **D-pad Right** to bring up the index.
3. On the **Story** tab (the journal icon), select Mandy's mission and press **X** (A on Xbox) to make it your active destination. The GPS draws the route.
4. When you reach the street, look for the **green car**. In GTA Series Videos' playthrough it's a convertible parked on the gravel out front.

The index is covered in full in our [index guide](../gta-6-index-find-missions/).

## How to collect Mandy's rent

Buttons are listed as **PS5 / Xbox**:

1. Chat with Mandy outside. She invites Jason in.
2. She leads you to the back bedroom. Hold **L2 / LT** to interact and press **Square / X** to ask if she has a **plunger**. She'll only pay up if Jason helps with a repair.
3. Cross the hall to the bathroom. The plunger is **next to the toilet**: pick it up with **Triangle / Y**.
4. Take it to the **kitchen sink** and press **Triangle / Y** again.
5. Tap **R3** over and over to work the plunger.
6. That doesn't clear it, so Jason crawls under the sink. **Hold R2** to turn the wrench. Mandy keeps talking the whole time; just keep going.
7. After **five turns**, you find what was blocking the pipe. GamesRadar+ only says it's disgusting. Then get out of the house.

For that last part, GamesRadar+ only gives the PS5 buttons (R3 and R2). KultZ backs up the ending: the job finishes with Jason under Mandy's kitchen sink.

## After Mandy

- **What's next:** with the rent paid, GamesRadar+ points you to Cal's texts, which lead into the next mission.
- **How much it pays:** none of the sources say what Jason earns from this job.
- **A plane crash:** Arekkz Gaming says that once you've progressed the story and met Mandy, crossing a bridge he marks on the map can trigger a small plane crashing into the nearby swamp. The wreck has valuables you can sell, but enemies show up if you hang around too long.

Every other early mission, with the same button prompts, is in the [Chapter 1 walkthrough](../gta-6-chapter-1-walkthrough/).
