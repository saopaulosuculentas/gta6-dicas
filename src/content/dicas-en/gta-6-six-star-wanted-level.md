To get a **6 star wanted level** in GTA 6, PC Gamer says you have to cause mass destruction and a huge body count. The only method it found was **stealing a tank from the military base and blowing up other tanks and vehicles** with it. MrDalekJD also hit six stars with a tank from the base, and says the base's fighter jet can get you there too.

Six stars is as notorious as you can get under the new wanted system. Getting there means killing a lot of cops and soldiers, and that comes with a price.

## How the top star levels work

Some things haven't changed from earlier games:

- **Breaking into the airport and stealing a passenger jet** gets you into the highest star levels. Here's [how to steal a plane from the airport](../gta-6-airport-steal-plane/).
- **Hijacking an APC at the military base** does the same. The way in is covered in the [military base guide](../gta-6-military-base-location/).
- At **5 stars**, the police send a **tank** after you.

The sixth star sits above all of that. According to PC Gamer, only **mass destruction and loss of life** will unlock it.

## What worked for PC Gamer

1. Head to the military base.
2. Steal a **tank**.
3. Use it to destroy **other tanks and vehicles** until the sixth star lights up.

The article **doesn't say** how many vehicles you need to wreck or how long it takes. PC Gamer also frames this as the only way it managed to do it, which isn't the same as the only way that exists.

## What MrDalekJD showed

MrDalekJD reached six stars on video, inside the military base. The route in is covered in the [military base guide](../gta-6-military-base-location/).

**With the tank:**

1. The moment he climbed into a tank inside the base, he got **5 stars instantly**.
2. Fighting the base security from the tank, he hit **6 stars** shortly after. Military helicopters joined the chase too.
3. The tank has a **tracker**. Even if you drive it out of the base, the police know where it is.

**With the fighter jet:**

1. Taking off in one of the base's jets also gives you **5 stars instantly**. He says it can go up to 6, but in his video it stayed at 5.
2. The jet starts with **no weapon selected**. Hold **L1 (LB on Xbox)** to open the weapon wheel and pick missiles or the machine gun.
3. The base sends other jets after you. The game's hint is to fly low to get away.

Both sources agree on the main point: a tank from the base can take you to six stars, and from there the police won't back off. The difference is the effort. PC Gamer had to destroy other tanks and vehicles. In MrDalekJD's video, the sixth star showed up while he was still fighting the base security.

## Know this before you try

- **There's no coming back from 6 stars.** At this level your heat and wanted level no longer go down, and the law keeps hunting you. MrDalekJD saw the same thing: at six stars, the police don't stop until they take you down.
- **Dying is the only real exit.** That's PC Gamer's takeaway, since hiding doesn't work.
- **Your criminal profile takes the hit.** Killing that many cops and soldiers can drag your [criminal profile](../gta-6-criminal-profile-explained/) way down. It works a lot like the honor system in Red Dead Redemption 2.

If you just want to shake off an ordinary chase, see [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).
