The body armor cheat in GTA 6 is **Down, Up, Left, Up, Up, Up, Up, Up, Down, Down**. What it does: adds body armor to your character. The middle of the sequence is easy to get wrong: it is five presses of Up in a row after Left.

## How to enter it

1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Down, Up, Left, Up, Up, Up, Up, Up, Down, Down.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to **complete 100 workouts**. Progress you made during the story counts.

## What you need to know

- No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
- Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
- Workouts at the gym and on the equipment at Jason's house count. See the [beginner tips](../gta-6-beginner-tips/).
- PowerPyx prints the same sequence and unlock as GamesRadar+, so it is confirmed.
