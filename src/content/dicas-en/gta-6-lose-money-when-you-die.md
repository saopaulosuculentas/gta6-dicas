**Yes, in GTA 6 you lose the cash you're carrying if you die or get busted.** WiLLiSGaming, JoblessGarrett, DPJ, MrDalekJD, BennyCentral and KultZ all warn about it: get wasted or busted with money in your pocket and it's gone. The money you've stashed at your safehouse or put in your account isn't affected.

## Where to keep your money

- **The stash at your safehouse.** At the start, Jason keeps everything **under the loose floorboards** in his bedroom. Anything you put there is safe. KultZ points out that early on you don't have a bank account yet, so this is the only safe spot you've got.
- **BuckMe.** Early in the story Lucia wants to open a bank account, but Jason has bad credit. The bank gives him the **BuckMe** app to build it up, and you have to put **$5,000** in it for the story to move on. That's the "family savings".

According to WiLLiSGaming, the game itself tells you to keep any extra cash you're not going to spend in your stash, your bank account, an ATM or BuckMe.

## The bank and ATMs

There's no account in the first hours, but it doesn't take long. According to MrDalekJD, the bank unlocks through a chapter 1 story mission called **Buck Me**, so he recommends playing the story until you reach it. Once your account is open:

- **Deposits only work at an ATM.** You have to walk up to one in person. There's no way to bank money from your phone, so you can't save your cash halfway through a shootout.
- **Your phone can find the closest ATM.** Open the BuckMe app and scroll down: it shows you the nearest ATM, so you don't have to search the full map.
- **Money in the bank stays put.** MrDalekJD says whatever is in your account isn't touched when you die or get busted. That's why he stops at an ATM before every mission, robbery or anything that might draw the police.

## Loot bags are lost too

It isn't only cash. MrDalekJD warns that if you die carrying **loot bags**, they're gone, along with all the work it took to fill them. After a score, skip the shortcuts through busy streets and don't start fights on the way. Stash it or take it to a [fence](../gta-6-fence-locations/) first.

GamingBolt found that a lost bag isn't always gone for good. Go back to where it happened and look for a **green bag icon**: it may be the one you dropped. The police could also have grabbed it, and then it's up to you whether getting it back is worth the trouble.

## How much to carry

Don't walk around with zero: you'll need cash for shops, repairs, fuel and taxis. DPJ's advice is to stash often but always keep a little on you. Take only what you'll spend on that trip. KultZ keeps just enough on him for the errands Lucia asks for in chapter 1 and hides the rest under the floor.

## When the risk is highest

- **Before a dangerous mission** or a robbery: stash your money first. WiLLiSGaming did Donnie's package runs and then hit a gang hideout with all his cash on him and had to fight about 30 enemies.
- **In the water:** sharks attack. JoblessGarrett nearly died to one after making $3,200 diving.
- **With the cops on you:** getting busted costs you the cash too.

## One more thing: chapter 2

According to WiLLiSGaming, your cash **resets to zero when chapter 2 starts**, no matter where it is. Only the BuckMe family savings carry over. See [how to make money fast in GTA 6](../gta-6-how-to-make-money/).
