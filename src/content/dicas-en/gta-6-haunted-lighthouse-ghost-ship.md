**The haunted lighthouse in GTA 6 is on Little Hardin Island, in Leonard County, in the northeast of the map.** After **midnight**, fog rolls in and **two ghosts fight** at the top of the lighthouse until one of them falls. Follow the clues and you'll find a **cursed knife** on the seabed and spot a **ghost ship** on the horizon.

Milo and GameSpot both show the ghosts. The full walkthrough comes from GameSpot.

## Before you go

- **Time:** after midnight.
- **Boat:** the island sits across deep water. Look for a boat on the beach, or take a RydeMe to a marina and steal one there.
- **Gear:** you'll be diving. Bring the **metal detector**, which unlocks in Chapter 1 with Kenton.
- According to GameSpot, you can do this one **with your partner along**, unlike some other mysteries.

## Step by step

1. **Climb the lighthouse.** In the fog you hear two men arguing. At the top, the two ghosts fight and one pushes the other off.
2. **Scan the lighthouse with WAiNK.** It's a **landmark** and counts toward the collection.
3. **Grab the photo.** Go down the stairs carefully. At the bottom there's a **photograph** of two men, **Godfrey and Frank**. Flip it over to read the back.
4. **Read the diary.** In the northwest of the island there's a **ruined house** where the two men lived. Go in, turn right and walk up to the fireplace: a **diary** falls. Read it.
5. **Take the boat and the diving gear.** At the dock on the island's north tip there's a boat and **diving gear**. Take both, or use the boat you came in.
6. **Sail east of the lighthouse**, past the point of land. At the right spot the boat **stops on its own**, you lose control, the fog comes back and the waves rise.
7. **Dive with the metal detector.** Aim it until you see **green blinks** and follow that direction to a **sunken wooden boat**.
8. **Take the cursed knife** from the skeleton. Swim up fast, get back in the boat and head back to the island. The storm gets worse and you can barely see, so check the map to stay on course.
9. **Climb the lighthouse again.** The ghosts are gone, and the game tells you there's something strange on the horizon.
10. **Look northeast.** That's the **ghost ship**. Pull out your **binoculars** quickly, because it disappears.
