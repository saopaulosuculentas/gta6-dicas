The dollhouse filter cheat in GTA 6 is **Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Down, Right**. What it does: applies a vivid filter with a narrow aspect ratio. It is one of 16 screen filters. Filters only change the look of the game, not the gameplay.

## How to enter it

1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Down, Right.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to **spend 8 hours with your partner**. Progress you made during the story counts.

## What you need to know

- No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
- Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
- The Cataclysm and Faded Glory filters have similar sequences, published only by GamesRadar+. They are in the [full cheats list](../all-gta-6-cheats-list/).
