Diving shipwrecks is one of the most fun ways to make money early in GTA 6. The jobs come from **Kenton**, a scuba instructor. You dive with a **metal detector**, collect the treasure and sell it back to him. According to WiLLiSGaming, **one dive can pay up to $4,000**. JoblessGarrett did a full round and made **$3,200 in under 30 minutes**.

## How to unlock it

1. Early in the story you meet Kenton on a job. That mission teaches you how to dive and hunt for treasure in a wreck.
2. Later, Kenton **calls you again** with a side job: he knows more wrecks.
3. Accept it. He texts you the **locations** and gives you the scuba gear.

## How to dive and find the treasure

1. Head to the marked spot and dive.
2. Use the **metal detector** to find the items.
3. Check **inside the ship** too: the chests are usually in there.
4. Collect everything. In JoblessGarrett's video one wreck had 7 items and another had 5, like a pipe, an antique candlestick, a ceremonial spoon and chests.
5. Go back to Kenton and sell.

## How much it pays

- One round of JoblessGarrett's treasure sold for **$2,600**.
- On the way he also found contraband floating at sea and sold it for **$1,600** at Brian's lockup.
- According to WiLLiSGaming, **the more dives you do, the more Kenton pays** and the more locations he sends. Whenever he calls, go.

## Watch out

- **Sharks.** JoblessGarrett got attacked by a tiger shark and nearly died. Don't stay in the water longer than you need to.
- **The warden.** At one wreck, a boat pulled up to warn that searching shipwrecks without a recovery permit is illegal and that finds must be reported. JoblessGarrett ignored it and sold everything anyway.
- **Stash your cash.** If you die or get busted with the money on you, you lose it. See [do you lose money when you die](../gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die/).

## Bonus: autopilot

On the way back, JoblessGarrett found you can switch on the **cinematic camera** and let your character **drive automatically**, like in Red Dead Redemption 2.
