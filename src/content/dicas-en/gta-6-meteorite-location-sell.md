The GTA 6 meteorite is sitting in a **wrecked campsite on the east side of Mount Kalaga**, the big mountain in **Lummox County** at the top of the map. You can pick up a chunk of the rock and sell it later. Robbin Rams and Milo both found the same spot on video. It isn't the only one, either: at least four more are out there, listed at the bottom of this page.

## Where the meteorite is

- **County:** Lummox County, in the far north of the map.
- **Mountain:** Mount Kalaga. Robbin Rams found the meteorite on the **right-hand (east) side** of the mountain.
- **Getting there:** Milo says to walk **east down the hill**. Robbin Rams flew in by helicopter and spotted the crash site while circling the mountain.
- **What to look for:** flattened tents, knocked-over camping chairs and a **crater glowing purple** in the middle. It looks like someone was camping there when the rock came down.

The only other things lying around are a few **bottles**. Robbin Rams searched the area for more loot and found nothing else.

## Mount Chiliad or Mount Kalaga?

In his video, Robbin Rams calls the place "Mount Chiliad". That's the mountain from GTA San Andreas and GTA 5. In GTA 6, the big northern mountain is **Mount Kalaga**, inside Mount Kalaga National Park in Lummox County.

Robbin Rams also says he's in "Lumas County", which is Lummox, and Milo places the crash site in Lummox County too. You can see all five counties in the [GTA 6 map guide](../gta-6-map-regions-counties/).

## How to pick it up

1. Walk up to the rock in the middle of the crater.
2. Press **Triangle** on PS5 (**Y** on Xbox).
3. A **gold box** icon with the meteorite shows up, and your character slips the rock into a pocket.

After that, Robbin Rams couldn't find the meteorite anywhere in his inventory, and the help pages in the menu don't mention it. ZacCoxTV had the same problem with the meteorite from the UFO house. So far, nobody has shown where the item ends up.

## Where to sell it

- **Lifeinvader marketplace:** right after grabbing the meteorite at the [UFO house](../gta-6-ufo-house-easter-egg/), ZacCoxTV got a line in the game explaining that it's a piece of a meteorite, that **plenty of them are scattered around**, and that people on the Lifeinvader marketplace **pay good money** for them.
- **Pawn shop:** ZacCoxTV says you can also take one to a pawn shop, but he doesn't say which. Hamlet Pawn, near Jason's house, is the one players use for selling collectible cards, as covered in the [collectible cards guide](../gta-6-collectible-cards/).

None of the videos show the sale going through or how much it pays. Until someone does, the value of a meteorite is unknown.

## The other meteorites

- **UFO house in Bellville:** there's a meteorite under the owner's bed. To get inside, you have to break in through the garage.
- **Crater at the top of Mount Kalaga:** ZacCoxTV showed a crater with a freshly fallen meteorite, still smoking, at the summit, next to a ring of stones nobody has figured out yet. He doesn't mention the campsite, so it may be the same spot as the one Robbin Rams and Milo found, or a second one close by.

Both Robbin Rams and ZacCoxTV say it reminds them of the meteorites in Red Dead Redemption 2.

## Three meteorites next to the carcasses

GameSpot found a collectible meteorite near each of the three **strange creature carcasses** in Mariana County. The carcasses are curiosities you can scan with the WAiNK app, so you can tick off both on the same trip:

| Carcass | How to get there | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Abismo Key | Take the highway south-southwest to Abismo Key, then cut through the mangrove to the south until you reach the beach | It looks like the skeleton of an alien |
| Goose Key | Inside the [military base](../gta-6-military-base-location/), Mile Zero Air Station, following the coastline northeast | Restricted area, so stay out of the soldiers' sight |
| Wetman Bay | In Wetman Bay, on the island north of the beach covered in dead sharks | It's out in a chain of islands, so bring a boat |

The rest of the curiosities are in [WAiNK curiosities and landmarks](../gta-6-waink-curiosities-landmarks/).
