GTA 6's "Bigfoot" isn't just a jump scare: it's the start of a **hidden side quest**. The creature is called the **Skunk Ape** (the characters call it **Mr. Mangrove**), it lives in the Grassrivers swamps, and hunting it down gets you a **unique, fully camouflaged sniper rifle**. JFM showed the quest from start to finish. T5G, ZacCoxTV and Punish also ran into the Skunk Ape or its effigy, The Easter Egg Hunter showed the start of the hunt with Kirk, and PC Gamer wrote up an easy route to the first sighting.

The catch: the game **doesn't tell you** what to do after the first encounter. Lots of players stop at the scare and never find the quest.

## Step 1: the first encounter

1. Drive the roads of **Grassrivers**. On one stretch the Skunk Ape shows up **eating a deer** in the middle of the road. ZacCoxTV and T5G saw this too, and T5G spotted signs around the map warning that the Skunk Ape is in the area.
2. It runs into the woods. Follow it into the trees.
3. You'll find an **effigy**, a human-shaped figure made of wood and vines.
4. Examine it. Jason **blacks out** and, for a moment, you see the Skunk Ape up close.

### Another way in: PC Gamer's boat route

PC Gamer describes a route that starts at home, by boat:

1. Start at **Jason's first house** and take **his boat** from the dock out back.
2. Cross **Gambit Bay** and head for the **dead-end road** on the inner side of the peninsula across the water.
3. Before you even reach land, a prompt tells you to switch on **Focus mode** and track down where the noises are coming from.
4. The Skunk Ape bolts into the mangroves. Keep using Focus to chase it. You'll lose sight of it and find the **effigy** next to a tree instead.

PC Gamer says the creature gives off a **green cloud of stink**, and that smell is what knocks Jason out when he checks the effigy, right after a close look at its huge feet. PC Gamer doesn't name the region for this spot, so it isn't clear whether it's the same stretch of swamp as the deer encounter above.

## Step 2: the note and the phone call

This is the part everyone misses. According to JFM, nearby there's a **note stuck on a wall next to an ATM**, asking you to call a number.

PC Gamer describes the lead a little differently: after the sighting, look for **Skunk Ape flyers** on storefronts and telephone poles around Leonida, which point you to the quest. It may well be the same notice in more than one place, but nobody has compared the two yet.

1. Find the note and read it.
2. **Call the number** on your in-game phone.
3. The man who answers is **Kirk**, a Skunk Ape hunter. He marks a spot labeled **KK** on your map, in the swamp.

In The Easter Egg Hunter's video, the call goes to **voicemail**. In the message, Kirk says he's out chasing the Skunk Ape and can be found in **Pelican Valley** most evenings.

## Step 3: the night hunt

1. Go to the KK spot **at night**. According to JFM, the quest won't continue during the day. Kirk's voicemail fits that, since he says he's out there most evenings.
2. Kirk checks your "readings" and decides you've seen Mr. Mangrove. In The Easter Egg Hunter's video he flies drones, checks Jason's EMF and says he still has the creature's **spores** on him.
3. To prove his point, Kirk has you grab a **stick** and hit his truck hard (The Easter Egg Hunter).
4. During the hunt, **follow the drone's light**. And keep your gun away: Kirk says a bullet could make the creature release a **toxic cloud**, and he wants it alive.
5. Chase the creature through the woods, following its roars. You'll pass its camps, bones and more effigies.
6. At one point something huge comes after you and you have to **run back** to the truck. Jason may black out again but doesn't die, and the quest continues.

## Step 4: the three effigies

A large area is then marked in pink on the map. Inside it there are **three effigies** to find.

JFM's tip: grab a **boat** and cruise the area with **Focus mode** on. The effigies light up in Focus. It takes about 10 minutes.

## Step 5: the helicopter and the ending

1. You hunt the Skunk Ape from a **helicopter**, already holding your new sniper rifle.
2. After you hit it, find it knocked out in one of the back rooms of the area.
3. Then things get weird: you turn into a **frog** and then a **fly**, and overhear Kirk planning to cut you out of the money.
4. In the end, you choose whether to help Kirk or let the Skunk Ape get him. JFM let the creature take Kirk.

The quest ends with the **camo sniper rifle** in your arsenal.
