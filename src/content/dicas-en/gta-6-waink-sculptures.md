GTA 6 has **23 sculptures** you can scan with the **WAiNK** app on your phone. The game's own counter shows up after the first one, reading **WAiNK Sculptures 1/23**, and each one counts toward the **You WAiNKer!** trophy, which asks for 100 scans in total.

## How scanning works

1. Explore. When you get close to one, the game tells you there's a WAiNK scan nearby.
2. Hold **Up on the D-pad** to open the WAiNK app.
3. Aim at the sculpture and scan with **R1** (RB on Xbox).
4. Your count goes up on screen.

That number is **your tally**, not the sculpture's ID. The first one you scan becomes 1 of 23 no matter which it is, so you can grab them in whatever order suits your route.

## The four in Mariana County (early game)

GameSpot found these four during the opening missions, close to Jason's house:

| Sculpture | Where it is |
|---|---|
| The Wonder Whale | A multicolored whale at Key Lento's main junction, near the rent-collecting job for Brian. It's at Sunbelt Hwy and Lemon St |
| Fatal Incursion Plaque | A memorial in the beachside park at the southwestern tip of Key Lento, past the barber and the clothing store |
| Triangler Sculpture | In the water at the meeting point for Go See Cal, northeast of Jason's house. It's a marlin |
| Grassrivers Visitor Center Statue | An animal statue outside the Grassrivers Visitor Center in the Shoeless Keys |

## The You WAiNKer! trophy

The trophy wants **100 WAiNK scans**. vAnother Level counts **108** in the game: 23 sculptures, 31 landmarks, 35 curiosities, 15 NoFlusher tags on the sidewalks and 4 billboards. That leaves eight spare if a couple of spots give you trouble. Landmarks and curiosities are covered in [WAiNK curiosities and landmarks](../gta-6-waink-curiosities-landmarks/), and the rest of the trophies are in the [trophy list](../gta-6-trophies-achievements-list/).

There's also a handy rumble cue for spotting scannables on foot, explained in [GTA 6 hidden features](../gta-6-hidden-features/).
