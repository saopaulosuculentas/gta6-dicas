The easiest rocket launcher to grab in GTA 6 is hidden in a **cellar under a rundown trailer**, right across the street from the **Griffin Shooting Range** in **Balsero**, west of Vice City. Down there you'll find a launcher with **4 rockets**, some grenades and the unique **Conspiracist Type 9** machine pistol. Five channels have now been to the spot: WiLLiSGaming, Milo, Millzaa, Rodwenn and FuriousFade. WiLLiSGaming also found a second launcher in a **Black Eagle warehouse**.

## Where it is: opposite the Griffin Shooting Range

The shooting range is labeled on the map, so use it as your landmark:

- Millzaa opens its map card: **Griffin Shooting Range**, in **Balsero**. It's a range that also sells guns and gear, and the waiver costs $5.
- Zoomed all the way out, the spot sits **just under the D and A of LEONIDA**, between **Kelly County** and **Vice-Dale County**.
- The trailer is **across the street** from the range. Millzaa, Rodwenn and Milo all park there and climb the fence.

WiLLiSGaming came in along the **SR-120**, with **Omega Pkwy** just to the south and **Buckskin Swamp** off to the west. In his voiceover he says "Mariana County", but both his map and Millzaa's show the Vice City area. He suggests waiting until **Chapter 2**, since the story sends you that way anyway.

## Step by step

1. Drive to the **Griffin Shooting Range** and pull over on the opposite side of the road. You don't need to go inside the range.
2. Hop the fence into a lot full of **old trailers** that looks like a junkyard. The game pops a reminder here that cops will spot you faster if your weapon is out in the open.
3. Go into the **trailer** (WiLLiSGaming describes an RV covered in antennas).
4. Find the way down. Rodwenn, Milo and Millzaa use a **hatch in the floor** that drops into a cellar under the trailer. WiLLiSGaming describes a **bunker door at the back** of the trailer instead. If you can't spot one, look for the other.
5. Downstairs there's a dead man in a chair and a phone with a **View Media** option. WiLLiSGaming says the clip on that phone shows what happened to him.
6. Rodwenn finds the **rocket launcher** behind the chair. The **Conspiracist Type 9** is in a gun case to the left, and the shelf holds **Frag Grenade** pickups, healing items and adrenaline.

In FuriousFade's video, the pickup prompt names the launcher **H-AT Launcher**.

## How many rockets you get

- **4 rockets.** The weapon wheel in WiLLiSGaming's video shows 1 loaded and 3 in reserve.
- WiLLiSGaming says rockets can't be bought, so hang on to these for late in the story or a real emergency.
- **The launcher isn't a unique weapon.** Die with it and it's gone, so keep it holstered until you need it.

WiLLiSGaming mentions other launchers around the map but doesn't say where, apart from the Black Eagle one.

## The second one: Black Eagle warehouse

The Black Eagle warehouse, home of the **Black Eagle Import M306 TMR** rifle, has another launcher. That makes **2 launchers and 8 rockets** by his count. WiLLiSGaming doesn't show where the warehouse is on the map, and getting in is fiddly: only Lucia fits through the gap that leads to the rifle. The details are in [unique weapons](../gta-6-unique-weapons-locations/).

## The gun that comes with it

The **Conspiracist Type 9** shows its name in yellow, the mark of a unique weapon. WiLLiSGaming says those stay with you even after you die.
