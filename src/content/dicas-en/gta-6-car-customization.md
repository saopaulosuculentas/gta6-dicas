You customize cars in GTA 6 at a **tuning shop**, and one opens up very early: **Coastal Auto Care**, close to Jason's house. You can upgrade the engine, swap the exhaust, fit a full body kit, change the paint and even redo the interior. Register the car first if you plan to keep it, because customizing is one of the perks that come with registration.

## Where to customize

StevieWunderKid says you get access to a shop near Jason's house pretty early in the story. Drive in with a car and pick what you want to change. The shop's name, Coastal Auto Care, shows up on the menu in Bombastic's video and on the banner behind the cars in Stevie's.

### Lose the cops before you pull in

A tuning shop won't let you in while you're wanted. During Elanip's stream, with the police after him, he pulled up to a shop and the game refused. The on-screen message explains that mod shops are off-limits inside a **heat area** when you're driving a **known vehicle**. He gave up on that one and drove to a Pay 'n' Spray instead. Shake the police first, then come back. Tips for that are in [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).

## What you can change

| Part | What it does | Shown by |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | Restore it, then upgrade it. Each level changes the engine pitch and rev limiter | StevieWunderKid, Bombastic |
| Exhaust | Mostly about sound: a new, much louder note plus backfires when you lift off the throttle | StevieWunderKid |
| Body | Full body kits (Full Kits) | Bombastic |
| Paint | Custom liveries | Bombastic |
| Interior | You can change it too | Bombastic |

In the engine menu from Bombastic's video, only **Full Engine** was available. **Intakes**, **Transmission** and **Boost** were locked, and the video doesn't explain what unlocks them.

Stevie says the jump between engine levels is easy to hear. Bombastic was amazed by how much you can change and wondered whether every car in the game gets this many options. None of the videos show what the parts cost.

## The Ultimate Edition kit

In the Full Kits section, Bombastic found a kit that was only there because he owns the **Ultimate Edition**, and he put it on his car. Camodo Gaming says the same: if you paid for the pricey edition, you get exclusive kits. The full list of extras is in the [Ultimate Edition bonuses guide](../gta-6-ultimate-edition-bonuses/).

## Register the car first

According to GameSpot, being able to customize a car at shops is one of the perks of **registering** it. A registered car can also be summoned with the Scooter Bros app for a small fee, stores all your weapons in the trunk automatically, and can be repaired if it gets destroyed.

- **Where to register:** at Moses's garage or a Pay 'n' Spray, once you've met Moses in Chapter 2.
- **Trackers:** a car with a tracker can't be registered until you've met the people who remove them. After that, any Pay 'n' Spray can pull it. More in [how to steal cars](../gta-6-how-to-steal-cars/) and [how to sell cars](../gta-6-how-to-sell-cars/).

## Repair before you repaint

A damaged car won't take a new paint job. Camodo Gaming tried to respray a car he had just smashed up, and the game told him to **repair it first**. Repair costs and the places that do it are in [how to refuel and repair your car](../gta-6-refuel-repair-car/).

Think twice before covering the factory paint. Once Camodo resprayed, the car's original paint scheme was gone. He guessed a tuning shop might bring it back but didn't try, and said he'd reload his save otherwise. If you like the stock look, **save the game first**.
