**GTA 6 got its first post-launch patch on November 23**, four days after the game came out. Rockstar's patch notes list **seven fixes**: crashes, autosave, weather that wouldn't change, phone calls that never came, a wanted level you couldn't shake, the gym deadlift bar and partners holding on to your bags. Kotaku ran through the list and linked to the notes on Rockstar Support.

## Everything the patch fixes

1. **Crashes.** Several issues that made the game crash are fixed. If yours still crashes, try the steps in [GTA 6 crashing or stuck on loading](../gta-6-crashing-loading-screen-fix/).
2. **Stuck weather.** The weather could stay the same for long stretches. If you want a change right away, there's a [cycle weather cheat](../gta-6-cycle-weather-cheat/).
3. **Autosave.** The game wasn't autosaving correctly. See [how GTA 6 autosave works](../gta-6-autosave-explained/).
4. **Missing phone calls.** Some calls didn't trigger, including ones that move the story forward or unlock missions. If you were stuck waiting for a call, update the game. Our [mission list](../gta-6-mission-list/) shows what comes next.
5. **Wanted level.** A bug could leave you unable to lose your stars. See [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).
6. **Gym deadlift.** The deadlift bar at the gym couldn't be used.
7. **Fence bags.** Your partner wouldn't hand the bags over, so you couldn't sell them to a fence. See [fence locations](../gta-6-fence-locations/).

## What it means for autosave

Plenty of players felt GTA 6 wasn't autosaving as often as it should. Kotaku says at least some of that seems to trace back to this bug. The game still only saves on its own at certain points, though, so **save manually before you quit**. Our [saving guide](../gta-6-how-to-save/) shows how.

## What didn't get fixed

- **Error CE-108255-1 on PS5.** The code isn't in the notes, which only talk about crashes in general. Kotaku says it's unclear whether this one is resolved. Here's [what we know about CE-108255-1](../gta-6-ce-108255-1-error/).
- **ATMs and the wanted profile.** Player complaints about ATMs and about how the wanted profile works aren't covered. Kotaku thinks they could be handled in later updates. Meanwhile, here's how [banks and ATMs work](../gta-6-bank-atm-deposit/).

Kotaku calls this a small patch and expects more in the coming weeks. We'll update this page when the next one lands.

## Still unconfirmed

The report we read doesn't give a version number or a download size, and it doesn't explain how to grab the update or check which version you have. We'll add those once an English source we can read spells them out.
