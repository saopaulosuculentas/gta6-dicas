**You can steal ATMs in GTA 6 by ripping them out of the wall with a winch pickup.** Then you blow them open with a **sticky bomb** and grab the cash. GameSpot and YouTuber Unknown Player show the same method, and Unknown Player made **$104,000 in under an hour**.

## What you need

- **A vehicle with a winch.** Tow trucks driving around the city work, but they're slow to escape in. The better pick is a **pickup with a winch**, like the Bravado Bison 1800 or the Vapid Montagne.
- **The Dye Pack Jammer.** Without it, the bills can come with a **pink dye pack** that explodes, ruins the cash and marks you for the cops. It unlocks in **Chapter 3** when you finish the **Credit Check** mission, and it stays active for good on both characters.
- **Sticky bombs.** Ammu-Nation sells them. According to Unknown Player, there's a table of **free** sticky bombs in the room behind **Yasmani** at his shop.

Unknown Player says you can do this from early in the game, but it gets much better from Chapter 3.

## How to install the winch

1. Take the pickup to a **mod shop** that works on that vehicle type. For pickups, GameSpot points to **Wasteland Off-road** in **Kelly County**. Ultimate Edition owners can also use **One-Eyed Willie's** in Leonard County.
2. Go to **Bodyworks > Towing** and buy the **winch**. GameSpot lists it at $1,595; Unknown Player paid about $1,700.
3. If the pickup was stolen with a tracker, take it through **Pay 'n' Spray** and register it first. Unknown Player often finds a pickup that **already has a winch** and no tracker in the lot **behind Yasmani's shop**.

With a winch fitted, your **minimap automatically shows** nearby ATMs you can steal. The **BuckMe** phone app also has a "Find Nearest ATM" option.

## Step by step

1. Find an ATM **outside**. Ones inside buildings don't work.
2. **Reverse** up to it with your back end facing it. The winch hooks on automatically.
3. Drive forward, reverse, and drive forward again a few times until the ATM **rips out**. This triggers the **alarm**, and the wanted stars follow soon after.
4. **Open the ATM**:
   - **Sticky bomb** (the best option, per Unknown Player): right after pulling it out, hop out, stick a bomb on it and move your vehicle away before it blows. The cassettes come out already open.
   - **Cut-off tool**, which unlocks in Chapter 3 during **Meet the Heders**.
   - **Brute force**: drag it into things until it breaks. Slow and luck-based.
5. **Four cash cassettes** drop out. Stomp or shoot them open and grab the cash.
6. Get away. The bills are **marked**, so sell them to a fence. Yasmani pays more for them.

## How much it pays

| ATM | Payout, per Unknown Player |
|---|---|
| Regular ATM | $7,000 to $8,000, sometimes more |
| Yasmani's 3 daily marked ATMs | $10,000 to $13,000 |

The **three special ATMs** show up on your map after you've done Yasmani's jewelry jobs and Credit Check.

## Tips to keep the cash

- **Don't blow up your own car** or anyone else's. It counts as another crime and brings the cops faster.
- **Stick to quiet areas.** Downtown has more ATMs but more police too.
- **Only stash full bags** in the vehicle. Winch vehicles hold **6 bags** at most, according to Unknown Player.
- **Double-tap triangle** (Y on Xbox) to hop out with the engine running.
- The same ATM **takes a while to come back**, so keep moving.
