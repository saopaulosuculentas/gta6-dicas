The raise wanted level cheat in GTA 6 is **Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Right, Up**. What it does: raises your wanted level (police stars). There is no cheat to remove wanted stars in the lists published so far; to lose the cops, see [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).

## How to enter it

1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Right, Up.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to **lose a 3-star wanted level 50 times**. Progress you made during the story counts.

## What you need to know

- No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
- Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
- So far only GamesRadar+ has shown this sequence, so it is marked unverified.
