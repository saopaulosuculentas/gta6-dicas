GTA 6 hides a secret government lab with alien experiments. It sits under an abandoned building at **South Point Airfield** in Hamlet, and at the far end there's a small **gray alien** you can set free. The keypad code **isn't the same for everyone**: it comes from the [alien dream](../gta-6-alien-dream-sequence-code/) that the abducted character has after the UFO event. ZacCoxTV was first to show the rescue (he credits the find to a player called The Truth Hurts), and GameSpot has since published the full route.

## Before you go: abduction and dream

The lab is the last step of a chain:

1. The [UFO abduction](../gta-6-ufo-abduction-truth-is-out-there/), at night in Kelly County.
2. The [alien dream](../gta-6-alien-dream-sequence-code/), which shows your code and a dry run of the lab.
3. The lab itself, where you rescue the alien.

## The code changes between playthroughs

This page used to give **2314** as the code, the number ZacCoxTV used. The code actually comes from the dream, and players are getting different ones:

| Who showed it | Code |
|---|---|
| Skill Games (Jason) | **2134** |
| ZacCoxTV (Jason) | **2314** |

To get yours, count the light bars in each group above the keypad in the dream, left to right. Each group is one digit. GameSpot says it's usually 2134, and that inside the lab the number is also marked on the wall to the left of the keypad.

## Where it is

- GameSpot names the spot **South Point Airfield**, in **Hamlet**, next to the radio tower.
- On the map in ZacCoxTV's video it's **east of Pelican Valley**, beside a **small airstrip**, at the end of **Boundary Rd**, south of the T.H. Power Station and north of Shoeless Keys.

Look for an old graffiti-covered building behind a fence, with **rusty radio towers** around it, restricted area signs and a locked gate.

## Step by step

1. **Get inside the fence.** The gate stays shut. GameSpot says part of the fence on the left side can be climbed.
2. **Go up to the roof.** Use the ladder on the far side of the building and drop in through the **open hatch**. Head downstairs.
3. **Enter your code** on the keypad next to the door with the red restricted area sign. The light turns green and the door opens.
4. **Ride the elevator** down to the basement. It's an abandoned lab full of skeletons of workers, soldiers and even cows, with cobwebs everywhere.
5. **Solve the control room panel.** GameSpot mentions a tape in there about why aliens keep taking cattle. Press the panel buttons until **every light is green**, then pull the **lever** to unlock the doors.
6. **Work through the three doors.** In GameSpot's order:
   - the **middle** one still has radiation that slowly drains your health, so move fast;
   - the **left** door and corridor lead to a desk with a **bottle of milk**; take it;
   - go back to the control room and through the **right** door, where a **capsule** takes the milk and opens up to reveal the alien.
7. **Grab the alien and run.** Picking it up releases a **toxic gas**. Get back to the elevator quickly and go up.
8. **Carry the alien** out of the facility.

Earlier versions of this page called it a glass of milk. GameSpot says bottle, and it's a bottle Jason picks up in the dream footage from Skill Games.

## How it ends

Outside, the alien wakes up and looks at your character before being beamed up into a flying saucer. Then government agents pull up. The saucer disappears, comes back and wipes out their vehicles. It looks like a thank-you for saving the alien.

## Jason or Lucia?

ZacCoxTV and Skill Games both played this chain as Jason. GameSpot says either Jason or Lucia can be the one abducted, and the dream goes to whoever was taken. We haven't seen an English video of Lucia doing the lab itself yet.

## The Lucia scene with planets

ZacCoxTV also showed another player's clip where Lucia walks through a place with portals, planets and a sphere showing the map. That's the [alien dream](../gta-6-alien-dream-sequence-code/), which comes after the abduction.
