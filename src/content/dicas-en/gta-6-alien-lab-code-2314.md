GTA 6 hides a secret government lab with alien experiments. It's under an abandoned facility, the door opens with the code **2314**, and at the far end of the lab there's a small **gray alien** you can set free. ZacCoxTV showed the whole route and credits the discovery to a player called The Truth Hurts.

## Where it is

From the map shown in the video:

- **East of Pelican Valley**, in a green area between the swamp and the highway.
- Next to a **small airstrip**.
- At the end of **Boundary Rd**, south of the T.H. Power Station and north of Shoeless Keys.

You'll find an old graffiti-covered building behind a fence, surrounded by **rusty radio towers** and no-trespassing signs.

## Step by step

1. Get through the fence and walk up to the building.
2. Find the door with the red **restricted area** sign. There's a keypad next to it.
3. Enter **2314**. The keypad light turns green and the door opens.
4. Take the **elevator** down. Below is the abandoned lab, with skeletons of workers, soldiers and even cows, and cobwebs everywhere.
5. Press the buttons in the rooms until the next door opens. You reach the lab itself, with boards full of notes on the experiments.
6. Pick up the **glass of milk**.
7. Go down the tunnel to the other room and **place the glass of milk** where indicated. That frees the alien.
8. **Carry the alien** out of the facility.

## How it ends

Outside, the alien wakes up and looks Jason in the eye before being beamed up into a flying saucer. Then government vehicles arrive. The saucer disappears, comes back and destroys all three of them. It looks like a thank-you for saving the alien.

## A second scene with Lucia

ZacCoxTV also shows another player's clip where Lucia is inside what looks like a flying saucer, with portals, planets and a sphere showing Earth from above the lab. Nobody knows yet how that scene starts.
