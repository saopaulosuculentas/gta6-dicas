The alien dream is the second step of GTA 6's UFO mystery, and it's where you get the **code for the secret alien lab**. After the abduction that pops "The Truth Is Out There", whoever was taken (Jason or Lucia) has a strange dream while sleeping. The code shows up as **groups of light bars** above a keypad, and the rest of the dream walks you through the lab before you ever set foot in it. GameSpot lays out the whole chain, and Skill Games recorded Jason's dream from start to finish.

## First, the abduction

No abduction, no dream. If you haven't done that part yet, see [the UFO abduction guide](../gta-6-ufo-abduction-truth-is-out-there/). GameSpot says it opens up once you've finished Chapter 1.

## When the dream starts

- Only **the character who was abducted** has it. In Skill Games' video that's Jason.
- It begins with a short scene of him **asleep in bed**. GameSpot says it comes after some time has passed, so don't expect it the same night.
- You're in control the whole way, walking around barefoot in white clothes.

## What you see

1. **A dark hallway** with round lights in the ceiling.
2. **A room packed with alien eggs** glowing blue. That's where the "alien eggs" in the video titles come from.
3. **A room with planets** and a sphere in the middle that shows a view of the map. GameSpot says the dream gives you glimpses of where to go and what to do.
4. **A keypad under rows of light bars.** That's your code (see below).
5. **A control panel** full of lights and switches.
6. **A bright white room with cows** and a small table with a **bottle of milk**, which Jason picks up.

It's a rehearsal. In the real lab, according to GameSpot, you also solve a light panel, grab a bottle of milk and put it into the alien's capsule.

## How to read the code

Look at the **light bars above the keypad**. Count the bars in each group from left to right. Each group is one digit.

In Skill Games' video the groups have **2, 1, 3 and 4** bars, and Jason starts typing **2134**. GameSpot also says the code is **usually 2134**, which suggests it isn't the same in every playthrough. Write down whatever your dream shows. GameSpot adds that inside the real lab the code is also marked on the wall to the left of the keypad.

## After you wake up

Jason wakes up in bed and the dream is over. Now head for the real thing: GameSpot puts the lab at **South Point Airfield in Hamlet**, by the radio tower. The full route inside, from the roof hatch to the alien's capsule, is in the [alien lab guide](../gta-6-alien-lab-code-2314/).

## The whole chain

1. Night-time UFO abduction in Kelly County, with "The Truth Is Out There".
2. The dream: eggs, cows, milk and the code in light bars.
3. The lab at South Point Airfield: code, milk and the alien rescue.
