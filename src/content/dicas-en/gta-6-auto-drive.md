GTA 6 has a built-in auto drive. Set a destination on the map, hold **down on the D-pad** while you drive and pick the **cinematic camera**. An **Enable Auto Drive** prompt then appears: press **circle** on PS5 (**B** on Xbox) and your character drives to the marker without you touching the sticks. It works in cars and boats.

Two channels showed it working: JuiceHead on Xbox and Milo on PS5.

## Step by step

1. **Set a destination** on the map first. Milo only got it to work with a waypoint already placed.
2. Get in a car or boat and start driving.
3. **Hold down on the D-pad** to open the vehicle's quick menu.
4. Pick the **cinematic camera**. The view switches to movie-style angles.
5. **Enable Auto Drive** shows up in the bottom corner. Press **circle** (B on Xbox).
6. The vehicle now drives itself. Press the same button, now labeled **Disable Auto Drive**, to take the wheel back. To leave the cinematic camera, the game tells you to **hold the D-pad**.

To find and mark places without opening the full map, see [how the map index works](../gta-6-index-find-missions/).

## Three speeds

While it is on, the corner of the screen shows **Speed 1/2/3**. On JuiceHead's Xbox, LT and RT move between them; on PS5 that is L2 and R2.

Even at the top speed, the car **follows traffic laws**. It stops at lights and waits its turn, so trips take longer than driving yourself. JuiceHead's trick is to switch it off, pass the slow car ahead, then switch it back on without stopping.

When you arrive, JuiceHead says the game **parks the car in a parking spot** for you.

## The Auto Drive setting

The **Gameplay** tab in the settings has an **Auto Drive** option with 2 values. The in-game text says it drives you to your set destination and can only be turned on in the cinematic camera. In JuiceHead's menu it sits on **Restricted**, which the game describes as available only in **safe scenarios**. The video never shows the other value. That same tab has more options that make the game easier; see [GTA 6 difficulty settings](../gta-6-difficulty-easy-mode-settings/).

## Boats are where it shines

There is hardly any traffic on the water, so a boat on auto drive gets there fast. JuiceHead used it all the time to head home: the boat sailed itself right up to the **ladder behind Jason's house**.

## Cop cars with the siren on

In a police car with the **siren on**, auto drive moves more freely through traffic, though it still sticks to the rules where it can, according to JuiceHead. Milo tested the siren while driving himself: the cars ahead and a group of people in his path moved aside.
