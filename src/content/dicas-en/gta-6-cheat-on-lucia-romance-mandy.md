No. In GTA 6, **Jason can't cheat on Lucia** and **you can't romance Mandy**. Mandy flirts with him all the time, but Jason turns her down every single time, and you have no control over it. Rockstar has said the game is built around the couple and that this kind of option doesn't fit their story.

Mandy is the tenant you meet in the Chapter 1 mission **Pipework**.

## Why Mandy isn't a romance option

According to GameSpot, which played through the mission:

- Mandy makes it obvious from the start that she's into Jason.
- **No dialogue choice** lets you steer things in a romantic direction. Jason is set on making things right with Lucia.
- At the end of the mission, after the sink is fixed, she makes **one last try** to get him to stay. Jason leaves, and the game doesn't let you choose otherwise.

The full rent collection walkthrough and where her house is are in [Mandy in GTA 6](../gta-6-mandy-house-rent-mission/).

## What Rockstar said

PC Gamer reports that a group of players was upset about not being able to pick up other women in the game. In a statement to Shacknews, Rockstar said GTA 6 is the story of **a couple on the run** and that everything in the game is there to support that story. A lot of features from earlier GTA games were left out for that reason.

You can still go to clubs, even as a couple, but there's no option to go further with anyone else. The only relationship the game tracks is Jason and Lucia's. See how it works in [Jason and Lucia's romance guide](../gta-6-romance-jason-lucia/).

## Can you go back to Mandy's place?

Yes. GameSpot says that later on, **walking, biking or driving past her house** can trigger more conversations between the two. Even so, she's still not a romance option, which is why GameSpot puts "relationship" in quotes.

## The secret in Mandy's sink

Plenty of people are looking up the "sink scene" from Pipework. Apex Films' video shows what happens:

1. Jason tries the **plunger** on the kitchen sink first. It doesn't work.
2. He crawls under the sink and uses a **wrench** to loosen the pipe. In that part of the video, the wrench prompt shows up on **RT**, an Xbox button. The PS5 buttons are on the Mandy mission page.
3. Out of the pipe comes a **small object completely covered in grime**. Jason holds it up near his face and grimaces.

The moment is quick and the object is filthy. The sources don't agree on what it is:

- **PC Gamer:** it looks like a **finger**, and Mandy grabs it out of Jason's hand right away and leaves with it. The article says this has made plenty of players suspicious of her.
- **GameSpot:** it only calls it a curious item that was clogging the pipes.
- **Apex Films:** in that part of the video, the object is caked in grime and hard to make out.

Rockstar hasn't explained the scene. For now, the "finger" is PC Gamer's reading, not something the game confirms.

The same video wanders around Mandy's yard and her green car looking for more clues, but doesn't show anything conclusive. For the whole mission with every button, see the [Chapter 1 walkthrough](../gta-6-chapter-1-walkthrough/).
