GTA 6's criminal profile is the system that tracks **how you treat people in the world**, much like the honor system in Red Dead Redemption 2. It comes down almost entirely to **how often you kill**. Wounding or incapacitating enemies keeps your profile clean, while killing cops, civilians and defenseless animals drags it down. **Jason and Lucia each have their own profile**, and according to GameSpot both of them **start at Hardened**. Playing non-lethally lifts you to Professional, while a high body count drags you to Violent, then Psycho, and finally Irredeemable.

## The five levels

Every level comes with an emoji-style "face with horns" icon that appears on screen.

| Level | Color | How you get there | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional | Yellow | Only the violence you need, incapacitate instead of kill, stay undetected | Non-lethal takedowns and kind acts toward citizens fill your focus meter |
| Hardened | Orange | Kill enemies, kill citizens only in self-defense, don't worry about being seen | Same as Professional |
| Violent | Purple | Skip non-lethal options, kill enemies and police, kill citizens at random | Headshots fill your focus meter |
| Psycho | Light gray | Kill lots of cops, regularly kill helpless citizens, kill pets and defenseless animals (hunting doesn't count) | Headshots fill focus, but you **can't surrender** to the police |
| Irredeemable | Dark gray | Stay Psycho for too long | No bonus listed |

Focus is the game's bullet time. If you've finished the story, there's even an [unlimited focus cheat](../gta-6-unlimited-focus-cheat/).

## What earns focus at each level

At **Professional** and **Hardened**, focus comes from non-lethal takedowns and small good deeds. GameSpot's examples are rescuing civilians, petting animals and even dropping trash in a bin. At **Violent** and **Psycho**, headshots are what fill the meter. For other ways to top it up, see [how to increase focus](../gta-6-how-to-increase-focus/).

## Irredeemable is forever

Across the first four levels, your profile moves up and down as you play. **Irredeemable** is different: once you hit it, **it stays**. Rockstar says it will have a lasting effect on Jason and Lucia, but nobody knows yet what that means.

You do get a warning. GameSpot says a **split appears in the Psycho icon** right before you tip over into Irredeemable, so that's your cue to stop killing. GameSpot's advice is to avoid the rank altogether, since it changes your playthrough in a big and lasting way. If you're curious anyway, keep a **backup manual save** first, ideally once the story is done or nearly done. Here's [how saving works](../gta-6-how-to-save/).

Keep the permanent rank in mind before chasing a [6 star wanted level](../gta-6-six-star-wanted-level/). Getting there means killing a lot of cops and soldiers, and PC Gamer warns that can hit your profile hard.

## Staying Professional

GameSpot lists what keeps you at the top tier:

- **Stay hidden.** Finish missions without being detected where you can.
- **Use non-lethal takedowns** instead of killing enemies.
- **Don't kill civilians,** and that includes running people over by accident while driving.
- **Defuse when offered.** Some missions give you a prompt to calm things down, which can lead to an outcome where nobody dies.
- **Spare people who surrender** when the game gives you that choice. The [Arnold decision](../gta-6-kill-or-spare-arnold/) is one example of a spare prompt.

## Can you shoot your way through and stay Professional?

PC Gamer's writer finished the main story with both characters at Professional, dipping into Hardened now and then. The approach was to stay non-lethal where possible, using focus to aim for knees and elbows in gunfights.

The body count still wasn't small. The endgame stats show **321 enemies and 58 cops killed**, against **461 enemies and 107 cops incapacitated**. There are also 98 animal kills on the record, and the writer suspects most were birds that refuse to get out of the way of vehicles.

## Checking your profile

Open the menu and go to the **Jason & Lucia** tab, which GameSpot also points to. In the image above, the profile sits at the top of Jason's page there. For more on how the game works early on, see the [beginner tips](../gta-6-beginner-tips/).
