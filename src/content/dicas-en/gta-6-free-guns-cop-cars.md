The quickest way to get free guns in GTA 6 is to raid a **police car trunk**. Cop cars can carry an **assault rifle, a shotgun, ammo and even body armor**. It's a handy way to gear up fast, as long as you accept the risk of messing with police property. PC Gamer laid out the trick, Milo showed it working, and JuiceHead added a couple of tips.

Older GTA games used to hand you a shotgun when you stole a cop car. Vice City's police are better equipped than that, and PC Gamer singles out shotguns and assault rifles as the main haul.

## Police pickups: no alarm at all

Cops sometimes drive pickup trucks such as the **Bravado Bison**. You'll see them both out in the country and in town.

1. Climb into the **truck bed**.
2. Open the **storage crate**.
3. Take the weapons, ammo and anything else inside. You might even find a **Stun Gun**.

Nothing goes off, which makes pickups the easy option.

## Patrol cars: bring the slim jim

Regular patrol cars have alarms on both the doors and the trunk. The way around that is the **slim jim**, a tool you get in the Chapter 2 mission **Moses**. More on it in [slim jim and snap gun](../gta-6-slim-jim-snap-gun/).

1. Slim jim the **driver's door** and get in.
2. Once you're inside, the **trunk unlocks**.
3. Walk around to the back and open it.
4. Help yourself to whatever is there: an assault rifle, a shotgun or body armor. The alarm stays quiet.

**No slim jim yet?** You can still pop the trunk. Your character pries it open with a screwdriver, but the **alarm will sound**.

## What Milo pulled out of one

Milo had just died and lost every gun he owned. He walked up to an ordinary cruiser parked at the curb, opened the trunk and came away with **assault rifles, a shotgun and pistol ammo**. If you've just been wasted and need to rearm, this is a quick fix.

## Traffic stops and night raids

JuiceHead says cop cars are both common and easy to rob, and he has two tips:

- **Traffic stops:** when officers pull someone over at the side of the road, you'll sometimes see **two cruisers parked nose to tail**. He says you can very often loot the one at the back.
- **Night time:** trunks on parked cars in general are soft targets after dark, because hardly anyone reacts to the alarm.

According to him, a cop car trunk gets you **long guns, body armor and better healing items** at a point in the game when you'd normally have none of that.

## Stash the guns in your own car

Walking around with a rifle in your hands or slung on your back draws attention. After the raid, go to your own car's trunk and you'll get a prompt to stow the weapons there. The full breakdown is in [how to store weapons in your trunk](../gta-6-store-weapons-trunk/).

## Where to find an empty cop car

- **Don't try it with officers inside.** PC Gamer makes the same point.
- Look for cruisers parked around town or outside **police stations**, such as the ones in **Port Gellhorn** and **Vice City**.
- If things go wrong and the cops come after you, see [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).
