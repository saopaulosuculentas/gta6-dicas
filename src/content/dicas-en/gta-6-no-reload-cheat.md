The no reload cheat in GTA 6 is **Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Up**. What it does: ammo never runs out and you never need to reload. It is the strongest of the three ammo cheats and the hardest to unlock.

## How to enter it

1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Up.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to **win 25 gold medals at shooting ranges**. Progress you made during the story counts.

## What you need to know

- No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
- Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
- The sequence matches in Bubs and GamesRadar+, so the badge is "Confirmed".

## Heads-up: the name varies between sources

Kotaku prints **this exact sequence** under the name **Infinite Ammo**. Our other sources call it no reload and give a different sequence, ending in down three times, for infinite ammo. Until we check it on screen, we keep the names as they are.
