Yes, you can keep playing GTA 6 after the ending. Once the credits finish, the game drops you back at **the moment right before the final mission**, and from there the whole map is open to you. Kotaku, which has already played through to the end, laid out how it works without spoiling the story.

## How the ending works

1. **The game warns you first.** When you reach the last mission, GTA 6 makes a big deal of asking whether you really want to start the finale. Kotaku's reviewer got there after **roughly 90 hours**.
2. **The finale is several missions long.** When it's over, you see one of the main endings and the credits.
3. **Then the clock rewinds.** You're back at the moment you were asked. This time Jason and Lucia say they aren't ready yet and head back out into Vice City.
4. **The map is yours.** From then on you can clean up whatever you left behind and explore as much as you like.

## You can see another ending

The **final mission marker stays on the map**. Play the finale again and you may land on a different ending.

GTA 6 has **more than one ending**, and which one you get comes down to your choices. Kotaku saw two of them and says they go in very different directions. We're keeping the details out of this page.

## What unlocks after the finale

Beating the final mission opens up two things that were locked before:

- **100% completion.** You can only finish everything after the ending. That includes the A Long Road trophy from our [GTA 6 trophy and achievement list](../gta-6-trophies-achievements-list/).
- **Cheat codes.** The challenges that reveal the game's cheats only show up once the story is done. See [every GTA 6 cheat](../all-gta-6-cheats-list/) and [how to use cheats](../how-to-use-cheats-gta-6/).

## Do you need to clear the map first?

No. There's a huge amount to do outside the story: main and side missions, activities, random events and races. Since the map stays open after the credits, you can push through the story and save the rest for later. To see how much story is left, our [GTA 6 mission list](../gta-6-mission-list/) has all 5 chapters in order.

## How it compares to other Rockstar games

Rockstar's earlier open worlds handled the post-game in different ways:

| Game | What happens after the ending |
|---|---|
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | You keep going as a different character |
| GTA IV | You keep playing as Niko, like in most GTA games |
| GTA 6 | The game rewinds to just before the finale, with Jason and Lucia |

## The catch

Kotaku points out one downside: after the ending, the world feels **frozen in time**. No big new storylines can kick off, since they could clash with how the story wraps up. What's left is the map, unfinished activities and the cheats.
