**The Purple Drank Emerus in GTA 6 is at Chatfield Storage & Logistics, on the Waxman Island docks in Vice-Dale.** It's a **Progen Emerus** supercar that comes **unlocked with no tracker**. The same spot has a **legendary silenced pistol** and a **safe with the code 5800**.

Maxemilio Gaming shows the route and the code. LunarGaming shows the location and the car on screen.

## What's at Chatfield

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Progen **Purple Drank Emerus** | Among the shipping containers |
| Legendary **ES 9** silenced pistol | Main office, upstairs |
| **Safe** (code **5800**) | Same office |
| Laptop with the code | Inside a shipping container, behind a privacy screen |

## Step by step

1. **Save first** and bring a **silenced** weapon. There are plenty of guards.
2. Head to **Chatfield Storage & Logistics** on **Waxman Island**. Maxemilio found a weak side with **a single guard**. Stay **stealthy** so the alarm doesn't go off.
3. **Find the laptop.** It's **inside a shipping container** with a privacy screen that hides it well. Maxemilio walked past it the first time. The code is **5800**.
4. **Go up to the main office.** The **legendary pistol** and the **safe** are there. Open it with **5800**.
5. **Take the Emerus.** It's unlocked, with no alarm and no tracker.

## Keeping the car

- **No tracker:** according to LunarGaming, it's the **only supercar without a tracker**, which is why you can keep it.
- **Registering:** you register cars with **Moses** from **Chapter 2**. From Chapter 3, the **Pay 'n' Spray on Waxman Island**, right next door, can register it too, as LunarGaming's video shows.
- **You can grab it in Chapter 1**, as LunarGaming's title says, but keep it safe: an unregistered car that gets destroyed or lost doesn't come back.
