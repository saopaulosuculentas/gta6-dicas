The easiest way to grab a helicopter or plane in GTA 6 without the cops is to go to an **airfield or helipad marked on the map**. LunarGaming says these spots have a free aircraft waiting **99% of the time**, and taking it doesn't alert the police or any gang.

His video covers about 16 spots, starting in Mariana County. He only talks during the intro, then shows each one on the in-game map. Not every location has a readable name on that map, so the list below sticks to the ones we could read for sure.

## Locations you can pin down

In the order they appear in the video:

| Where (from the map in the video) | Type | What was there |
|---|---|---|
| Southwind, next to Mile Zero Air Station | Helipad on a pier | Helicopter |
| Near T.H. Power Station | Helipad | Green helicopter |
| On Boundary Rd | Airfield | Blue biplane |
| East of Port Gellhorn | Grass airfield | McKittrick Farm crop duster |
| Northern Lummox County | Helipad | Helicopter |
| Leonard County | Airfield | Lapwing Mammatus, parked on a paved path between houses |
| Between Lake Leonida and Waning Sands | Airfield with a hangar | Plane inside the hangar |
| North tip of Vice Beach | Helipad | Helicopters, including a Nagasaki Sea Sparrow |

The map in the video also marks a spot near **MacAlister Gold Mine**, but you can't tell which aircraft sits there.

A few more spots show up without a readable name on the map:

- an airstrip on an island with a beach, home to a black and orange stunt plane with **Black Dragon** painted on the wings;
- a seaplane base with a hangar and a dock;
- a helipad with a **Bottcher Buzzard**. Climbing in made the game show a **Military Vehicles 1/8** counter.

## Where not to go

- The **military base**, the **lifeguard stations** and the **international airport** all have aircraft, but LunarGaming says they'll get you a Wanted Level.
- ZoneX adds that the yellow zones that appear on the map as you get close are restricted areas, like the base and the airport, and the stars come almost instantly.

If you really want a jet, check [how to steal a plane at the airport](../gta-6-airport-steal-plane/) and [how to get into the military base](../gta-6-military-base-location/).

## Helipads in Vice City

LunarGaming leaves the city out of his list. According to him, most tall buildings and big hotels in Vice City have a helipad on the roof, and they're already marked on the map. He also mentions seaplanes and helicopters scattered along the coast, around Lake Leonida and in Grassrivers.

## The crop duster

- It's at the spot east of Port Gellhorn. The plane has the **McKittrick Farm** logo on the tail and a spray bar under the wing.
- When you walk up, a man asks if you're there for the "dusting job". The video doesn't show that job.
- ZoneX also suggests grabbing a crop duster from an airstrip for early flights. Flying clears the map fog much faster than driving, and a jet taken from the base gets chased down and shot at. He doesn't name the airstrip.
- In the vehicle menu shown in the video, the **Lapwing 300-H** is described as a light plane built for dusting crops that also handles aerial stunts.
