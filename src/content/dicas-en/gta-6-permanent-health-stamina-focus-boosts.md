**Five secrets in GTA 6 permanently boost your health, stamina or focus.** The **alien** one improves everything. **Hell** gives max health. And three give max stamina: **Kyle's strawberry**, the **mushroom parkour** and the **No Flushers sewer**.

The five secrets and their boosts come from Arekkz Gaming's video. PowerPyx confirms the name of the sewer mission.

## Summary

| Secret | What it gives | When |
|---|---|---|
| Aliens (abduction, dream and lab) | Health, stamina and focus | Chapter 2 onward, late at night |
| Hell | Max health, a legendary shotgun, tattoos and a T-shirt | At night, in Port Gellhorn |
| Kyle's strawberry | Max stamina | From Chapter 2 |
| Mushroom parkour | Max stamina | Any time |
| No Flushers sewer | Max stamina | Any time |

## 1. Aliens: every stat

This is the longest one, in three parts:

1. **Abduction:** late at night, find a **parked car with flashing lights** on a country road near Port Gellhorn and follow the trail to the UFO. Help the woman and fetch her car. You get **The Truth Is Out There** trophy. See the [abduction walkthrough](../gta-6-ufo-abduction-truth-is-out-there/).
2. **Dream:** sleep until morning for 2 to 4 nights until you have the dream. In it you learn the **keypad code**, which changes from player to player. See [the alien dream](../gta-6-alien-dream-sequence-code/).
3. **Lab:** use the code at the hidden complex near Hamlet, in the starting area. See [the alien lab](../gta-6-alien-lab-code-2314/).

When you leave, your **health, stamina and focus** go up.

## 2. Hell: max health

It starts at a **garage in Port Gellhorn**, next to a robbery spot, **at night**.

1. Talk to the two men out front. A van pulls up: get in.
2. Walk to the **campfire** and look into the fire.
3. You get a **shotgun with infinite ammo** and face a boss who summons goats and exploding monsters.

The reward is **max health**, the legendary **Hell Spawn** shotgun next to the throne, new **tattoos** at the parlor and a **T-shirt**. See the [hell walkthrough](../gta-6-satan-boss-fight-easter-egg/).

**If you die during the boss fight:** according to Arekkz, you fail and get no boost, so save first.

## 3. Kyle's strawberry: max stamina

1. In **Chapter 2**, take the **pilot** flyer from the wall in the motel reception.
2. Do the **crop dusting** job by plane.
3. Wait for **Kyle's** call and go pick up a **van**. They ask for **$5,000**: you can pay, haggle or take it on credit. Arekkz paid $3,000.
4. Drive the van carefully to the **barn** and **eat the strawberry**: max stamina.

The rest of this story is in [the Red Hulk](../gta-6-red-hulk/).

## 4. Mushroom parkour: max stamina

It's at a **construction site next to a lake in the north of the map**:

1. Behind some men, inside a **yellow container**, there's a **mystery box with an eye** on it. Use it and **eat the mushroom**.
2. Keep going until you **pull the power lever**.
3. Follow the **purple light**. At the fork, go **right**, toward the light, not down the dark hallway.
4. In the **parkour** room, if you fall you start over. Use running jumps. Your stamina doesn't drain in the water, but stay away from the jellyfish.
5. At the end you see **yourself** in the container. **Jump** on yourself: max stamina.

## 5. No Flushers sewer: max stamina

This is the **The Final Flush** mission, which also counts for the **Strangers & Freaks** trophy, according to PowerPyx:

1. Go to the **public restroom on Corsair Key**, the small island with a single building, close to where the game starts. Arekkz used the **men's** restroom with Jason.
2. **Scan the QR code** on the flyer with your phone.
3. Answer **"truth"** to the message and go to **Frank's Waffles**.
4. Drive the man's **van**, following the **spoken directions**, to the **sewage treatment plant** south of Vice-Dale. Park under the bridge and go on foot.
5. Skip the main entrance: go around to the right to the **lit side entrance**.
6. Climb the stairs. On the panel, set **all three buttons to green** and press the big button. A passage opens under the pump.
7. **Flush him** and climb into the pod. You wake up at home with **max stamina**.
