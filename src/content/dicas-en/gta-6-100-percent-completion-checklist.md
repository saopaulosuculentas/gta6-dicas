**GTA 6's 100% asks for a lot less than everything.** According to PowerPyx, the checklist is closer to **60% of the game's actual content**: most side missions don't count, and many collectibles only need part of the total. Finishing it unlocks the gold trophy **A Long Road**.

The checklist comes from PowerPyx, and vAnother Level's video shows the same items on the in-game screen.

## Where to track it

- Go to **Progress > Stats > 100% Completion**.
- The checklist **only appears after you finish the story**, but everything you do before then **already counts**.
- **Nothing is missable**: it all stays available in free roam after the ending.

## What needs everything

Only five things require full completion: **robberies** (gang operations, banks, Payday Loans and bank vaults), all **33 underwater discoveries**, **shooting ranges**, **drone races** and **Bret's 15 hunting quests**. Everything else only needs part of the total.

## What to do during the story to save time

- **Scan every new vehicle** with the **WAiNK** app: hold the phone button, then the scan button. Boats and aircraft count too. Start early and the 200 come naturally.
- **Study animals with binoculars**: pick the binoculars from the weapon wheel, zoom in and hold to study. No hunting needed. Every dog and cat breed counts separately.
- **Do optional missions as they appear.** They have purple icons or black icons with pink letters. Each car delivery for **Moses** and **Randy** counts as one, and meditating as both Jason and Lucia at the same spot counts as two.
- **Save the large bank vaults for last.** They only unlock in **Chapter 4**, with the optional mission That's Where The Money Is..., and each one can pay up to **$205,000**.
