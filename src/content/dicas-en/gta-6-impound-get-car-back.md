To get your car back from the impound in GTA 6, **pay the Scooter Bros app** on your phone to release it, or **head to the impound lot and take it back yourself**. This works for your **personal vehicle**, the one registered in your name. The map index tells you which lot it's sitting in.

## Why the cops took your car

GamesRadar+ lists three ways your ride ends up in impound:

- You **ditched it** to shake a Wanted Level. That clears your Vehicle Heat, but the cops will most likely tow it.
- You got **Wasted** while driving it.
- You got **Busted** while driving it.

More on shaking the cops in [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).

## Where is my car?

- **Personal vehicle:** open the map index and scroll to the **Personal** tab. Your registered car is always marked there, and if it's impounded, that's where you see which lot is holding it.
- **Last car you used:** GamesRadar+ says the last unowned vehicle you dropped is also marked under Personal, so you can find a stolen ride again.

## Option 1: pay Scooter Bros

**Scooter Bros** is an app on Jason's and Lucia's phones. It brings your car to you, repairs it and **gets it out of the police impound**. Kotaku and BennyCentral both walk through those three uses.

- **It's pricey.** GamesRadar+ calls it a significant fee, and Kotaku says impound recovery isn't cheap. Neither gives an exact number.
- **Risk to your loot:** going through Scooter Bros may cost you any **stash bags** hidden in the trunk, per GamesRadar+.
- **Premium:** for **$12,500** you get **three free uses every in-game day**. Kotaku and BennyCentral both recommend it once you can afford it, since it pays for itself over time.

## Option 2: go get it

GamesRadar+ suggests this route if you care about what was in the car:

1. Check the **Personal** tab in the index to see which lot has your car.
2. Go there and **steal your own car back**.
3. If the cops already pulled the loot out of your trunk, **check the cop cars parked nearby**. Your goods may be in one of them.

The game teaches you how to slip into an impound lot during the Chapter 1 mission **Unwanted Attention**: hop the fence, stay crouched and toss a bottle to distract the guard. The steps are in the [Chapter 1 walkthrough](../gta-6-chapter-1-walkthrough/). No source has tested that approach with your own car.

## What about the trunk?

The sources don't fully agree:

- **PC Gamer** says that even if the cops impound your car, you can recover the jewelry, drugs and marked bills from the trunk later and sell them.
- **GamesRadar+** warns that the stash bags may be lost if Scooter Bros does the recovery.

So it's worth selling your haul quickly at a [fence](../gta-6-fence-locations/). And don't stash goods in a car you don't own: PC Gamer warns you might lose them along with the car.

## Registered cars only

Per GamesRadar+, Scooter Bros recovers your **personal vehicle** when it's lost, impounded or destroyed, always for a fee. An **unowned** car that gets destroyed is gone for good, trunk and all. To register a car, drive it into a **Pay 'n' Spray** and pay the fee, which depends on the make and model. Cars with a standard or advanced tracker can only be registered once you're further into the story.
