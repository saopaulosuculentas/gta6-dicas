**The Truffade Thrax is the fastest stock car in GTA 6**, according to GameSpot. There's more than one on the map, but the one you can **register as your own** belongs to **Andrew Node** and sits in a mansion on **Starfish Island**, in Vice City. It has **no tracker**, and registering it costs **$70,000**.

For now, this comes from GameSpot only.

## Every Thrax on the map

| Thrax | Where | Can you keep it? |
|---|---|---|
| Andrew Node's | Mansion garage on Starfish Island, Vice City | **Yes.** No tracker, $70,000 to register |
| Randy's | Dalton Island Club, on Dalton Island | No, it goes to Randy |
| Boone Speedway | Can spawn near Boone Speedway | Only by luck, not guaranteed |
| End of the story | In a mission near the end | No, according to GameSpot |

## Andrew Node's Thrax (the one you keep)

The mansion is on **Starfish Island**, in the **Vice-Dale** district. On the map, it's the long house just below the word "Island", on the south side of the main oval road.

**The normal route**, according to GameSpot:

1. In **Chapter 3**, **Cal** texts you about the **No Flusher** group.
2. From then on, use **WAiNK** to scan the **manhole covers with a blue toilet painted on them**. There are **10**.
3. Once you have all 10, open a **shed in Ocean Beach** with the code **8554013**.
4. Inside the shed there's a **laptop** with **Andrew Node's address**.
5. Go to the mansion on Starfish Island, open the garage and take the **blue Thrax**.

**Not enough cash to register it?** Park it in one of your [garages](../gta-6-garage-locations-prices/), where it won't disappear, until you've saved up the $70,000.

> GameSpot also mentions a shortcut that uses a **glitch** to get into the garage. We don't teach it: it can break your save and may stop working after an update.

## Randy's Thrax

The Thrax is one of the last cars on **Randy's list**. According to GameSpot:

1. Deliver the **Mamba GT** to Randy and **wait for his call** about the Thrax. If you go before the call, the car **isn't there**.
2. Head to **Dalton Island**, a resort island southeast of Vice-Dale. No boat? Take the **ferry** from the south port of **Waxman Island** for **$50**.
3. On the island, grab a **golf cart** and drive to the **Dalton Island Club**, in the south.
4. In the **valet lot on the west side**, take down the guard near the **dark green Thrax**.
5. Take the ferry back and deliver the car to Randy's shop in Balsero.

## The Boone Speedway Thrax (luck)

GameSpot says a third Thrax can spawn near **Boone Speedway**, but it's down to luck. Don't plan around it. If you want a Thrax of your own, Andrew Node's is the sure one.
