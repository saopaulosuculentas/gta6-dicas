GTA 6 is led by **Jason Duval**, played by **Dylan Rourke**, and **Lucia Caminos**, played by **Manni L. Perez**. Both names sit at the top of the main cast section in the **official credits** Rockstar published on the game's website at launch.

## The main characters

These are the eight characters Rockstar featured on its official site before launch, with the actor credited for each one.

| Character | Who they are | Actor |
|---|---|---|
| Jason Duval | Ex-soldier who grew up around con artists and ended up running for dealers in the Keys. He wants a quieter life | Dylan Rourke |
| Lucia Caminos | Fresh out of prison, ambitious and a lifelong fighter who wants to make her mother proud | Manni L. Perez |
| Cal Hampton | Jason's friend, a homebody techie who listens in on restricted radio channels | Bobby Moynihan |
| Brian Heder | Old-school Keys drug runner and Jason's landlord | Stephen Root |
| Boobie Ike | A Vice City local legend | Mike Forbs |
| Dre'Quan Priest | Always more of a dealmaker than a gangster | Naiqui Macabroad |
| Real Dimez | Duo of Bae-Luxe and Roxy, friends since school | Hood Brat (Bae-Luxe) and Krissy Celess (Roxy Rashad) |
| Raul Bautista | Veteran bank robber who's always scouting talent | Manny Perez |

Watch out for the similar names: **Manni L. Perez** (Lucia) and **Manny Perez** (Raul) are listed as two different people in the credits.

## About the leads

**Dylan Rourke** has appeared in Grey's Anatomy, 9-1-1: Lone Star, Station 19 and Modern Family, according to GamesRadar+. After launch he posted a thank-you to the developers he worked alongside for years, signing off by saying he'd see everyone in Leonida.

**Manni L. Perez** has credits in Chicago P.D., The Blacklist and Law & Order: SVU, and voiced small parts in GTA 5. Her role as Lucia came out before launch through her Best Performance nomination at The Game Awards 2026.

GamesRadar+ also notes that trailer watchers had already worked out that Stephen Root was Brian.

**Bobby Moynihan**, who voices Cal, spent close to ten years in the Saturday Night Live cast. **Stephen Root** is best known for King of the Hill, Office Space and Barry, and PC Gamer describes his Brian as the criminal boss Jason and Lucia answer to.

## Who plays Mandy

Mandy, Jason's ex who rents one of Brian's houses and is behind on what she owes him, is played by **Lexie Shoaibi**. According to PC Gamer, she revealed the role on Instagram after launch, saying she provides both Mandy's voice and her motion capture, and asked fans to tell her if they spot her in the game.

You meet Mandy early in the story, and she has quickly become one of the most talked-about characters. See [Mandy's house and the rent mission](../gta-6-mandy-house-rent-mission/).

## Famous faces in the credits

- **Bad Bunny** plays Mitico Mario and hosts Cocoteo FM as **El Martillo**.
- **Denzel Curry** plays DWNPLY.
- **Michael Jai White** plays Kordell Grant.
- **Bobby Lee** plays Andre Ye.
- **Roselyn Sanchez** plays Mia Caminos.
- **Sergio Castellitto** plays Giorgio Gallo.
- **Kodak Black** and **Sexyy Red** appear as themselves.
- **"Weird Al" Yankovic** contributed writing for the TV and radio content and hosts **Vice City FM**, the '80s station, as DJ Al alongside actress Brigitte Nielsen. PC Gamer adds that he's also the voice of the Burger Shot ads.

The radio lineup also includes Lana Del Rey, Jeremy Irons, Jason Sudeikis and Gucci Mane, among others. The full list is in [GTA 6 radio stations and songs](../gta-6-radio-stations-songs/).
