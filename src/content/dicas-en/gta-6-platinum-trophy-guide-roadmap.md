**The GTA 6 platinum takes about 200 hours and rates 4 out of 10 in difficulty**, according to PowerPyx. There are **55 trophies**, all in story mode, and **none are missable**: anything you skip can be done after the ending, in the open world.

The roadmap comes from PowerPyx. The quick trophies match GTA6 Drops' video.

## The platinum in numbers

| Item | According to PowerPyx |
|---|---|
| Difficulty | 4 out of 10 |
| Time | About 200 hours |
| Trophies | 55 (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze) |
| Missable | None |
| Online | No trophies |
| Does the difficulty setting matter? | No: the game has a single difficulty |
| Cheats | **Turn off trophies and saving** for that session |
| Hardest | A Long Road (100% completion) |
| Saves | 15 manual and 1 autosave |

**Save tip:** since you can't replay missions, PowerPyx suggests keeping a save at the start and end of each chapter, and rotating the other slots during the final cleanup.

## The roadmap in 3 steps

1. **Story, no rush (60 to 80 hours).** Do side missions as they come up, **scan every new vehicle** with WAiNK and **study animals** with your binoculars. That gets you ahead on 100%.
2. **The 100% checklist (about 100 hours).** The list shows up under Progress after the ending. See [everything that counts toward 100%](../gta-6-100-percent-completion-checklist/). You need 70 of the 80 challenges, so you can skip the most annoying ones.
3. **Cleanup (about 10 hours).** **Stranger missions** don't count toward 100%, but they have a trophy. See the 8 that count in [Strangers & Freaks](../gta-6-strangers-and-freaks/).

## Trophies you can get early

These are quick and don't depend on finishing the story:

| Trophy | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **In The Beginning...** | At the rooftop bar of the **Ocean View** in Ocean Beach, order an **Apple Martini** and drink it. GTA6 Drops says to take the drink to the **railing** and look out at the view |
| **Challenging Tank** | Steal a **tank** at **Mile Zero Air Station**, the island in the southwest of the map. The tanks are in the south of the base |
| **Catch Me If You Can** | At Vice City airport, get in the big plane parked at **terminal E3/E4**, reverse onto the runway, take off and fly about **1 km** away |
| **The Goodtime State** | Clear the fog from the **whole map**. A plane is the fastest way |
| **The Truth Is Out There** | Late at night, find the car with flashing lights and follow the trail to the UFO. See the [abduction](../gta-6-ufo-abduction-truth-is-out-there/) |
| **Capital Offense** | With the **Key Cloner** (Chapter 3), steal the **blue Banshee** from the dealership in south Vice City and drive out **through the showroom window** |
| **Sneakthief** | After [Yasmani's jewelry jobs](../gta-6-yasmani-jewelry-safe-codes/), rob a jewelry store through the back with the Snap Gun without being seen. Do it before Retail Therapy |
| **Retail Therapy** | Rob 3 stores in a row with your partner without losing your wanted level. See the [Retail Therapy guide](../gta-6-retail-therapy-trophy/) |
| **Let's Go Bowling** | At **Kingpin Bowling**, grab the 3 valuables: the watch, the **Gutterball** shotgun and the safe (code **0300**). See the [bowling alley safe code](../gta-6-bowling-alley-safe-code/) |
| **Ten Pin** | On a motorcycle, knock down **10 pedestrians with kicks** within 60 seconds. Do it somewhere busy, like Ocean Beach |
| **Bad Luck, I Guess** | After the **Credit Check** mission (Chapter 3), go back with **Lucia alone** to the **Payday Loans in Little Cuba**, rob it again and escape **on foot** |

## Trophies that take longer

- **Personal Best:** reach **strength level 4** with both Jason and Lucia. If you train together, your partner gains too. Jojo Gaming Guides shows that in **Chapter 5** the new house has a gym: work out, drink the **protein shake** from the fridge, sleep and repeat.
- **The Shootist:** gold in every **shooting range** challenge. According to GTA6 Drops, there are two ranges: **Griffin Shooting Range** in northwest Vice City and **Rogers Ranch** in northeast Port Gellhorn. A pass costs **$5**. There are 12 challenges and 42 medals.
- **Fair Game:** do **Bret's** hunts, three animals at a time. After about three rounds he texts you about a buck he saw on Snapmatic and marks **Brett's Legendary Buck** on the map. The huge-antlered deer shows up **red** in your binoculars. Take it down with the rifle Bret gave you, walk to the body and **take a photo**: Bret calls and the trophy pops. You can do it after the story ends, according to Jojo Gaming Guides.
- **Master of None:** try each activity once: fishing, hunting, golf (return the clubs at the end), shooting range, pool (the story match doesn't count), basketball, a video game, a race and a stunt. Gaming With Josie did the last one with a parachute jump through markers; PowerPyx says to win the race and do any stunt.
- **Gunslinger:** take down 5 enemies in a single use of **combat Focus**. Eat or shower first to fill your Focus, use adrenaline and a rifle, and look for big groups in gang operations.
- **Hot Together:** lose **4 stars** with your partner. PowerPyx points to the **train tunnel north of Ambrosia**. It can also pop on its own during the bank vaults in Chapter 4.
