**Wyman**, the mechanic from the Thrillbilly Mud Club, builds **5 classic cars** for you. You just have to find and **scan 15 parts** scattered around the map. The collection comes with the **Special or Ultimate Edition** of GTA 6.

The guide is **LunarGaming's**. The part locations come from **Clips Leonida**, which uses footage from the same channel. We still need a second independent channel, so this is unverified.

## How to unlock it

1. In **Chapter 2**, talk to Wyman at the **Starlet Motel**.
2. Finish the **first Thrillbilly Mud Club race**.
3. Wait for **Wyman's text** about the classic cars. From then on you can hunt the parts.

## How it works

- You don't have to finish one car before finding parts for the next: grab all 15 in one go and he builds them one after another.
- He charges **$1,500 per car**.
- After the **first four**, pay another **$1,000** for the **fifth car** plus a free **jacket**. Clips Leonida says the fifth car is a **Deviant** and the jacket is the **Patriot Jacket**.
- **Expect waiting:** Wyman takes at least an in-game day to build each car, plus a day between you collecting one and him starting the next. Much of it is waiting for his texts.
- **One part needs the cutting tool** to open a shipping container. It only unlocks midway through **Chapter 3**.
