The infinite ammo cheat in GTA 6 is **Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Down**. What it does: your ammo never runs out. You still have to reload; for no reloading at all, see the [no reload cheat](../gta-6-no-reload-cheat/).

## How to enter it

1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Down.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to **win 25 silver medals or better at shooting ranges**. Progress you made during the story counts.

## What you need to know

- No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
- Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
- PowerPyx prints the same sequence and unlock as GamesRadar+, so it is confirmed.
