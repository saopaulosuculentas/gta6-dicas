CE-108255-1 is a **PS5 crash** in GTA 6: the game closes mid-session and throws that code. **There is no official fix yet.** Sony's only advice for the code is to update the game, and when GamesRadar+ covered it on the evening of November 19, GTA 6 didn't have a single patch to download. The first one arrived on November 23, but it doesn't mention this error (more below).

## What players are running into

GamesRadar+ dug up more than a dozen Reddit threads describing the same crash:

- It hits **after certain story scenes early in the game**, then repeats at the same point, so affected players can't progress.
- One player couldn't get past the **opening cutscene**. Skipping it just led to a freeze right after.
- Reports come from **launch PS5, PS5 Digital and PS5 Slim** owners.
- The outlet found no recent reports of the code in other games, which points to GTA 6 rather than the hardware.

The article spells the code two ways, CE-108255-1 in the headline and CE-108225-1 in the body, so check the exact number on your screen.

## What people have tried that didn't work

Based on the posts GamesRadar+ collected, none of these has stopped the crash so far:

| Attempt | Reported result |
|---|---|
| Clearing the console cache | Crash came back |
| Rebuilding the database | Crash came back |
| Reinstalling the game | Crash came back |
| Updating | Crash came back |
| Restoring licenses | Crash came back |

Some Redditors put together long lists of attempted fixes, and none of them worked.

## What Rockstar and Sony say

- Rockstar's error troubleshooting page has no fix of its own. It points console players to PlayStation and Xbox support.
- Sony's page for this code says to **update the game**, and if that doesn't help, to go through **PlayStation Repairs**.
- One player who contacted Sony's repair service was quoted **£300**.

GamesRadar+ reached out to Sony for comment and said it would update the story if it heard back.

## Does the November 23 patch fix it?

We can't say yet. GTA 6's first patch went out on November 23, and Rockstar's notes say it fixes **several issues that made the game crash**, but they don't list any error codes. Kotaku, which covered the patch, says it's unclear whether CE-108255-1 is one of them. Everything that changed is in [GTA 6 patch notes](../gta-6-patch-notes-update/).

## What you can do right now

1. **Check for a GTA 6 update** in your PS5 game library and install the November 23 patch. It's the only step Sony lists for this code, even if there's no guarantee it helps.
2. If your problem is a different kind of crash, such as the game not launching, a stuck download or a license error, follow the official steps in [GTA 6 crashing or stuck on loading: how to fix](../gta-6-crashing-loading-screen-fix/).
3. Keep an eye on Rockstar and Sony. The November 23 patch notes don't name this code, so watch for the next one.

## It isn't hitting everyone

GamesRadar+ itself notes that a handful of error posts on social media doesn't mean the problem is widespread. The writer plays on PS5 and hadn't had a single crash after several hours.
