The weather cheat in GTA 6 is **Down, Right, Up, Down, Right, Up, Right**. What it does: cycles through the weather conditions. Every time you enter it, the weather moves on to the next type. It is the only weather cheat; there is no separate rain or sun cheat.

## How to enter it

1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Down, Right, Up, Down, Right, Up, Right.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to **reveal the entire map**. Progress you made during the story counts.

## What you need to know

- No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
- Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
- Bubs says finishing the story is enough. If it does not show as unlocked after the ending, reveal the whole map.
- The sequence matches in Bubs, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer, so the badge is "Confirmed".
