In GTA 6 you **only buy a parachute once**, at an Ammu-Nation that sells clothing. You can also find free ones at every base jump spot and on top of some buildings. PC Gamer explained where to buy one, GameSpot covered the base jump spots and controls, and Millzaa and Arekkz Gaming grabbed and used the free one on the Key Lento radio tower.

## Where to buy one

- At **Ammu-Nation stores that sell clothes**. On the map, look for the Ammu-Nation with the **shirt icon**.
- The parachute hangs on a **rack** inside the store.

## Where to find one for free

- **Base jump spots:** according to GameSpot, all **25 base jump spots** in the game come with a **free parachute**. You'll know you've found one by the parachute sitting between **two green cones** with energy drinks on the ground, and a **purple parachute icon** shows up on your map. Most are on top of skyscrapers in Vice City, often on the helipad: walk into the lobby and take the elevator up. Typical Gamer found one at the top of a tower with a "PARACHUTE (FULL)" prompt to grab it. The full list is in [GTA 6 base jump locations](../gta-6-base-jump-locations/).
- **Key Lento radio tower:** close to Jason's house, right at the start of the game. Climb all the way to the top: the parachute hangs on the railing. Millzaa and Arekkz Gaming both grabbed it. The same climb has a sniper and collectible cards; see [GTA 6 secret early items](../gta-6-secret-early-items/).
- **Skyscraper roofs:** some have a box of parachutes up top.
- **Mount Kalaga:** the tallest peak in Mount Kalaga National Park has a parachute at the top. It's the hard way, but it makes for a nice trip.

## How to use it

1. Jump out of a plane mid-flight or off a tall building.
2. In freefall, press the on-screen prompt to pull the chute.
3. Steer left and right. Lean forward to drop faster or pull back to drop slower.

### The buttons, according to GameSpot

- **Jump:** near the edge, press **Cross (A on Xbox)**. On a base jump, go off right between the two cones.
- **Deploy:** press **Cross (A)** again while you're in the air. Millzaa does the same thing, tapping jump a second time to pull the chute.
- **Brake:** hold **Cross (A)**. The canopy pulls up and you lose speed for a moment. You can't hold it forever.
- **Land:** just above the ground, press **Triangle (Y)** to ditch the chute and drop onto your feet. On a base jump, hitting the ground with the chute still open fails the challenge.

## What to know

- **Reset it after use.** You only pay once, but after a jump you have to go back to a gun store and pick it off the rack again to add it to your inventory. Or grab one from a rooftop.
- **The tower chute is single use.** Millzaa points out that the parachute on the Key Lento tower only works once: he jumped, opened it, and Jason tossed it away after landing. If you want to keep it, take the ladders back down. Arekkz adds that the tower isn't all that high for a jump, so deploy quickly.
- **Use it to escape.** If the police shoot your plane down, bail out and pull the chute. See [the GTA 6 airport](../gta-6-airport-steal-plane/).
- After the story there's a [parachute cheat](../all-gta-6-cheats-list/), unlocked with 50 skydives or base jumps from 300 feet or higher.
