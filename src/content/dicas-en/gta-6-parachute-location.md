In GTA 6 you **only buy a parachute once**, at an Ammu-Nation that sells clothing. You can also find free ones on top of some buildings. PC Gamer explained where to get one and how it works.

## Where to buy one

- At **Ammu-Nation stores that sell clothes**. On the map, look for the Ammu-Nation with the **shirt icon**.
- The parachute hangs on a **rack** inside the store.

## Where to find one for free

- **Skyscraper roofs:** some have a box of parachutes up top.
- **Mount Kalaga:** the tallest peak in Mount Kalaga National Park has a parachute at the top. It's the hard way, but it makes for a nice trip.

## How to use it

1. Jump out of a plane mid-flight or off a tall building.
2. In freefall, press the on-screen prompt to pull the chute.
3. Steer left and right. Lean forward to drop faster or pull back to drop slower.

## What to know

- **Reset it after use.** You only pay once, but after a jump you have to go back to a gun store and pick it off the rack again to add it to your inventory. Or grab one from a rooftop.
- **Use it to escape.** If the police shoot your plane down, bail out and pull the chute. See [the GTA 6 airport](../gta-6-airport-steal-plane/).
- After the story there's a [parachute cheat](../all-gta-6-cheats-list/), unlocked with 50 skydives or base jumps from 300 feet or higher.
