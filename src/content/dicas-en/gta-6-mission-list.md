GTA 6's main story is made up of **91 missions** spread over **5 chapters**, based on the list PC Gamer put together. Chapter 3 is the big one with 26 missions, while Chapter 5 has only 7 and closes out the game.

PC Gamer kept **3 mission names** off the list to avoid spoilers: one in Chapter 1, the last one in Chapter 4, and the finale. The notes below are written to give nothing important away either.

## Missions per chapter

| Chapter | Name | Missions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Just Like Paradise | 22 |
| 2 | Paradise Lost | 11 |
| 3 | T**s Ass Drug Money | 26 |
| 4 | Vice City, Baby | 25 |
| 5 | Top of the World, Ma | 7 |
| **Total** | | **91** |

Only story missions count here. Stranger missions, side activities and random events on the map aren't part of this list.
