Stealing a car in GTA 6 isn't just smashing a window and driving off anymore. Almost every car has an **alarm**, **keyless** cars won't hotwire, and some have **trackers** that call the police. As the story moves on you get tools for all of that. PC Gamer mapped which mission gives you each tool, and MrDalekJD and KhrazeGaming show the system in their beginner videos.

## Scan it first

The **Waink It** app tells you what you're dealing with before you touch the car. MrDalekJD checks every car with it before he takes it: the app shows whether it's **locked**, **alarmed** or fitted with a **tracker**, which is exactly what decides whether the police end up on your tail.

KhrazeGaming warns that the pool of easy cars is small early on. Lots of them need a **slim jim** or a **key cloner**, and Waink It tells you which tool each one calls for. The cars you can still just smash and hotwire are mostly older models.

## The basics: smash and hotwire

1. Walk up to the door and press interact. The game tells you if it's **locked**, **alarmed** or **keyless**.
2. Smash the window. If it's alarmed, the alarm goes off and draws attention.
3. Inside, **hotwire** it: use the sticks to find the "sweet spot". It takes a second or two.

Keyless cars **won't start** with hotwiring. For those you need the key cloner.

## Carjacking

You can still pull people out of their cars, but many fight back. You have to mash a button to win. Lose, and they slam your head into the car and drive off. And plenty of Leonida drivers are armed.

## The tools and when you get them

| Tool | What it does | When you get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Waink It** app | Scans a car: make, lock, alarm, keyless, tracker, condition, sale price and registration cost | Chapter 1, mission 6 (Unwanted Attention), with Cal |
| **Slim jim** | Pops the lock without breaking the window or setting off the alarm. Slower than your elbow | Chapter 2, mission 5 (Moses) |
| **Key cloner** | Clones the key with your phone: unlocks and starts keyless cars | Chapter 3, mission 16 (Steal the Zorruso for Randy) |

The slim jim doesn't work if someone is sitting in the car. Then you have to break the window.

MrDalekJD confirms the slim jim comes from Moses in chapter 2. Once you have it, it stays in both Jason's and Lucia's inventory for good with no need to equip it, and it also lets you open a car's **trunk** quietly to loot it. More in [the slim jim and snap gun guide](../gta-6-slim-jim-snap-gun/).

To clone a key: stand by the door, press interact, pick **Clone Key** and use the stick to find the sweet spot and speed it up.

## Trackers

- **Standard tracker:** doesn't call the cops. It just stops you modifying the car at a mod shop until you register it.
- **Advanced tracker:** alerts the police and pinpoints you. Keep your speed up so it can't send a signal. Slow down and it starts beeping, and you have a few seconds to speed up again.

In MrDalekJD's view, an advanced tracker is no problem for a short dash away from the scene, but pick another car if you plan to stay hidden for a while.

## Registering a car to keep it

- **You can't register anything in chapter 1.** According to MrDalekJD, you're stuck with the starter car. You can still steal cars off the street, but they won't be saved.
- **Registration unlocks in chapter 2**, after the main story mission **Moses** in Port Gellhorn.
- **Where:** drive the car into a **Pay 'n' Spray**. GamingBolt warns that not everything fits: a monster truck they found while exploring was too big to get inside, so it couldn't be registered.

Trackers and registration are still a bit unclear. MrDalekJD says that once it unlocks you can register cars that **don't have a tracker**. PC Gamer says a standard tracker only stops you modifying the car until it's registered. Until someone tests it properly, pick a car without a tracker if you want to keep it.

## Sell it or keep it

- **Pay 'n' Spray:** sells stolen cars, with a limit of **3 per day**. It's also where you **register** a car to make it yours, from chapter 2 on.
- **Moses** (chapter 2): buys older, low-profile cars and often tells you where the ones he wants are. After a sale he needs a while before taking another.
- **Randy** (chapter 3): pays more, but only wants flashy, expensive cars.
- **Storage:** a garage bought through the real estate app on your phone (one car per garage), or the parking spots outside your safehouse. See [every garage and price](../gta-6-garage-locations-prices/).

Waink It tells you if a car suits Moses or Randy. Selling cars is one of the best earners in chapter 2: see [how to make money fast](../gta-6-how-to-make-money/). To shake the cops after a theft, see [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).
