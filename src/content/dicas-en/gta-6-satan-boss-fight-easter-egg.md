One of GTA 6's wildest hidden quests starts in a **Port Gellhorn** garage in the middle of the night. Two metalheads invite you to a ritual, the ritual drops you into **hell** for a Doom-style run through waves of demons, and it ends with a boss fight against **Satan** himself. Win and you walk away with a **unique shotgun**, an outfit, tattoos and **a permanent health boost**.

**MrDalekJD** made a full guide, and **T5G** shows the same house, ritual and boss in its 30 secrets video. The steps below follow MrDalekJD.

## Before you go

- **No map marker.** Nothing tells you this exists.
- **Night only.**
- **Go alone:** as Jason or as Lucia. With both characters together, it won't start.
- **Save first.** The boss fight is tough.

## 1. The metalheads' house

1. Head to **north Port Gellhorn**, on the streets north of **Gellhorn Avenue** looking out over **Draper Bay**. The house is north of the **Stash House** robbery.
2. In daylight it's a **faded yellow single-story house** with a yard full of junk and a "welcome to hell" tag sprayed on the front.
3. Come back **between midnight and 4 a.m.** When the quest is active, the **garage door is up**, with red lights and metal music blasting. MrDalekJD has heard of people getting it around 10 p.m. too.

**Garage closed?** Don't wait outside. Drive half a mile to a mile away and come back **on the next hour**. Shut at midnight? Try 1 a.m., then 2 a.m., and so on. It doesn't spawn every hour.

## 2. The invitation

1. Two metalheads hang out in the garage. They offer you a beer and ask if you want to stick around before their ritual. Pick the option to **go along** and **sit down and listen**.
2. **Very important:** don't hit them, bump into them or pull a gun. Spook them and the whole thing shuts down.
3. After a while their friend shows up. Say yes when they invite you and **get in the back of the van** with the grim reaper on the side.

## 3. The ritual

1. The van drops you at a clearing in the woods with a **campfire** and a big **wooden throne**.
2. When you get the prompt to **look at the fire**, keep staring until you pass out.
3. Missed it? Come back another night and **eat the food on the throne**. That also sends you to hell.

## 4. Hell

You wake up holding a shotgun in a level full of demons.

- **Keep moving** and grab every health pack and ammo drop. Stand still and you won't last.
- The **small demon is the fastest**: kill it first. The big ones are armored.
- If one gets right up close, your character shotguns it in the face on their own.

**If you die:** you wake up by the ritual campfire. If it's still night, walk to the throne and eat the item again. A cutscene sends you straight back to where you died.

## 5. Satan

At the end you come face to face with **Satan**, and a boss health bar shows up at the bottom of the screen.

1. At first the bar is **black**: he's **invulnerable** and shooting him does nothing.
2. He sends out **black goats** and **exploding enemies**. Take out the **exploders first**.
3. When his bar turns **red**, he can take damage: **unload everything on him**.
4. Repeat: clear the wave, wait for the red bar, attack.

## The rewards

You wake up the next morning **sitting on Satan's throne**. According to MrDalekJD:

- A **unique red shotgun** under the throne. The name wasn't clear in the auto captions, so we're leaving it out.
- A **Circles of Hell** top, which your character is already wearing.
- **Exclusive tattoo designs** at the tattoo parlor: one each on the chest, back, left arm and left leg, all hell themed.
- **A permanent bump to max health.** Check the top left when you stand up from the throne. Unlike the health you build at the gym, this boost never goes away.

You can also sit on Satan's very tall throne afterwards.
