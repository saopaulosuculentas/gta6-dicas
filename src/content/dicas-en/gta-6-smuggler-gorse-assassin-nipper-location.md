The **Assassin Nipper .38** and the **Smuggler Gorse 425** are in **two shipwrecks** on the small Mariana County islands near **Mile Zero Air Station**. You can take both early on without a fight. Arekkz Gaming and Vexies showed the same spots, and both weapon names come from their voiceovers.

## Getting there

- **By helicopter:** follow the edge of Key Lento round to **Southwind**. Vexies found a free helicopter on the pad there.
- **By boat:** it's a small island group, so a boat or a swim works too, as Arekkz points out.

Stay out of the airspace over the [military base](../gta-6-military-base-location/) or you'll pick up a wanted level.

## Assassin Nipper .38: suppressed pistol

1. Head for the **tip of the pointy islet at the south end** of the group. Vexies counts the islands on the map and stops at the bottom of the fourth.
2. Look for the abandoned boat, which the WAiNK app labels a **derelict spy vessel**.
3. Walk all the way inside and use the prompt to **open the hatch**.
4. Take the pistol. Arekkz says the magazine holds about 8 rounds.

It's a great early stealth gun: Arekkz notes a suppressor alone costs close to $1,000 in stores.

## Smuggler Gorse 425: shotgun

1. Move up to the island with a **strip of sand between two green areas**.
2. Land on the sand and walk to the north side.
3. Find the **small wrecked boat**. This one can't be scanned with WAiNK.
4. The shotgun is **glowing in plain sight** by the boat.

In Vexies' video it has **7.5 damage**, against 7.4 for the regular shotguns they compare it with. Arekkz says it fires almost like a semi-auto.

## Can you lose them?

Both are unique variants, so a lost copy comes back from the Ammu-Nation weapon locker. See [how to store weapons in your trunk](../gta-6-store-weapons-trunk/) and all the [legendary and unique weapon locations](../gta-6-unique-weapons-locations/).
