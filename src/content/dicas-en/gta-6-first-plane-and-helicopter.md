You can fly almost from the start of GTA 6. Near Jason's house, in **Southwind**, there are **seaplanes** and a **helicopter** nobody is guarding. According to PC Gamer, you can take them without getting a single star.

## How to get there

From Jason's house in **Key Lento**, drive about 2.5 miles southwest:

1. Stay on **Sunbelt Highway (Route 1)** the whole way.
2. Go southwest through Key Lento, then west through **Abismo Key**.
3. You'll cross **two long bridges**. After them the highway bends north and you enter Southwind.
4. On your right there's a concrete barrier along the road. Where it ends, **turn right into the parking lot** at the sign for **SBW (Sherwin Boatworks)** and **Chicki's Tiki Bar**.

Southwind is right next to the military base, Mile Zero Air Station.

## The plane: Mammoth Dodo seaplane

Facing the water (east), on your right (south) there's a **small hangar** with a few **Mammoth Dodo** seaplanes in and around it, and sometimes a couple more moored off the dock.

- They're not the fastest planes, but they **land on water and on land** and need very little room to take off. Perfect for exploring.
- Drive one into the water and take off, or grab one from the dock.

## The helicopter: Buckingham Maverick

From the parking lot entrance, look **straight east**. An open gate leads to a dock with a **Buckingham Maverick**. Ignore the no-trespassing sign, run to the dock and hop in.
