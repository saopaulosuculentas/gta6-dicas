You don't need to spend a cent early in GTA 6 to kit yourself out. Without any mission, you can pick up **four snipers, a rocket launcher, several unique guns, a jet ski, Paul Walker's car** and loot bags worth **up to $10,000** each. Four channels went to the same spots: Rodwenn, Arekkz Gaming, Millzaa and FuriousFade.

## The stashes at a glance

| Spot | Where | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Radio tower | Key Lento, about 0.36 miles from Jason's house | SM-69 sniper, 2 trading cards, a parachute |
| Teddy bear warehouse | South Vice-Dale County, east of Hamlet | $6,000 bag, drugs, Duke 556 rifle, a card worth $6,000 |
| Manatee Motel | Northern Pelican Valley, just south of Hamlet | Safe, drugs and the unique Kingfishers Laird 350 sniper |
| Swamp shack | Pelican Valley | Unique Cruel Huntsman's SM-69 sniper and Swamp Bait revolver |
| Crab lab | Pelican Valley | Crabshack VF5 SMG, $10,000 bag, a jet ski |
| Griffin Shooting Range trailer | Balsero, Vice City | H-AT launcher, Conspiracist Type 9 SMG, grenades |
| Vespucci Island garage | Vespucci Island, Vice City | Paul Walker's green Eclipse |

## Key Lento radio tower

This one is basically next door: Millzaa's map put it 0.36 miles from Jason's house. Scan it with your phone first, since it counts as a landmark, then start climbing.

1. Go through the gate and take the stairs up inside until they lead you out a door.
2. Climb the outside ladder, enter the next room and take another ladder.
3. A **trading card** sits on a table up there.
4. The **SM-69 sniper** leans on the balcony outside, scope included. A **second card** is inside the phone box. Arekkz also found around $150 in a side cabinet.
5. A **parachute** waits at the very top. It's single use: Millzaa jumped, and Jason tossed it away after landing.

The tower SM-69 is a regular gun, so you lose it if you die.

## Teddy bear warehouse (South Vice-Dale)

It's the red warehouse by the quarry, east of Hamlet. Everyone inside is already dead, so there's no fight. Bring a truck.

- The teddy bear van in the yard has drugs stuffed in the toys.
- Inside, a **$6,000 bag** of marked bills sits on a table near a **Duke 556** rifle and an SMG.
- Each drug brick is worth **$400**, per the on-screen pickup in Arekkz's video.
- The office safe opens with the right stick. It holds the **Leroy Rook (Silver)** card from the Vice City Narcos set, which the Hamlet pawn shop buys for **$6,000**.

Full walkthrough: [early money warehouse](../gta-6-early-money-warehouse/).

## Manatee Motel

It's at the north end of **Pelican Valley**, across the water just south of Hamlet. You'll hear gunfire and a tiger as you arrive, with a man shooting at it and yelling for help.

1. Wait for the tiger and the man to go down, then step into the room.
2. Grab the cash on the table, the flare gun and the back-room safe: Millzaa got **$917**.
3. The next room over has **$640** in drugs and a case holding the **Kingfishers Laird 350** sniper. The game tells you you've unlocked a **unique weapon variant**.
4. Come armed. In Millzaa's video, a group showed up shooting right after.

## Swamp shack

Deep in the **Pelican Valley** swamp there's a shack with dead gators on the porch. The unique **Cruel Huntsman's SM-69** hunting rifle lies on the floor. Using focus to zoom around, Millzaa also spotted the **Swamp Bait** revolver close by. The water is full of live gators, so cross carefully.

## Crab lab

Also in Pelican Valley. Arekkz took the boat from the dock behind Jason's house. The payoff is the **Crabshack VF5**, a **$10,000** bag of marked bills and close to **$1,100** in the safe. The boathouse garage holds a **jet ski** for the ride out, handy for the next stops. The codes are in our [giant crab mystery guide](../gta-6-giant-crab-mystery-codes/).

## Griffin Shooting Range trailer

This is the long drive. Griffin Shooting Range is in **Balsero**, on the west side of Vice City.

1. Park across the street from the range and hop the fence into the trailer park.
2. Head into the trailer: there's a **hatch in the floor**.
3. The cellar holds the **H-AT** launcher, the unique **Conspiracist Type 9** SMG in a gun case, grenades and healing items.

The launcher isn't unique and disappears if you die. More in [where to find a rocket launcher](../gta-6-rocket-launcher-location/).

## Three more guns along the way

Arekkz chained these together on the jet ski from the crab lab:

- **Kingfishers W-22**, a suppressed small game rifle: in a gun case in the main cabin of a small island in **Wetman Bay**, southwest of Hamlet. Expect enemies.
- **A regular sniper**: on the Grassrivers beach west of Watson Bay, through a gap in a compound's fence, no fight needed.
- **Turnt Nipper .38**, a unique pistol with a red dot: a glider is stuck in the trees on a sandy islet in **Bobcat Bight**. Shoot the ropes until it drops; the pistol is inside.

## Tips

- **Unique guns stay, regular ones don't.** Unique finds carry the gold gun icon and can be recovered at a gun store locker. Everything else is gone when you die, so use the trunk. See every one in [GTA 6 unique weapon locations](../gta-6-unique-weapons-locations/).
- **Marked bills aren't spendable yet.** The bags have to go to a fence such as Brian (code 7700) or Donnie.
- **The Fast & Furious car.** The green Eclipse sits in a garage on Vespucci Island with a HAD YOU plate. Millzaa and Rodwenn both picked it up early.
