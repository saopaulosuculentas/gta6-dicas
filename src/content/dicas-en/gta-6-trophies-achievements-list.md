GTA 6 has **55 trophies** on PS5, including the platinum **Vice Got a Price!**, and the Xbox achievements are worth **1,000 Gamerscore** in total. GamesRadar+ breaks it down as **1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze**, and PC Gamer's list has the same awards.

A good chunk will pop just from playing the story. Others are a real grind, like the one for **100% completion**. PC Gamer notes the list comes from the console version and that the platinum could change once the game reaches PC.

| Trophy | How to unlock | Grade | G |
|---|---|---|---|
| Just Like Paradise | Finish the mission Gone Fishing | Bronze | 5 |

Each chapter trophy is named after the chapter that comes next. To see where each mission falls in the story, check the [GTA 6 mission list](../gta-6-mission-list/).

## Platinum

**Vice Got a Price!** is the platinum, and it unlocks once you have every other trophy. Going by the descriptions alone, the longest hauls are A Long Road (100%), Cunning Stunts (250 stunts), The Shootist (42 gold medals), You WAiNKer! (100 locations) and Actions Speak Louder.
