GTA 6 has **10 garages**. Some come **free** with the story, and the rest you buy through the **Vice City Real Estate** app on your phone or by visiting them in person. Prices run from **$4,000 to $40,000**: the closer to Vice City and the richer the neighborhood, the pricier.

Garages let you keep a stolen car **without paying to register it**. Space is tight, though: according to PC Gamer, **each garage holds one car**.

## How to buy one

1. Open your phone (Up on the D-pad).
2. Go to the **Vice City Real Estate** app.
3. Pick a garage and buy it. You can also go to the garage and buy it there.

## Every garage and price

| Garage | Price | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mitchel Auto Center | $4,000 | Central Kelly County, in the middle of the "Kelly County" label on the map, east of Port Gellhorn |
| Persistence Road Garage | $9,500 | Western Bellville, north of Vice City in South Leonard County |
| 7th Avenue Garage | $12,000 | Just east of the airport in south Vice-Dale County, off the main highway northwest of Tequesta |
| Store 'N' Bolt | $16,800 | Northern Ambrosia, near the big monument |
| Wyatt Motors | $21,600 | Central Ambrosia, right by the Pay 'n' Spray |
| Vela Rota Drive Garage | $30,000 | The middle of Capri |
| Bonita Court Garage | $34,000 | Central Washington Island |
| Atlantic Court Garage | $40,000 | Ocean Beach, just east of the "Vice Beach" label, south of the Y Hotel |
| Port Gellhorn Station Garage | Free | Port Gellhorn, north of Moses' auto shop. Comes with the story |
| Paradise Garage | Free | Northeast Watson Bay, southeast of Grassrivers. Comes with the Ultimate Edition's Dominator Buggy |

GameSpot also found garages that only appear when the game stores a vehicle for you. Those can't be bought. And there are no garages in Lummox County.

## Other ways to keep a car

- **Parking spots at home:** you can leave a stolen car in the spots outside your current safehouse.
- **Register it:** at a Pay 'n' Spray you can register a stolen car as yours. See [how to steal cars](../gta-6-how-to-steal-cars/).

## Can you buy a house?

So far, only **garages** have shown up for sale in the real estate app. No source has shown a house for sale early in the game. This page will be updated if that changes.
