**The La Cascara Motel safe code in GTA 6 is 6969.** It worked for GosuNoob, WoW Quests, Accelerated Ideas and ConCon, each in their own game, so you can punch it in straight away. Inside you'll find cash and a big haul of pills.

## Where the motel is and when it shows up

La Cascara sits on the waterfront in **Port Gellhorn**, close to the bridge. It's one of the hideouts run by the **Kolt & Associates** gang, described on the map as an old, crumbling motel that's heavily guarded.

ConCon and Accelerated Ideas both place the robbery in **Chapter 2**. ConCon adds that the motel only appears on your map once **Lucia calls** and tells you to hit it.

## Where the code is written

You won't find the number at the motel. It's on the **Port Gellhorn map hanging on the office wall at Brooks Boatworks**, next to a whiteboard. In ConCon's video you can read **6969** scribbled by the motel, along with **6823** for Brooks Boatworks itself and **0300** for the bowling alley. Once you've looked at the map, the game remembers the codes.

There's no need to make that trip just for this safe. If you want the other two codes, see the [Brooks Boatworks safe code](../gta-6-brooks-boatworks-safe-code/) and the [bowling alley safe code](../gta-6-bowling-alley-safe-code/).

## How to reach the safe

The safe is up on the **roof**, inside a small house built on top of the motel. Here's ConCon's route:

1. Bring a **silenced pistol**. Every floor has armed gang members, and you'll often hear them talking before you see them.
2. Slip in from the **lower end of the street** and work your way up the **left side** of the building, crouched.
3. Break the **gate** blocking the way to the top, then follow the burnt-out walkway past the generators.
4. On the top floor there's a **GLR40** grenade launcher on a low table if you want it.
5. Head into the **rooftop house**. There's another weapon in there, plus **sticky bombs**.
6. The safe is in a **small room with yellow, graffiti-covered walls**. Interact with it and enter **6969**.

Accelerated Ideas follows the same path to the top floor. GosuNoob made it through unseen and picked up a **Stealth Bonus** when the safe opened.

## No code? Blow it

If you don't have the number, ConCon notes that the **sticky bombs** in the rooftop house will open the safe for you.

## Don't leave without the Scrapper VF5C

The unique **Scrapper VF5C**, a compact military SMG, is lying **on a desk in the rooftop house**, close to the safe. GosuNoob grabs it right after opening the safe, and ConCon picks it up in the same spot. If you're already carrying too many guns, do what ConCon does: hold **right on the D-pad**, switch to Lucia and have her pick it up. See more [legendary and unique weapons](../gta-6-unique-weapons-locations/).

## After the job

There's so much loot here that you'll end up with a second bag. Hold **right on the D-pad** and press **Circle** (B on Xbox) next to Lucia to hand it over.

To leave, ConCon drops down on the right side and drives off with Lucia. Accelerated Ideas warns that you should expect to shake off the cops afterwards. Any pills you take can be sold, as covered in [fence locations](../gta-6-fence-locations/).
