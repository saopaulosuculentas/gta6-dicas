**The Chapter 3 laptop password in GTA 6 is Muffins, Queen, 15.** You don't type it in: you pick each part, in that order, from options mapped to the D-pad. Both ConCon and SimDEX log in this way. Once you're in, a message hands you the code **92665**, which opens a lock-up in **La Perle** holding a free pink car, **Vanessa's Growler**.

## Where the laptop is

It's in **apartment 201 in Bayside**, the new place you move into early in Chapter 3. The laptop sits in the **bedroom**. SimDEX calls it Boobie's laptop, but the chats inside belong to **Vanessa**, the woman who lived there before you.

## The three clues

They're all inside the apartment, so check each one before you sit down at the laptop.

1. **Muffins:** in the hallway, by the front door, there's a **small pink picture frame** with a photo of a dog named Muffins.
2. **Queen:** in the bedroom, near the bed, a **notepad sketch** shows the same dog wearing a crown and sitting like royalty.
3. **15:** flip the notepad over. The back mentions a **Class of '15 reunion** next Friday.

## How to log in

1. In the bedroom, press **Triangle** (Y on Xbox) at the laptop.
2. Pick **Muffins** on the D-pad. The other choices look similar, such as Fluffy and Pawprints. ConCon warns that the direction may not match his, so read the label before you press.
3. Next, pick **Queen**. The alternatives are King and Princess.
4. Finally, pick **15**.

SimDEX points out that snooping through the laptop has no negative consequences.

## The message with code 92665

Inside, open the WhatUp messages and look for the **Unknown** contact. They tell Vanessa to go to **La Perle, right next to Auntie's Place**, and use **92665** to collect a gift. When you close the laptop, the game saves the number to your notes.

## Where the garage is

1. Head to **La Perle** in Vice City.
2. Find **Auntie's Place**. The lock-up is right beside it.
3. Look for the garage door with a **pink bow painted on the wall** and a "Do Not Park Here" sign. The keypad is there.
4. Enter **92665**.

Inside is Vanessa's pink **Pfister Growler**. ConCon also shares a map tip: buildings shown in dark blue are the ones you can enter.

## The car and the garage

In ConCon's game, the notice was clear: Vanessa's Growler became an **owned vehicle**, and the garage unlocked too. He adds that the garage reportedly **disappears if you sell the Growler**. Since you can only have one active personal vehicle, your previous ride gets sent to another garage. See the other [garage locations and prices](../gta-6-garage-locations-prices/).

## Don't leave it for later

ConCon says this is reportedly **missable**, so grab it while you're still in Chapter 3, before you move on with the story.
