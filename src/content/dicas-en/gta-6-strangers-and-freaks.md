Stranger missions in GTA 6 are requests from characters you run into around the map, and the **Strangers & Freaks** trophy asks you to finish **8 mission strands** of them. **There's no complete list yet**: neither Rockstar nor the big guide sites have said which eight count. Below is what players have turned up on day one.

## The trophy

| Trophy | Requirement | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Strangers & Freaks | Complete 8 mission strands for strangers across the state of Leonida | Silver, 30G |

The word to notice is **strands**. A single stranger can have more than one mission in a row, like Shirley does.

## Found so far

### Shirley, the neighbor

She lives on the **corner by Jason's house** and yells at you as you drive past early in the story.

1. **First favor:** she asks for a bottle of **Salty Squid** rum from the **Three Sheets** liquor store, as OutlawGarry shows. RageGamingVideos says bringing it back gives you a **permanent focus boost**.
2. **Second favor:** keep visiting and she'll ask for help involving her friend **Peggy** and Peggy's mobility scooter. The reward is being able to **borrow Shirley's car** whenever you like, per Arekkz. It's not a registered personal vehicle, but it drives well.

### Kirk and the Skunk Ape

A hidden quest in the Grassrivers swamps. JFM shows it starting when you find an **effigy** made of branches in the woods, near where the creature is seen eating a deer on the road. The game doesn't point you anywhere after that. **Kirk**, a Skunk Ape hunter, joins in, and there's a choice at the end. The reward is a **unique camo sniper rifle**. Full steps in the [Skunk Ape mission guide](../gta-6-skunk-ape-mission-sniper-rifle/).

### Kenton, the treasure hunter

Kenton shows up after the story mission Hey Arnold!, at his dive shop on the Key Lento beach, according to KultZ. He teaches you to dive for wrecks, and WiLLiSGaming says he **calls again later** with more dive spots. There's even a trophy just for him, **This Was No Boat Accident**, for finishing Kenton's final mission. Nobody has confirmed whether his strand counts as a stranger strand. More in [sunken treasure and shipwrecks](../gta-6-sunken-treasure-shipwrecks/).

## Street encounters

These pop up in the open world. It's not known whether they count toward the trophy.

- **D-Bag, the gym bro:** on **Shore Drive**, across from the **Hotel Dixon**, he bets you **$1,000** you can't make him tap out of a chokehold. MrBossFTW files him under strangers and random encounters. See the [D-Bag challenge](../gta-6-gym-bro-dbag-1000/).
- **Todd and the sniper:** west of the highway into Hamlet, near Grassrivers, you'll hear a gunshot. It's two hunters, one of them **Todd**, with a sniper rifle on the ground. Drive them to the hospital and one of them **hands you the rifle**, per KhrazeGaming. More in [unique weapons](../gta-6-unique-weapons-locations/).
- **The missing spouse:** Kotaku describes someone who needs help finding their spouse after a night of partying. It's one of the random events you can stumble into while exploring.

## Other strands on the trophy list

Besides Kenton, the trophy list has awards for finishing the **final mission** of four more characters: **Andy** (Road to Rehabilitation), **Billy** (The Lone Rider), **Randy** (Supply & Demand) and **Tyrone** (Spin On This!). The list doesn't say which of these count as strangers.

## How to find more

- **Map index:** GamesRadar+ says the **Exploration** tab lists optional quests. MrDalekJD shows that favors for your neighbors show up there too.
- **Listen:** Kotaku's advice is to follow shouting and loud noises. Press **L3 + R3** (focus) to see where a sound is coming from, then go take a look.

This list **isn't complete**. We'll update it as more strands are found.
