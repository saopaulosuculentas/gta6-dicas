GTA 6's Ultimate Edition and pre-order bonuses **don't all show up on day one**. Each one unlocks in a specific chapter, from 1 to 5, and sits at a specific spot on the map. Below is when and where to grab every item. The pre-order pack applies to both the Standard and Ultimate Editions; everything else is Ultimate only.

## Where to check your bonuses

Finish mission 11 in Chapter 1, **Coming Home**. That unlocks the **store in the main menu**, where every bonus has an entry telling you which chapter it arrives in and how to collect it.

For the pre-order pack, open the main menu, go to the store and scroll down past GTA+ to the **Vintage Vice City Pack**.

Almost everything is tracked through the **map index**. If you haven't used it yet, see [how to use the map index](../gta-6-index-find-missions/).

## Grab these three in Chapter 1

Both sources mark the **Ganado Retro Build**, the **Dinka Enduro** and the **Crest Kayak** as **Chapter 1 only**. Neither says what happens if you move on without them, so don't wait.

## Chapter 1

| Bonus | What it is | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dinka Enduro** | Motorcycle in army fatigue colors | In the driveway under Jason's house |
| **Crest Kayak** | Kayak for exploring the Leonida Keys | Behind Jason's house |
| **Ganado Retro Build** | Retro kit for Jason's Vapid Ganado | Drive the Ganado to Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento. Pro Builds / Full Kits section |
| **One-Eyed Willie's** | Off-road custom shop with hand-painted liveries | North Leonard County, just north of Lake Leonida. Index: Vehicles, custom shops tab |
| **Rideout Customs** | Custom shop for interiors, rims and donk styling | Crosstown, Vice City. Index: Vehicles, custom shops tab |
| **Stock 305** | Streetwear and skate clothing store | Stockyard, Vice City. Index: Style > Casual & Street |
| **Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor** | Over 50 signature tattoos designed by the art collective FAILE | Stockyard, Vice City. Index: Style > Tattoos |
| **Vice City Merch** | Clothes and accessories featuring Macca the Gator, from a Leonida TV show | Mannequins at The Wonder Whale, Key Lento. Index: Style > Souvenir & Swimwear |
| **PTT Youngin$ Compound** | Gang compound raids that pay out cash, special items and contraband | After you've visited Southside. Index: Robberies > Illegal Operations |
| **Jason's pistol** | Engraved Girardi ES9 | Weapon locker at any Ammu-Nation, Variants section |
| **Sara's Unisex Salon** | Signature looks, like facial hair for Jason and makeup and nails for Lucia | Hamlet. Index: Style > Hair & Beauty. Signature looks arrive in Chapter 2, with more in Chapter 4 |

The personalized pistols are **free** to equip.

## Chapter 2

## Chapter 3: the pre-order bonus

The **Vintage Vice City Pack** goes to everyone who pre-ordered, on Standard or Ultimate. You can only claim it in Chapter 3:

1. **'55 Vapid Stanier and garage:** a task shows up in the Exploration section of the index. The car is parked outside the **Ocean View Hotel**. Collecting it also unlocks the **Bonita Court** garage, north of Washington Beach.
2. **80s outfits and hairstyles:** once you have the car, the outfits are in your **wardrobe** and the hairstyles are in the **Special** section of any salon.
3. **Palm tree weapon pattern**, a nod to Tommy Vercetti's shirt: once you have the car, head to any Ammu-Nation and apply it under **Cosmetics / Wraps** in the weapon's modify menu.

| Bonus | What it is | Where to get it |
