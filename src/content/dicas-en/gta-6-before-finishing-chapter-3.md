**Before finishing Chapter 3 in GTA 6, check four things:**
- grab the **golden Monty's 1922 pistol**;
- don't leave **Halifax's** mission behind;
- run the **Race Against Yago** before it expires;
- claim the **pink Growler** in the La Perle garage.

This list only includes what **two or more channels** show. Anything only one channel mentions is flagged. Make a separate save before the chapter's final mission.

## 1. The golden Monty's 1922

In a Chapter 3 mission, a **photo shoot** ends abruptly and a shootout starts. Once you leave the room, **go back in**: **Monty's 1922**, a **gold, engraved** pistol, is on the floor. It's a **unique variant** and can't be modded.

Caspahz and RageGamingVideos show the same gun. Caspahz names the mission **The Missing Link**. Move on without it and it's gone.

## 2. Don't turn Halifax down for good

In **Dubious Providence**, the second Chapter 3 story mission, **Halifax** wants your statue at an antique shop in **Capri** and offers a watch in exchange.

- **If you refuse,** the mission goes away. But you can **call him back** as long as Chapter 3 isn't over, according to KhrazeGaming. Caspahz says to keep calling until Jason and Lucia decide to go find him.
- **If you finish the chapter without calling,** it's gone for good, along with what it gives you.
- **The reward,** per KhrazeGaming: cash, the **Crowax watch** and a **Vapid Hustler**, a hot rod with an exposed engine.

When the game asks whether you trust him, Caspahz says to **trust him**. More on [Benedict Halifax](../gta-6-benedict-halifax-statue/).

## 3. Race Against Yago has a deadline

**Yago** has a chain of **4 races**: Join a Street Race, the mentoring, **Race Against Yago** and Brother to Brother. **Race Against Yago** shows an **hourglass** on the map: if time runs out, it's gone. Caspahz and KhrazeGaming both warn about it.

According to KhrazeGaming, finishing all four leaves you with **Yago's race car**, styled after the Nissan Skyline, which disappears once you reach Chapter 4. Only he mentions the car. Yago's questline also counts for [Strangers & Freaks](../gta-6-strangers-and-freaks/).

## 4. The pink Growler and the free garage

In the Chapter 3 apartment, the laptop opens with **Muffins, Queen, 15** and gives you the code **92665** for a garage in **La Perle**, next to Auntie's Place. Inside is **Vanessa's pink Growler**, with no tracker, and the garage becomes yours. Diggy, KhrazeGaming and Tylarious all show it, and Diggy says to do it **before Chapter 3 ends**. Full steps in the [Chapter 3 laptop password](../gta-6-chapter-3-laptop-password/).

## Mentioned by one channel only

- **Safe Word mission:** if you give up on the practice vault, Caspahz says you can come back later and open it for cash and a weapon.
- **Mercenary LMG 6:** Caspahz grabs a unique machine gun from a **yellow container** during the lock-cutting mission. RageGamingVideos got the same gun later in free roam, so it may not be missable.
- **Apartment parking spots:** Caspahz recommends registering or selling any cars parked there before the chapter ends.
