Money is tight at the start of GTA 6, and there's no shortcut: the [$250,000 money cheat](../gta-6-money-cheat/) only works after you beat the story. Until then, these are the methods players have shown working, with how much each one paid.

## First: chapter 1 money resets

WiLLiSGaming, who already got past chapter 2, warns that **your cash goes back to zero when chapter 2 starts**. The only money that carries over is the **family savings**: the **$5,000** the story asks you to put into the **BuckMe** app (Jason has bad credit, so the bank gives him the app to build it). So in chapter 1, get the $5,000 and keep the story moving. Don't spend hours grinding.

## Early game (chapter 1)

### Shipwreck dives with Kenton
Kenton, a scuba instructor, texts you shipwreck locations. Dive with his gear and a metal detector, collect the treasure and sell it back to him. Up to $4,000 per dive, and he pays more the more you do. Full guide: [GTA 6 sunken treasure](../gta-6-sunken-treasure-shipwrecks/).

### Dog Island
Swim with no boat in the middle of Gambit Bay until a dog on a jet ski rescues you. On its island, a dog digs up about $1,200. See [the jet ski dog](../gta-6-jet-ski-dog-location/).

### Donnie's lost packages and Brian's lockup
Donnie loses packages that show up on your map. Pick them up and bring them to **Donnie's trailer**: according to WiLLiSGaming he pays more than Brian. Use **Focus mode**, because the packages have a tracker that makes a sound Focus can show you. Anything else illegal you find can be sold at **Brian's lockup** (enter the code on the keypad and drop it in his strongbox).

### Mailbox
Check the mailbox at Jason's house after main missions. There's often cash waiting, usually with a text from Donnie letting you know. ImBones points out that rewards from main missions and side quests both land there, so if you never check it, the money just sits.

### RV lockboxes and trading cards
Some RVs out in the wild have lockboxes, and the code is usually written on the wall. WiLLiSGaming says they almost always have good loot, like a rare **sports trading card**. Cards sell at pawn shops: LoDj1c got **$6,000** for one at the **Hamlet** pawn shop. He found that card in a safe inside a building in South Vice-Dale County, near Jason's place, along with cash and cargo worth about $10,000 in total.

### The loot warehouse near Hamlet
DPJ showed the same kind of warehouse in South Vice-Dale County, near Hamlet, with everyone inside already dead: **no fight needed**. He found a bag with **$4,800**, drugs in the back of a truck, a **Duke 5.56 assault rifle** and the safe with the card (rotate the right stick to open it). Bring a car: your bags fill up fast and you can stash the rest in the trunk. The cash comes as **marked bills**, which you have to take to a [fence](../gta-6-fence-locations/). GrandTheftIntel hit the same warehouse and walked out with around $11,000 plus a card worth $6,000. Full walkthrough: [the early money warehouse](../gta-6-early-money-warehouse/).

### Random events at sea
Stop when something weird happens. RageGamingVideos saw an explosion off the coast **east of Key Lento**, found a shark attacking a man, killed it with the speargun from the scuba kit and picked up beeping packages from the water: about **$2,000** worth of drugs to sell.

### Gang hideouts
King Fishers gang bases are packed with contraband that sells well, and WiLLiSGaming found a unique rifle at one. Bring an empty bag and come prepared: he fought about 30 enemies.

## Later in the story (PC Gamer's list)

### Rob the robbers
Listen for sirens, alarms, gunshots and screams. When you see a bank job, jewelry store heist or armored car robbery in progress, take down the crooks running off and **grab their bags**. Then get out fast, because their heat becomes yours. With Jason and Lucia together you can carry more bags.

### Sell stolen cars
All stolen goods (drugs, jewelry, marked bills) turn into cash at a fence. See [GTA 6 fence locations](../gta-6-fence-locations/).

In chapter 2 you meet **Moses**, a mechanic who buys older, low-profile cars and often tells you where the ones he wants are. In chapter 3, **Randy** pays more for flashy, expensive cars. See [how to steal cars in GTA 6](../gta-6-how-to-steal-cars/).

### Demolition derby at Ambrosia Raceway
You win by default once every other car is wrecked, so you don't need to drive well. The oval pays over $3,800 and the figure-eight nearly double, up to $6,300. You can repeat it as often as you like and **rent a car at the track** for a few hundred dollars.

### Hunting for Bret
In chapter 2, Jason takes hunting jobs from his old army buddy **Bret** in Lummox County. He pays more for bigger animals, and there are **legendary animals**. PC Gamer got $2,500 for a big boar.

### Rob stores through the back door
In chapter 3 you get a tool to pick locks. From then on, pick stores with a **back door**: sneak in, subdue the staff in the back, kill the alarm, destroy the security footage and find the safe before anyone notices. See [how to get safe codes](../gta-6-safe-codes/).

## Protect your money

If you die or get busted with cash in your pocket, you lose it. Stash it at home. See [do you lose money when you die in GTA 6](../gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die/).
