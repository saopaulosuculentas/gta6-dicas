Every clothing store, tattoo parlor and hair salon in GTA 6 shows up under the **Style** tab of the map index. If you want to hide your face, the one confirmed option is a **bandana**, which you'll usually find at **Ammu-Nation** gun stores. Nobody has found a dedicated mask shop yet.

## Finding the shops on the map

1. Open the map and press **right on the D-pad** to bring up the [index](../gta-6-index-find-missions/).
2. Go to the **Style** tab, the one with the shirt icon.
3. GamesRadar+ says this tab tracks every place you've discovered to buy clothes, get tattoos, and change hair and makeup for both Jason and Lucia.

Opening hours are never a problem. Rockstar told Kotaku the game has **more than 700 stores** and every one of them is **open 24/7**. The studio picked convenience over realism, since showing up to a locked shop wasn't fun. A 3 AM haircut is fine.

## Masks: what we know

This is the shakiest part of the guide, so here's exactly what has been confirmed:

- **Bandanas at gun stores.** Arekkz Gaming suggests buying a bandana at a gun shop to cover your face during crimes. Game Rant agrees: bandanas are among the first masks you can buy and usually turn up at **Ammu-Nation**, even though accessories aren't on the store's list.
- **A neck gaiter in the Keys.** Game Rant also found a gaiter for sale at **Chuck's Tackle & Shot** in the Keys.
- **How to wear it.** It sits in the top slot of the item wheel and has two styles. Around your neck it hides nothing. Pulled up over your face it hides your identity, but walking around like that draws attention, especially from cops.
- **Take it off late.** Remove it only once you're mostly in the clear. Pull it down too early and the police get a look at you.

What is **not** confirmed: a real mask store selling rubber or costume masks. JoblessGarrett went looking early in the game and couldn't find a mask at a clothing store or anywhere else. We'll update this page if one turns up.

## Clothing stores

- **Changing rooms work like a closet.** Kotaku and BennyCentral both point out that any clothing store with a changing room lets you build outfits, manage what you own and stash clothes in your car's **trunk**, so you don't have to drive home.
- **Name your outfits.** You can rename custom outfits with preset words like "clubbing" or "hunting", which makes the trunk much easier to sort through.
- **Shop as a couple.** Bring Jason and Lucia together and you can buy and try on clothes for both. Buying for your partner also earns [romance](../gta-6-romance-jason-lucia/) points.
- **Every store has exclusives.** Kotaku says most clothing stores and salons carry a few items you won't find anywhere else.
- **Ammu-Nation sells outfits too.** In the GamesRadar+ walkthrough for the Chapter 1 mission Unwanted Attention, you shake off the cops by buying an outfit from the mannequins at an Ammu-Nation for **$59 to $96**.
- **Robbing a clothing store.** MrBossFTW notes you can grab a temporary outfit during the robbery. It's gone for good once you change out of it.

A fresh look also helps when you're wanted. See [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).

## Haircuts, beards and makeup

- **At home:** Kotaku says the bathroom mirror lets you shave your beard and your head, but you can't pick a new style there. MrBossFTW adds that Jason can swap a few cuts and his facial hair, and Lucia can change her makeup and hair, but anything new needs a salon.
- **At a salon:** the menu splits styles into **Signature** and **Standard** tabs. In GamingWithOva's video at Sara's Unisex Salon, cuts ran from **$38** (Crew Cut) to **$71** (Beachy Waves, an exclusive).
- **In the story:** KultZ points out that one of Lucia's optional Chapter 1 errands is getting Jason a haircut.

## Ultimate Edition shops

Three style shops come with the Ultimate Edition and unlock in Chapter 1, according to PC Gamer and GamesRadar+:

| Shop | What it sells | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Stock 305 | Streetwear | Stockyard, Vice City. Index: Style > Casual & Street |
| Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor | 50+ tattoos by the art collective FAILE | Stockyard, Vice City. Index: Style > Tattoos |
| Sara's Unisex Salon | Hair, beards, makeup and nails | Hamlet. Index: Style > Hair & Beauty |

Outside those, the regular shops in the same tab sell similar clothes and cuts, each with a few exclusives. The full bonus list is in [Ultimate Edition bonuses](../gta-6-ultimate-edition-bonuses/).

## One more useful shop

NorZZa found **Zeke's Electronics** in Hamlet, just north of the Keys. It sells **phone cases** and **headphones**, which let you listen to music and podcasts while you walk around.
