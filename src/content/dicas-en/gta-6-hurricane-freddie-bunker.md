**Hurricanes in GTA 6 don't just happen: they unlock after a hidden mission at Freddie's bunker.** Freddie is a doomsday prepper who lives in a compound full of mines and drones in **northern Leonard County**. You break in twice, go to a party in the bunker, and the first hurricane hits soon after.

**Heads up:** according to GameSpot, after the first hurricane they can hit **at any time**, in the middle of whatever you're doing.

GameSpot and AFGuidesHD both show the mission. The exact way in comes from GameSpot.

## First break-in

1. Go to Freddie's compound in **northern Leonard County**. At the gate, a recording warns you about the automated defenses. It doesn't matter what you say into the speaker: he won't let you in.
2. Walk around the concrete bunker **to the right** until you find a **yellow tractor** in front of an **orange container**.
3. Use the tractor to **move the container**. Underneath there's a hole with a ladder.
4. Climb down, follow the **sewer** and go up the next ladder. You come out **inside the compound**.
5. Watch for **mines** on the ground. You can shoot them from a distance. Stick to the grass and high ground.
6. A **drone** starts searching for you. Find it with **Focus mode** and shoot it down.
7. Reach the bunker door, the round metal building in the northwest corner. **Freddie** is furious, says he'll **upgrade his defenses** and tells you to come back when he lets you know.

## Freddie's text

It can take **a few in-game days**. Don't wait around: keep playing. When he texts, he offers you **money** to test his new defenses.

## Second break-in

1. Now there are **mines near the gate**, and the old route is closed.
2. Go around the outside of the compound to the **water tower**. Climb one level and jump onto the roof just below, or climb to the top and **parachute** in. Watch out for mines.
3. Take down the **drones** on your way to the door.
4. The door handle gives you a **shock**, but this time Freddie opens the door and comes out with some friends.

## The party and the hurricane

- Freddie shows you around the bunker, which he says is **flood-proof and nuke-proof**, makes you a drink and invites you onto his **podcast**.
- A **hurricane alert** comes on TV. He seals the door and asks whether you're staying or leaving.
- In AFGuidesHD's video, Lucia **gets money and Freddie's car**. According to GameSpot, you can take his **Dubsta** when you leave.
- Outside, the sky goes dark and the wind picks up. Your partner may text to check that you're okay.

## Trophy

**Retest Freddie's Defenses** is one of the **8 strands** that count for the **Strangers & Freaks** trophy, according to PowerPyx. See the others in [Strangers & Freaks missions](../gta-6-strangers-and-freaks/).
