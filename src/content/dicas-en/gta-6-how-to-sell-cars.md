You can sell a stolen car in three places in GTA 6: a **Pay 'n' Spray**, which takes up to **three cars a day**, and the story's two car buyers, **Moses** (Chapter 2) and **Randy** (Chapter 3). If you'd rather keep a car than sell it, you have to **register** it, and that only unlocks in Chapter 2.

## Where to sell

| Where | What they buy | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Pay 'n' Spray | Any stolen car, 3 per day | PC Gamer doesn't say |
| Moses | Older, low-end models | Chapter 2, mission 5 (Moses), Port Gellhorn, Kelly County |
| Randy | Only certain flashy, expensive cars | Chapter 3, mission 16 (Steal the Zorruso for Randy), Balsero, Vice City |

All of that comes from PC Gamer. The cars on Randy's list, and where to find them, are in our [Randy rare car guide](../gta-6-rare-car-locations-randy/).

The **7 cars Moses asks for** in Chapter 2 are in [Moses drop-off locations](../gta-6-moses-drop-off-locations/).

## Scan the car first with Waink It

You get the **Waink It** app in Chapter 1, during Unwanted Attention, when Cal installs it on your phone. Point it at a car and, according to PC Gamer, it shows:

- make and model, lock, alarm, whether it's keyless and whether it has a tracker;
- the car's **condition** and **how much it sells for**;
- what it costs to **register**;
- whether the car **suits Moses or Randy**.

How to get into each kind of car, and when the slim jim and key cloner unlock, is in [how to steal cars](../gta-6-how-to-steal-cars/).

## Selling to Moses again and again

Ashy shows a simple loop for selling several cars to Moses without leaving his block. It's just the normal sale, repeated:

1. **Unlock Moses.** He shows up in Chapter 2, in the mission Moses. Ashy needed roughly 10 hours of play to get there.
2. **Head to his shop.** The sign on screen reads **Watkins Auto Parts**, owned by Moses "Mo" Watkins.
3. **Check the corner by the shop.** Ashy says there's almost always a car parked there.
4. **Scan it with Waink It.** Look at what it's worth and whether it has a tracker. Only a **car with no tracker** will do.
5. **Pop the driver's door with the slim jim.** Gang members hang around that spot, so get in without them seeing you. More on the tool in our [slim jim and snap gun guide](../gta-6-slim-jim-snap-gun/).
6. **Deliver it to Moses.** He pays by **transfer straight into your bank account**, not in cash.
7. Walk back to the corner and go again.

**There's a cap.** After a few deliveries Moses stops taking cars. Play another mission and he opens up again.

Ashy plays as **Lucia** here, so this isn't a Jason-only thing.

## How much you make

PC Gamer's money guide puts stolen cars at anywhere from $1,000 to $20,000 each, depending on the model and the buyer. In Chapter 2, a decent car for Moses usually brings in **one to a few thousand dollars**. PC Gamer calls it the best quick profit at that stage for little risk: it beats most smash-and-grabs you'll be doing then, and Moses pays better than the Chapter 2 goods fence. The rest of the fences are listed in our [fence locations guide](../gta-6-fence-locations/).

Ashy got less than that: **$670** for one car and **about $700** for another, both everyday cars from the corner by the shop. The price depends on the car, and Waink It tells you what it's worth before you drive it over.

Rare cars are a different story. Typical Gamer, about halfway through the campaign, found a **Progen Royal Ruby Emerus** at a mansion, unlocked, with its alarm off and no tracker. Waink It valued it at **$30,000**.

### GTA+ adds 10% to car sales

According to GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer, **GTA+** members get **10% more** when they sell a car they own at a Pay 'n' Spray. GamesRadar+ notes one exception: cars that come with the **Ultimate Edition can't be sold**. The rest of the membership perks are in [every GTA+ reward in GTA 6](../gta-6-gta-plus-rewards/).

## Keeping a car: registration

**Your first registration at Moses is free.** ConCon shows that the first car you register at Moses' garage costs nothing; after that you pay full price. Choose well: bring a car you actually want, or at least one that's pricey to register. His respray is free too, according to ConCon. WAiNK shows the **registration cost** of each car along with its sell value.

**Registered cars come to you.** In the **Scooter Bros** app, pick the car and pay the delivery fee: it was $110 for ConCon.

**Advanced trackers:** sports and super cars carry a tracker that pings the cops. According to LunarGaming, you can only steal them with the **key cloner**, which you get in a Randy mission in Chapter 3, and you can only keep or sell them after finishing Randy's car missions.


- **Not in Chapter 1.** MrDalekJD says you're stuck with the starter car. You can steal cars off the street, but they aren't saved for good. DPJ also thinks an unregistered car is gone once you reload your game.
- **Unregistered means still stolen.** The police keep treating the car as stolen until it's registered, MrDalekJD says.
- **Unlocks in Chapter 2**, after the main story mission **Moses** in Port Gellhorn.
- **Where:** at a **Pay 'n' Spray**, per PC Gamer and GamingBolt. DPJ drove his car back to Moses and registered it there.
- **Cost:** Waink It shows the price for each car. DPJ paid **around $6,750** to register a rare car, and registering it **swapped the license plate**.
- **Size limits:** GamingBolt found a **monster truck** that wouldn't fit inside the Pay 'n' Spray, so it couldn't be registered.

### Inside the La Perle Pay 'n' Spray

Typical Gamer took the Emerus to the **Pay 'n' Spray in La Perle**, and the shop handles everything from one menu: **Repair**, **Respray** ($200 for red), **Register** and **Sell**. So the same place can make a car yours or buy it off you. A second tab, **Stored Vehicles**, lists the cars you've already put away.

Registering the Emerus cost **$29,900**, nearly its full resale value, and the price matched what Waink It had shown out on the street. The game describes registering as swapping the plates and putting the car in your name. He couldn't cover it with $25,392 in the bank and $9,671 in cash. On screen, the registration price only shows the BuckMe bank icon, so it seems to come out of your bank balance alone. No source has tested that yet.

Trackers are still a gray area. MrDalekJD says you can register cars that **don't have a tracker**. PC Gamer says a standard tracker only stops you from upgrading the car at a shop until it's registered. When in doubt, keep a car with no tracker.

GamingBolt has one more tip for once you own a few cars: swapping rides at a Pay 'n' Spray helps throw off the police.

## Where to store it

- **A garage:** buy one through the real estate app on your phone. PC Gamer says each garage fits one car; GameSpot says one or a couple.
- **Moses' garage:** GameSpot says Moses hands you a free garage during the story, **Port Gellhorn Station**, just north of his shop.
- **Outside your safehouse:** the parking spots out front also work for a stolen car, according to PC Gamer.

## Getting a car out of the impound

Open the **Scooter Bros** app on your phone. Kotaku and BennyCentral both say it gets your car out of the police impound, for a fee. BennyCentral adds that the same app brings your car to you and repairs it, with **every use costing money**. He suggests saving up for **Scooter Bros Premium**: **$12,500** for **3 free uses per in-game day**.

The sources only talk about your own vehicles. None of them say whether an unregistered stolen car ends up in the impound.
