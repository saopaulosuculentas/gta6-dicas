The exploding balls cheat in GTA 6 is **Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Right**. What it does: sports balls explode on impact. Any sports ball turns into a bomb that goes off when it hits something.

## How to enter it

1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Right.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to **hit 20 enemies with explosive damage**. Progress you made during the story counts.

## What you need to know

- No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
- Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
- It matches the [attack force cheat](../gta-6-attack-force-cheat/) except for the last press: Right here, Down there.
- The sequence matches in GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer, so the badge is "Confirmed".
