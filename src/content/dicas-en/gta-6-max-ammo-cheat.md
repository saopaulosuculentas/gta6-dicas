The max ammo cheat in GTA 6 is **Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Up, Down, Left**. What it does: refills all your ammo to the max. It refills your ammo once; it does not make it infinite. That is the [infinite ammo cheat](../gta-6-infinite-ammo-cheat/).

## How to enter it

1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Up, Down, Left.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to **win 25 bronze medals or better at shooting ranges**. Progress you made during the story counts.

## What you need to know

- No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
- Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
- PC Gamer lists 20 bronze medals instead of 25. If it does not unlock at 20, keep going to 25.
- The three ammo cheats start the same way and only change at the end, so one wrong press triggers a different cheat or none.
- The sequence matches in Bubs and GamesRadar+, so the badge is "Confirmed".
