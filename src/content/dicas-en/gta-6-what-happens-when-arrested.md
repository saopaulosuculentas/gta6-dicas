Get arrested in GTA 6 and you'll see the **Busted** screen, followed by the bill: your **pocket cash** is gone, any **weapons you don't own** are gone, and if you were in your personal car, it's headed to the **impound lot**. Money in the **bank**, cash in your **home stash** and **guns you bought** all survive.

## What you lose

**1. Your pocket cash.** MrDalekJD, MrBossFTW and KultZ all give the same warning: getting busted takes the cash you're carrying. MrBossFTW says you drop all of the loose money on you. Dan Allen Gaming's video shows it in action: right after the arrest, the HUD flashes **-$244** and the wallet reads **$0**. PC Gamer describes the penalty as **a few hundred dollars** and notes you can carry at most **$20,000** on your person anyway. Carry less, lose less.

**2. Weapons you don't own.** GamesRadar+ splits guns into owned and unowned. Anything you **picked up from enemies or found in the world** is unowned, and it's lost for good if you're Busted or Wasted.

**3. Your loot bag.** GamingBolt warns that getting busted or killed by the cops can cost you the bag from a heist. It isn't always gone: head back to the scene and look for a **green bag icon**. The cops may also have taken it.

**4. Your personal car, if you were driving it.** GamesRadar+ says getting Busted in your Personal Vehicle sends it to the **impound lot**. You have two ways to get it back:

- Pay a **hefty fee** to the **ScooterBros** app to pull it out. That route may cost you any stash bags in the trunk.
- Check the **Personal** tab of the index to see where it's being held and steal it back yourself. If the cops already took the bags out, check nearby police cars.

## What stays safe

- **The bank.** MrDalekJD and MrBossFTW both say money in your BuckMe account isn't touched when you're arrested. See [bank accounts and ATMs](../gta-6-bank-atm-deposit/).
- **Your home stash.** PC Gamer reminds you that cash hidden under the bedroom floorboards in Key Lento is safe too. See [losing money when you die](../gta-6-lose-money-when-you-die/).
- **Guns you paid for.** Weapons bought at Ammu-Nation come back. MrDalekJD shows your full collection waiting in the **weapons locker** at any Ammu-Nation, and GamesRadar+ notes you can also grab them from your personal car's trunk. See [storing weapons in your trunk](../gta-6-store-weapons-trunk/).
- **Goods in your own trunk.** PC Gamer says stolen goods stored in your car's trunk can be recovered even if the car is impounded. Just don't stash anything in a car you don't own, because losing the car means losing the load.

## Is there jail time?

None of the guides or videos we read show a character **serving a sentence**. In Dan Allen Gaming's clip, the camera sweeps over the Keys after the Busted screen and Jason is back on his feet on the street, free, with an empty wallet. Exactly where the game drops you after an arrest isn't explained by any source we could read.

Two things are clear:

- **The game autosaves.** GamesRadar+ says the hidden autosave slot kicks in when you're busted, when you die and after big open-world encounters.
- **The prologue arrest is scripted.** In the Prologue mission, Lucia's two-star escape always ends in Busted because it's part of the story, as the GamesRadar+ walkthrough points out.

## How to avoid getting busted

- **Talk your way out.** Kotaku explains that a one-star wanted level can be cleared by talking to the cop: hold the **left trigger** to focus on them with no gun out and choose to **defuse**. Choosing to provoke makes things worse.
- **Tasers put you down.** In Dan Allen Gaming's video, a taser hit drops Jason and the game shows a prompt to get up. The cops pinned him moments later.
- **Bank first.** Hit an ATM or your stash before a robbery.
- **Escape properly.** Change clothes, swap cars and get out of the search area. The full method is in [how to lose the police](../gta-6-how-to-lose-the-police/).
