**The Brooks Boatworks safe code in GTA 6 is 6823.** ConCon finds the number on a wall map and opens the safe with it, and RaidAway opens the same safe with **6-8-2-3** in his own game.

## Where Brooks Boatworks is

It's the **Kolt & Associates** boatyard in **Port Gellhorn**, on the bay right by the bridge. On the map it's flagged as a robbery opportunity, and the card ConCon pulls up says the gang moves prescription meds through the docks. Security is **high** and the estimated take is **$5,300 to $7,900**. ConCon says it's a **Chapter 2** job, and by then the boatyard is already marked on your map.

## Where to find the code

ConCon grabs the code without firing a shot. Bring a silenced pistol anyway, in case someone spots you.

1. Skip the **main entrance on the left**: the guards there will stop and question you. Go around to the **right side** instead.
2. Park near the **blue tarp fence** next to the shed with the "Port Gellhorn" sign and hop it.
3. Stay **crouched**, cut through the brush and squeeze through the **gap in the next fence**.
4. Hug the fence on your right so the armed guard doesn't notice you.
5. Slip into the **small building with outside stairs** and climb up to the office full of monitors.
6. Check the **Port Gellhorn map on the wall**, next to the whiteboard.

Three numbers are scrawled on it: **6823** (Brooks Boatworks), **6969** ([La Cascara Motel](../gta-6-la-cascara-motel-safe-code/)) and **0300** ([Kingpin Bowling](../gta-6-bowling-alley-safe-code/)). Examining the map updates your game map, so the Brooks Boatworks card now reads "Safe code: 6823", and the number is saved to your **Notes** in the corner of the screen.

RaidAway got the same 6823 another way: he found it while robbing a different warehouse run by the gang, before heading to the boatyard.

## Grab the rifle while you're there

In that same office, ConCon picks up the unique **Clunker 556** assault rifle from a table. See more [legendary and unique weapons](../gta-6-unique-weapons-locations/).

## Where the safe is

ConCon says the boatyard has **two safes**. One is small and easy to break into without a code. The big one, the one that asks for 6823, is in the big warehouse:

1. Head for the **big warehouse** in the yard and expect plenty of armed guards. ConCon found it too hard to sneak through and shot his way in.
2. Go in, keep **left** and take the stairs to the **upper floor**.
3. The safe sits in a small room covered in posters. Interact with it and choose **Enter Code**. There's also an option to plant an explosive.
4. Enter **6823** and take the cash. In ConCon's video the safe held about **$2,400**.
5. Close by there's a **pool table** with pills on it.

RaidAway went upstairs too and found cash, pills and the safe. Around the yard he also picked up a gold weapon and a sticky bomb.

## Leave by water

Both players escape **by boat** from the dock, and ConCon recommends heading out to sea. On the way in, RaidAway even dove into the water and swam to stay out of sight of the crew on a boat, and the cops were closing in as he left. Pills can be sold, as covered in [fence locations](../gta-6-fence-locations/).
