In GTA 6 you get body armor by **buying it at a gun store**, then you put it on from the **weapon wheel**. While you wear it, a **blue bar sits above your health** and soaks up bullets before your health does. You can also find armor **for free** in police cars and in houses you raid.

## Where to buy it

At a **gun store**. JorRaptor grabbed his at the store nearest Jason's house, Chuck's Tackle & Shot, and says you can **choose the color**. His video doesn't show the price.

## How to put it on

1. Open the **weapon wheel**.
2. Armor has its **own slot** on the wheel, apart from your guns.
3. Select it and the **blue bar** appears on top of your health bar.

JorRaptor only straps it on right before a shootout.

## How it works

Here's how JorRaptor explains it:

- Incoming fire **drains the blue bar first**. Your health only takes hits once it's gone.
- If the vest **survives** the fight, the bar **refills over time** and you can use it again.
- If the bar **hits zero**, the vest breaks and you need to **buy a new one**.
- One trick: **take the vest off** just before it breaks. You keep it, but every bullet after that goes straight to your health.

## Free armor

- **Police cars:** BennyCentral opened the trunk of a cop car at the Hamlet impound and walked off with a rifle, a shotgun and a vest. Save first, since the cops don't take it well. Full steps in [free guns from cop cars](../gta-6-free-guns-cop-cars/).
- **The gun nut's house:** according to BennyCentral, a stilt house in Key Lento that looks a lot like Jason's holds armor, a machine gun and a rifle, plus the unique Gun Nut SMS01 sniper. The owner is home and comes at you.
- **Mansions:** Typical Gamer broke into a random mansion, forced open a safe and came out with armor, a rare rifle and cash.
- **Cheat:** there's a [body armor cheat](../gta-6-body-armor-cheat/), but it only unlocks after 100 workouts.
