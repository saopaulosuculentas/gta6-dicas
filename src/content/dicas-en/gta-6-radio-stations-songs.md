Rockstar has previewed **6 GTA 6 radio stations**: The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM, Afro Bank FM, Back Country Radio, Cocoteo FM and Dirty South Classics. Each preview opens with a quick intro from the hosts. PC Gamer gathered those stations along with every song that has played in official GTA 6 footage.

## The 6 stations Rockstar showed

| Station | Hosts |
|---|---|
| The Chamber 106.6 | DJ Tom and DJ Kerry |
| Flash FM | Robyn and Alex |
| Afro Bank FM | Burna and Palmsy |
| Back Country Radio | MW and Delta Dawne |
| Cocoteo FM | El Martillo and La Gata |
| Dirty South Classics | Trick and Trina |

That isn't the whole lineup. In the gameplay shot at the top of this page, the car's radio wheel also lists **Emotion 98.3** and **Gloriana Blues Radio**, which weren't among the six.

## Songs heard so far

Keep one thing in mind: PC Gamer **can't promise all of these are on the in-game radio**. They come from official material, like the trailers, the Netflix video and the game's album, and the list doesn't say which station plays what. PC Gamer also put them all in a Spotify playlist.
