Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

What the site collects on its own

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, comments or forms that send us data. We store only two things on your device: your console choice (PS5 or Xbox) in the cheats table and your last 5 searches on the site. They never leave your browser, and what you type in the search box is not sent to us. To delete your searches, use the "Clear" button in the search itself.

Google ads

The site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and identifiers to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and to measure how those ads perform.

  • You can turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Learn how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to count how many people visit the site, which pages are read most and where visits come from (search, social media, direct). It uses cookies and receives data such as device type, browser and approximate region. We do not use this data to identify anyone. You can block it with the Google Analytics opt-out add-onor in your browser's cookie settings.

YouTube videos

Videos only load when you click to watch, and they use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). From that click on, YouTube's privacy rules apply.

Fonts and hosting

Fonts come from Google Fonts and pages are delivered by a hosting service. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address in order to deliver the content.

Your rights

You can ask for information about, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Since the site stores no personal data, in practice this applies to messages you send through the contact page.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes too.
