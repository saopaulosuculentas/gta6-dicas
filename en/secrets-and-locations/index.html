GTA 6 Military Base Location: How to Get Into Mile Zero Air Station

The GTA 6 military base is Mile Zero Air Station on Goose Key, in the southwest corner of the map. The easiest way in is the stunt jump ramp on the southeast side. Inside: jets, tanks and helicopters.

GTA 6: Where to Find Your First Plane and Helicopter (Near Jason's House)

In GTA 6, seaplanes and a helicopter sit unguarded in Southwind, about 2.5 miles from Jason's house in Key Lento. You can take them without getting a wanted level.

GTA 6 Jet Ski Dog Location: How to Find Dog Island and Get $1,200

In GTA 6, if you swim with no boat in the middle of Gambit Bay, a dog in a life jacket and goggles shows up on a jet ski. It takes you to Dog Island, where a dog digs up about $1,200.

GTA 6 Bigfoot (Skunk Ape) Mission: How to Start It and Get the Camo Sniper Rifle

GTA 6's Bigfoot is the start of a hidden side quest. After you black out at the effigy in Grassrivers, find a note, call the number and hunt the Skunk Ape at night. The reward is a unique camo sniper rifle.
