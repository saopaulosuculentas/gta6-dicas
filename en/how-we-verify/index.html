How we verify

The internet is full of "GTA 6 cheats" that don't work. Our rule is simple: only what someone has shown working in the game gets published, and you always see who showed it.

Where the tips come from

We follow GTA 6 videos and streams on YouTube several times a day. An AI program reads the transcripts, picks out the parts about cheats, secrets, easter eggs and money, and when a detail only appears on screen (a button sequence, a spot on the map) it checks the frame at that moment.

We also follow what Rockstar Games publishes and the articles of major gaming outlets. An official source counts on its own. A press article counts as one source and needs a second one before the tip gets the confirmed badge.

The English pages use English-language sources: the video embedded on each page, at the exact timestamp, is in English. The text is written from scratch by us, and credit for the find goes to whoever found it.

What each badge means

  • Confirmed: the same information showed up in at least two independent sources (channels or major outlets), or came from Rockstar Games itself.
  • Unverified: only one source has shown it so far. We publish so you don't miss out, but it may still change.

If an unverified tip turns out to be false, it comes down. If a confirmed tip changes after a game update, the page is corrected and the change is logged in its history.

What we never publish

  • Rumors, "leaks" and cheat lists published before launch.
  • Hacks, paid mods and anything that gives an advantage online.
  • "Money generators" and sites that ask for your account login. Those are scams.
  • Codes we couldn't read with certainty. No guessing.

Images

Some pages show a frame from the gameplay video they are based on, with the creator credited and a link to the video at that moment. We only use the game image, never the creator's face or logo. If you are the creator and want it removed, contact us.
