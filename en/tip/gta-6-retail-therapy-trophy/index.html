GTA 6 Retail Therapy Trophy: How to Unlock It

To unlock Retail Therapy, rob 3 shops back to back with Jason and Lucia together while you're still wanted. It can't be done before Chapter 2.

Unverified · 4 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 6:13 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

To unlock the Retail Therapy trophy in GTA 6, rob 3 shops back to back with Jason and Lucia together, and don’t let the Wanted Level drop between robberies. It’s a bronze trophy worth 20G on Xbox, according to the GamesRadar+ trophy list.

When you can do it

Not before Chapter 2. PowerPyx’s video has an on-screen note saying that’s the chapter where you unlock playing as Jason and Lucia together. Since the trophy needs your partner with you, there’s no point trying earlier.

Rules to keep in mind

  • Three different stores. You can’t hit the same store twice in a row. Mr Lawrence says it shows as closed or unavailable for a while, with a small red heat area drawn around it.
  • Each robbery gives you 1 or 2 stars, he adds. The trophy description asks you to hold on to that Wanted Level until the third store is done.
  • It’s good for the relationship too. Mr Lawrence says robbing as a couple raises Jason and Lucia’s relationship.

PowerPyx’s route: 3 jewelry stores in a row

There’s no voiceover in PowerPyx’s video, only game audio, so everything here comes from text on the screen. He picked three jewelry stores near each other, and the map card is nearly identical for all of them:

Order Store Map card
1 Covgari Small high-end jewelry store, medium security, $14,000 to $25,000
2 Blanc & Giroux Small high-end jewelry store on NE 19th St, medium security, $14,000 to $25,000
3 Roval Jewelry Large high-end jewelry store, medium security, $14,000 to $25,000

Between the second and third stores, the game shows the neighborhood name: Decor District.

What shows up on screen during the run:

  1. On the sidewalk with Lucia next to you, you get Talk and Hold Hands prompts. That’s how you know your partner is with you.
  2. Inside, the Jewelry prompt grabs pieces from the display cases one at a time.
  3. Right after the first robbery, a Crime reported message pops up.
  4. The back door gives you Use Snap Gun or Break Open, and the safe in the back room offers Plant Explosive.
  5. The stars keep climbing from store to store. By the third one, the video shows 4 stars and police at the door.

With no commentary, the video never says why he went for jewelry stores over convenience stores.

Another option: the stores in the Keys

Musty Sky shows a cluster of stores near Jason’s house in the Keys. His video is about making money, not this trophy:

  • a gas station with 2 stores;
  • 2 more down the road on the left. The clerk at one of them tends to carry a shotgun.

He says not to shoot anyone, since fewer stars make it easier to keep robbing. The catch: in his video the star disappears as he drives down the road. For the trophy, that can’t happen before the third store. None of the videos we watched tried this route for Retail Therapy.

The rest of the job

For masks, safes and how much each store pays, see how to rob stores in GTA 6. After the third store you’re still wanted, so check how to lose the police for the getaway.

Quick answers

What do you need to do for Retail Therapy in GTA 6?

Rob 3 shops back to back with your partner without losing your Wanted Level. It's a bronze trophy worth 20G on Xbox.

When can you get Retail Therapy?

From Chapter 2 on. PowerPyx's video points out on screen that this is when you can play Jason and Lucia together.

Can you rob the same store twice in a row in GTA 6?

No. Mr Lawrence says a store you just hit shows as closed for a while, so you need 3 different ones.

Which stores did PowerPyx rob for the trophy?

Three jewelry stores close together: Covgari, Blanc & Giroux and Roval Jewelry. On the way between them, the game names the area as the Decor District.
