GTA 6 Safe Codes: How to Find the Combination and Open Safes in Robberies

Safe codes in GTA 6 robberies are randomized. Find the code written down on the premises with Focus mode, or threaten an employee with the Safe Code prompt. Codes are 4 digits.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 10:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

There’s no single safe code in GTA 6. In story missions the game gives you the combination, but in robberies it’s randomized every time. To open the safe you need to find the 4-digit code on the spot. PC Gamer explains the two ways.

Option 1: find it written down

The code is often written somewhere inside the store or bank:

  • on a note on a board in the back office;
  • carried by a guard you knocked out.

Turn on Focus mode (press L3 and R3 together) to highlight important objects.

Option 2: threaten an employee

Aim your weapon at an employee. You get Threaten and Safe Code? prompts. Pick the safe code and they’ll hand it over. In some robberies this also makes the cashier empty the register, like in older GTA games.

Opening the safe

Once you have the code, it appears in the lower right corner when you interact with the safe. Enter the 4 digits and it opens.

Be quick: the sooner it’s open, the sooner your bag is full and you’re gone.

Pick your target on the map

Select a store or bank on the map before you go. It shows whether it has a safe, its security level (low to high) and the approximate value of what’s inside, so you can choose a target worth the risk.

Tip: go in the back

In chapter 3 you get a tool to pick locks, and from then on back doors are the way to go: subdue the staff in the back, kill the alarm and destroy the security footage before opening the safe. More in how to make money fast in GTA 6.

Quick answers

What is the safe code in GTA 6?

There's no fixed code: in robberies it's randomized. You find it written down on the premises or get it from an employee. In story missions the game gives it to you.

How do you find a safe code in GTA 6?

Use Focus mode (L3 and R3) to spot the note with the code, often on a board in the back office or on a knocked-out guard. Or aim at an employee and pick Safe Code.

How do you know if a store has a safe in GTA 6?

Select it on your map. It shows whether it has a safe, the security level and the approximate value of the goods.
