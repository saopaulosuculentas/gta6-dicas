The Red Hulk in GTA 6: Who Kyle Is and Where He Comes From

GTA 6's Red Hulk is Kyle, a farmer who turns into a red giant after using sketchy chemicals to grow huge strawberries. At the end you can hug him for a Focus boost.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:47 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s Red Hulk is Kyle, a farmer who turns into a red-skinned giant. According to Kotaku, he comes from a side mission where you help the farmer grow extremely large strawberries. To pull that off he uses some questionable chemicals, and the transformation is what you get.

Who Kyle is

His name is right there on screen in Aaraz Gaming’s video: the prompt reads “Talk to Kyle”, and the name Kyle sits in the corner when you get close. He’s not a random monster, either. Kotaku explains that by the time this scene happens, Jason already knows the farmer from the strawberry quest.

He blew up thanks to a stream by IShowSpeed, one of the first streamers to reach this mission. His clip spread fast, and Kotaku asked Rockstar what was going on.

What happens at the farm

In the video, the player is controlling Lucia. The scene takes place at a farm with a red-doored barn and cars on fire nearby, and Kyle is down on one knee, huge and red, in ripped jeans.

  • Talking to him brings up dialogue options. One of them is “You gotta go”, on Cross on PS5.
  • When Kyle opens his arms, the screen shows Hug on Triangle and Decline on Square.
  • After the hug, a “Focus buffed” message pops up: your character’s Focus gets a boost. See what Focus does in how to increase Focus.

None of the sources say where the farm is or what the missions are called, so there’s no route to mark yet. Meanwhile, more side missions with oddball characters are in Strangers and Freaks.

What Rockstar said

Kotaku asked Rob Nelson, Rockstar’s head of production, why a creature like this made it into the game. According to Nelson:

  • adding this kind of zany stuff mostly comes down to feel, and now and then he wonders, “Are we going too crazy?”;
  • the team gets pitched loads of ideas from across the studio and encourages people to try them out. If it’s fun or funny enough, it stays in;
  • Rockstar loves playing with players: you never know whether the paranormal stuff is real or fake, like whether it’s really Bigfoot or a guy in a Bigfoot mask.

Other strange creatures

Kyle isn’t alone. Kotaku notes that players have already found Bigfoot, spotted UFOs and run into a giant crab that nobody has explained yet.

Quick answers

Is there a Hulk in GTA 6?

There's a red, Hulk-like giant. It's Kyle, a strawberry farmer who transforms after using chemicals on his crops.

How does the farmer become the Red Hulk in GTA 6?

According to Kotaku, he uses questionable chemicals to make his strawberries grow huge, and the transformation is the result.

Can you hug the Red Hulk in GTA 6?

Yes. At the end of the scene you get a Hug prompt (Triangle on PS5), and the hug gives you a Focus buff.
