GTA 6 Chapter 4: Mistakes to Avoid, the Right Free Car and What You Can Miss

In Chapter 4, don't pay for Eddie Acosta's party, finish Bad Driver before Power Play and move your money into savings before picking up the Y Hotel reception message. Here's the list and the right free car.

Confirmed · 5 sourcesUpdated Nov 26, 4:09 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s Chapter 4 has a few traps:

  • don’t pay for Eddie Acosta’s party;
  • finish the Bad Driver job before the Power Play mission;
  • move your money into savings before you pick up the Y Hotel reception message.

This list only includes what two or more channels show, like GTA6Digest, Pixden and SpookyFairy. Make a save in a new slot at the start of the chapter and another before the reception.

1. Don’t pay at Eddie Acosta’s party

Eddie Acosta shows up in Chapter 4 at a bar and invites you to a party at Heathcliff Heights. There he takes you to the rooftop, locks the door and says you can only leave if you invest.

  • Paying only gets you an image (NFT) on your phone. No car, mission or money follows. GTA6Digest, Pixden and SpookyFairy all got the same result.
  • The price varies: in gameplay videos the button asked for $20,000; written guides say $50,000.
  • Intimidating him does nothing: the door stays locked, according to SpookyFairy.
  • Refusing costs nothing.

2. Finish Bad Driver before Power Play

Bad Driver, Andrea’s job in Millport where you track down Felipe, disappears for good once you start the main mission Power Play. GTA6Digest and Pixden both warn about it. Its ending has a choice: one option pays $20,000 and the other unlocks a new job. Save first.

3. Take the hourglass jobs

Jobs with an hourglass on the map run on a timer: miss the deadline and they’re gone. One example is a call from an unknown number asking Lucia to pick up Shayna. Answer and do it right away, as GTA6Digest and Pixden recommend.

4. Story seems stuck? Check your phone

At one point in the chapter, no main mission shows up on the map. That’s because a BuckMe payment has come in that you need to cover for the story to continue. You can pay in parts with whatever’s in your account. After the last payment, wait or sleep. GTA6Digest and Pixden both show it.

5. Move your money before the Y Hotel reception

Near the end of the chapter, the map says someone left a message at the Y Hotel reception. Once you pick it up, the last missions roll one after another and your checking account gets frozen. In Pixden’s game, more than $277,000 went to zero. Money in the family savings is safe, but you can’t withdraw it.

Before picking up the message: move what you want to keep into savings, keep only what you’ll spend, and save in a new slot.

6. Other mistakes the channels flag

  • Don’t tailgate the armored truck. Get too close and you alert it, failing the job. See how to rob bank vaults.
  • Don’t pay to have your car delivered. The Y Hotel has a free driver: press the button on the car out front. Your old cars are in a garage in Ocean Beach. Rental cars can’t be registered, sold or recovered.
  • Weapons you didn’t buy are lost if you die in combat or get arrested. You can only keep one of each model.
  • Watch your criminal profile: Irredeemable is permanent. See the criminal profile.
  • Keep Jason and Lucia close: holding hands, dates, answering messages and doing crimes together help. Ignoring calls hurts.

The free car: which one to pick

In Chapter 4, Jason and Lucia each get one free car at a dealership, as Daylight Heist and Pixden show. Lucia picks first. Each car has a stats panel next to it.

According to Daylight Heist, who opened the panels:

Car Speed Acceleration Asphalt Off-road Strength
Pfister Comet S2 7.8 8.0 7.2 3.6 3.0
Grotti Carbonizzare 6.3 7.1 6.0 3.6 3.0
Canis Kamacho 3.7 n/a 3.4 6.7 6.2
Gallivanter Baller ST-D 5.3 6.4 4.2 5.0 3.0

Take the Comet S2 first, with Lucia. For the second car, the Carbonizzare is better on asphalt and the Kamacho off-road. The fifth car, the Comet Retro, wasn’t opened in the video.

Quick answers

Should you pay Eddie Acosta in GTA 6?

No. Paying only gets you an image (NFT) on your phone, with no car, mission or money afterward. Refusing costs nothing.

Which free car should you pick in GTA 6 Chapter 4?

According to Daylight Heist, the Pfister Comet S2, which has the best speed and acceleration. Lucia picks first, so take the S2 with her.

What can you miss in GTA 6 Chapter 4?

The Bad Driver job disappears once you start Power Play, hourglass jobs expire, and your checking account money can freeze near the end of the chapter. Move it into savings first.

GTA 6 Chapter 4 story seems stuck. What now?

Check your phone: there may be a BuckMe payment you need to make for the story to continue. You can pay it in parts. Then wait or sleep.
