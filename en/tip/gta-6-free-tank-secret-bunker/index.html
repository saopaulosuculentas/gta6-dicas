GTA 6 Free Tank Forever: The Secret Bunker Mission and Door Codes

A hidden quest that starts at a Vice City memorial leads to two bunkers. You walk away with the legendary Russo's 1922 pistol, a rifle and a tank that always respawns. Codes 010317 and 085355.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 24, 7:30 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 hides a secret quest that ends with a tank you keep forever. It starts with a man at a Vice City memorial, runs through an apartment full of clues about a president’s assassination and ends in two secret bunkers. Along the way you pick up the legendary Russo’s 1922 pistol and a rifle.

KackisHD (YouTube) and MrDalekJD (TikTok, over a million views) both followed the same route. The door codes show on the in-game keypad display in KackisHD’s video.

Before you go

  • Go in daylight. KackisHD went at night and the man at the memorial wasn’t there. He slept at his safehouse until morning.
  • Bring a flashlight for the second bunker, which starts out dark.
  • Both bunkers are on an island or deep in the woods, so part of the trip is swimming or on foot.

1. The man at the memorial

  1. Head to the memorial in east downtown Vice City. KackisHD calls it the War Memorial; MrDalekJD calls it the Friends of Freedom landmark.
  2. Talk to the man sitting by the wall and ask what really happened. He talks about a Cold War plot involving Cuba and the assassination of President Russo.
  3. He gives you the address of his apartment in Little Cuba. It has a Cuban flag out front.

2. The apartment

  1. Inside there’s a fallout shelter blueprint on the wall and a tape recorder. Listen to all of the recordings.
  2. Check the photo (the back has a date on it) and the phone.
  3. One photo says Shark Tooth Island, and the map on the wall has spots circled. That’s your next stop.

3. The island bunker (Russo’s 1922 pistol)

  1. Head to Shark Tooth Island, a remote island just north of Waning Sands in Leonard County. The road ends before it, so swim the rest.
  2. At the bunker door, enter 010317.
  3. Inside, follow the tunnel and turn right to switch on the power, then go through the doors on the left.
  4. You’re in the president’s bunker: presidential seal, desk and red phone. There’s also a photo of the president meeting an alien on Mount Chiliad.
  5. Sit in the desk chair, check the drawer and pick up the phone. That turns on a radio in another room: follow the beeping and play the recording, which mentions Protocol 2.
  6. Back at the desk, the drawer now has a classified document about Protocol 2 with the safe code. Both videos say the same number, but neither shows the safe being opened, so read it off the document in your game.
  7. The safe holds the Russo’s 1922 Pistol, a legendary pistol that stays in your weapon wheel.
  8. There’s a photo under it. Flip it over: the back says obsessed, the clue for the bigger bunker.

4. The woods bunker (the tank)

  1. Go to the woods north of Watson Bay, in west Vice-Dale County, at one of the spots circled on the apartment map.
  2. The big bunker’s front entrance is shut. Climb the hill behind it and look in the tree line for a metal hatch in the ground and a keypad on a tree.
  3. Enter 085355 and climb down the ladder.
  4. It’s dark. Head all the way to the far left and switch on the generator in the storage room.
  5. Follow the beeping to another recording and listen to it.
  6. Crouch under the door into the control room and press the button on the panel: the hangar door opens.
  7. The hangar holds the tank and, according to MrDalekJD, a Cold War assault rifle.

Afterwards

  • The tank is yours for good. The hangar door stays open and the tank respawns there every time you come back.
  • It gets you to 5 and 6 stars in no time. In KackisHD’s video the main gun never seemed to run out of ammo.

Quick answers

How do you get a free tank in GTA 6?

Do the hidden quest that starts with a man at the memorial in east downtown Vice City. It runs through a bunker on an island north of Waning Sands and ends in a hidden bunker in the woods, where a tank always respawns.

What is the bunker code in GTA 6?

The island bunker door opens with 010317. The second bunker's hatch, on a keypad attached to a tree, opens with 085355. The safe code in the first bunker is on a classified document in the president's desk drawer.

Does the bunker tank respawn in GTA 6?

Yes. Once you open the hangar, the door stays open and the tank is there every time you come back, according to both videos.
