How to Rob Bank Vaults in GTA 6: Armored Trucks, Yasmani's Tips and Up to $205K

Large bank vaults in GTA 6 only open when an armored truck makes a delivery. Here's when they unlock, how to use Yasmani's tips, how to get into the vault and how much each job pays.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 26, 4:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Large bank vaults in GTA 6 can’t be opened with a code or explosives: they only open when an armored truck makes a delivery. You tail the truck to the bank, wait for the guard to walk in with a briefcase and open the vault, then slip in behind him. According to GameSpot, it’s the best money-maker in the game.

GameSpot and PowerPyx describe the same method.

When it unlocks

  • Vault robberies unlock in Chapter 4 with the optional mission That’s Where The Money Is…, which walks you through the first one, according to PowerPyx.
  • Before that you need the Dye Pack Jammer from Chapter 3 so the marked bills don’t get ruined. See all equipment unlocks.
  • To buy tips from Yasmani, PowerPyx says you first need to finish his earlier jobs, like robbing two Payday Loans.

Step by step

  1. Buy a tip from Yasmani. It costs $10,000 and marks an armored truck with a purple icon. It’s repeatable: go back to him after each job. You can also stumble on armored trucks around town.
  2. Tail the truck quietly. Don’t commit crimes near it, or the driver spots you and speeds off.
  3. Wait for it to stop at a bank. A guard gets out with a briefcase and walks in the front.
  4. Follow him in, ideally without a visible gun on your back, and give him a few seconds to open the vault.
  5. Get to the vault. It’s in the back. Solo, sneak past the guards. To rob the whole bank, bring your partner for crowd control.
  6. Grab the marked bills from the cart in front of the deposit boxes. If you have time, open some deposit boxes along the walls.
  7. Hand bags to your partner when yours is full by holding the prompt. You can’t carry it all alone.
  8. Escape. The vault gives you 4 stars instantly, according to PowerPyx. A fast motorcycle cuts through traffic, and switching vehicles helps you disappear.
  9. Sell the bills to Yasmani, who pays the most for marked bills.

How much it pays

What How much
Marked bills from the vault $80,000 to $100,000, per GameSpot
Drawers and small safe at the same bank Another $25,000 to $50,000, per GameSpot
Top haul from one bank Up to $205,000, per PowerPyx

Cloud cameras

At high-security spots, a camera icon with a cloud means the footage goes to the cloud and can’t be destroyed, according to GameSpot. Your only option is to shoot cameras as you go. If one catches you, the cops only get the footage once the alarm goes off or you leave.

Trophies and 100%

  • Maximum Withdrawal: rob 5 vaults and escape.
  • A Classic Blitz Play: rob an armored truck and escape. It pops with your first vault.
  • 100%: all 8 large bank vaults count. See the 100% checklist.

Quick answers

How do you open a bank vault in GTA 6?

You can't open it with a code or explosives. Wait for an armored truck to stop at the bank: the guard carries a briefcase inside and opens the vault for a few seconds. That's your window.

When can you rob bank vaults in GTA 6?

In Chapter 4, after the optional mission That's Where The Money Is..., which walks you through the first one, according to PowerPyx.

How much does a bank vault pay in GTA 6?

GameSpot says $80,000 to $100,000 in marked bills from the vault alone, plus $25,000 to $50,000 if you also hit the drawers and the small safe. PowerPyx saw up to $205,000 from one bank.
