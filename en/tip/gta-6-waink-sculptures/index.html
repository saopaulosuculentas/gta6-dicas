All 23 WAiNK Sculpture Locations in GTA 6 for the You WAiNKer! Trophy

GTA 6 has 23 sculptures to scan with the WAiNK app, and every one counts toward the You WAiNKer! trophy. Here's where they are and how scanning works.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:51 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has 23 sculptures you can scan with the WAiNK app on your phone. The game’s own counter shows up after the first one, reading WAiNK Sculptures 1/23, and each one counts toward the You WAiNKer! trophy, which asks for 100 scans in total.

How scanning works

  1. Explore. When you get close to one, the game tells you there’s a WAiNK scan nearby.
  2. Hold Up on the D-pad to open the WAiNK app.
  3. Aim at the sculpture and scan with R1 (RB on Xbox).
  4. Your count goes up on screen.

That number is your tally, not the sculpture’s ID. The first one you scan becomes 1 of 23 no matter which it is, so you can grab them in whatever order suits your route.

The four in Mariana County (early game)

GameSpot found these four during the opening missions, close to Jason’s house:

Triangler Sculpture In the water at the meeting point for Go See Cal, northeast of Jason’s house. It’s a marlin
Grassrivers Visitor Center Statue An animal statue outside the Grassrivers Visitor Center in the Shoeless Keys

Don’t mix up the Monument of Leonida with the Monument of Leonidan Supremacy; they’re two separate sculptures. To find each area, use the map of regions and counties.

The You WAiNKer! trophy

The trophy wants 100 WAiNK scans. vAnother Level counts 108 in the game: 23 sculptures, 31 landmarks, 35 curiosities, 15 NoFlusher tags on the sidewalks and 4 billboards. That leaves eight spare if a couple of spots give you trouble. Landmarks and curiosities are covered in WAiNK curiosities and landmarks, and the rest of the trophies are in the trophy list.

There’s also a handy rumble cue for spotting scannables on foot, explained in GTA 6 hidden features.

Quick answers

How many WAiNK sculptures are in GTA 6?

There are 23. The counter pops up on screen with your first scan, reading WAiNK Sculptures 1/23.

How do you scan a sculpture in GTA 6?

When the game says a WAiNK scan is nearby, hold Up on the D-pad to open the app, aim at the sculpture and scan with R1.

Do WAiNK sculptures have to be scanned in order?

No. The number on screen is your running count, so whichever sculpture you scan first becomes 1 of 23.

How many scans does the You WAiNKer! trophy need?

One hundred. vAnother Level counted 108 scannables in the game, so you can skip a few awkward ones.
