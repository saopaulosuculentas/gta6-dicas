All GTA 6 Equipment and When It Unlocks: Snap Gun, Key Cloner, Dye Pack Jammer and More

GTA 6 has 12 pieces of equipment, like the Snap Gun, the Key Cloner and the Dye Pack Jammer. They all unlock through the story. Here's which mission gives you each one and what it does.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 25, 9:27 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has 12 pieces of equipment, and they all unlock through the story. They’re always on you, so there’s nothing to carry: you pick some from the item wheel, next to your weapons, and others kick in on their own when you interact with something, like the Key Cloner when you steal a sports car. The list is under Progress > Equipment.

The list comes from PowerPyx. GameSpot confirms the missions for the main ones.

All 12 items

Equipment When it unlocks What it does
Binoculars Chapter 1, Gone Fishing See far away and study animals (counts toward 100%)
Flashlight Chapter 1, Gone Fishing Light up dark places
Duffel Bag Chapter 1, Lab Fees Carry stolen goods. Used automatically
Headphones Buy them at an electronics store Listen to music on your phone
Crowbar Chapter 3, Meet the Heders Open crates. Used automatically
Masks In some story robberies; others at Ammu-Nation Hide your face
Metal Detector Chapter 1, Island Dreams (Kenton) Find treasure on the seabed
Slim Jim Chapter 2, Moses’s mission Open cars without setting off the alarm
Snap Gun Chapter 3, Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs Open locked doors without the alarm
Cut-off Tool Chapter 3, Meet the Heders Cut padlocks and open ATMs
Key Cloner Chapter 3, Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ GS Steal sports cars with an advanced tracker
Dye Pack Jammer Chapter 3, Credit Check Stop dye packs from ruining stolen cash

The ones that change the game most

  • Slim Jim: Moses gives it to you in Chapter 2. It opens older cars without the alarm. See how to sell and register cars and the Slim Jim and Snap Gun.
  • Snap Gun: gets you in through the back of stores without the alarm. According to GameSpot, it’s the missing piece for a clean robbery. See Yasmani’s jewelry store safe codes.
  • Key Cloner: opens up Randy’s list and the most expensive cars. See Randy’s rare cars.
  • Dye Pack Jammer: without it, cash from banks, Payday Loans and ATMs can blow up in pink dye and become worthless. After Credit Check, it stays active for good. See how to steal ATMs.
  • Cut-off Tool: opens the padlocks that need it, like the container holding one of the parts for Wyman’s classic cars.

Quick answers

How do you get the Snap Gun in GTA 6?

It unlocks in Chapter 3, in the Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs mission. Hand the first item to Yasmani and he gives you the Snap Gun, which opens doors without setting off the alarm.

How do you get the Key Cloner in GTA 6?

In Chapter 3, in the mission Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS, where you steal the Zorrusso for Randy. It lets you steal sports cars that have an advanced tracker.

Where do you get the Dye Pack Jammer in GTA 6?

It unlocks in Chapter 3, in the Credit Check mission with Raul's crew. It stops dye packs from ruining the cash you take from banks and ATMs.

Do you need to equip your equipment in GTA 6?

No. It's always on you. You pick some items from the item wheel, next to your weapons, and others kick in on their own when you use something in the world.
