How to Pet Dogs and Cats in GTA 6

To pet a dog or cat in GTA 6, walk up to it, press L2 or LT and praise it. Once you've praised it, a Pet option shows up, on Triangle on PS5.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:27 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

To pet a dog or cat in GTA 6, get close and press L2 (LT on Xbox). That brings up options to praise, scold and study the animal. Praise it, and a Pet option appears. GamesRadar+ laid out the steps, and icePlaysGTA showed the whole thing working on a Labrador on PS5.

Step by step on PS5

  1. Walk up to a dog or cat. Unlike people, animals don’t show a button prompt before you engage them, which is why a lot of players miss this.
  2. Press L2. Praise, Scold and Study pop up in the corner of the screen, with the animal type listed next to L2.
  3. Press X to praise it.
  4. Praise drops off the list and Pet takes its place on Triangle. Press it and your character crouches down to give the animal some love.

GamesRadar+ warns that it can take a few rounds of praise, and the pet option doesn’t always show up. If it gets stuck, keep praising or try a different animal.

Option PS5 button What it does

Study R1 Adds the animal to your collection

Study and the animal collection

According to GamesRadar+, Study is the only way to add an animal to your collection. In icePlaysGTA’s clip, the game simply labeled the animal “Dog” before the study. Afterward it showed the breed: Labrador Retriever.

You can study from a distance too. Dndo showed the binoculars method: aim at the animal, zoom in until you can see it clearly, then hold R1 (RB on Xbox) until the circle fills. That’s how he logged a bird.

In the animals list in the menu, every entry tracks how many times you’ve praised, petted, fed and scolded it, how many you’ve killed and how often you’ve been attacked by one. Dndo showed those stats on screen.

Which animals you can pet

  • Domestic animals only: dogs and cats. Wildlife won’t let you.
  • There are different breeds out there, so it’s worth studying every dog and cat you run into.
  • The one exception is a single alligator you can pet, at the yard sale just around the corner from Jason’s house.

Animals to find early

  • The Mini Mart cat: a cat that hangs out at the Key Lento Mini Mart in Chapter 1. GamesRadar+ calls it one of the cutest cats it has seen in a game.
  • Argula, Shirley’s dog: Jason’s neighbor Shirley has a dog. GamesRadar+ says petting it counts as a thoughtful action that can raise your focus bar, depending on your criminal profile.
  • The yard sale gator, near Jason’s house.

Petting, focus and Lucia

Petting animals is one of the small things that feed your focus. The rest are in how to increase focus in GTA 6, and the wild animals you can hunt or catch are in GTA 6 fishing and hunting.

GamesRadar+ also slips in a tip for anyone trying to stay on Lucia’s good side: stop at the Mini Mart and buy groceries before you pick her up from jail. You’ll meet the store cat on the way. More on that in the Jason and Lucia relationship.

Quick answers

How do you pet a dog in GTA 6?

Walk up to it, press L2 (LT on Xbox) and praise it. After the praise, a Pet option appears, mapped to Triangle on PS5.

Why can't I pet animals in GTA 6?

The Pet option only shows up after you praise the animal, and sometimes not even then. It also only works on domestic animals like dogs and cats.

Can you pet an alligator in GTA 6?

Just one: the gator at the yard sale around the corner from Jason's house. Wild alligators can't be petted.
