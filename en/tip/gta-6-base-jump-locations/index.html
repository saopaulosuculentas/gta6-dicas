All GTA 6 Base Jump Locations and How to Complete Them

GTA 6 has 25 base jump challenges, mostly on Vice City skyscrapers. Here are the spots found so far and how to land in the ring without failing.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:30 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

There are 25 base jump challenges in GTA 6, according to GameSpot, and most of them start from the roof of a Vice City skyscraper. A few are outside the city, like the one on the tallest radio tower on the map. Only four spots have been shown for certain so far, and nobody has published the full list yet.

Finding a base jump

  • Look for the purple parachute icon on the map. Base jumps also show up under the Exploration tab of the index.
  • Each one has a free parachute sitting between two green cones, with a few energy drinks on the ground.
  • For skyscrapers, walk in on the ground floor and ride the elevator to the roof. The jump often starts from the helipad.
  • Once you’re up there, the game tells you to equip a parachute and to use Focus (L3 + R3) to spot the cones that mark the jump-off point. Both Typical Gamer and ZacCoxTV get that prompt on camera.

How to complete one

  1. Put on a parachute, either your own or the free one at the spot.
  2. Run to the edge between the two cones and press X (A on Xbox) to jump.
  3. Press X (A) again in mid-air to open the canopy.
  4. Steer with both sticks and fly through the pink rings in the air.
  5. To slow down for a tight ring, hold X (A). The canopy flares and kills your speed for a moment. You can’t hold it forever, but it buys enough time to line up the next checkpoint.
  6. At the end there’s a big pink ring on the ground. When you’re right above it, press Triangle (Y) to drop the parachute and land on your feet.
  7. Finish under the par time.

If the run goes wrong, hold Square (X on Xbox) to restart from the top.

What makes a jump fail

  • Touching down with the canopy open. It counts as a fail even inside the ring. Cut away with Triangle (Y).
  • Cutting away too high. The landing only counts if the drop is short.
  • Clipping the structure. The radio tower course loops around the tower itself, and Typical Gamer’s run ended when he flew into the cables.
  • Going over the par time.

Quick answers

How many base jumps are in GTA 6?

There are 25 base jump challenges, according to GameSpot. Most sit on top of Vice City skyscrapers, with a few outside the city.

How do you land a base jump in GTA 6?

When you're right above the pink ring on the ground, press Triangle (Y on Xbox) to cut away the parachute. Floating all the way down with the canopy open fails the challenge.

Do you need your own parachute for base jumps in GTA 6?

No. Every base jump has a free parachute waiting at the jump-off point, between the two cones.

How do you find base jumps on the GTA 6 map?

Look for the purple parachute icon. Base jumps are also listed under the Exploration tab of the index.
