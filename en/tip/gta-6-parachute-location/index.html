GTA 6 Parachute Location: Where to Buy One, Free Spots and How to Use It

In GTA 6 you buy a parachute once at an Ammu-Nation that sells clothes (shirt icon on the map). Free ones sit on some skyscraper roofs and on the highest peak of Mount Kalaga.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 10:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

In GTA 6 you only buy a parachute once, at an Ammu-Nation that sells clothing. You can also find free ones on top of some buildings. PC Gamer explained where to get one and how it works.

Where to buy one

  • At Ammu-Nation stores that sell clothes. On the map, look for the Ammu-Nation with the shirt icon.
  • The parachute hangs on a rack inside the store.

Where to find one for free

  • Skyscraper roofs: some have a box of parachutes up top.
  • Mount Kalaga: the tallest peak in Mount Kalaga National Park has a parachute at the top. It’s the hard way, but it makes for a nice trip.

How to use it

  1. Jump out of a plane mid-flight or off a tall building.
  2. In freefall, press the on-screen prompt to pull the chute.
  3. Steer left and right. Lean forward to drop faster or pull back to drop slower.

What to know

  • Reset it after use. You only pay once, but after a jump you have to go back to a gun store and pick it off the rack again to add it to your inventory. Or grab one from a rooftop.
  • Use it to escape. If the police shoot your plane down, bail out and pull the chute. See the GTA 6 airport.
  • After the story there’s a parachute cheat, unlocked with 50 skydives or base jumps from 300 feet or higher.

Quick answers

Where do you get a parachute in GTA 6?

Buy one at an Ammu-Nation that sells clothes, marked with a shirt icon on the map. It hangs on a rack in the store.

Are there free parachutes in GTA 6?

Yes. Some skyscrapers have a box of parachutes on the roof, and there's one at the top of the tallest peak in Mount Kalaga National Park.

Is there a parachute cheat in GTA 6?

Yes. It unlocks after 50 skydives or base jumps from 300 feet or higher, after beating the story. The sequence hasn't been published yet.
