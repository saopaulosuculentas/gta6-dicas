Is Niko Bellic in GTA 6? The Chapter and Mission Where He Appears

Yes. GTA 4's Niko Bellic has a cameo near the end of GTA 6's story: Chapter 5, Top of the World, Ma, in the mission Taking Liberties. His original voice actor is back.

Confirmed · 4 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:39 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Spoiler warning: this page tells you where in the story Niko shows up. It doesn’t say what happens in the scene.

Yes, Niko Bellic, the hero of GTA 4, is in GTA 6. He makes a cameo in Chapter 5, Top of the World, Ma, during the mission Taking Liberties, according to PC Gamer. That’s the last chapter, so you won’t see him until you’re near the end of the story.

Where and when

What Detail
Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma
Mission Taking Liberties
Timing The last stretch of the story
Cutscenes Two, according to Kotaku

In the mission list, Taking Liberties is the 2nd mission of Chapter 5. If you’re still early on, you have a long way to go: Chapter 5 is short, but it comes after almost the whole campaign. For a sense of the hours, see how long it takes to beat GTA 6.

What Niko looks like now

GTA 4 came out in 2008, and Niko is back a lot older. Kotaku and ZacCoxTV’s video both show him with grey hair in the mission’s cutscenes. The channel reacts to footage captured by another player and shows both moments where he appears.

It’s a story cameo. None of the sources say you get to play as Niko.

The original voice actor is back

Niko is voiced once again by Michael Hollick, the actor from GTA 4. Fans got suspicious before anyone reached Chapter 5: his name was on the cast list Rockstar shared, credited only as “The Hitman”. Kotaku and GamesRadar+ confirmed that role is Niko, hidden in the credits to protect the surprise.

GamesRadar+ points out that Hollick had complained years ago about his pay for GTA 4, which is part of why his return turned heads. The rest of the cast is in GTA 6 characters and voice actors.

Real or fake?

It’s real. When the first clips went around, plenty of people assumed they were edited. Kotaku played that far and confirmed the scene is in the game. More nods to older Rockstar games are in the easter eggs list.

Quick answers

What mission is Niko Bellic in GTA 6?

Taking Liberties, in Chapter 5 (Top of the World, Ma), close to the end of the story.

Who voices Niko in GTA 6?

Michael Hollick, the same actor from GTA 4. The credits list him only as The Hitman to keep the surprise.

Does Niko show up early in GTA 6?

No. The cameo is in the final chapter, so you have to get through most of the story first.
