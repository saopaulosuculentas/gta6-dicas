GTA 6 Crashing or Stuck on Loading Screen: How to Fix

If GTA 6 crashes, freezes or won't load, check PlayStation or Xbox server status and restart the game first. On PS5, restore your licenses; on Xbox, fully power cycle the console.

Confirmed · 6 sourcesUpdated Nov 23, 8:05 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

If GTA 6 is crashing, freezing or stuck on a loading screen, start simple: check whether PlayStation Network or Xbox services are down, then restart the game. If that doesn’t do it, the official fix on PS5 is to restore your licenses, and on Xbox it’s a full power cycle, holding the console’s power button for 10 seconds.

Rockstar hasn’t published its own list of GTA 6 errors. Its support page points console errors to Sony’s and Microsoft’s help pages, so every step below comes from those official pages.

First, make sure the game is updated

GTA 6 got its first patch on November 23. According to Rockstar’s notes, as reported by Kotaku, it fixes several issues that made the game crash, along with autosave problems and phone calls that never came through. If your game keeps closing on its own, check that the update is installed before you work through the steps below. The full list is in GTA 6 patch notes.

Step one: rule out an outage

  • PS5: check the PlayStation status page. If there’s an alert, wait for the service to come back before trying again.
  • Xbox: check the Xbox status page and the known issues list for new game launches.
  • GTA 6 unlocked at midnight local time on November 19. Before that, it won’t start even if it’s fully installed.

PS5: won’t launch or license problems

  1. Use the right account. If the profile you’re on didn’t buy the game, the account that owns it needs Console Sharing and Offline Play turned on for that PS5: Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Console Sharing and Offline Play > Enable.
  2. Restore licenses. Go to Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Restore Licenses and pick the ones to restore. You can also highlight the game, press Options and choose Restore License.
  3. Download didn’t finish? Follow the next section.

PS5: stuck download or install errors

  1. Open Downloads/Uploads in the control center, select the game, then hit Pause and Resume.
  2. Still stuck? Select Cancel and Delete, then download it again from your Game Library.
  3. Update your system software: Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings.
  4. Check your free space. In the Game Library, open the game, go to “…” > View Product and the required space shows on the right. Rockstar says it can take up to 212 GB.
  5. As a last step, boot the PS5 in Safe Mode and choose Clear Cache and Rebuild Database.

Xbox: freezing, crashing or won’t start

Xbox Support lays out this order:

  1. Restart the game. Press the Xbox button, highlight the game, press Menu and choose Quit. Then launch it again.
  2. Power cycle. Hold the Xbox button on the front of the console for at least 10 seconds. Turn it back on, and repeat if you don’t see the green boot animation. This doesn’t erase games or data.
  3. Test your network: Profile & system > Settings > General > Network settings > Test network connection.
  4. Try another profile. If the game works there, remove yours (Settings > Account > Remove accounts) and add it back.
  5. Last resort: delete the local save and resync from the cloud, under Manage game and add-ons > Saved data. Xbox says saves are stored in the cloud while you’re connected, but only do this after everything else.

Your boxed code won’t redeem

The boxed version of GTA 6 only has a download code inside. Rockstar says PS5 codes are locked to the region of your PlayStation account, so buy from a store in your account’s country. Xbox codes aren’t region-locked. If you’re told the code was already used, check your order history and make sure it wasn’t redeemed on another account.

You got an error code

Rockstar’s support page sends you to Sony’s PS5 error code search and Microsoft’s Xbox error code search. Type in the exact code on your screen to get the matching fix.

PS5: error CE-108255-1

A number of PS5 players are hitting this one. GamesRadar+ found more than a dozen Reddit threads about it, and here’s what they describe:

  • the game crashes after specific story scenes early on, then crashes again at the same point, so you’re stuck;
  • it shows up on base PS5 consoles as well as Digital and Slim models;
  • clearing the cache, rebuilding the database, reinstalling and restoring licenses didn’t help anyone in those reports;
  • Sony’s page for the code only says to update the game, then contact PlayStation Repairs if that fails. As of November 19 there was no GTA 6 patch to download;
  • the first patch arrived on November 23 with several crash fixes, but the notes don’t mention this code. Kotaku says it’s unclear whether it’s been resolved.

The article itself spells the code two ways (CE-108255-1 in the headline, CE-108225-1 in the body), so check the exact number on your screen before you search. What we know so far is on our CE-108255-1 error page.

Frame drops aren’t a bug

Losing a few frames in a big chase or shootout is normal in GTA 6. Digital Foundry measured dips on every console, and more often on Xbox Series S. See GTA 6 FPS and performance for the numbers.

Who to contact

PlayStation splits it like this: download, install, launch and code problems go to PlayStation Support, while in-game bugs and patches go to Rockstar Support. Xbox’s own help page also sends GTA 6 questions to Rockstar.

Before you reinstall, read up on how autosave works and how to save manually so you know where your progress lives.

Quick answers

What do I do if GTA 6 won't launch on PS5?

Make sure you're on the account that bought it, then go to Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Restore Licenses. If the download failed, delete it and download again from your Game Library.

How do I fix GTA 6 freezing on Xbox?

Quit the game from the guide and restart it. If that doesn't work, hold the Xbox button on the console for 10 seconds to fully power cycle it.

Why won't my GTA 6 code work?

PS5 codes are tied to the region of your PlayStation account, according to Rockstar. Xbox codes aren't region-locked. If it says the code was already used, check your order history.

Does GTA 6 need the internet to play?

No. PlayStation lists offline play as enabled, and Xbox Support has a guide for playing single-player games offline.
