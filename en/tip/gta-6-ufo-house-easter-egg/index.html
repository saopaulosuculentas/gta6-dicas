GTA 6 UFO House Easter Egg: Where the San Andreas House Is and How to Get In

The UFO house Rockstar teased in the San Andreas remaster is in GTA 6, in Bellville, Leonard County. The front door is fake, but you can break in through the garage.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 4:28 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The UFO house is in GTA 6. Back in 2021, Rockstar slipped an image of a house with a flying saucer over it into the GTA San Andreas remaster, and fans bet it was a hint at the next game. ZacCoxTV found that same house in Bellville, Leonard County, and Milo got inside too, holding it up against the 2021 image. The front door is fake, but the garage opens, and whoever lives there is deep into conspiracy theories.

Where it is

  • Region: Leonard County, north of Vice City.
  • Town: Bellville, a small neighborhood of houses with white picket fences.
  • The house: yellow, with a double garage and a for-sale sign in the yard. There’s usually a car parked out front.

The map is huge, so ZacCoxTV recommends flying in by helicopter. From the air you can also spot a rude joke a neighbor mowed into his lawn.

How to get in

  1. Head to the house in Bellville.
  2. The front door and windows don’t open. Go to the garage.
  3. Get close and the game shows a break in prompt. Break into the garage and walk in.

Breaking in calls the cops. ZacCoxTV got a wanted level right away and was busted on his first try. There’s also a dog in the yard and a snake in the garage that attacked him. Save before you go.

The car out front

Milo found one more scare: pop the trunk of the car parked at the house and a huge snake comes out. That was all he found in there, so open it carefully.

What’s inside

The garage is a paranoid neighbor’s hideout:

  • Conspiracy boards: clippings about a woman who walks through walls, “America’s most abducted man” and the Skunk Ape. Milo also found a newspaper story about that woman.
  • A Skunk Ape magazine: Milo found it lying on the bed. It’s about the creature that has its own mission in the game (see the Skunk Ape mission).
  • Radio and food: a radio and snacks you can pick up, including a meteorite-themed candy bar.
  • A piece of meteorite: you can grab it and sell it. In the game, a buyer on the Lifeinvader marketplace says they’ll pay for what you find.
  • A collectible card: a card from The Legends of Ratface set, which counts toward the game’s card collection.

ZacCoxTV didn’t find anything else on his first visit. The clippings could be hinting at other secrets, but so far nobody has shown an actual UFO connection. Another UFO hunter’s hideout sits in the watchtower shed on Mount Kalaga, behind a keypad; see the easter eggs list.

Why it’s an easter egg

The UFO house image showed up in the San Andreas remaster in 2021. Finding that exact house in GTA 6, covered in abduction clippings, is Rockstar landing the joke five years later.

Quick answers

Where is the UFO house in GTA 6?

In Bellville, a small town in Leonard County north of Vice City. It's a yellow house with a double garage and a for-sale sign.

Can you go inside the GTA 6 UFO house?

Not through the front door. ZacCoxTV got in by breaking into the garage, where the owner keeps a conspiracy hideout.

What's inside the UFO house?

A conspiracy den: corkboards full of clippings, a Skunk Ape magazine, a radio, snacks and a collectible card.
