GTA 6 UFO Location: How to See the Abduction (The Truth Is Out There Trophy)

The GTA 6 UFO shows up after 2 a.m. in the woods east of Port Gellhorn, Kelly County. If a car with blinking hazard lights is parked there, the abduction plays out and you get the trophy.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 4:52 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The GTA 6 UFO shows up late at night, after 2 a.m., in a stretch of woods east of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County. If you arrive and there’s a car with blinking hazard lights parked on the road, the scene plays out: a woman gets abducted, you help her, and in the end you get taken too. That’s how you earn The Truth Is Out There trophy, a nod to The X-Files.

Where and when

  • Location: east of Port Gellhorn, along a road that cuts through the woods. PowerPyx marks the exact spot on the map in his guide.
  • Time: after 2:00 a.m. You can sleep at home to skip ahead.
  • Solo: PowerPyx says to go solo, playing as one character.

The sign: the blinking car

According to PowerPyx, the event is random:

  • Blinking car is there: the UFO will show up.
  • Car isn’t there: drive away and come back later. Repeat until it appears.

What happens

  1. Near the car you’ll find strange clues: a dropped bag, stones laid out like a ritual, clothes on the ground and a flashing phone.
  2. Head into the woods following the blue light. You’ll see a woman being pulled up into the beam.
  3. She comes back dazed and asks you to bring her car.
  4. It isn’t over: your character gets abducted next, and the screen goes white.
  5. You wake up somewhere else and the The Truth Is Out There trophy pops.

ZacCoxTV woke up the next day. Milo says the event is rare and only happens at night in northern Kelly County.

Quick answers

Where is the UFO in GTA 6?

In a stretch of woods east of Port Gellhorn, Kelly County. Go after 2 a.m.: if a car with blinking hazard lights is parked on the road, the abduction will happen.

How do you get The Truth Is Out There trophy in GTA 6?

Watch the abduction scene all the way through. Follow the light into the woods, help the abducted woman, and then you get taken too. The trophy pops right after.

The UFO didn't show up. What now?

PowerPyx says it's random: if the blinking car isn't there, drive away and come back later, always after 2 a.m.

What happens after you get abducted in GTA 6?

The screen goes white and you wake up somewhere else. ZacCoxTV woke up the next day.
