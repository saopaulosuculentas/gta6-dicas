GTA 6 UFO Location: How to See the Abduction (The Truth Is Out There Trophy)

The GTA 6 UFO shows up at night in the woods east of Port Gellhorn, Kelly County, once Chapter 1 is done. If a car with blinking lights is parked there, the abduction plays out and the trophy pops.

Confirmed · 6 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:45 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The GTA 6 UFO shows up at night (PowerPyx and GameSpot say after 2 a.m.) in a stretch of woods east of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County. If you arrive and there’s a car with blinking hazard lights parked on the road, the scene plays out: a woman gets abducted, you help her, and in the end you get taken too. That’s how you earn The Truth Is Out There trophy, a nod to The X-Files. It’s also the first step of a longer chain that continues with the alien dream and the secret alien lab.

Where and when

  • Unlocks: GameSpot says it’s only available after you finish Chapter 1.
  • Location: east of Port Gellhorn, along a road that cuts through the woods. PowerPyx marks the exact spot on the map in his guide. GameSpot describes it as northwest of the Starlet Motel in Kelly County, and InkTV found the car close to the building where Chapter 2 begins.
  • Time: PowerPyx and GameSpot both say after 2:00 a.m. InkTV, though, got there at about 11:30 p.m. and the UFO still showed up. If nothing happens before midnight, wait until after 2 a.m. You can sleep at home to skip ahead.
  • Solo: PowerPyx and GameSpot both say to go alone, playing as one character.

The sign: the blinking car

According to PowerPyx, the event is random:

  • Blinking car is there: the UFO will show up.
  • Car isn’t there: drive away and come back later. Repeat until it appears.

What happens

  1. Near the car you’ll find strange clues: a dropped bag, stones laid out like a ritual, clothes on the ground and a flashing phone. GameSpot notes the car faces a trail heading into the woods.
  2. Head into the woods following the blue light. You’ll see a woman being pulled up into the beam.
  3. She comes back dazed and asks you to bring her car. In InkTV’s video the game marked the car as an objective, and he got taken before he could drive it back.
  4. It isn’t over: your character gets abducted next, and the screen goes white.
  5. You wake up somewhere else and the The Truth Is Out There trophy pops.

ZacCoxTV woke up the next day. In InkTV’s video, Lucia calls Jason afterwards and tells him he’s been gone for about three days. Milo says the event is rare and only happens at night in northern Kelly County.

After the abduction: the dream and the lab

The abduction is only the start. According to GameSpot, some time later the character who was taken has a dream while asleep. The dream shows the code for a secret lab as light bars, plus a dry run of an alien rescue.

  1. Learn how to read the code in the alien dream guide. It isn’t the same in every playthrough.
  2. Then head to the secret alien lab at South Point Airfield in Hamlet and free the alien.

The trophy can pop elsewhere too

The Truth Is Out There hasn’t only unlocked after the abduction:

  • Milo got it right after watching ghostly figures fight at the top of a lighthouse at night, with no abduction in the video.
  • Robbin Rams got it while opening the safe in the haunted hotel.

Nobody has worked out the rule yet. The trophy may cover more than one of the game’s supernatural mysteries.

More alien stuff in GTA 6

  • The alien dream, which follows the abduction and gives you the lab code.
  • The secret alien lab, with an alien locked inside.
  • The San Andreas UFO house in Bellville.
  • More in the GTA 6 easter eggs list.

Quick answers

Where is the UFO in GTA 6?

In a stretch of woods east of Port Gellhorn, Kelly County; GameSpot places it northwest of the Starlet Motel. Go at night after finishing Chapter 1: if a car with blinking lights is parked on the road, the abduction will happen.

How do you get The Truth Is Out There trophy in GTA 6?

Watch the abduction scene all the way through. Follow the light into the woods, help the abducted woman, and then you get taken too. The trophy pops right after.

The UFO didn't show up. What now?

PowerPyx says it's random: if the blinking car isn't there, drive away and come back later, always after 2 a.m.

What happens after you get abducted in GTA 6?

The screen goes white and you wake up somewhere else, sometimes days later. Later on, the abducted character has a dream that reveals the code for the secret alien lab.
