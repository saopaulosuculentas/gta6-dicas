GTA 6 Autosave Explained: When the Game Saves and How to Avoid Losing Progress

GTA 6 only autosaves after story missions and bigger side missions. Small errands and free roam aren't saved on their own, so save manually from the Game menu before you switch off.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 4:10 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 does autosave, but only after story missions and larger side missions, based on Kotaku’s testing. Whatever you get up to between missions, like holding up a store or running small errands, isn’t saved for you. So before you turn the console off, save manually.

Plenty of players learned this the hard way. Kotaku reports that people who spent their last 10, 15 or even 60 minutes on optional activities, then shut down for the night, woke up to find none of it had stuck.

When the game saves on its own

  • After a main story mission. In Kotaku’s test, stealing a car from the impound lot for Cal and getting away from the cops, as part of the story, triggered a save.
  • After a larger side mission.

When it doesn’t

  • Small favors. Fetching rum for your neighbor didn’t save the game.
  • Free roam after a mission, such as robbing a liquor store or doing errands.
  • Mid-mission, ever. Anything between starting and finishing a mission is lost if the console turns off or the power goes out.

How to save manually

  1. Pause the game and open the menu.
  2. Go to the Game section.
  3. Pick the save option. Loading lives in the same place.

Our full saving guide covers save slots and the rest.

When saving is blocked

The menu won’t let you save while you are:

  • in the middle of a mission;
  • in combat;
  • being chased by the police;
  • on the move, for example jumping off a bridge.

Before calling it a night, finish the mission, shake the cops, and stand still somewhere calm. Then save.

Reports of lost progress

There are open questions about whether saving always works. Kotaku pointed to two cases, neither of them confirmed:

  • A manual save that didn’t stick. One player said on X that he found around $10,000 in bagged stash money, saved by hand, then reloaded later and was back at his house without the cash or a collectible card he’d picked up. He and others suspect a bug.
  • 11 hours gone. Streamer JasonTheWeen says he accidentally switched off his PS5 during a stream and lost 11 hours of progress, as IGN reported. Kotaku says it’s unclear how that happened. He didn’t seem to be saving manually, so it may have been too much faith in autosave, a fault in the feature itself, or both.

Quick checklist

  • Don’t rely on autosave alone.
  • Save manually every so often, and always before you quit.
  • After saving, make sure it went through before you turn the console off.

Also remember that cash in your pocket disappears when you die. Our guide on keeping your money safe shows where to stash it, and the beginner tips cover other early mistakes.

Quick answers

Does GTA 6 have autosave?

Yes, but it only kicks in after story missions and larger side missions. Small errands and free roam activity aren't saved automatically.

Does GTA 6 save during a mission?

No. According to Kotaku, nothing between starting and finishing a mission is kept if the console shuts off.

When can't you save in GTA 6?

During missions, in combat, while the cops are chasing you, or while your character is moving, like mid-jump off a bridge. Stop somewhere quiet and save.
