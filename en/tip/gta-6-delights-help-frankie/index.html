GTA 6 Delights Mission: Should You Help Frankie or Not?

In the Chapter 2 Delights robbery, Frankie asks Jason if he can dance. Say yes or no, you still rob the club: the choice only changes the scene before the heist.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 20, 10:27 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

It makes no difference to the heist: help Frankie or turn him down, you still rob the Delights. The choice only changes the scene that plays out before the robbery. That’s PC Gamer’s finding after going through both versions of the Chapter 2 mission.

Heads up: this page covers one mission choice from Chapter 2 and nothing else from the story.

Where and when

  • Chapter 2, in Port Gellhorn.
  • Jason and Lucia are helping Vicky rob her boss Frankie, who runs the Delights club.
  • In PC Gamer’s mission list, the Chapter 2 mission where Jason and Lucia visit a nightclub is called Afternoon Delight. The full order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

Frankie’s question

Jason slips in through the club’s back door while he and Lucia set up the robbery, and runs straight into Frankie. There’s a show that night and one of the dancers can’t go on. Mario, the lead dancer, takes one look at Jason and gets an idea. Frankie has the same one and asks, in a hurry, whether Jason can dance.

You get only a couple of seconds to answer, so make up your mind before you reach that point.

What each answer changes

Answer What happens The heist
Help Jason steps in for the missing dancer and joins the show on stage, with Lucia watching from the crowd Goes ahead as normal
Don’t help Mario walks off and the show is canceled. Frankie tries to work up the crowd by himself until Jason fires a shot into the ceiling and announces the robbery Goes ahead as normal

Which one to pick

PC Gamer doesn’t mention any consequence beyond the scene itself: both paths end with you robbing the place. The outlet thinks both are worth seeing, but helping is the funnier one, mostly because of Lucia’s reaction to seeing Jason up there.

Want to see both? GTA 6 doesn’t let you replay a finished mission, so make a manual save before you head into the club, as covered in how to replay missions in GTA 6.

Another story choice people keep asking about comes in Chapter 1: kill or spare Arnold.

Quick answers

Should you help Frankie in the GTA 6 Delights mission?

It doesn't matter for the heist, which happens either way. PC Gamer found the version where you help a lot more fun.

What happens if you don't help Frankie in GTA 6?

The show gets canceled, Frankie tries to work the crowd on his own, and Jason cuts him off with a shot into the ceiling to announce the robbery. The heist carries on as normal.

What chapter is the Delights mission in?

Chapter 2, in Port Gellhorn, when Jason and Lucia help Vicky rob her boss, Frankie.
