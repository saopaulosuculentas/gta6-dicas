All GTA 6 Fence Locations: Where to Sell Stolen Goods

GTA 6 has 5 fences for drugs, jewelry and marked bills, plus 2 car buyers. Each fence pays best for a different item, and some only pay after you leave.

Confirmed · 9 sourcesUpdated Nov 23, 8:05 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

In GTA 6, the stuff you steal doesn’t turn into money on its own. Drugs, jewelry and marked bills have to go to a fence, who swaps them for clean cash. There are 5 fences for goods and 2 car buyers, and each one unlocks during a story mission.

The game spells it out: when you pick up marked bills, a prompt tells you to take them to a drop-off to sell them for usable cash.

Every fence

What each robbery gives you

GamesRadar+ broke down what each robbery type in the index pays out:

Robbery type What you get
Illegal Operations Jewelry, marked bills, narcotics and pocket cash
Smash ‘n’ Grabs Jewelry, marked bills and pocket cash
Bank Jobs and Payday Loans Jewelry, marked bills and pocket cash

Pocket cash is yours right away. Everything else has to go through a fence.

How to sell

  1. Lose the cops first. PC Gamer’s advice is to sell only after a clean getaway.
  2. Go to any fence, any time of day.
  3. Open the index, go to the Robberies tab and scroll to Drop-offs - Goods: those are the fences you’ve unlocked so far. In Chapter 1 that’s just Brian and Donnie.
  4. Hover over each fence’s name to see who pays most for drugs, jewelry and marked bills. It pays to match the load to the fence.

Brian’s lockup code

Brian’s lockup in South Key Lento has a keypad on the door. The code is 7700: KhrazeGaming, DooDooPoo and JoblessGarrett all used the same one. The game remembers it for you after the first time. Inside, drop your bags in the container and choose to sell. DooDooPoo sold one full stash bag for $10,000.

How to sell at Donnie’s

Donnie is the second fence you get in Chapter 1, out of his trailer in Key Lento. FuriousFade brought two full bags from a warehouse and walked through it:

  1. Pull up near Donnie’s trailer. Back the car in so the bags come out of the trunk easily.
  2. The bags go in the old toolbox next to the trailer. Donnie picks them up later.
  3. Before you confirm, the prompt already shows how much you’ll get. In his video it read “Sell stash bags (+$11,600)” for drugs and marked bills together.
  4. You can’t split it: FuriousFade noticed the game sells the drugs and the marked bills in one go.
  5. Soon after, Donnie texts you that the money is in your mailbox. Swing by home to collect it.

Donnie or Brian? BennyCentral compared both on the map before selling a warehouse bag: Donnie’s trailer paid $800 more than Brian’s lockup for the same load, and the bag went for about $10,000. It’s worth checking both prices before you pick, as covered in “How to sell” above. That warehouse is in the early money warehouse.

Cash now or cash later

Not every fence pays on the spot:

  • Pay right away: Tyrone (at his apartment) and Yasmani (at his shop).
  • Pay later: Brian (his lockup), Donnie (the drop box at his trailer) and Dunk (the trunk of his car). You drop off the goods and the money shows up later in your mailbox. GamesRadar+ says some fences can take several in-game days.

Two shortcuts

  • Send your partner: hand the goods to Jason or Lucia (whoever you’re not playing) and tell them to fence it. They take care of it, but you get a bit less money.
  • Garage pickup: after two Payday Loan jobs for Yasmani, you can leave stolen goods in one of your garages and someone comes to collect them. That convenience comes with an extra fee.

Partner wouldn’t hand over the bags?

That was a bug. At launch, your partner could hang on to the bags instead of passing them to you, which left you with nothing to sell to the fence. GTA 6’s first patch, released on November 23, fixes it, according to Rockstar’s notes as reported by Kotaku. If it happened to you, update the game. See everything else the patch changed.

Why it matters

Robbing and fencing is one of the fastest ways to make money early on. See the rest in how to make money in GTA 6, and for robberies with a safe, the safe codes guide.

Quick answers

Where do you sell drugs and jewelry in GTA 6?

At a fence. The first one is Brian in Key Lento, unlocked in the Chapter 1 mission Lab Fees. Donnie, Dunk, Tyrone and Yasmani come later.

What do you do with marked bills in GTA 6?

Take them to a fence or drop-off. Marked bills can't be spent until they're swapped for clean cash.

Where do you sell stolen cars in GTA 6?

To Moses in Port Gellhorn, from Chapter 2. High-end cars go to Randy in Balsero, Vice City, in Chapter 3.
