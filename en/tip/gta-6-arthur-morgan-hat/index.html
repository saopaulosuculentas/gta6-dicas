Where to Find Arthur Morgan's Hat in GTA 6 and How Much It Costs

Arthur Morgan's hat from Red Dead Redemption 2 is for sale at a yard sale in Port Gellhorn, Kelly County. In game it's the Genuine Cowboy Hat, and it costs $35 on screen.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:47 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Arthur Morgan’s hat from Red Dead Redemption 2 is for sale at a yard sale in Port Gellhorn, in Kelly County. In GTA 6 it’s called the Genuine Cowboy Hat, and it costs $35 in IGN’s footage of the purchase.

Where the yard sale is

  • Area: Port Gellhorn, Kelly County. IGN puts the house right next to Wild Hog Point, roughly 8 miles northwest of Jason and Lucia’s house.
  • The house: blue, with a tall American flag right out front. According to IGN, it’s the only house on the block with a yard sale, so it’s hard to miss.
  • Getting there: PC Gamer stumbled on it while driving around Port Gellhorn. Along the road there are “Yard Sale” signs with balloons tied to them and arrows. Follow them and they lead you straight to the sale.
  • Landmark: a Ferris wheel is visible in the background on the hat’s purchase screen.

IGN says the yard sale only shows up from Chapter 2 onward. Before that, you’ll just find a regular house there. For help reading the map, see GTA 6 regions and counties.

How to buy it

  1. Show up with at least $35 on you.
  2. The hat sits on one of the tables. Walk up to it to get the prompt to look at it.
  3. On the item screen, choose to buy it or to buy and wear it straight away.

$35 or $5?

PC Gamer wrote that it only paid $5. In IGN’s video, though, the tag clearly reads $35, and IGN tells you to bring that much. Carry $35 so the trip isn’t wasted.

Why it’s Arthur’s hat

IGN’s video puts the hat side by side with a shot of Arthur in Red Dead Redemption 2: same brown leather, same braided band around the crown. IGN’s take is that it’s just missing some wear and tear. PC Gamer says that seen from the back, there’s no mistaking it.

Who can wear it

In IGN’s footage, Lucia is the one who tries it on and buys it. None of the sources show Jason wearing it. Other clothing and accessory shops are in mask, clothes, tattoo and barber shops, and more nods to other Rockstar games are in the easter eggs list.

Quick answers

Where is Arthur Morgan's hat in GTA 6?

At a yard sale in Port Gellhorn, Kelly County, next to Wild Hog Point. The house is blue with a tall American flag out front.

How much is Arthur's hat in GTA 6?

IGN's video shows a $35 price tag. PC Gamer mentioned $5, but bring $35 to be safe.

What is Arthur Morgan's hat called in GTA 6?

Genuine Cowboy Hat, in brown leather.

Can Jason wear Arthur Morgan's hat?

Unknown so far. In the footage, Lucia is the one who buys and wears it.
