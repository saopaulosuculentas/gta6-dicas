GTA 6 Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs Safe Codes: Casa de Empeño, Richardson and Donovan

In Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs, a Chapter 3 mission, each jewelry store has a safe with a fixed code: 1680, 6815 and 0104. Here's where each safe is, the best order and what Yasmani wants.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 25, 4:58 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The safe codes for Yasmani’s jewelry stores in GTA 6 are 1680 (Casa de Empeño), 6815 (Richardson Jewelry) and 0104 (Donovan Banks & Co.). They’re part of the Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs mission in Chapter 3, and they’re fixed: they work for everyone.

That’s different from regular robberies. At any ordinary store or bank, the code is randomized and changes even if you rob the same place twice, according to GameSpot. See how to find safe codes for those.

The three codes

Store Code Where the safe is What Yasmani wants
Casa de Empeño 1680 Small back office Buffalo skull chain
Richardson Jewelry 6815 Northeast corner of the center room Diamond ring
Donovan Banks & Co. 0104 Halfway down the long back hallway Amethyst necklace

At Donovan, type the leading zero: 0104.

Start with Casa de Empeño

You can go in any order, but PowerPyx recommends Casa de Empeño first:

  1. It has no back door, so the alarm will go off either way. It’s the smallest store, so you’re in and out fast.
  2. Hand the chain to Yasmani right after. He gives you the Snap Gun, which opens locked doors without triggering the alarm.
  3. With the Snap Gun, go in through the back doors at Richardson and Donovan without alerting the guards up front.

Inside each store

  • Near the safe or in the back room you’ll find the box to destroy the camera footage and the panel to disarm the alarm. Use both so the cops don’t get your description.
  • If you came in quietly, you can leave through the back without anyone noticing. For extra cash, head to the front and smash the display cases.
  • When you’re done, go back to Yasmani’s store and sell everything. According to PowerPyx, the three items pay $20,000, plus whatever you grab from the cases.

For the Sneakthief trophy

Sneakthief asks you to rob a jewelry store undetected. After this mission, use the Snap Gun on the back door, disarm the alarm, destroy the footage and open the safe. PowerPyx suggests Richardson Jewelry. See the trophy list.

Quick answers

What is the Casa de Empeño safe code in GTA 6?

1680. The safe is in the small back office and holds the buffalo skull chain Yasmani wants.

What is the Richardson Jewelry safe code in GTA 6?

6815. The safe is in the northeast corner of the center room and holds the diamond ring.

What is the Donovan Banks & Co. safe code in GTA 6?

0104, including the leading zero. The safe is halfway down the long back hallway and holds the amethyst necklace.

Which of Yasmani's jewelry stores should you do first?

Casa de Empeño. It has no back door and it's the smallest, and once you hand over the item Yasmani gives you the Snap Gun, which opens the other two stores' back doors without setting off the alarm.
