How to Get Free Guns and Body Armor From Cop Car Trunks in GTA 6

GTA 6 police cars carry assault rifles, shotguns and even body armor. Pickups just need you to open the crate in the bed. Patrol cars need a slim jim if you want to skip the alarm.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 4:11 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The quickest way to get free guns in GTA 6 is to raid a police car trunk. Cop cars can carry an assault rifle, a shotgun, ammo and even body armor. It’s a handy way to gear up fast, as long as you accept the risk of messing with police property.

Older GTA games used to hand you a shotgun when you stole a cop car. Vice City’s police are better equipped than that, and PC Gamer singles out shotguns and assault rifles as the main haul.

Police pickups: no alarm at all

Cops sometimes drive pickup trucks such as the Bravado Bison. You’ll see them both out in the country and in town.

  1. Climb into the truck bed.
  2. Open the storage crate.
  3. Take the weapons, ammo and anything else inside. You might even find a Stun Gun.

Nothing goes off, which makes pickups the easy option.

Patrol cars: bring the slim jim

Regular patrol cars have alarms on both the doors and the trunk. The way around that is the slim jim, a tool you get in the Chapter 2 mission Moses. More on it in slim jim and snap gun.

  1. Slim jim the driver’s door and get in.
  2. Once you’re inside, the trunk unlocks.
  3. Walk around to the back and open it.
  4. Help yourself to whatever is there: an assault rifle, a shotgun or body armor. The alarm stays quiet.

No slim jim yet? You can still pop the trunk. Your character pries it open with a screwdriver, but the alarm will sound.

Stash the guns in your own car

Walking around with a rifle in your hands or slung on your back draws attention. After the raid, go to your own car’s trunk and you’ll get a prompt to stow the weapons there. The full breakdown is in how to store weapons in your trunk.

Where to find an empty cop car

  • Don’t try it with officers inside. PC Gamer makes the same point.
  • Look for cruisers parked around town or outside police stations, such as the ones in Port Gellhorn and Vice City.
  • If things go wrong and the cops come after you, see how to lose the police.

Curious which guns are in the game? Check the GTA 6 weapons list.

Quick answers

How do you get free guns in GTA 6?

Open the trunk of an empty police car. It can hold an assault rifle, a shotgun, ammo or body armor.

Does opening a cop car trunk set off the alarm?

On a patrol car, yes, unless you slim jim the door and get in first. The crate on a police pickup has no alarm.

Where can you find parked cop cars in GTA 6?

At police stations, such as the ones in Port Gellhorn and Vice City. Stay away from cars with officers inside.
