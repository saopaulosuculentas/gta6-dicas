How to Save in GTA 6: Manual Saves, 15 Slots and Why Saving Gets Disabled

In GTA 6 you save from the main menu: pick Game, then Save Game. There are 15 manual save slots. Using a cheat disables saving until you quit the game.

Confirmed · 4 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 6:04 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has manual saving, so you choose when to create a checkpoint. The quickest way is the main menu: choose Game and then Save Game. There are 15 manual save slots, according to PC Gamer and ZoneX.

How to save

  1. Open the main menu.
  2. Select Game.
  3. Choose Save Game and pick one of the 15 slots.

You load an older save from the same menu.

Tip: one save per chapter

PC Gamer suggests keeping a save at the start of each chapter. With 15 slots you have room to go back to any point in the story and try a mission a different way.

There’s no mission replay

Unlike GTA 5, GTA 6 won’t let you replay a mission once it’s done. According to ZoneX, the only way back in is to load a manual save from before it, so he saves before every mission and before any big story choice. Vice City Dispatch, which pieces its videos together from reviews, makes the same point: finished missions don’t come back, so you can’t earn their payout twice.

That’s separate from restarting the mission you’re currently playing, which you can still do from the menu (see below).

Saving often also protects your money: the cash on you is lost if you die, so stash it and save.

How to quit GTA 6

There’s no quit option in the game. GamesRadar+ says you have to go back to the PS5 or Xbox home screen and close the app from there. Save first, or you’ll load back in from the last autosave.

The autosave slot

On top of the 15 manual slots there’s a 16th slot reserved for the autosave. It saves after missions, when you get wasted or busted, and after some big open world moments. It happens less often than you’d think: see GTA 6 autosave explained.

You can’t save from the menu during a mission. From the Start menu you can restart the mission from the beginning or from the last checkpoint.

When you can’t save

After using a cheat, saving is disabled until you quit the game. To save again, quit without saving and load back in. That’s why it’s worth saving before you try any cheat. Since cheats only unlock after the story, you don’t need to worry about it early on.

Quick answers

How do you quit GTA 6?

There is no quit option in the game. Save, go back to the PS5 or Xbox home screen and close the game from there.

How do you save in GTA 6?

Open the main menu, choose Game, then Save Game, and pick one of the 15 slots.

How many save slots does GTA 6 have?

15 manual save slots, according to PC Gamer and ZoneX, plus a separate slot for the autosave.

Why can't I save in GTA 6?

If you used a cheat, saving is disabled until you quit the game. Quit without saving and load back in.
