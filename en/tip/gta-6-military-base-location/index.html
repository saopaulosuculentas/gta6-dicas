GTA 6 Military Base Location: How to Get Into Mile Zero Air Station

The GTA 6 military base is Mile Zero Air Station on Goose Key, in the southwest corner of the map. The easiest way in is the stunt jump ramp on the southeast side. Inside: jets, tanks and helicopters.

Confirmed · 7 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 11:01 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The GTA 6 military base is Mile Zero Air Station, and it’s the easiest place to grab a fighter jet, a tank or a military helicopter. The simplest way in is a stunt jump ramp that launches you over the fence. PC Gamer and T5G both showed it.

Where it is

  • On Goose Key, in Mariana County, in the southwest corner of the map.
  • It’s out on the big bridges to the southern keys. It feels far, but it’s close to Jason’s first house in Key Lento.

How to get in

  1. Head to the southeast corner of the base along the southern access road.
  2. Get past the concrete barricade checkpoints. They’re lightly manned, but expect some gunfire.
  3. Look for a big dirt mound against the barbed wire fence. That’s the Mile Zero Air Station Southeast stunt jump.
  4. Hit it at speed. You get credit for the stunt jump and land inside the base.

TGG recommends hitting the ramp on a dirt bike, which weaves through the checkpoints more easily. If the jump fails, go faster. The main gate works too, but it has far more guards and a vehicle blocking the entrance.

T5G points out it’s the same idea as the classic ramp into the military base in GTA 5.

Where the tanks are parked

Once you land, turn left. Maka91Productions finds the tanks on the left side of a huge building just past the fence, with big and small ones parked together. The Rhinos are the big ones, and they sit closest to the fence you jumped over. Arekkz Gaming took a different route, driving past the bunkers and the jets, and found a whole row of Rhinos at the back of a corner of the base.

  • Getting in: walk up and press Triangle (Y on Xbox). Your character climbs in through the hatch automatically, but Arekkz warns you can still take bullets while that happens.
  • Firing: the Rhino has a single main gun. Maka91Productions fires it with RB (R1 on PS5).
  • Watch the sky: the gun can’t tilt up far enough to hit anything overhead, and rocket helicopters show up once your stars climb.

Climbing into a Rhino also pops the Challenging Tank achievement. The full walkthrough is in how to get a tank in GTA 6.

The Goose Key Carcass

The base also hides one of the WAiNK app’s curiosities. According to GameSpot, if you follow the coastline northeast inside Mile Zero Air Station, you’ll find the Goose Key Carcass, the remains of some strange creature, with a collectible meteorite right next to it. It’s a restricted area, so try to get there without the soldiers spotting you. The rest are in WAiNK curiosities and landmarks, and what to do with the rock is in the meteorite guide.

Can you keep the jet or the tank?

Not for good. RageGamingVideos points out that military vehicles have permanent trackers: you can take one out and have fun, but you can’t keep it as your own. He also shows the quickest route: take the highway exit on the east side of the base, turn right right after it, and the ramp points you straight over the fence. Inside, the jets sit in a row, the tanks are further in, and there are APCs around the corner.

Make an extra save before you go in. With GTA 6’s criminal profile system, causing chaos on base can follow you, and the save lets you take it back.

Expect a fight

Before you go, TGG suggests:

  • buying body armor at Ammu-Nation (up to 3, at the counter, under Equipment);
  • packing Zombix and adrenaline to heal on the way;
  • saving your game, since dying with cash on you means losing it.

Once inside, the military tries to box you in: head straight for the jets. The jet has a machine gun and missiles, but it also carries a red tracker, and fighter jets come after you. In the video, stealing the tank earned a six-star wanted level. See how to get six stars.

If you’re spotted on base you get a wanted level and an immediate gunfight. The plan: jump in over the ramp, go straight to the vehicle you want and leave. If you took the jet, fly low to be seen less. See how to lose the police.

If you just want a plane to explore, there are seaplanes and a helicopter nobody is guarding in Southwind, right next to the base: see where to find your first plane and helicopter.
