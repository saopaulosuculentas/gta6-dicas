GTA 6 Trophy and Achievement List: All 55 and What Each One Asks For

GTA 6 has 55 trophies on PS5, platinum included, and its Xbox achievements add up to 1,000 Gamerscore. Here is what each one asks for, grouped by type, no spoilers.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 4:11 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has 55 trophies on PS5, including the platinum Vice Got a Price!, and the Xbox achievements are worth 1,000 Gamerscore in total. GamesRadar+ breaks it down as 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, and PC Gamer’s list has the same awards.

A good chunk will pop just from playing the story. Others are a real grind, like the one for 100% completion. PC Gamer notes the list comes from the console version and that the platinum could change once the game reaches PC.

Platinum

Vice Got a Price! is the platinum, and it unlocks once you have every other trophy. Going by the descriptions alone, the longest hauls are A Long Road (100%), Cunning Stunts (250 stunts), The Shootist (42 gold medals), You WAiNKer! (100 locations) and Actions Speak Louder.

Quick answers

How many trophies are in GTA 6?

55 on PS5 counting the platinum: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze.

How much Gamerscore is GTA 6 worth?

1,000G on Xbox. The biggest single achievement is A Long Road, for 100% completion, at 100G.

Is there a 100% completion trophy in GTA 6?

Yes. A Long Road asks for 100% completion and is one of the game's two gold trophies.

Does the GTA 6 trophy list have spoilers?

Barely. Story trophies use chapter and mission names, and a few have cryptic descriptions, but none of them gives away the ending.
