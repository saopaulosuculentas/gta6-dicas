GTA 6 Moses Drop-Off Locations: All 7 Cars and the Secret Sultan Reward

In Chapter 2, Moses asks for 7 cars that never show on the map. Here's where each one is, how to sell to him without wasting money and the fully customized car he gives you at the end.

Unverified · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 25, 9:27 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

In Chapter 2, Moses, who runs a garage in Port Gellhorn, asks you to steal 7 cars for him. The catch: none of them show on your map. Finish the list and he hands you a fully customized car.

The locations come from Mislead, in a TikTok video with over 500,000 views. That’s one source for the locations so far, so this page is unverified. The selling tips come from Vice Clips.

All 7 cars

Car Where it is
Declasse Yosemite 1500 West side of Port Gellhorn, next to a small inlet. It may be getting towed: just get in anyway.
Annis Hellion 95 Far south of Port Gellhorn, in front of an L-shaped house.
Canis Crawler 82 The roundabout in north Port Gellhorn. It’s the first one the game sends you after, and it counts.
Karin Futo 85 Draper Island, next to Port Gellhorn, inside the long building on the south end. Sometimes there are armed guards.
Declasse Vamos Grayson, Lummox County, among the small houses on the far right: a single garage at a run-down spot covered in stop signs. The garage is locked.
Western Nightblade Ambrosia, inside the bikers’ warehouse, with plenty of armed guys around.
BF Bandito Buckskin Swamp. A group of rednecks may be driving it around, so you might have to chase it down and take it.

Car not there? If the purple marker doesn’t appear when you get close, you still need to wait for Moses’ next text. He unlocks the requests a few at a time.

Lost a car or got arrested with it? It ends up in the Port Gellhorn impound lot (or another impound marked on the map). Steal it back and deliver it anyway.

The reward: Moses’ Sultan

According to Mislead, once all 7 are delivered, go back to his garage and head to the door on the right. Moses lets you open it, and inside is a fully customized Sultan for you to keep.

Selling to Moses

Vice Clips sums up the rules: low-key cars only, no trackers, and sleep at a safehouse to skip his cooldown between sales.

They also found four cars around Port Gellhorn worth over $20,000 to Moses in one go:

Car Max value in the video
Orange Caracara in a gang compound $5,855
Caracara behind a broken fence (shoot the gate switch to drive it out) $6,490
Firebug Dominator, the most valuable $7,205
Chino, nobody guarding it $2,850

Even shot up, the cars sold close to their max.

Quick answers

Where are Moses' cars in GTA 6?

Around Port Gellhorn and up north: the Yosemite 1500 being towed on Port Gellhorn's west side, the Hellion 95 in the south, the Crawler 82 at the north roundabout, the Futo 85 on Draper Island, the Vamos in a Grayson garage, the Nightblade in an Ambrosia biker warehouse and the Bandito in Buckskin Swamp.

What do you get for finishing Moses' drop-offs?

According to Mislead, once all 7 are delivered you go back to his shop and open the door on the right: a fully customized Sultan is waiting for you to keep.

Does Moses buy cars with trackers?

No. Stick to low-key cars with no tracker, and sleep at a safehouse to skip his cooldown between sales.
