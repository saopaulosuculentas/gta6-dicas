GTA 6 Sunken Treasure: How to Dive Kenton's Shipwrecks and Make Money

In GTA 6, dive captain Kenton opens up the shipwrecks with the mission Island Dreams. Dive with the metal detector, collect the treasure and sell it back to him: one dive can pay up to $4,000.

Confirmed · 9 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 11:08 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Diving shipwrecks is one of the most fun ways to make money early in GTA 6. The jobs come from Kenton, a captain who runs boat trips for divers. You dive with a metal detector, collect the treasure and sell it back to him. GameSpot says the game calls this activity Kenton’s Treasures. According to WiLLiSGaming, one dive can pay up to $4,000, and JoblessGarrett did a full round and made $3,200 in under 30 minutes.

How to unlock it

  1. Kenton’s first job is Island Dreams, in Chapter 1. KultZ says it shows up after the mission Hey Arnold, at Kenton’s dive shop on the beach in Key Lento.
  2. That mission teaches you how to dive and hunt for treasure in a wreck. It pays $400, one time only.
  3. In the same mission you get scuba gear and two tools: the Arras Bullhead speargun and the metal detector, according to GameSpot.
  4. Later, Kenton calls you again with more wrecks. He texts you the locations; you collect everything, bring it back and get paid. After a while he offers a fresh batch. KhrazeGaming notes these spots show up on the map with a purple icon and count toward an achievement.
  5. Kenton’s shop is marked on the map, and you can take his boat from the dock next to it. If you own another boat, GameSpot says Kenton has the scuba gear, speargun and detector delivered to that boat. RageGamingVideos also got a scuba suit to use on his own boat.

Underwater controls and HUD

Garrett Lindsay’s video shows what diving looks like on PS5:

  • Going down: the prompt reads DIVE R1, so R1 takes you deeper.
  • Coming up: the prompt reads SURFACE X, so X brings you back to the surface.
  • Oxygen: your air bar sits in the top-left corner. Glance at it before you swim into a wreck.
  • Flashlight: the scuba gear comes with one.
  • Wildlife: seals swim past in his footage, and he warns that sharks are around too.

He only shows the PS5 version. On Xbox the buttons should sit in the same spots on the pad (RB and A).

Garrett found the gear highlighted in white on a pier while exploring the map, and picked it up with Triangle. The name of the spot isn’t clear in the video. His own video description ties the gear to Kenton’s diving activities and the mission Island Dreams, so it’s most likely the same kit Kenton gives you.

How to use the metal detector

  1. At the wreck, pull out the detector and slowly pan it around until you see a green flash.
  2. Swim that way. The pings speed up and the green light blinks faster as you get closer (GameSpot and KultZ).
  3. At the right spot, a prompt shows up to dig up or grab the treasure.
  4. Check inside the ship too: the chests are usually in there.
  5. Every wreck has several relics and coins. Once you’ve found them all, that spot counts as done.
  6. Head back to Kenton and sell.

BennyCentral says the detector takes a little getting used to, but once you’ve got it, wrecks go by fast.

How many treasures each wreck has

  • NoobBlox found all 7 treasures at Nuestra Señora de la Noche, scattered inside and around the ship, including a purple gemstone and a chest.
  • In JoblessGarrett’s video one wreck had 7 items and another had 5, like a pipe, an antique candlestick, a ceremonial spoon and chests.
  • BennyCentral showed where each of the 12 treasures sits across two wrecks near Key Lento: 7 in the first and 5 in the second. The counts and items match JoblessGarrett’s, and the first wreck, with its purple gemstone and chest, looks like the same Nuestra Señora de la Noche NoobBlox covered.

All 12 treasures in the two Key Lento wrecks

BennyCentral’s routine: mark the dive sites on your map, take your boat there (auto drive will steer for you), put on the scuba gear and pull out the metal detector. Its light turns from red to green when you’re right on top of a treasure.

First wreck (7 treasures):

  1. Serpentine statue: under the hole, by the big kelp plant.
  2. Candlestick: out in the rubble, near a barrel.
  3. Dragon pipe: next to a fishing net.
  4. Purple gemstone: in the kelp, by a fallen pipe.
  5. Ivory saint: at the bottom of the slope, against the hull.
  6. Pewter gauntlet: in the seagrass, by the timbers.
  7. Buried chest: in a dark rocky area. You have to dig it up. Benny says this is the one everybody misses.

Second wreck (5 treasures):

  1. Golden pendant: in the debris field.
  2. Ceremonial spoon: at the base of a coral.
  3. Chest: tucked under a coral overhang. Pull it out.
  4. Golden chalice: by a block just outside the hole.
  5. Snuff box: in the seagrass, right against the hull wall.

Take everything back to Kenton at his shop in Key Lento. Benny got $2,400 for the whole lot of 12. The name of the first wreck isn’t clear in his voiceover.

Gold doubloons (99 across the map)

According to BennyCentral, there are 99 gold doubloons scattered around Leonida, and plenty of them sit on the seabed away from Kenton’s wrecks.

  • Bobcat Key dome houses: the dome-shaped houses on stilts out at sea have doubloons on the seabed underneath them. Dive with the detector and wait for the green light.
  • Landmarks by the water: those dome houses are a Waink landmark. Benny’s tip is that whenever a landmark sits by the water, there’s very likely treasure nearby. Scan it, then search with the detector.

Doubloons can be sold at pawn shops too. See collectible cards for how to check collectible values from the map.

Kenton’s shipwrecks

GameSpot has found 6 wrecks so far, all in southern Leonida:

  1. Nuestra Señora del Ocho
  2. Nuestra Señora de la Noche
  3. Nuestra Señora de la Etica Laxa
  4. Nuestra Señora Lujuriosa
  5. Urca de Cinco Torres
  6. Urca de Santa Sierra

GameSpot says the list may grow. KhrazeGaming also found plenty of dive spots scattered around the Leonida Keys.

How much it pays

  • The first mission, Island Dreams, pays $400 (KultZ).
  • One round of JoblessGarrett’s treasure sold for $2,600.
  • On the way he also found contraband floating at sea and sold it for $1,600 at Brian’s lockup.
  • RageGamingVideos cleared the first wreck marked on the map with just the metal detector and sold the haul for about $1,400, very quickly.
  • KhrazeGaming says every treasure you bring back is worth $2,400 and every coin $200, with 5 to 7 coins per spot. By his math you’ll come back with at least $2,000 per spot. Other channels got less per round, so the payout probably changes from wreck to wreck.
  • BennyCentral got $2,400 for all 12 treasures from two wrecks combined. That’s the same figure KhrazeGaming gives, but for the whole lot rather than per treasure.
  • According to WiLLiSGaming, the more dives you do, the more Kenton pays and the more locations he sends. Whenever he calls, go.

Watch out

  • Sharks. JoblessGarrett got attacked by a tiger shark and nearly died, and BennyCentral and Garrett Lindsay warn about them too. Don’t stay in the water longer than you need to. GameSpot says the Arras Bullhead doesn’t hold much ammo, but it’s your only defense down there. RageGamingVideos killed a shark with it.
  • The warden. At one wreck, a boat pulled up to warn that searching shipwrecks without a recovery permit is illegal and that finds must be reported. JoblessGarrett ignored it and sold everything anyway.
  • Stash your cash. If you die or get busted with the money on you, you lose it. See do you lose money when you die.

Bonus: autopilot

On the way back, JoblessGarrett found you can switch on the cinematic camera and let your character drive automatically, like in Red Dead Redemption 2. Set a destination, hold Down on the D-pad and press Circle. KhrazeGaming says it works even better on a boat, since there’s barely any traffic at sea. More in hidden features.

Quick answers

How do you find sunken treasure in GTA 6?

Do Kenton's diving side jobs. He texts you shipwreck locations; dive with the gear he gives you and follow the green flash on the metal detector.

What mission unlocks Kenton's diving in GTA 6?

Island Dreams, in Chapter 1. KultZ says it opens up after the mission Hey Arnold, at Kenton's dive shop on the beach in Key Lento.

How much does sunken treasure pay in GTA 6?

Up to $4,000 per dive, according to WiLLiSGaming. JoblessGarrett sold one round of treasure for $2,600, and the first mission, Island Dreams, pays $400.

Do you need a permit to dive for treasure in GTA 6?

A character warns you that searching shipwrecks without a permit is illegal, but JoblessGarrett ignored it and sold everything with no problem.
