Mandy in GTA 6: Where Is Mandy's House and How to Collect Her Rent

Mandy's house is a short drive from Jason's place: look for the green car in the driveway and the American flag. Here's how to find it in the index and finish Pipework.

Confirmed · 7 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 11:01 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Mandy’s house is a short drive from Jason’s place. GamesRadar+ gives two landmarks: a green car in the driveway and an American flag flying outside. None of our sources name the street, so the quickest way there is to set the mission as your destination from the map index.

Mandy is the tenant in Pipework, a Chapter 1 mission where Brian sends Jason to collect her overdue rent.

When the mission shows up

According to GamesRadar+, Brian calls a couple of minutes after you drop the Moonstone Boutique stash at his lockup at the end of Lab Fees. Answer with D-pad Up when your phone rings. He’s got another tenant behind on rent and wants Jason to go collect.

The sources don’t agree on the order. GamesRadar+ puts Pipework right after Lab Fees, while PC Gamer’s list has it as mission 7 of Chapter 1, after Unwanted Attention, summed up as Jason doing some DIY. If Brian hasn’t called yet, check the mission list to see what comes first.

Finding the house with the index

  1. Open the map (touchpad on PS5).
  2. Press D-pad Right to bring up the index.
  3. On the Story tab (the journal icon), select Mandy’s mission and press X (A on Xbox) to make it your active destination. The GPS draws the route.
  4. When you reach the street, look for the green car. In GTA Series Videos’ playthrough it’s a convertible parked on the gravel out front.

The index is covered in full in our index guide.

How to collect Mandy’s rent

Buttons are listed as PS5 / Xbox:

  1. Chat with Mandy outside. She invites Jason in.
  2. She leads you to the back bedroom. Hold L2 / LT to interact and press Square / X to ask if she has a plunger. She’ll only pay up if Jason helps with a repair.
  3. Cross the hall to the bathroom. The plunger is next to the toilet: pick it up with Triangle / Y.
  4. Take it to the kitchen sink and press Triangle / Y again.
  5. Tap R3 over and over to work the plunger.
  6. That doesn’t clear it, so Jason crawls under the sink. Hold R2 / RT to turn the wrench. Mandy keeps talking the whole time; just keep going.
  7. After five turns, you find what was blocking the pipe. GamesRadar+ only says it’s disgusting. Then get out of the house.

For that last part, GamesRadar+ only gives the PS5 buttons (R3 and R2). In Apex Films’ video, played on an Xbox controller, the wrench prompt sits on RT. KultZ backs up the ending: the job finishes with Jason under Mandy’s kitchen sink.

What comes out of Mandy’s sink

This is the bit that has so many people searching for the “GTA 6 sink mission”. In Apex Films’ video, Jason loosens the pipe and pulls out a small object caked in grime. He holds it close to his face and grimaces. It’s over in a second and the object is filthy. PC Gamer says it looks like a finger, and that Mandy grabs it out of Jason’s hand right after. GameSpot doesn’t say what it is, only that a curious item was clogging the pipes. Rockstar hasn’t explained it.

For a closer look at the scene with screenshots, and why nobody has confirmed what the object is yet, see the sink section of can you cheat on Lucia or romance Mandy?.

After Mandy

  • How the visit ends: GameSpot says that once the sink is sorted, Mandy makes one more attempt to get Jason to stay. He says no and walks out, and the game gives you no say in it.
  • Going back later: according to GameSpot, passing by Mandy’s house on foot, by bike or by car can kick off more conversations with her. She still isn’t a romance option, though. See can you romance Mandy?.
  • What’s next: with the rent paid, GamesRadar+ points you to Cal’s texts, which lead into the next mission.
  • How much it pays: none of the sources say what Jason earns from this job.
  • A plane crash: Arekkz Gaming says that once you’ve progressed the story and met Mandy, crossing a bridge he marks on the map can trigger a small plane crashing into the nearby swamp. The wreck has valuables you can sell, but enemies show up if you hang around too long.

Every other early mission, with the same button prompts, is in the Chapter 1 walkthrough.

Quick answers

Where is Mandy's house in GTA 6?

A short drive from Jason's house. Look for the green car in the driveway and the American flag, or set the mission as your destination from the map index.

What is Mandy's mission in GTA 6?

It's Pipework, in Chapter 1. Brian sends Jason to collect Mandy's overdue rent, and she only pays once he unclogs her kitchen sink.

Where is the plunger in Mandy's house?

In the bathroom across the hall, right next to the toilet. Grab it with Triangle (Y on Xbox) and take it to the kitchen sink.

What comes out of Mandy's sink in GTA 6?

A small, filthy object stuck in the kitchen pipe. Jason pulls it out, looks at it up close and pulls a face, but you can't make out what it is. GameSpot just calls it a curious item.
