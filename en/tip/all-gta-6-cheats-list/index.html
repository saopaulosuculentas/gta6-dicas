All GTA 6 Cheats: Full List of 61 Codes and How to Unlock Each One

GTA 6 has 61 cheats for PS5 and Xbox: money, ammo, armor, wanted level, weather, vehicles and screen filters. Here is every known sequence and the challenge that unlocks it.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 10:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has 61 cheats for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. But they don’t work from the start: first you have to beat the main story, and then each cheat unlocks with its own challenge. Below is the full list, with the button sequence for each one (when it has been published) and what you need to do to unlock it.

The list comes from GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer, who played until they unlocked the cheats, plus the sequences Bubs showed on YouTube. When the same sequence shows up in two of them, the cheat is marked confirmed on its own page.

How to use cheats

  1. Beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer).
  2. In the pause menu, go to the Progress tab. A Cheats option appears there, with what you have already unlocked.
  3. In the game, press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
  4. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox) and enter the sequence on the D-pad.

Heads up: using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first. Full walkthrough in how to use cheats in GTA 6.

Watch out for fake lists

Before launch, a “GTA 6 cheat list” with a jetpack, a tank and PlayStation face buttons like circle and triangle went viral. It is made of GTA 5 and San Andreas codes. Real GTA 6 cheats use only the D-pad.

Quick answers

How many cheats are in GTA 6?

61: 10 player cheats, 11 combat, 3 environment, 21 vehicle spawns and 16 screen filters, according to GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer.

How do you unlock cheats in GTA 6?

Beat the main story first. Then each cheat unlocks with its own challenge, like winning medals at shooting ranges or finding collectibles.

Is there a money cheat in GTA 6?

Just one: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. It gives $250,000 and unlocks when you beat the story.

Is there a tank cheat in GTA 6?

Yes, the Hauners Rhino Tank, unlocked by completing all survival challenges. Its sequence has not been published yet.

Is there a jetpack cheat in GTA 6?

No. The official list of 61 cheats has no jetpack. Viral lists with a jetpack are recycled GTA San Andreas codes.

Can you save after using a cheat in GTA 6?

No. Using a cheat turns off saving, trophies and achievements until you quit the game.
