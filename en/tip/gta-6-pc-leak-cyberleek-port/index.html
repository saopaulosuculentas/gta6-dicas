GTA 6 on PC: What We Know About the Cyberleek Xbox Leak and Why You Can't Play It Yet

Cyberleek released the Xbox build's executable and fans are racing to run GTA 6 on PC. So far it only reaches a black screen or the menus. Here's what's real, what's fake and the official way to play on PC.

Confirmed · 5 sourcesUpdated Nov 24, 11:50 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

You can’t play GTA 6 on PC yet. On November 23, the leaker Cyberleek released the executable for the Xbox build they had leaked, and fan groups are racing to get it running on PC. So far they’ve shown the game almost booting, to a black screen, or its menus working. Nobody has shown anyone driving around Leonida.

Rockstar hasn’t announced a PC version. This page is news only: no links, no instructions, and here’s why you shouldn’t download anything that promises GTA 6 on PC.

What happened

  • Cyberleek had already leaked the files of the Xbox Series build. One was missing: the executable, the file that starts the game.
  • They promised to drop it if their memecoin hit a certain market cap. According to Code Bear, it never got there, and the file came out anyway on November 23.
  • According to GameSpot, Cyberleek has been taunting Take-Two for failing to stop the leaks, and already cashed out hundreds of thousands of dollars from the coin in August and October.
  • In the message that came with the file, quoted by GamesRadar+, Cyberleek themselves tells people to verify any file before trusting it.

Does it run on PC?

No. A console game reaches PC in stages: the program starts, it draws a picture, the menus work, the world loads, and only then is it playable. As of November 24, according to GameSpot, GamesRadar+ and Code Bear’s fact check:

Stage Status on November 24
The program starts Yes
It draws a picture Yes, but the most-cited project, Project Leonida, still gets a black screen and the audio cuts off
Menus Some groups showed the menus at 30 frames per second
The world loads One group reached the first mission’s title card, but the scene stays dark at about 1 frame per second
Playable No. Nobody has shown anyone walking or driving

According to GameSpot, the groups think they’ll have a playable build within a week, using AI to translate the Xbox code. That’s their forecast, not a fact.

Be careful with “GTA 6 running on PC” videos

A lot of what’s going around isn’t the game running on a computer:

  • It’s console footage. Linus Tech Tips and Hardware Unboxed both showed GTA 6 “at 60 FPS” on a PC, but the game is running on a PS5. The picture goes through a capture card and the PC adds generated frames. Both warn it adds visual artifacts and input lag. According to Code Bear, the viral high-end graphics card numbers came from this trick, with PS5 Pro footage.
  • It’s only setup screens. Code Bear went through the most-viewed clips frame by frame: the real ones show start screens and menus at most. Captions like “100% confirmed” and “running at 110 FPS” don’t match the picture.

Don’t download “GTA 6 for PC”

There’s no playable version to download. So every “GTA 6 PC” download out there right now is fake or unfinished. According to Code Bear, security firms have counted hundreds of sites pushing fake launchers, and one of those files was flagged by more than half of the antivirus engines that scanned it. Some scams steal crypto wallets and quietly use your PC to mine.

There’s also the legal side: this is piracy. According to Code Bear, Take-Two has already had fan projects taken down on GitHub and, in 2021, sued the people behind fan rebuilds of the old GTA 3 and Vice City. GamesRadar+ notes that Take-Two’s CEO says the company has its legal team on standby.

What about an official PC version?

Rockstar hasn’t announced one or given a date. According to GameSpot, rumors point to early 2027, a short gap by Rockstar standards. GamesRadar+ expects the official version within a year or two, as with GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2.

The official way to play on PC today: Remote Play

If you own the console and the game, you can stream GTA 6 from your console to your PC or phone. The game still runs on the console.

On Xbox Series X|S (Microsoft confirmed support, according to GameSpot):

  1. On the console, go to Settings > Devices & connections > Remote features and tick Enable remote features. There’s also a test for your connection there.
  2. On PC: open the Xbox app, click your profile and go to Accessories & Remote Play.
  3. On a phone: go to the Xbox Remote Play website and pick your console.

On PS5: according to GameSpot, the game’s PlayStation Store page now says Remote Play is supported.

Quality depends on your internet, and the game still runs at 30 frames per second, as it does on console.

Quick answers

Can you play GTA 6 on PC yet?

No. As of November 24, no project has shown anyone playing on PC. The furthest anyone got is the game booting to a black screen, or its menus running.

What did Cyberleek release?

The executable (.exe) for the Xbox build they had already leaked. It was the missing file needed to start the game, and it came out on November 23.

Is it safe to download GTA 6 for PC?

No. There's no playable version to download, so every GTA 6 PC download is fake or unfinished, and many carry malware. It's also piracy.

When is the official GTA 6 PC release?

Rockstar hasn't announced one. According to GameSpot, rumors point to early 2027.

How can you play GTA 6 on PC officially?

Through Remote Play, streaming from your own Xbox Series X|S or PS5 to your PC or phone. You need the console and the game.
