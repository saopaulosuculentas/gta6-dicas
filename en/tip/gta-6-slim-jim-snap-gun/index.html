GTA 6 Slim Jim and Snap Gun: What They Do and Which Mission Unlocks Them

The slim jim opens locked cars without smashing glass and comes from Moses in the Chapter 2 mission Moses. The snap gun opens locked doors quietly and comes from Yasmani in Chapter 3.

Unverified · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 6:04 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The slim jim pops open a locked car without breaking the glass, and you get it in Chapter 2, during the mission Moses. The snap gun picks locked doors quietly, and you get it in Chapter 3, after the mission Dubious Provenance. Both are tied to story progress, so neither will help you in the opening missions.

Slim jim

How to get it

Moses gives you the slim jim in the Chapter 2 mission named after him, Moses. It comes right after Girls’ Trip, the mission where Lucia goes shopping and makes a new friend.

GameSpot shows the same thing on video: in Chapter 2 you meet Moses, and once the introductions are done he gives you the slim jim and shows you how it works.

Before you have it

According to GameSpot, in Chapter 1 the only way into a locked car is to smash the window and hotwire it. Smashing the window sets off the alarm, so hotwire quickly and get away. The alarm shuts off after a while, but until then the people around will be suspicious.

How to use it

  1. Walk up to a car door.
  2. Pick slim jim to start a short sequence.
  3. Work the analog stick to find the sweet spot and hold it for a second. The door pops open.

What it can and can’t do

  • It gets you into older cars without the alarm. GameSpot says it’s the only way to break into an older parked car without setting the alarm off. With it, you can quietly get into any car that doesn’t need the key cloner.
  • It opens the trunk without an alarm. Worth a look, since trunks can hold weapons, drugs or cash.
  • It won’t start a keyless car. Some cars only need unlocking before you hotwire them, but keyless models need the key cloner. GameSpot says the cloner arrives in Chapter 3 through Randy, and cars you open with it don’t need hotwiring at all.

Snap gun

How to get it

Yasmani gives you the snap gun in Chapter 3, once you’ve finished the jewelry store job in the mission Dubious Provenance.

How to use it

  1. Equip it and walk up to a locked door.
  2. Pick the Use snap gun option.
  3. Use the left stick to find the lock’s sweet spot.
  4. Squeeze the right trigger (R2 on PS5, RT on Xbox) until the meter fills. You’re in.

Watch the alarm

The snap gun gets you through the door without setting off the alarm, but the alarm is still armed. It can still go off if you kick in another door, smash a display case, or a store worker trips it.

PC Gamer’s tip: use the snap gun on the back door. That route takes you to the office, where you can switch the alarm off completely.

Quick answers

When do you get the slim jim in GTA 6?

In the Chapter 2 mission Moses, right after Girls' Trip. Moses hands it over himself.

How do you get the snap gun in GTA 6?

Yasmani gives it to you in Chapter 3, after you pull off the jewelry store job in the mission Dubious Provenance.

Can the slim jim start a keyless car in GTA 6?

No. It only unlocks the door. Keyless cars need the key cloner.

Does the snap gun turn off a store's alarm?

No. It gets you through the door without tripping the alarm, but the alarm stays armed. Go in the back and reach the office to shut it off for good.
