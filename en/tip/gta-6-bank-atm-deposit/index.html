GTA 6 Bank Account and ATMs: How to Deposit and Protect Your Cash

Your GTA 6 bank account unlocks in the Chapter 1 mission Buck Me, and then any ATM takes deposits. Until then, hide your cash under the bedroom floorboards in Key Lento.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 4:08 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Your GTA 6 bank account unlocks in Chapter 1, during the mission Buck Me. From then on you can deposit cash at any ATM on the map. Before that, the safe place for money is the stash at home.

Why you should stash cash

Early on, a few hundred dollars can make or break you, and the money in your wallet is never fully safe:

  • Dying or getting arrested costs you. PC Gamer puts the penalty at a few hundred dollars.
  • Strangers on the street may try to rob you.
  • There’s a cap: you can only carry up to $20,000 on you.

So the habit to build is simple: stash often.

Before the bank: the floorboard stash

For the first few chapters you have no account. You can still hide money under the floorboards in the bedroom of Jason’s house in Key Lento. Whatever sits there stays put if you die or get busted.

More on that in do you lose money when you die.

Stolen goods go in the trunk

Jewelry, drugs and marked bills from robberies can sit in your car’s trunk. Even if the cops impound the car, you can get the haul back later and sell it.

Just don’t use a car you don’t own. Lose the car and you lose what’s inside. When it’s time to sell, see the fence locations.

How to deposit at an ATM

  1. Play up to the mission Buck Me in Chapter 1. It opens your account.
  2. On the map, look for the hand with a credit card icon. Those are ATMs.
  3. Walk up to one and interact with it.
  4. Choose to deposit.

Having an account also unlocks some weapons for purchase. PC Gamer mentions an interest-bearing savings account too, but doesn’t explain how it works.

Don’t bank every dollar

This is the guide’s key advice. Plenty of GTA 6’s street moments only happen if you have cash in hand:

  • a stranger selling secrets, each one pricier than the last;
  • a bribe to get quick access to a restricted area;
  • a pool hustler in a dive bar;
  • vendors who only take cash.

So whenever you deposit, keep a few hundred in your wallet. That way you won’t have to run off looking for an ATM in the middle of one of those encounters.

To fill the account up, see how to make money in GTA 6.

Quick answers

How do you open a bank account in GTA 6?

It unlocks during the Chapter 1 mission Buck Me. After that, ATMs accept deposits.

Where are the ATMs in GTA 6?

Look for the icon of a hand holding a credit card on the map. Walk up to the machine and interact with it to deposit.

How much cash can you carry in GTA 6?

Up to $20,000 on your person, according to PC Gamer. Anything beyond that should go into your stash or the bank.

Should you deposit all your money in GTA 6?

No. Keep a few hundred dollars in your wallet for bribes, pool games, cash-only vendors and other street encounters.
