GTA 6 Money Cheat: $250,000 Instantly

The GTA 6 money cheat is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. Adds $250,000 to your wallet instantly. Unlock it when you beat the main story.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 10:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series
PS5
Xbox Series
How to activate
Open the in-game phone (Up on the D-pad), hold L1 + R1 (LB + RB on Xbox) and enter the sequence on the D-pad.

What it does: Adds $250,000 to your wallet instantly.

The money cheat in GTA 6 is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. What it does: adds $250,000 to your wallet instantly. It is the only money cheat in the game, and it does not work until you have finished the story.

How to enter it

  1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
  2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
  3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to beat the main story. Progress you made during the story counts.

What you need to know

  • No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
  • Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
  • There is no infinite money cheat. You can enter this one again, but each use gives $250,000.
  • The sequence matches in Bubs, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer, so the badge is “Confirmed”.
