How to Increase Focus in GTA 6: Every Method

Fishing is the fastest way to raise focus in GTA 6, at about 5% per fish. Basketball, the snake, petting animals, food, pool, golf, the shooting range and Shirley's rum run help too.

Confirmed · 8 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 4:35 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The fastest way to increase focus in GTA 6 is fishing: Arekkz Gaming got about 5% focus per fish, against 1% per basket on the basketball hoop. You can also raise it by feeding Jason’s snake, petting dogs and cats, eating before you head out, playing pool or golf, using the shooting range and doing side jobs like Shirley’s rum run. This guide pulls together tips from seven English-speaking YouTube channels and Kotaku.

Focus is the ability you turn on by clicking both sticks (L3 + R3 on PS5, LS + RS on Xbox). It slows time in gunfights, shows enemy weak points and highlights loot. The higher your focus level, the longer you can keep it running, and BennyCentral calls it the stat you want to train the most.

Fishing: the best method

BennyCentral, Arekkz Gaming, KhrazeGaming and GamingBolt all point to fishing. There are fishing rods behind Jason’s house, so you don’t have to travel. Grab one and walk down to the water.

  1. Press L3 + R3 to turn on focus. Fish get highlighted, so you know where to cast.
  2. Cast your line, then reel in slowly to lure a fish.
  3. When you feel a nibble, press R2 right away to hook it.
  4. Don’t reel in yet. Let the fish fight until it wears itself out. If it swims one way, pull the rod the opposite way.
  5. Once it calms down, reel in as fast as you can. If it starts fighting again, repeat step 4.

Step 1 is from Arekkz Gaming, and steps 2 to 5 are KhrazeGaming’s technique. KhrazeGaming adds that fishing, like other minigames that raise your stats, also improves focus. BennyCentral found it the most effective way to train it: every fish you land moves your focus level up.

RageGamingVideos treats it as a daily routine. Stat training runs on timers, so he does a strength workout, a cardio workout and a focus workout once per timer reset, for each character, because Jason and Lucia are tracked separately. MrDalekJD adds that working out every day for three days in a row builds up both strength and focus.

Permanent focus boosts

GamingBolt lists four ways to permanently raise focus, all without violence: fishing, pool, golf and the shooting range. The shooting range also helps you get used to aiming and test different settings.

Your criminal profile counts too. According to GamingBolt, the Professional and Hardened profiles get a higher max focus, while Violent and Psycho refill focus with headshots. The details are in criminal profile explained.

Shirley’s rum run

Early in the story, Shirley, the neighbor on the corner by Jason’s house, yells at you as you drive past. Stop and talk to her and she asks you to pick up some rum. RageGamingVideos says that bringing it back gives a permanent upgrade to focus. MrDalekJD found this favor in the map’s Exploration tab, which lists jobs for your neighbors, and a later favor for Shirley lets you use her car.

Pets, garbage and food

These come from Kotaku’s launch tips:

  • Pet dogs and cats. Hold the left trigger (L2 or LT) on one to zoom in, then praise or pet it. It buffs focus and earns good behavior points for your criminal profile.
  • Throw away your garbage. Not littering boosts focus and also earns good behavior points.
  • Eat and drink before heading out. Food and drinks buff health, stamina and focus. MrDalekJD gives the same tip: eat or drink at home before you leave, especially before a story mission.

DPJ also lists helping people, feeding pets and feeding your own snake as ways to earn focus. None of these sources says how much each one gives, or how long the buff lasts.

The cheat, for later

Once you beat the story, the unlimited focus cheat stops the meter from ever running out. For other uses of focus, like finding the source of shouting or spotting alarmed doors, see hidden features.

Exact numbers are still thin. The 5% per fish and 1% per basket come only from Arekkz Gaming, and the permanent boosts from pool, golf and the shooting range come only from GamingBolt.

Quick answers

What is the fastest way to increase focus in GTA 6?

Fishing. Arekkz Gaming got about 5% focus per fish, using the rods behind Jason's house.

How do you permanently increase focus in GTA 6?

Go fishing, play pool or golf, use the shooting range and bring Shirley her rum. GamingBolt and RageGamingVideos say these raise focus for good.

How much focus does basketball give in GTA 6?

About 1% per basket, according to Arekkz Gaming. RageGamingVideos says the hoop only gives a certain amount per day.

How do you turn on focus in GTA 6?

Click both sticks at the same time: L3 + R3 on PS5, LS + RS on Xbox.
