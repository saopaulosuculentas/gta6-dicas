GTA 6 Cars List: Every Make, Model and Vehicle Type So Far

Every GTA 6 car and motorbike spotted so far, by make and model, from Jason's Vapid Ganado to the '95 Grotti Cheetah. The fastest cars in the game are still unknown.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 4:08 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has sedans, coupes, pickups, sports cars, motorbikes, dirt bikes and ATVs, and this page lists every model identified so far by make and name. It’s based on PC Gamer’s running list, which was put together largely by pausing Rockstar’s trailers and official screenshots before launch. That means a lot of these names are educated guesses, and PC Gamer says outright that many makes and models are still unconfirmed.

Cars tied to the game and its editions

These have a confirmed name or show up in edition material:

Car What we know
Vapid Ganado Jason’s ride. A two-door coupe based on the 1970 Ford Ranchero. The Ultimate Edition gives it a retro body kit
’95 Grotti Cheetah Ferrari-style classic. Ultimate Edition only
’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy Off-road take with huge tires. Looks like an Ultimate Edition extra
Sirius Muscle car that resembles a classic Dodge Charger. Part of the Ultimate Edition’s Classic Car Collection
Vapid Riata Returning model with a retro paint job, also in the Classic Car Collection
Schyster Deviant Wears a Deviant badge in the Classic Car Collection art, with a slightly different hood
Canis Kamacho Long four-door pickup seen in a shot of One-Eyed Willie’s shop. Unclear whether the truck or just the paint job is exclusive
’55 Vapid Stanier The game’s version of the 1955 Ford Crown Victoria. Comes with the Vintage Vice City pre-order pack

Cars spotted in the trailers

PC Gamer matched these from tail lights, grilles and window shapes. They’re the outlet’s IDs, not Rockstar’s:

  • Albany Buccaneer Custom: sits very low, parked between the PMP and the Tulip M-100.
  • Benefactor Schafter V12: four-door luxury sedan.
  • Bravado Banshee: white with bright blue chrome, in the line of sports cars outside the Dominion Hotel in Trailer 2.
  • Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire: muscle car, half hidden by traffic in Trailer 1.
  • Declasse Tulip M-100: magenta, modeled on a late ’70s to early ’80s Chevy Malibu.
  • Grotti Carbonizzare, Grotti Cheetah Classic and Grotti Furia (the Furia is a supercar).
  • Invetero Coquette: PC Gamer admits this one is a shaky match.
  • Oceanic: yellow, a nod to the Oldsmobile Super 88.
  • Pfister Comet S2 Cabrio: two-door convertible.
  • Schyster PMP: green, with a more modern look than in GTA 4.
  • Vapid Caracara 4x4: a giant F-150-style pickup owned by Brian Heder, Jason’s landlord.

Motorbikes

Bike What we know
Dinka Enduro Off-road bike. An Ultimate Edition bonus
Alvino V1 New to the series, based on the Ducati Panigale V2 and Aprilia RS 660. It’s on the cover art
Nagasaki Carbon RS Seen in red and white with what looks like a custom exhaust
Miabatsu Sanchez The series’ classic dirt bike, seen in Trailer 1
Miabatsu Manchez Nearly identical to the Sanchez, minus the front mudflap

Boats and aircraft

The trailers show motorboats, kayaks, jet skis, airboats, yachts and even cruise ships. In the sky there are helicopters, airliners, crop dusters and a blimp. If you want to fly, see where to find your first plane and helicopter.

Which cars are the best?

Truffade Z-Type No
Truffade Adder No
Grotti Turismo R No

Brands so far

Cars: Vapid, Grotti, Bravado, Declasse, Albany, Benefactor, Invetero, Pfister, Schyster and Canis. Bikes: Dinka, Nagasaki, Miabatsu and Alvino.

Once you find a car you like, check the garage locations and prices to keep it, or read how to steal cars to grab one off the street.

Quick answers

What is Jason's car in GTA 6?

The Vapid Ganado, a two-door coupe based on the 1970 Ford Ranchero. The Ultimate Edition adds a retro body kit for it.

What is the fastest car in GTA 6?

Nobody knows yet. PC Gamer only spotted three of GTA 5's fastest cars in the trailers: the Vapid Dominator, Grotti Cheetah and Bravado Banshee.

Are there motorbikes in GTA 6?

Yes. The Alvino V1, Nagasaki Carbon RS, Dinka Enduro and the Sanchez and Manchez dirt bikes have all been spotted, and there are ATVs too.
