GTA 6 Romance Guide: How to Raise Jason and Lucia's Relationship and What It Gets You

Jason and Lucia's relationship has 4 levels. Holding hands, texting back, going out and pulling off crimes together add hearts, while neglect and failed joint missions take them away.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:56 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

To raise Jason and Lucia’s relationship in GTA 6, act like a couple: hold hands, talk, go out together, answer texts and pull off crimes as a team. Every action that counts plays a chime and puts a +♥ on the right side of the screen, according to PC Gamer. There are 4 levels. The couple starts Chapter 1 on good terms, but reaching the top still takes a while. It pays off: GameSpot says a maxed-out relationship is one of the two things the best ending asks for.

The 4 levels

Level Name
1 of 4 Running on empty
2 of 4 Partners in Crime
3 of 4 Crazy in Love
4 of 4 Hot Together

Your level moves with how you play and what you choose, in story missions, in random crimes and in everyday life at home.

What adds hearts

PC Gamer lists these, and says there are plenty more:

  • Affection: holding hands, a hug or a kiss (look for the prompt with a heart), greeting your partner when you meet up, and complimenting new clothes or a new haircut.
  • At home: going to bed at the same time, hanging out on the couch, starting a conversation, and picking Go Out when you’re both home.
  • Dates: getting your hair done together, minigolf, scuba diving, dancing at a club, and jumping out of a plane together with a parachute.
  • Gifts: buying food or drinks and handing them over, or buying clothing accessories for each other. Both of you have to be at the store.
  • Phone: replying to texts, checking in by text, sending selfies, and texting goodnight when you’re away from home.
  • Manners: opening the car door for each other.
  • Money and crime: successful joint crimes and deposits into the Buck Me savings account.

For more early game advice, including fixing up the house, see our beginner tips. Shopping together also comes up in hidden features.

What costs hearts

You can lose points too. It didn’t happen often in PC Gamer’s run, and these were the causes:

  • failing a joint mission;
  • ignoring your partner for a while;
  • some mission choices your partner doesn’t agree with;
  • hitting them with your car.

Play as both of them

Rockstar’s advice is that a strong relationship needs both sides. Be good to Lucia while you play as Jason, and be good to Jason while you play as Lucia. PC Gamer’s writer finished the story at 4/4 after spending almost the same amount of time with each character.

They each have solo missions, storylines and activities, and some of those are required. You can spend long stretches with one character. Just check in with your partner now and then, answer their texts, and switch over every so often.

What you get out of it

  • Crimes go better as a pair. A second person can hold people at gunpoint, handle crowd control during a robbery, shoot out a cop’s tires while you drive, and carry off more loot than one person could.
  • Intimate couple scenes. You can’t trigger them on demand. The game offers them at certain moments, shown by double hearts over the home icon on the map or by a short timed prompt. In PC Gamer’s playthrough, every one happened at 4/4, including one after a big savings milestone and one after a large heist.
  • A better ending. The relationship feeds into how the story ends. The section below covers what 4/4 gets you over 1/4.

4/4 versus 1/4: what’s different

No story spoilers: this part only covers what the relationship changes, not what happens.

This page used to say nobody knew yet, because PC Gamer had played the story once and kept the relationship high almost the whole way. Part of the answer is in now:

  • It shapes your ending. GameSpot says the best ending needs the couple at the top relationship level and their shared savings maxed out at $2 million. Max out the relationship but not the savings and you get a good ending, though not the best one. What a low relationship leads to is still being tested, and GameSpot expects one ending is tied to hitting neither goal.
  • Some scenes play out differently. Rockstar senior design director Luke Howard told Game Informer that a number of story scenes were recorded in more than one version, depending on the relationship and your criminal profile. GamesRadar+ reports the changes go from small touches to bigger differences. In one case, Jason takes Lucia aside after a robbery to see how she’s doing, and the feel of that talk depends on where the two of them stand.
  • A cold relationship doesn’t cost you any story. If the romance fades, Jason and Lucia stay partners in crime and you still play the whole thing. The more romantic versions of scenes are layered on top.

For how many endings there are and what each one needs, see how many endings GTA 6 has. To fill the savings account, see how to deposit at the bank.

Can you romance anyone else?

No. Jason and Lucia’s relationship is the only one the game keeps track of. In a statement to Shacknews, quoted by PC Gamer, Rockstar said everything in the game is there to support the couple’s story. That’s why there’s no way to cheat on Lucia or get together with Mandy. More on that in can you cheat on Lucia or romance Mandy?.

Quick answers

How many relationship levels are there in GTA 6?

Four: Running on empty (1/4), Partners in Crime (2/4), Crazy in Love (3/4) and Hot Together (4/4).

How do you know the relationship went up in GTA 6?

You hear a chime and a +♥ shows up on the right side of the screen. That's the only signal.

What makes you lose hearts with Lucia?

Failing a joint mission, ignoring your partner for too long, some mission choices they disagree with, and running them over with your car.

Does the relationship affect the ending in GTA 6?

Yes. According to GameSpot, the best ending needs the relationship maxed out and $2 million in the couple's savings. A maxed relationship without the full savings gets you a good ending, but not the best one.
