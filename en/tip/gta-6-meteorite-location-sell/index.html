GTA 6 Meteorite Location: Where to Find and Sell It

The GTA 6 meteorite crashed into a campsite on the east side of Mount Kalaga, in Lummox County. Grab it with Triangle and sell it on the Lifeinvader marketplace or at a pawn shop.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 6:09 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The GTA 6 meteorite is sitting in a wrecked campsite on the east side of Mount Kalaga, the big mountain in Lummox County at the top of the map. You can pick up a chunk of the rock and sell it later. Robbin Rams and Milo both found the same spot on video.

Where the meteorite is

  • County: Lummox County, in the far north of the map.
  • Mountain: Mount Kalaga. Robbin Rams found the meteorite on the right-hand (east) side of the mountain.
  • Getting there: Milo says to walk east down the hill. Robbin Rams flew in by helicopter and spotted the crash site while circling the mountain.
  • What to look for: flattened tents, knocked-over camping chairs and a crater glowing purple in the middle. It looks like someone was camping there when the rock came down.

The only other things lying around are a few bottles. Robbin Rams searched the area for more loot and found nothing else.

Mount Chiliad or Mount Kalaga?

In his video, Robbin Rams calls the place “Mount Chiliad”. That’s the mountain from GTA San Andreas and GTA 5. In GTA 6, the big northern mountain is Mount Kalaga, inside Mount Kalaga National Park in Lummox County.

Robbin Rams also says he’s in “Lumas County”, which is Lummox, and Milo places the crash site in Lummox County too. You can see all five counties in the GTA 6 map guide.

How to pick it up

  1. Walk up to the rock in the middle of the crater.
  2. Press Triangle on PS5 (Y on Xbox).
  3. A gold box icon with the meteorite shows up, and your character slips the rock into a pocket.

After that, Robbin Rams couldn’t find the meteorite anywhere in his inventory, and the help pages in the menu don’t mention it. ZacCoxTV had the same problem with the meteorite from the UFO house. So far, nobody has shown where the item ends up.

Where to sell it

  • Lifeinvader marketplace: right after grabbing the meteorite at the UFO house, ZacCoxTV got a line in the game explaining that it’s a piece of a meteorite, that plenty of them are scattered around, and that people on the Lifeinvader marketplace pay good money for them.
  • Pawn shop: ZacCoxTV says you can also take one to a pawn shop, but he doesn’t say which. Hamlet Pawn, near Jason’s house, is the one players use for selling collectible cards, as covered in the collectible cards guide.

None of the videos show the sale going through or how much it pays. Until someone does, the value of a meteorite is unknown.

The other meteorites

  • UFO house in Bellville: there’s a meteorite under the owner’s bed. To get inside, you have to break in through the garage.
  • Crater at the top of Mount Kalaga: ZacCoxTV showed a crater with a freshly fallen meteorite, still smoking, at the summit, next to a ring of stones nobody has figured out yet. He doesn’t mention the campsite, so it may be the same spot as the one Robbin Rams and Milo found, or a second one close by.

Both Robbin Rams and ZacCoxTV say it reminds them of the meteorites in Red Dead Redemption 2. For more aliens, UFOs and other secrets, see the GTA 6 easter eggs list.

Quick answers

Where is the meteorite in GTA 6?

In a wrecked campsite on the east side of Mount Kalaga, in Lummox County at the top of the map. There's another one under the bed in the UFO house in Bellville.

Where do you sell the meteorite in GTA 6?

On the Lifeinvader marketplace through your phone, or at a pawn shop, according to ZacCoxTV. No video has shown the price yet.

Is Mount Chiliad in GTA 6?

No. Robbin Rams uses the GTA 5 name in his video, but the mountain he's on is Mount Kalaga, in Lummox County.
