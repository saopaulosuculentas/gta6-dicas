GTA 6 Black Eagle Import M306 Location (Free Suppressed Rifle)

The Black Eagle Import M306 TMR is hidden in the warehouse beside the Black Eagle gun store, near Griffin Shooting Range. Here are both ways into the room.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 22, 10:40 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The Black Eagle Import M306 TMR is in a hidden room inside the warehouse next to the Black Eagle gun store, west of Vice City. It’s a semi-auto marksman rifle with a suppressor and scope already attached. WiLLiSGaming, Arekkz Gaming and Vexies have all picked it up.

Map location

  1. Zoom out until you can read LEONIDA.
  2. Look slightly below the space between the I and the D, as Vexies shows.
  3. Find the Black Eagle store down the road from Griffin Shooting Range. The warehouse is the big building to its right.

Before you go in

  • Save your game. Staff will call the police, and a surrounded building makes the rifle very hard to reach.
  • Cover your face so you’re harder to identify.
  • Deal with the three ground floor workers. Arekkz threatens them onto their knees and stays off the upper floor.

Route 1: the forklift

This is how Arekkz Gaming and Vexies opened the room:

  1. Go through to the back storage area.
  2. Hotwire the forklift.
  3. Slide the forks under the pallets blocking the room. The left stick raises and lowers them.
  4. Clear the pallets (Arekkz counted three) and walk in.

Route 2: the gap, as Lucia

WiLLiSGaming squeezed through a narrow gap on the left as you enter the warehouse. According to the channel, Jason is too big to fit. The rifle is in a case on the other side.

What else is in the warehouse

  • A regular rocket launcher. It isn’t a unique weapon, so you lose it when you die. See rocket launcher locations.
  • An office safe with a pistol. Vexies got it open, and in the video the door and the safe use different codes. We’ve only heard those numbers in the voiceover and haven’t checked them on screen, so they aren’t printed here.

While you’re there

The Conspiracist Type 9 is in a bunker across the road from the same shooting range. The M306 is a unique variant, so a lost one can be recovered from the Ammu-Nation locker. See how to store weapons in your trunk.

Quick answers

Where is the Black Eagle M306 in GTA 6?

In the warehouse next to the Black Eagle gun store west of Vice City, down the road from Griffin Shooting Range.

Do you need to play as Lucia?

For the narrow gap, yes, according to WiLLiSGaming. The other route is to move the pallets blocking the room with a forklift.

Does the Black Eagle M306 have a suppressor?

Yes, it comes with a suppressor and a scope already fitted.
