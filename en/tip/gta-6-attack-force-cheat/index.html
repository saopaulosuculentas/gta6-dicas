GTA 6 Attack Force Cheat: Bullets and Punches Launch Targets

The GTA 6 attack force cheat is Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Down. Bullets and melee attacks launch targets on impact. Unlock it when you beat the main story.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 10:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series
PS5
Xbox Series
How to activate
Open the in-game phone (Up on the D-pad), hold L1 + R1 (LB + RB on Xbox) and enter the sequence on the D-pad.

What it does: Bullets and melee attacks launch targets on impact.

The attack force cheat in GTA 6 is Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Down. What it does: bullets and melee attacks launch targets on impact. It is one of the few cheats that is ready as soon as the story ends.

How to enter it

  1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
  2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
  3. Without letting go, enter on the D-pad: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Down.

The sequence only uses the D-pad, so it is the same on PS5 and Xbox.

How to unlock it

According to GamesRadar+, you need to beat the main story. Progress you made during the story counts.

What you need to know

  • No cheat works before you beat the main story (91 missions, according to PC Gamer). After that, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the pause menu.
  • Using any cheat turns off trophies, achievements and saving until you quit the game. Save first.
  • Exploding balls uses the same sequence except for the last press.
  • The sequence matches in GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer, so the badge is “Confirmed”.
