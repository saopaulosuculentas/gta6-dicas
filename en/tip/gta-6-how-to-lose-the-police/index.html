How to Lose the Police in GTA 6: Heat, Search Zones and Disguises

To lose the cops in GTA 6, get out of the search zone and stop matching your description: respray your car at Pay 'n' Spray, change outfits or get a haircut. Watch out for stolen car trackers.

Confirmed · 15 sourcesUpdated Nov 23, 8:05 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The police in GTA 6 don’t just chase you: they learn what you look like. They remember your car, your weapon and your outfit, and keep searching the area until you stop matching that description. Losing them means getting out of the search zone and changing your look. GameSpot, PC Gamer and DPJ explained how it works, and MrDalekJD, KhrazeGaming and GamingBolt showed how to wipe each clue.

How the search works

According to GameSpot, when someone sees you commit a crime the police get called. A small blue circle shows up on the map: the area cops will search. If they spot you, it turns red and gets bigger.

That’s GTA 6’s heat system. The police put out your description over the radio, for example “white male in his 30s, driving a yellow Ganado”, and it shows up on the heat map.

MrBossFTW frames the colors a little differently: the zone stays blue while you haven’t been tied to the crime, and turns red once the police have heat icons on you. Either way, it’s centered on the spot where the crime happened.

The cops aren’t on your minimap

If you’re used to GTA 5, this one hurts: patrol cars no longer show up on the minimap, so you can’t just steer around them. According to MrDalekJD, the police now track you down through witnesses and cameras. Before you do something shady, look around: how many people can see you, and is there a camera on that building? The fewer eyes on you, the less the cops have to work with. Mr Lawrence makes the same point: with no patrol cars on the radar, every getaway gets a bit harder.

Slap the phone out of their hand

According to MrBossFTW, if someone you’ve upset pulls out their phone to call the police, you can walk up and press Square (X on Xbox). Your character slaps the phone away and the call never goes through. It stops working once a crowd has seen you, since somebody will dial before you can reach everyone.

The heat icons: what the police know

Next to your wanted stars you get a row of icons showing what the police have on you. Each icon you clear is one less thing they can use to spot you.

What they know How to clear it
Your outfit Change clothes at a store, at home or from your trunk.
Your appearance Put on or take off sunglasses, a hat or a mask. A new haircut works too.
Your vehicle Ditch it or respray it at a Pay ‘n’ Spray.
A visible weapon Put your gun away (GamingBolt).
CCTV footage Destroy the recording at the scene. GamingBolt warns the alarm will go off sooner or later.
Both of you did it Split Jason and Lucia up. MrDalekJD says this icon shows up later in the game.

What the star colors mean

Your wanted stars change color and shape depending on what the police know. MrBossFTW breaks down all five:

Stars What it means
White outline The police know a crime happened, but not that it was you.
Solid white They know it was you and they can see you. You’re being chased.
Gray They know it was you but lost sight of you. They’re still searching, so get out of the search zone.
Red outline A crime happened in the area you’re in, but you no longer match the description. They won’t recognize you right away, though they’re on alert.
Solid red They know you committed a crime in this area. They’ve lost you for now, but nearby cops are looking for someone who matches how you look right now.

Hollow stars

KhrazeGaming lands on the same reading: when the stars are hollow (just an outline, red or white), the police don’t have your description. You can even hang around in the search area, as long as you don’t do anything suspicious.

He showed it after a store robbery. He’d parked behind the building where nobody saw, so the cops only had his face and his outfit. At his trunk he swapped outfits and took off his sunglasses and cap, both icons disappeared, and he could walk back into the red zone without anyone bothering him.

Don’t walk around with a rifle out

Carrying a big gun in the open draws attention in GTA 6.

  • KhrazeGaming explains that two long guns don’t both fit on your back, so one ends up in your hands. People nearby get nervous and walk off.
  • GamingBolt adds that machine guns and shotguns in plain sight make bystanders react and even call the cops. If you want to stay low-key, carry a pistol.

KhrazeGaming turns this into a trick before a robbery: he walks around with a long gun out until the witnesses leave, then puts everything back in the trunk before going in.

Step by step to lose them

  1. Get out of the search circle and stay out until your heat fades.
  2. Check the icons next to your stars to see what they have on you.
  3. Change the car: take it to a Pay ‘n’ Spray and pick Respray in a different color. According to MrDalekJD, a fresh paint job is enough for patrol cars to drive by without noticing you. Mr Lawrence says a respray costs $200, which can wipe out most of a small store robbery’s take. Swapping cars costs nothing.
  4. Change your outfit: a clothing store or Ammu-Nation works, sunglasses and hats included.
  5. Change your face: get a haircut at a salon or a tattoo. GamingBolt also suggests a mask.
  6. On the spot: hold L1 (LB on Xbox) and press R1 (RB) to take off pieces like your hat or change how you wear them, DPJ showed. MrDalekJD uses the same shortcut.
  7. Put the gun away and, if Lucia is with you, split up.

MrDalekJD’s habit: put on sunglasses and a cap before you do anything dodgy. That way you always have something to take off when you need to vanish.

When they lose your description, you’ll hear the radio say they lost sight of you.

Stars that just wouldn’t go away?

It may have been a bug. GTA 6’s first patch, released on November 23, fixes an issue where players couldn’t lose their wanted level, according to Rockstar’s notes as reported by Kotaku. If you did everything in the steps above and the stars stuck around, update the game. See everything else the patch changed.

You can’t start a mission while wanted

In Bombastic’s video, the game told him he couldn’t start a mission with the cops on his tail. If a mission marker is nearby, lose them first and then head over.

Mod shops won’t take you either

Tuning shops are off the table too. During Elanip’s stream, with the police after him, he pulled up to a mod shop and the game turned him away: no entry inside a heat area while you’re in a known vehicle. He headed to a Pay ‘n’ Spray instead. More on tuning in how to customize your car.

Escape by boat

With five stars right at the start of the game, JuiceHead got away by stealing a boat and following the coastline until the cops dropped off. He had Accessible Combat on, which cuts the damage you take, and soaked up a lot of shots with no armor. He says you can switch it on mid-chase from the bottom of the Gameplay settings, and that it doesn’t turn off achievements. The details are in how to make GTA 6 easier.

Red lights won’t get you a star

Milo had seen on TikTok that cops chase you for any traffic violation, like running a red. He tried it right in front of a patrol car and the officer didn’t react at all.

Keep spare outfits in your trunk

BennyCentral, KultZ, KhrazeGaming and GamingBolt all give the same advice: keep a couple of outfits in your car’s trunk so you can pull over, change and leave the police with the wrong description. BennyCentral notes you can manage what’s in your trunk at any clothing store that has a changing room, and KhrazeGaming loads his from the wardrobe at the safehouse.

Stolen car trackers

A stolen car stays stolen until you register it, DPJ reminds. Some come with trackers:

  • Yellow tracker: the cops locate the car if you stop for a few seconds.
  • Red tracker: it pings the police if you slow down. Keep your foot down.

PC Gamer adds that a red (advanced) tracker beeps when you slow down and gives you a few seconds to speed back up. See how to steal cars in GTA 6.

Raising the heat on purpose

After the story, there are cheats to raise your heat level and your wanted level. There’s no cheat to remove stars.

Quick answers

How do you lose your wanted level in GTA 6?

Stay outside the search zone until your heat drops, and change your look: respray the car at Pay 'n' Spray, change clothes, or get a haircut or tattoo.

What is heat in GTA 6?

It's what the police know about you: your look, outfit and car. They radio out your description, and the more you match it, the longer they keep looking.

How do you get rid of trackers in GTA 6?

You can't remove them on the spot. With a yellow tracker, don't stop; with a red one, keep your speed up so it can't ping the police.

What do hollow stars mean in GTA 6?

According to KhrazeGaming, hollow stars (just the outline, red or white) mean the police don't have your description. You can stay in the area as long as you don't act suspicious.
