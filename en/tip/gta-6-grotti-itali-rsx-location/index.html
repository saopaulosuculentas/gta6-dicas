Where to Find the Grotti Itali RSX in GTA 6: Vespucci Island Garage

The Grotti Itali RSX sits in a garage at the north tip of Vespucci Island in the Rialto Islands. It's alarmed, has an advanced tracker and needs the key cloner.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:43 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The Grotti Itali RSX is parked in a garage at the north tip of Vespucci Island, one of the Rialto Islands just off Vice City. That’s the same island where you find Paul Walker’s car. Two channels went to the same house and found the same black and white RSX: Imack Studios during the day and KingAlexHD at night.

Where it is

  • Island: Vespucci Island in the Rialto Islands. On Imack Studios’ map it sits east of Catalan Island, joined to it by a bridge, with Casanova Island and Salton Memorial Island nearby.
  • Spot: the north tip of the island, at a house on the bend where the road loops around the end of the island.
  • Out front: a wooden gate, with a white luxury car parked on the driveway.

Imack Studios points out that nearly every house on this island has something expensive in the garage, so it’s worth a slow drive around.

Getting to the car

  1. Park outside the house and hop the wooden fence next to the gate.
  2. Walk around to the side door of the garage, the one with small glass panes.
  3. Pick Break Open and head inside.
  4. The Itali RSX is right there, next to a yellow ladder leaning on the wall.

What Waink It says

Both videos scan the car and get the same card:

Field Result
Access Key Cloner
Security Alarmed
Tracker Advanced

At the driver’s door you get two prompts: Clone Key or Smash Window. Smashing the window isn’t enough, because a car that needs the cloner won’t start on a hotwire. The cloner unlocks later in the story; how to steal cars in GTA 6 covers when you get it.

The advanced tracker is the real headache. Imack Studios warns that it makes this one much harder to get away with, since it gives away where the car is. Neither video actually drives the RSX out of the garage.

More expensive cars on the same island

Imack Studios stopped at two more houses on Vespucci Island:

  • A red supercar behind the fence of another house. It also offers Clone Key or Smash Window. His advice is to jump the fence without breaking anything so you don’t draw attention.
  • A Furia on the driveway of a mansion close by. Its card matches the RSX: key cloner, alarm and advanced tracker. That garage door opens with a lock minigame where you find the sweet spot and fill the circle fast. According to Imack Studios, there are collectible cards on a table inside. See what they sell for in collectible cards.

Randy’s wish list points to a different Furia, at a mansion in Peacock Estates.

Quick answers

Where is the Grotti Itali RSX in GTA 6?

In the garage of a house at the north tip of Vespucci Island, in the Rialto Islands, right where the road bends. It's the same island as Paul Walker's car.

How do you steal the Itali RSX in GTA 6?

With the key cloner. Waink It shows it's alarmed and has an advanced tracker, so hotwiring won't work.

Is the Itali RSX there at night in GTA 6?

Yes. Imack Studios found it around 2 pm in-game and KingAlexHD found it at night in the same garage.
