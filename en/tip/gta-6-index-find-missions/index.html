GTA 6 Index: How to Find Missions, Shops and Set a Destination

In GTA 6, the map index opens with Right on the D-pad and splits into 9 tabs, from Story to Food. Here's what each tab lists, how to set a destination and how Quick Navigation works.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 2:38 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s map fills up with icons fast, and the easiest way to find what you want is the index. It sorts missions, robberies, shops and activities into tabs. This guide is based on GamesRadar+, and Kotaku describes the same Quick Navigation shortcut.

How to open it

  1. Open the map with the touchpad (PS5).
  2. Press Right on the D-pad. The index bar shows up on the side of the screen.
  3. Pick a tab and hover over each entry to read more about it.

The 9 index tabs

Tab Icon What it lists
Personal arrow Your vehicles, residence, owned garages and ATMs for cash deposits
Story diary Story missions; the ones under “Optional” can be done any time
Robberies cash sack Discovered banks, vehicle drop-offs, stick-ups, gang hideouts and more
Activities weights Races, shooting ranges, golf, hunting, fishing, gyms and clubs
Exploration magnifying glass Points of interest, optional quests, stunt jumps, base jumps and pawn shop collectors
Vehicles car Mod shops, Pay ‘n’ Spray, gas stations, transport hubs, ferries and your unlocked garages
Weapons gun Ammu-Nation stores, split into Everyday, Premium, Hunting and Pawn
Style shirt Clothing stores, tattoo parlors, hair and makeup for Jason and Lucia
Food knife and fork Bars, cafes, convenience stores, fast food and liquor stores

Anything you just discovered shows up in the index with an orange diamond.

How to set a destination

  • From the map or index: hover over the task and press X. It becomes your active destination and the GPS shows the route.
  • Quick Navigation, no map needed: while roaming, hold Down on the D-pad. Time slows down and nearby points of interest appear (missions, gas stations, shops). Press R3 on a marker to set the route. Handy when you’re low on fuel.
  • Extra markers: you can only have one active destination. You can still drop as many markers as you like: with the map open, point at a spot and press Square.

Tip

Between chapters, check the Robberies and Exploration tabs. That’s where banks, gang hideouts and collectors show up, and they pay well. See also how to make money in GTA 6 and the beginner tips.

Quick answers

How do you open the index in GTA 6?

Open the map (touchpad on PS5) and press Right on the D-pad. The index bar shows up on the side, split into tabs.

Where are my missions in GTA 6?

In the index, open the Story tab (the diary icon). Story missions come first; the ones under Optional can be done any time.

Can you set more than one waypoint in GTA 6?

Only one active destination at a time. You can still drop as many map markers as you like by pressing Square on a location.

What does the orange diamond mean in the GTA 6 index?

It marks something you just discovered. Check the index now and then so you don't miss new stuff.
