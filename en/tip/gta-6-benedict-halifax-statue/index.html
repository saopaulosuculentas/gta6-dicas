Should You Give the Statue to Benedict Halifax in GTA 6?

Go ahead and give Benedict Halifax the statue in Dubious Provenance, Chapter 3. Refusing only slows you down: the mission plays out the same and you end up handing it over anyway.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:47 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Go ahead and give the statue to Benedict Halifax. According to PC Gamer, the mission plays out the same way no matter what you pick. Refusing only drags things out, because you’ll have to drive back to Capri later and give it to him anyway.

The choice comes up in Dubious Provenance, the 4th mission of Chapter 3.

How the mission starts

  1. It kicks off after Cal comes to the Bayside apartment you’re borrowing from Boobie to look at the hard drive.
  2. Once Cal leaves, a statuette icon shows up at the closet door. That’s the bronze statuette you picked up along with the hard drive back in Chapter 1.
  3. Interact with it and head to Capri, a ritzy town on the other side of the map.
  4. At the antiques store, the owner, Evelyn Andrews, turns the piece down. In Vice City Standard’s video she says it’s either a very good fake or stolen, and threatens to call the authorities.
  5. Outside, Benedict Halifax approaches the couple behind her back. He introduces himself as someone who authenticates and sells art, hands over his card and offers to appraise the statue.

Where the mission sits in the story is in the mission list.

The choice: give or reject

When the decision comes, the screen goes grey and two options appear. In Vice City Standard’s video, played on Xbox, they show up as Give (A) and Reject (X).

Choice What happens
Give Halifax leaves you a watch as collateral and then disappears for a while. You have to call him several times to track him down again
Reject You keep the statue, but the mission stalls. To move on, you go back to Capri later and give it to him

The watch he offers is a 1972 Crowex Chronograph, which he describes as very valuable. In the same conversation he identifies the statue as a rare bronze Venus, if it’s genuine.

Why you should just hand it over

The outcome is the same either way. Refusing only adds an extra trip back to Capri. If you want to keep the story moving, give him the statue the first time and then keep calling Halifax until he picks up.

Does it change the ending?

Nobody knows yet. GameSpot lists trusting Halifax as one of the choices that might matter at the end of the story, with no confirmation. What’s known about the endings is in how many endings GTA 6 has.

Another story choice where the next scene barely changes is whether to kill or spare Arnold in Chapter 1.

Quick answers

Should I give Halifax the statue in GTA 6?

Yes. According to PC Gamer, the mission ends the same way either way, and refusing just means driving back to Capri later to hand it over.

Which mission has Benedict Halifax?

Dubious Provenance, the 4th mission of Chapter 3. It starts from the statue in the closet of the Bayside apartment after Cal's visit.

What does Halifax give you for the statue?

A watch as collateral, the 1972 Crowex Chronograph, which he leaves with you while he appraises the piece.

Does giving Halifax the statue change the ending?

Unknown. GameSpot thinks it's possible this choice matters later, but nothing has been confirmed.
