GTA 6 Radio Stations and Songs: Every Station, Host and Track Revealed So Far

Flash FM and V-Rock alone hold 150 songs in GTA 6, and Weird Al hosts Vice City FM. Here are the stations, the hosts named in the credits and the songs on each station.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 22, 10:40 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 shipped with far more radio than Rockstar showed before launch. Back then we had 6 stations: The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM, Afro Bank FM, Back Country Radio, Cocoteo FM and Dirty South Classics. Now that the game is out, the official credits name more stations and hosts, including “Weird Al” Yankovic on Vice City FM, and Kotaku counted 150 songs on Flash FM and V-Rock alone.

The 6 stations Rockstar showed before launch

Each one got a short preview from Rockstar that opens with a quick intro from the hosts:

Station Hosts
The Chamber 106.6 DJ Tom and DJ Kerry
Flash FM Robyn and Alex
Afro Bank FM Burna and Palmsy
Back Country Radio MW and Delta Dawne
Cocoteo FM El Martillo and La Gata
Dirty South Classics Trick and Trina

That isn’t the whole lineup. In the gameplay shot at the top of this page, the car’s radio wheel also lists Emotion 98.3 and Gloriana Blues Radio, which weren’t among the six. The credits add even more, as you’ll see below.

More stations and hosts from the credits

Rockstar put the full credits online on launch day, and the radio section is packed with famous names. Here’s what PC Gamer picked out:

Station Host
Vice City FM (nonstop ’80s) “Weird Al” Yankovic as DJ Al, alongside actress Brigitte Nielsen
Flash FM (pop) Robyn and model Alex Consani
Back Country Radio Lana Del Rey
Cocoteo FM Bad Bunny
Dirty South Classics Trina, Trick Daddy and Gucci Mane
Kaleidoscope FM Oscar winner Jeremy Irons

Two things to keep in mind:

  • In Rockstar’s preview, Back Country Radio was hosted by MW and Delta Dawne. PC Gamer lists Lana Del Rey on that station too, and it isn’t clear yet whether she shares the show with them.
  • Bad Bunny is El Martillo on Cocoteo FM, as covered in our GTA 6 cast guide.

Weird Al confirmed the gig himself on social media. Besides hosting Vice City FM, he said he wrote some of the in-game commercials and voices the Burger Shot ads.

Over on talk radio you’ll hear Jason Sudeikis (Ted Lasso), Janelle James (Abbott Elementary), John Leguizamo and comedian Stavros Halkias, who plays a conservative talk host. Keith Richards is in the credits as well, though PC Gamer doesn’t say which station he’s on.

Flash FM and V-Rock: 150 songs

Both stations first appeared in GTA Vice City, but they’ve grown a lot. Kotaku ran the numbers using Rockstar’s credits:

Station In GTA Vice City In GTA 6
Flash FM (pop) 15 songs 80 songs
V-Rock (classic rock) 17 songs 70 songs

That’s 150 songs between just two stations. Vice City had 106 across every station combined.

Two secret playlists (needed for 100%)

PC Gamer found that two playlists start locked and open up when you scan posters with WAiNK.

The Sniper of La Perle, hosted by Kodak Black, needs four posters around La Perle and Crosstown:

  1. TWA Ponya Tattoo: on the small table by the door, to your right.
  2. Drip Drops: on the register counter.
  3. Aunt Zola’s Soul Food: on the corkboard facing the register.
  4. Jack of Hearts: in the second floor recording studio, on the table next to a fan. It only opens in Chapter 3.

The Vault Radio, hosted by DJ Pretzel, needs one poster: at The Vault club near Downtown Vice City, on the back wall past the entrance corridor.

Quick answers

What radio stations are in GTA 6?

Rockstar previewed 6 before launch: The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM, Afro Bank FM, Back Country Radio, Cocoteo FM and Dirty South Classics. The game has more, including V-Rock, Vice City FM, Kaleidoscope FM and Emotion 98.3.

Who made the GTA 6 soundtrack?

The official score includes singles by Justice, such as Flower in a Hurricane and Pusher, according to PC Gamer.

Will every one of these songs be on the GTA 6 radio?

PC Gamer's pre-launch list came from official footage, so there was no guarantee. Several have now turned up on air: Mine O' Mine and Sexy Magic are on Flash FM, and Love Is a Long Road is on V-Rock, per Kotaku.

How many songs are in GTA 6?

There's no official total yet. Flash FM (80) and V-Rock (70) alone add up to 150 songs, according to Kotaku, which is more than every station in GTA Vice City combined.
