GTA 6 Map: All 5 Counties and What's in Each One

The GTA 6 map is split into 5 counties: Vice-Dale, Leonard, Kelly, Lummox and Mariana. Here are the main spots in each, from Vice City and the airport to the military base and Key Lento.

Unverified · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 22, 10:40 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The GTA 6 map of Leonida is split into 5 counties: Vice-Dale, Leonard, Kelly, Lummox and Mariana. Each one has at least one landmark worth knowing, from the packed streets of Vice City down to the Key Lento islands where the story opens.

It’s a lot of ground. Rockstar called the map twice the size of GTA 5’s, and PC Gamer says it feels even bigger than that: crossing it takes ages, even in a plane. According to PC Gamer, Rockstar also said it beats Red Dead Redemption 2’s map, and the two older maps combined. On November 19, 2026, Rockstar put the full map up on its website in high resolution (more below).

Full official map

The official map lives on Rockstar’s site: full high-resolution map of Leonida. It’s a single large poster-style image covering Vice City and the rest of the state. PC Gamer notes that it shows the small roads, highways and neighborhoods.

It’s handy even if you’re already deep into the game. PC Gamer gives three reasons:

  • The in-game map starts out under a fog of war that only lifts as you explore.
  • Even with everything uncovered, you can’t fit the whole map on screen no matter how far you zoom out. PC Gamer’s Chris Livingston cleared it all after 100 hours of play and still couldn’t see it in one view.
  • Smaller roads and neighborhood names only show up once you zoom in close.

From what PC Gamer shows, Rockstar’s release is an image rather than an interactive map. For setting waypoints and finding places, you still use the in-game map (see the end of this page).

All 5 counties at a glance

County Main locations
Vice-Dale County Vice City, Vice Beach, VCIA Airport
Leonard County Ambrosia, Lake Leonida
Kelly County Port Gellhorn, Gellhorn Raceway, Capri
Lummox County Mount Kalaga National Park
Mariana County Mile Zero Air Station, Key Lento

Vice-Dale County

  • Vice City: the centerpiece, packed with jewelry, cars and drugs to steal. It’s by far the densest part of the map.
  • Vice Beach: a long run of beachfront east of downtown.
  • VCIA Airport: where the planes sit until somebody takes one. Our airport guide covers how to get in.

Leonard County

  • Ambrosia: the name sounds pleasant, the place less so. It’s home to a biker gang and a sugar refinery.
  • Lake Leonida: the largest inland lake on the map, right next to Ambrosia.

Kelly County

  • Port Gellhorn: a former tourist spot gone to seed, with dead malls and grimy hotels. These days the local money comes from drugs and drinks. The town also shows up in our fence locations guide.
  • Gellhorn Raceway: the place for track racing and drag racing.
  • Capri: high-end shopping, expensive homes and police with zero patience for street performers.

Lummox County

  • Mount Kalaga National Park: the big draw in northern Leonida, with room to hunt, fish and go off-road.

Mariana County

  • Mile Zero Air Station: the military base in the southern keys and the easiest place to grab a tank. Walking in uninvited earns you a steep Wanted level. See how to get into the base.
  • Key Lento: a strip of outlying islands. This is where you start the game as Jason.

How long it takes to fly across the map

PC Gamer timed 4 minutes and 18 seconds in a Mammoth Dodo seaplane from the southern tip of the islands to the far north, just under an hour of game time. Faster planes probably exist. Steer around the military base on the way, or you’ll get four wanted stars.

Finding your way

The in-game map lets you set destinations and look up shops and missions. Our map index guide walks through it.

Quick answers

How many counties are on the GTA 6 map?

Five: Vice-Dale, Leonard, Kelly, Lummox and Mariana, according to PC Gamer.

Where can I see the full official GTA 6 map?

On Rockstar's website. On November 19 it posted the full map of Leonida in high resolution, with the small roads, highways and neighborhoods. The link is in the Full official map section of this page.

Is there an interactive GTA 6 map?

The official map Rockstar posted is one big image, not an interactive map. In the game, the pause menu map lets you zoom, set waypoints and look up shops and missions through the Index, but it starts out covered in fog until you explore.

Is the GTA 6 map bigger than GTA 5's?

Yes. Rockstar says it's twice the size, and per PC Gamer it's bigger than the GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 maps put together. It takes ages to cross even by plane.
