GTA 6 Criminal Profile Explained: The Honor System and All 5 Levels

GTA 6's criminal profile tracks how many people you kill. There are 5 levels, from Professional to Irredeemable, each with its own bonus. Jason and Lucia have separate profiles, and the last one sticks.

Confirmed · 4 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:56 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s criminal profile is the system that tracks how you treat people in the world, much like the honor system in Red Dead Redemption 2. It comes down almost entirely to how often you kill. Wounding or incapacitating enemies keeps your profile clean, while killing cops, civilians and defenseless animals drags it down. Jason and Lucia each have their own profile, and according to GameSpot both of them start at Hardened. Playing non-lethally lifts you to Professional, while a high body count drags you to Violent, then Psycho, and finally Irredeemable.

The five levels

Every level comes with an emoji-style “face with horns” icon that appears on screen.

JorRaptor explains how to read it. After you do something, a demon icon often pops up on the right side of the screen. A yellow or orange one means you were careful: few kills, or you got away without being spotted. A purple one means you went after civilians, cops and every enemy in sight. Those colors line up with the table below.

Level Color How you get there Bonus
Professional Yellow Only the violence you need, incapacitate instead of kill, stay undetected Non-lethal takedowns and kind acts toward citizens fill your focus meter
Hardened Orange Kill enemies, kill citizens only in self-defense, don’t worry about being seen Same as Professional
Violent Purple Skip non-lethal options, kill enemies and police, kill citizens at random Headshots fill your focus meter
Psycho Light gray Kill lots of cops, regularly kill helpless citizens, kill pets and defenseless animals (hunting doesn’t count) Headshots fill focus, but you can’t surrender to the police
Irredeemable Dark gray Stay Psycho for too long No bonus listed

Focus is the game’s bullet time. If you’ve finished the story, there’s even an unlimited focus cheat.

What earns focus at each level

At Professional and Hardened, focus comes from non-lethal takedowns and small good deeds. GameSpot’s examples are rescuing civilians, petting animals and even dropping trash in a bin. At Violent and Psycho, headshots are what fill the meter. For other ways to top it up, see how to increase focus.

Irredeemable is forever

Across the first four levels, your profile moves up and down as you play. Irredeemable is different: once you hit it, it stays. Rockstar says it will have a lasting effect on Jason and Lucia, but nobody knows yet what that means.

You do get a warning. GameSpot says a split appears in the Psycho icon right before you tip over into Irredeemable, so that’s your cue to stop killing. GameSpot’s advice is to avoid the rank altogether, since it changes your playthrough in a big and lasting way. If you’re curious anyway, keep a backup manual save first, ideally once the story is done or nearly done. Here’s how saving works. JorRaptor gives the same advice if you just want to cause chaos without thinking about your profile: save first, then load that save afterward.

Keep the permanent rank in mind before chasing a 6 star wanted level. Getting there means killing a lot of cops and soldiers, and PC Gamer warns that can hit your profile hard.

Staying Professional

GameSpot lists what keeps you at the top tier:

  • Stay hidden. Finish missions without being detected where you can.
  • Use non-lethal takedowns instead of killing enemies. JorRaptor shows one way to do it in a gunfight: with focus active, aim at the yellow marks that show up on an enemy. The shot hurts them badly without killing them.
  • Don’t kill civilians, and that includes running people over by accident while driving.
  • Defuse when offered. Some missions give you a prompt to calm things down, which can lead to an outcome where nobody dies.
  • Spare people who surrender when the game gives you that choice. The Arnold decision is one example of a spare prompt.

Can you shoot your way through and stay Professional?

PC Gamer’s writer finished the main story with both characters at Professional, dipping into Hardened now and then. The approach was to stay non-lethal where possible, using focus to aim for knees and elbows in gunfights.

The body count still wasn’t small. The endgame stats show 321 enemies and 58 cops killed, against 461 enemies and 107 cops incapacitated. There are also 98 animal kills on the record, and the writer suspects most were birds that refuse to get out of the way of vehicles.

Does your profile change the story or the ending?

No story spoilers: this only covers what the profile affects, not what happens.

  • Story scenes: yes. Rockstar senior design director Luke Howard told Game Informer that a number of story scenes were recorded in different versions, depending on the couple’s relationship and their criminal profile. GamesRadar+ reports the differences range from small details to bigger changes.
  • The ending: unknown so far. GameSpot says some players suspect the profile plays into the ending, but nothing confirms it. JorRaptor, for one, says in his video that it affects the endings, without showing it. What GameSpot does confirm is that the relationship and the couple’s savings decide the ending.

See what’s known in how many endings GTA 6 has, and how to raise the relationship in the romance guide.

Checking your profile

The quickest way, according to JorRaptor, is to hold D-pad Right while you play. Later on, it also shows up in the Jason & Lucia menu.

For the menu, open it and go to the Jason & Lucia tab, which GameSpot also points to. In the image above, the profile sits at the top of Jason’s page there. For more on how the game works early on, see the beginner tips.

Quick answers

What are the criminal profile levels in GTA 6?

There are five: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable. Jason and Lucia both start at Hardened, according to GameSpot. The more people you kill, the further down you go.

Is the Irredeemable profile permanent in GTA 6?

Yes. You land there by staying Psycho for too long, and you can't climb back out. A split showing up in the Psycho icon means you're close. The other four levels move up and down as you play.

How do you keep a Professional profile in GTA 6?

Keep violence to what's necessary, incapacitate enemies instead of killing them and stay undetected. Don't kill cops or civilians for no reason.

Do Jason and Lucia share a criminal profile?

No. Each of them has a criminal profile of their own.
