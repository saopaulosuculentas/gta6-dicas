GTA 6 Criminal Profile Explained: The Honor System and All 5 Levels

GTA 6's criminal profile tracks how many people you kill. There are 5 levels, from Professional to Irredeemable, each with its own bonus. Jason and Lucia have separate profiles, and the last one sticks.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 4:11 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s criminal profile is the system that tracks how you treat people in the world, much like the honor system in Red Dead Redemption 2. It comes down almost entirely to how often you kill. Wounding or incapacitating enemies keeps your profile clean, while killing cops, civilians and defenseless animals drags it down. Jason and Lucia each have their own profile.

The five levels

Every level comes with an emoji-style “face with horns” icon that appears on screen.

Level Color How you get there Bonus
Professional Yellow Only the violence you need, incapacitate instead of kill, stay undetected Non-lethal takedowns and kind acts toward citizens fill your focus meter
Hardened Orange Kill enemies, kill citizens only in self-defense, don’t worry about being seen Same as Professional
Violent Purple Skip non-lethal options, kill enemies and police, kill citizens at random Headshots fill your focus meter
Psycho Light gray Kill lots of cops, regularly kill helpless citizens, kill pets and defenseless animals (hunting doesn’t count) Headshots fill focus, but you can’t surrender to the police
Irredeemable Dark gray Stay Psycho for too long No bonus listed

Focus is the game’s bullet time. If you’ve finished the story, there’s even an unlimited focus cheat.

Irredeemable is forever

Across the first four levels, your profile moves up and down as you play. Irredeemable is different: once you hit it, it stays. Rockstar says it will have a lasting effect on Jason and Lucia, but nobody knows yet what that means.

That’s worth remembering before chasing a 6 star wanted level. Getting there means killing a lot of cops and soldiers, and PC Gamer warns that can hit your profile hard.

Can you shoot your way through and stay Professional?

PC Gamer’s writer finished the main story with both characters at Professional, dipping into Hardened now and then. The approach was to stay non-lethal where possible, using focus to aim for knees and elbows in gunfights.

The body count still wasn’t small. The endgame stats show 321 enemies and 58 cops killed, against 461 enemies and 107 cops incapacitated. There are also 98 animal kills on the record, and the writer suspects most were birds that refuse to get out of the way of vehicles.

Checking your profile

In the image above, the profile sits at the top of Jason’s page in the Jason & Lucia menu. For more on how the game works early on, see the beginner tips.

Quick answers

What are the criminal profile levels in GTA 6?

There are five: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable. The more people you kill, the further down you go.

Is the Irredeemable profile permanent in GTA 6?

Yes. You land there by staying Psycho for too long, and you can't climb back out. The other four levels move up and down as you play.

How do you keep a Professional profile in GTA 6?

Keep violence to what's necessary, incapacitate enemies instead of killing them and stay undetected. Don't kill cops or civilians for no reason.

Do Jason and Lucia share a criminal profile?

No. Each of them has a criminal profile of their own.
