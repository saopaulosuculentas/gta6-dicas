How to Rob Stores in GTA 6 Without Getting Caught: Step by Step and Payouts

To rob a store in GTA 6 without getting caught, park out of sight, clear out witnesses, hide your face and change outfits after. Early on, a register pays around $1,000.

Confirmed · 8 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 6:04 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

To rob a store in GTA 6 without getting caught, hide your car, get rid of witnesses, cover your face and change clothes as soon as you’re done. That way the cops end up with no usable description and you can move on to the next store. KhrazeGaming, Arekkz Gaming, KultZ, GamingBolt, JoblessGarrett, Mr Lawrence and TheSpazo all walked through their methods on video. According to Mr Lawrence, not every store shows up on the map, but you can rob almost any store you can walk into.

Before you go in

  • Cover your face. A cap and sunglasses already help, as KhrazeGaming showed. Mr Lawrence says a mask, hat or glasses keeps the store’s CCTV from recognizing you. Arekkz Gaming suggests a bandana, which you can buy at the gun shop. Early in the game, JoblessGarrett looked for a mask to buy and couldn’t find one.
  • Hide your car. Park behind the building or in a quiet spot nearby. KhrazeGaming says this keeps the vehicle from being tied to the robbery. Mr Lawrence likes to have a fast car or a bike ready for the getaway.
  • Keep a spare outfit in the trunk. Put an outfit together at a wardrobe, like the one at your safehouse, and store it in your car. KultZ and GamingBolt give the same advice.
  • Go at night. KultZ found it the best time: fewer people on the street, and the shop is often empty. TheSpazo noticed the same thing, with fewer cops around at night and an easier time losing them.
  • Solo or as a couple. You can rob a store as Jason, as Lucia or with both of them together. Mr Lawrence says robbing as a couple helps grow their relationship.
  • Start with a clothing store. Arekkz Gaming picks these first because you can grab a spare outfit while you’re inside. TheSpazo prefers clothing stores too; see the payouts at the bottom of the page.

Clear out the witnesses

Anyone hanging around will call the police the moment things go wrong. There are two ways to empty the place without firing a shot:

  • Provoke them: walk up to a customer and choose the Provoke option. It annoys them and they usually walk out (Arekkz Gaming). JoblessGarrett did this in a Key Lento liquor store and the customers left.
  • Carry a long gun: take your long guns out of the trunk. You can’t fit two of them on your back, so one stays in your hands. People get nervous and leave, except for the clerk (KhrazeGaming).

Be careful with the second one. GamingBolt warns that walking around with machine guns or shotguns out in the open makes people react and can even get someone to call the cops. KhrazeGaming stows his extra weapons back in the trunk before going in, so they don’t end up in his description. If you want to stay low-key, GamingBolt suggests a pistol.

The robbery, step by step

  1. Walk in with your face covered and your robbery outfit on.
  2. Aim at the clerk. The Threaten prompt shows up on screen. According to KhrazeGaming, most clerks go along with it and hand over the register cash.
  3. Or do it the Arekkz Gaming way: knock the clerk out with a couple of punches and take whatever is in the register and around the shop. To open the register yourself, Mr Lawrence presses Triangle (Y on Xbox).
  4. Take every last dollar. JoblessGarrett found only $300 at first, almost left, and then turned up more cash inside the store.
  5. Wipe the camera footage. JoblessGarrett destroyed it inside the shop. GamingBolt points out you can do this at the scene, but the alarm will go off eventually.

Vice City Dispatch, which rounds up what the reviews say, puts it in the best order: kill the alarm and the cameras before the robbery and nobody calls it in. If a camera already caught you, wipe the tapes in the security room and the police are left without a description.

Safes in the back

Some stores have a safe. Arekkz Gaming says certain clerks will tell you there’s one and hand over the combination if you threaten them with a gun. In the staff area, press L3+R3 to turn on Focus mode, which highlights things you can interact with, safes included. For more on finding the combination, see the safe codes guide.

No code? You can use explosives. Mr Lawrence warns that the blast damages some of the cash inside, while hunting for the code gives the police more time to show up. Vice City Dispatch says the same: blowing the safe gets you out faster, but with less money.

After the robbery

  • Expect 1 or 2 stars. That’s the wanted level a store robbery gives you, according to Mr Lawrence. He adds that the police will usually know your location, your car and the outfit you’re wearing.
  • Get out fast. With no witnesses nearby, it takes the clerk a while to call the police (KhrazeGaming).
  • Your car stays clean. When the cops showed up after KhrazeGaming’s robbery, they only had his face and clothes. The car parked behind the store wasn’t part of the description.
  • Change your look. Head back to the trunk, put on a different outfit and take off the cap and sunglasses. The recognition markers go away and you don’t even have to leave the area (KhrazeGaming).
  • Swap cars or respray. If the cops saw your car, switch vehicles or hit a Pay ‘n’ Spray. Mr Lawrence points out that a respray costs $200, which can eat most of what one store pays.
  • Leave the red zone. Once the stars are gone, a red heat zone stays on the map. Get out of it to lose the police for good (Mr Lawrence).
  • Bring a partner: GamingBolt recommends robbing as a pair, both masked. One keeps the crowd under control and warns you when the cops are close. If you’re carrying several bags, hand one to the other character, who can also pull the police away. Split up when you run.

To shake the cops for good, read how to lose the police. Anything that isn’t pocket cash has to go through a fence: see fence locations.

Can you rob the same store again?

Not right away. Mr Lawrence says a store you just hit shows up as closed or unavailable for a while. A small red heat area is also marked around it, and if you rob it again while that area is active, the cops get there faster. TheSpazo makes the same point about clothing stores: once you’ve robbed one, you can’t go back to it any time soon, so you have to work through several.

How much it pays

Channel What they got
KhrazeGaming Around $1,000 or a bit more per register early in the game
KultZ Close to $600 from one store; over $2,000 across several on a good night
JoblessGarrett $300 at first, plus more after searching the store
Mr Lawrence A couple hundred dollars in most registers
TheSpazo $600 to $900 from some clothing stores; about $3,100 after a string of stores, mostly in Vice City

Are clothing stores worth it?

The sources disagree here. TheSpazo calls clothing stores the best early target: some paid $600 to $900 each, and a string of stores, mostly in Vice City, got him to about $3,100. Vice City Dispatch, quoting Forbes, says the cash from a clothing store probably isn’t worth it. Payouts change from store to store, so try a few. The only place TheSpazo tells you to skip is the tattoo shop, which he calls a waste of time.

For a robbery with hidden cash, check out the parrot money at the Three Sheets liquor store.

Quick answers

How much money do you get from robbing a store in GTA 6?

It varies a lot. KhrazeGaming got around $1,000 from a register early on, while Mr Lawrence says most registers only hold a couple hundred. TheSpazo pulled $600 to $900 from some clothing stores.

How do you get customers to leave a store in GTA 6?

Talk to them and pick the Provoke option, or walk near them with a long gun in your hands. Either way, they usually leave.

How do you avoid being recognized after a store robbery in GTA 6?

Go in with a cap and sunglasses or a bandana, then change into a spare outfit from your trunk right after. Leave your car out of sight behind the store.

Do stores have safes in GTA 6?

Some do. The clerk may give up the combination if you threaten them with a gun, and Focus mode (L3+R3) highlights the safe in the back.
