How to Make Money Fast in GTA 6: Best Early and Late Game Methods

The fastest ways to make money in GTA 6: Kenton's shipwreck dives (up to $4,000), Dog Island, Donnie's packages, robbing other criminals, selling stolen cars and the Ambrosia demolition derby.

Confirmed · 16 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 11:09 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Money is tight at the start of GTA 6, and there’s no shortcut: the $250,000 money cheat only works after you beat the story. Until then, these are the methods players have shown working, with how much each one paid. For a route of early stashes packed with cash and guns, see GTA 6 secret early items.

First: chapter 1 money resets

WiLLiSGaming, who already got past chapter 2, warns that your cash goes back to zero when chapter 2 starts. The only money that carries over is the family savings: the $5,000 the story asks you to put into the BuckMe app (Jason has bad credit, so the bank gives him the app to build it). So in chapter 1, get the $5,000 and keep the story moving. Don’t spend hours grinding.

Early game (chapter 1)

Shipwreck dives with Kenton

Kenton, a scuba instructor, texts you shipwreck locations. Dive with his gear and a metal detector, collect the treasure and sell it back to him. Up to $4,000 per dive, and he pays more the more you do. Full guide: GTA 6 sunken treasure.

Dog Island

Swim with no boat in the middle of Gambit Bay until a dog on a jet ski rescues you. On its island, a dog digs up about $1,200. See the jet ski dog.

Donnie’s lost packages and Brian’s lockup

Donnie loses packages that show up on your map. Pick them up and bring them to Donnie’s trailer: according to WiLLiSGaming he pays more than Brian. Use Focus mode, because the packages have a tracker that makes a sound Focus can show you. Anything else illegal you find can be sold at Brian’s lockup (enter the code on the keypad and drop it in his strongbox).

Mailbox

Check the mailbox at Jason’s house after main missions. There’s often cash waiting, usually with a text from Donnie letting you know. ImBones points out that rewards from main missions and side quests both land there, so if you never check it, the money just sits.

KultZ lays out an easy first payday: after the mission Gone Fishing, do Lab Fees, the job listed as collecting Brian’s rent. Drop the bag in the container at Brian’s lockup in South Key Lento. Your wallet stays empty at first; give it a little while, then check the mailbox in front of the house for the $600. He notes this job only pays out once.

RV lockboxes and trading cards

Some RVs out in the wild have lockboxes, and the code is usually written on the wall. WiLLiSGaming says they almost always have good loot, like a rare sports trading card. Cards sell at pawn shops: LoDj1c got $6,000 for one at the Hamlet pawn shop. He found that card in a safe inside a building in South Vice-Dale County, near Jason’s place, along with cash and cargo worth about $10,000 in total.

The loot warehouse near Hamlet

DPJ showed the same kind of warehouse in South Vice-Dale County, near Hamlet, with everyone inside already dead: no fight needed. He found a bag with $4,800, drugs in the back of a truck, a Duke 5.56 assault rifle and the safe with the card (rotate the right stick to open it). Bring a car: your bags fill up fast and you can stash the rest in the trunk. The cash comes as marked bills, which you have to take to a fence. GrandTheftIntel hit the same warehouse and walked out with around $11,000 plus a card worth $6,000. Full walkthrough: the early money warehouse.

BennyCentral showed the same red warehouse and a way to squeeze more out of it: compare your fences on the map before you sell. Donnie’s trailer was paying $800 more than Brian’s lockup for the exact same bag, which came to about $10,000, plus the loose cash you grab on the way. The payment arrives in the mailbox outside your house.

Southside trap house

BennyCentral rates this a medium security job and a quick earner. It’s in Southside, Vice City, where the PTT Youngs gang grows weed and stashes stolen goods.

  1. Clear the yard first, then head inside.
  2. There’s cash on the desk by the money counter, weed on the kitchen counter and in the grow room, pills in the bedroom and jewelry on a table.
  3. Don’t leave without the luxury watch next to the kitchen sink.
  4. An assault rifle is lying on the bedroom floor.

He made over $3,000 in a couple of minutes. Benny doesn’t say whether it needs the Ultimate Edition; that edition has a similar bonus, the PTT Youngin$ Compound, also in Southside. See Ultimate Edition bonuses.

Read the map card first

According to BennyCentral, hovering over a robbery spot on the map brings up a card with its security level and take value. A high security warehouse, for example, pays up to $6,400. Start with the low security ones and work your way up, and use your binoculars to tag enemies from a distance before going in. JorRaptor adds that these illegal operations run on a cooldown: after a while you can rob them again, but for less loot than the first time, so hitting them early means they come back sooner. When you leave, check the icon on the map to see whether you got every valuable.

Gellhorn Raceway

If doing the same robberies over and over gets old, MrRoflWaffles suggests racing at Gellhorn Raceway, which has its own icon on the map. Renting a car costs around $500, a win pays up to $4,000 and a race takes about five minutes. It’s decent driving practice too.

Random events at sea

Stop when something weird happens. RageGamingVideos saw an explosion off the coast east of Key Lento, found a shark attacking a man, killed it with the speargun from the scuba kit and picked up beeping packages from the water: about $2,000 worth of drugs to sell.

Milo explains what to watch for: if you’re out on a boat and see another boat explode, a shark just hit it. Head for the wreckage, which is full of loot, but the shark is still around and will come for you. Get back on your boat, grab the speargun, take the shark out, and then collect everything to sell to Brian.

Gang hideouts

King Fishers gang bases are packed with contraband that sells well, and WiLLiSGaming found a unique rifle at one. Bring an empty bag and come prepared: he fought about 30 enemies.

KultZ did the payback job against the King Fishers on an island. To get there fast, steal a boat or seaplane; you’ll usually find one near any stretch of water. Clear out the gang, pocket the loose cash and bag the rest, which is mostly drugs. Drop the haul at Brian’s lockup and collect the money from your mailbox later. That one raid made him about $2,000, and he says another King Fishers job tends to pop up later, on your way to pick up Lucia.

Store holdups

You can rob convenience stores from the very start. KhrazeGaming got about $1,000 out of a register early on, and KultZ took almost $600 from one shop. KultZ likes going out late at night, when the streets are quieter and the stores are often empty. His take per night ranged from a few hundred dollars to more than $2,000 on his best nights. Wear sunglasses and a cap, and keep a spare outfit in your trunk to change into on the way out. For the safe in the back, see how to get safe codes, and to shake the cops afterward, how to lose the police.

The parrot behind the liquor store

Shirley’s rum errand leads to a liquor store with something hidden out back. The full walkthrough is in the parrot money guide.

Later in the story (PC Gamer’s list)

Hold off on banks early

BennyCentral warns against robbing banks and loan stores early on. The cash can come with dye packs, and the jammer that deals with them doesn’t unlock until later in the story, so you risk losing the haul.

Rob the robbers

Listen for sirens, alarms, gunshots and screams. When you see a bank job, jewelry store heist or armored car robbery in progress, take down the crooks running off and grab their bags. Then get out fast, because their heat becomes yours. With Jason and Lucia together you can carry more bags.

Sell stolen cars

All stolen goods (drugs, jewelry, marked bills) turn into cash at a fence. See GTA 6 fence locations.

In chapter 2 you meet Moses, a mechanic who buys older, low-profile cars and often tells you where the ones he wants are. In chapter 3, Randy pays more for flashy, expensive cars. See how to steal cars in GTA 6.

Demolition derby at Ambrosia Raceway

You win by default once every other car is wrecked, so you don’t need to drive well. The oval pays over $3,800 and the figure-eight nearly double, up to $6,300. You can repeat it as often as you like and rent a car at the track for a few hundred dollars.

Hunting for Bret

In chapter 2, Jason takes hunting jobs from his old army buddy Bret in Lummox County. He pays more for bigger animals, and there are legendary animals. PC Gamer got $2,500 for a big boar.

Rob stores through the back door

In chapter 3 you get a tool to pick locks. From then on, pick stores with a back door: sneak in, subdue the staff in the back, kill the alarm, destroy the security footage and find the safe before anyone notices. See how to get safe codes.

Protect your money

If you die or get busted with cash in your pocket, you lose it. Stash it at home. Once the bank unlocks in the chapter 1 mission Buck Me, MrDalekJD also stops at an ATM before every job, since money in your account is safe; you can only deposit at an ATM, not from your phone. See do you lose money when you die in GTA 6.

Quick answers

What is the fastest way to make money in GTA 6?

Early on, Kenton's shipwreck dives and Dog Island. Later, according to PC Gamer, robbing other criminals mid-heist, selling stolen cars and the Ambrosia Raceway demolition derby, which pays up to $6,300 a win.

Does your money reset in GTA 6 chapter 2?

According to WiLLiSGaming, yes: your cash goes back to zero when chapter 2 starts. Only the family savings, the $5,000 in the BuckMe app, carries over.

Is there a money cheat in GTA 6?

Yes, it gives $250,000, but it only works after you beat the story.

Can you race for money in GTA 6?

Yes. MrRoflWaffles races at Gellhorn Raceway, marked with an icon on the map: renting a car costs about $500, a win pays up to $4,000 and each race takes about five minutes.
