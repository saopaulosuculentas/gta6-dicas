How to Make Money Fast in GTA 6: Best Early and Late Game Methods

The fastest ways to make money in GTA 6: Kenton's shipwreck dives (up to $4,000), Dog Island, Donnie's packages, robbing other criminals, selling stolen cars and the Ambrosia demolition derby.

Confirmed · 5 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 10:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Money is tight at the start of GTA 6, and there’s no shortcut: the $250,000 money cheat only works after you beat the story. Until then, these are the methods players have shown working, with how much each one paid.

First: chapter 1 money resets

WiLLiSGaming, who already got past chapter 2, warns that your cash goes back to zero when chapter 2 starts. The only money that carries over is the family savings: the $5,000 the story asks you to put into the BuckMe app (Jason has bad credit, so the bank gives him the app to build it). So in chapter 1, get the $5,000 and keep the story moving. Don’t spend hours grinding.

Early game (chapter 1)

Shipwreck dives with Kenton

Kenton, a scuba instructor, texts you shipwreck locations. Dive with his gear and a metal detector, collect the treasure and sell it back to him. Up to $4,000 per dive, and he pays more the more you do. Full guide: GTA 6 sunken treasure.

Dog Island

Swim with no boat in the middle of Gambit Bay until a dog on a jet ski rescues you. On its island, a dog digs up about $1,200. See the jet ski dog.

Donnie’s lost packages and Brian’s lockup

Donnie loses packages that show up on your map. Pick them up and bring them to Donnie’s trailer: according to WiLLiSGaming he pays more than Brian. Use Focus mode, because the packages have a tracker that makes a sound Focus can show you. Anything else illegal you find can be sold at Brian’s lockup (enter the code on the keypad and drop it in his strongbox).

Mailbox

Check the mailbox at Jason’s house after main missions. There’s often cash waiting, usually with a text from Donnie letting you know.

RV lockboxes and trading cards

Some RVs out in the wild have lockboxes, and the code is usually written on the wall. WiLLiSGaming says they almost always have good loot, like a rare sports trading card. Cards sell at pawn shops: LoDj1c got $6,000 for one at the Hamlet pawn shop.

Gang hideouts

King Fishers gang bases are packed with contraband that sells well, and WiLLiSGaming found a unique rifle at one. Bring an empty bag and come prepared: he fought about 30 enemies.

Later in the story (PC Gamer’s list)

Rob the robbers

Listen for sirens, alarms, gunshots and screams. When you see a bank job, jewelry store heist or armored car robbery in progress, take down the crooks running off and grab their bags. Then get out fast, because their heat becomes yours. With Jason and Lucia together you can carry more bags.

Sell stolen cars

In chapter 2 you meet Moses, a mechanic who buys older, low-profile cars and often tells you where the ones he wants are. In chapter 3, Randy pays more for flashy, expensive cars. See how to steal cars in GTA 6.

Demolition derby at Ambrosia Raceway

You win by default once every other car is wrecked, so you don’t need to drive well. The oval pays over $3,800 and the figure-eight nearly double, up to $6,300. You can repeat it as often as you like and rent a car at the track for a few hundred dollars.

Hunting for Bret

In chapter 2, Jason takes hunting jobs from his old army buddy Bret in Lummox County. He pays more for bigger animals, and there are legendary animals. PC Gamer got $2,500 for a big boar.

Rob stores through the back door

In chapter 3 you get a tool to pick locks. From then on, pick stores with a back door: sneak in, subdue the staff in the back, kill the alarm, destroy the security footage and find the safe before anyone notices. See how to get safe codes.

Protect your money

If you die or get busted with cash in your pocket, you lose it. Stash it at home. See do you lose money when you die in GTA 6.

Quick answers

What is the fastest way to make money in GTA 6?

Early on, Kenton's shipwreck dives and Dog Island. Later, according to PC Gamer, robbing other criminals mid-heist, selling stolen cars and the Ambrosia Raceway demolition derby, which pays up to $6,300 a win.

Does your money reset in GTA 6 chapter 2?

According to WiLLiSGaming, yes: your cash goes back to zero when chapter 2 starts. Only the family savings, the $5,000 in the BuckMe app, carries over.

Is there a money cheat in GTA 6?

Yes, it gives $250,000, but it only works after you beat the story.
