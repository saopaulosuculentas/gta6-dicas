GTA 6 Gun Store Unique Weapons: Prices, Locations and Bundles

Every GTA 6 gun store sells one or two unique weapons you can't buy anywhere else, like Big Shots' The Silver 1922, plus up to three bundles with mods. Prices and store locations inside.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 20, 10:39 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Gun stores in GTA 6 carry one or two unique weapon variants that only that store sells, along with up to three bundles of guns that come with mods already fitted. Arekkz Gaming went through three stores and the names and prices are right there on screen. He’s the only channel to show them so far, so treat this as unverified for now.

How to spot a store’s unique weapon

  1. Walk into any gun store and check the minimap: unique weapons show up as a gold gun icon.
  2. Get close to the display and press Triangle (Y on Xbox) when View pops up.
  3. The card shows an Exclusive tag, the price and a line naming the only store that sells it.

Two things the screen tells you before you buy:

  • You can’t modify them. Every one carries the “Variants can’t be modified” warning. What you see is what you get.
  • You pay from BuckMe. The price uses the banking app’s icon. At Phil’s, Arekkz had $6,905 in his wallet and $1,774 in BuckMe, and a $3,780 purchase was blocked for low BuckMe funds. Hit an ATM first.

The map lists Chuck’s inventory as Limited and the other two as Expanded, so the bigger stores simply stock more. Arekkz rates The Silver 1922 as one of the harder-hitting pistols; its card shows 5.5 damage against 4.2 for the Sand Storm ES9.

Bundles

Talk to the clerk (L2 when you’re close) and open Weapons and Mods. The first tab is Bundles, with up to three per store.

Watch the small print: some bundles only unlock after you buy more guns at that store, and others read “Currently sold out”. The video doesn’t show when stock comes back. The Duke 556 bundle at Chuck’s is mods only, and you can grab the rifle itself for free at the early money warehouse.

Worth it?

If you only care about performance, the free unique guns around the map cover the early game; see GTA 6 unique weapon locations. The store variants are for the look and for collectors. Once bought, they come back from the gun store locker if you lose them. To keep them on hand, see how to store weapons in your trunk.

Quick answers

Can you buy unique weapons in GTA 6?

Yes. Each gun store stocks one or two exclusive variants marked with a gold gun icon. Big Shots in Hamlet sells The Silver 1922 pistol for $2,210.

Can you customize a unique weapon from a gun store?

No. The purchase screen says variants can't be modified, so they keep their own paint job and attachments.

What are gun store bundles in GTA 6?

Weapons sold with mods already fitted, found in the Bundles tab after you talk to the clerk. Some unlock only after you buy more guns, and some can be sold out.

Why does the gun store say I don't have enough money?

Prices come out of your BuckMe account, not your wallet. In Arekkz's video the store turned him down for low BuckMe funds while he had almost $7,000 in cash.
