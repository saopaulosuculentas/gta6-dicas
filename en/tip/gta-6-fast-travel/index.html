How to Fast Travel in GTA 6: Taxis, RydeMe, Buses and Trains

GTA 6 has no free map-click fast travel. You pay to skip the trip instead: a Paradise Taxi, a RydeMe car, or a bus or train where you hold Triangle to jump ahead.

Confirmed · 4 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:46 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

There’s no free fast travel in GTA 6; you can’t click a spot on the map and warp there. Skipping a long trip costs money: call a Paradise Taxi or a RydeMe car and ask to go straight to the destination, or ride a bus or train and skip to another stop. GamesRadar+ lists those three options, and every one of them costs a bit of cash.

Paradise Taxi (Chapter 1)

You get Paradise Taxi’s number during a Chapter 1 mission, and it lives in your phone’s Contacts.

  1. Set a waypoint on the map before you call.
  2. Press Up on the D-pad, open Contacts and ring Paradise Taxi.
  3. The cab shows up fast. In Imack Studios’ video it pulled up within seconds.
  4. Get in and confirm the destination with the driver.
  5. To skip the drive, focus on the driver with L2 (LT on Xbox) and ask with Triangle (Y). That’s $100 on top of the fare, the same amount BennyCentral pays.

Paradise Taxi only takes pocket cash, so hit an ATM first if your wallet is empty. Imack Studios called a cab in Vice City to get back to Jason’s house, and the fare came to $268.

RydeMe (Chapter 2)

The RydeMe app unlocks in Chapter 2, according to GamesRadar+, and it sits on your phone with the other apps.

  • Request a ride back to your safehouse or type in any other destination.
  • Rides are charged to your BuckMe bank account, not your wallet.
  • Skipping works the same way: L2 on the driver, then Triangle, for an extra $100.
  • In Chapter 3 you can sign up for a BuckMe Premium account, and one of its perks is 20% off every RydeMe ride.

Buses and trains

Leonida has working buses and trains. They don’t come to you, though; you wait at the stop or station.

  1. Hold Down on the D-pad to see whether there’s a stop nearby.
  2. Walk up to the stop to see when the next service is due.
  3. Board and take a seat. On the train, Kibbles Gaming held Triangle (Y) to sit down at the Port Gellhorn station.
  4. Pick a stop on the line and hold Triangle (Y) to skip the journey.

Kibbles Gaming skipped ahead to another stop and got dropped off there, with no way back onto the same train. He also found a side benefit: just riding the train around reveals the parts of the map it passes on the way.

Quick answers

Is there fast travel in GTA 6?

Not for free. Pay for a taxi or a RydeMe car plus $100 to skip the drive, or catch a bus or train and skip the journey once you're aboard.

How do you skip a taxi ride in GTA 6?

Inside the car, focus on the driver with L2 (LT on Xbox) and ask to skip with Triangle (Y). It costs $100 on top of the fare.

When do you unlock RydeMe in GTA 6?

In Chapter 2, according to GamesRadar+. It charges your BuckMe account, and the Chapter 3 Premium account takes 20% off every ride.

Can you ride trains in GTA 6?

Yes. Wait at a station, board the train, hold Triangle (Y) to sit and then skip the journey to another stop.
