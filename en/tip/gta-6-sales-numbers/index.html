How Many Copies Has GTA 6 Sold? The Estimates So Far

Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 has sold about 24.58 million copies and made around $2.36 billion. These are analyst estimates, not official Rockstar figures.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 20, 10:27 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

According to analytics firm Sensor Tower, GTA 6 has sold roughly 24.58 million copies across all platforms and pulled in about $2.36 billion. That is not an official number. It’s an estimate, reported by GamesRadar+ on November 20, the day after launch, which is why this page stays marked as unverified until an official figure comes out.

The estimate at a glance

Metric Sensor Tower estimate
Copies sold, all platforms 24.58 million
Revenue About $2.36 billion
PS5 share 75% of copies
Xbox Series X/S share 25% of copies

If that 75/25 split holds for the whole total, it works out to around 18.4 million copies on PS5 and 6.1 million on Xbox. That math is ours, based on the figures above, not something Sensor Tower published.

Where the number comes from

Sensor Tower tracks how releases perform across the games industry and keeps a live database of what each one earns. GamesRadar+ pulled the figures from that tool and notes that the bulk of the revenue most likely came from pre-orders.

Analyst estimates can shift and can be off. They give a sense of how big the launch was, but the number that counts is the one Rockstar eventually reports.

Why PS5 is so far ahead

GamesRadar+ says the gap was expected well before release:

  • there are simply far more PS5 consoles out there than Xbox Series machines;
  • Sony partnered with Rockstar and leaned hard into marketing;
  • GTA 6 was the PS5’s startup graphic for a while, and it came back for launch.

For how the game actually runs on each console, see GTA 6 FPS and performance on PS5 Pro and Xbox.

What about PC?

PC isn’t part of the count because GTA 6 hasn’t come out on PC, and GamesRadar+ points out that Rockstar hasn’t even confirmed that version yet. More in is GTA 6 on PC, PS4 or Xbox One.

Quick answers

How many copies has GTA 6 sold?

Sensor Tower puts it at roughly 24.58 million copies across all platforms. Rockstar hasn't put out an official figure of its own.

How much money has GTA 6 made?

Around $2.36 billion, according to Sensor Tower's estimate. Most of it likely came from pre-orders.

Did GTA 6 sell more on PS5 or Xbox?

PS5, by a wide margin. Sensor Tower estimates a 75% share for PS5 and 25% for Xbox Series X|S.
