How Many Copies Has GTA 6 Sold? 32.5 Million Before Launch

Official figures: GTA 6 sold 32.5 million copies before release and made $3.79 billion in day one retail sales, according to Take-Two.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 22, 10:40 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 sold 32.5 million copies before release and made $3.79 billion in day one retail sales. These are official figures from the Rockstar and Take-Two announcement on November 20.

The official numbers

Metric Take-Two announcement
Copies sold before release 32.5 million
Day one retail sales $3.79 billion

The company notes that the dollar figure is its own estimate through the end of November 19, worldwide, and calls it the biggest entertainment launch in history. A copy count including sales since launch isn’t out yet.

Before the announcement, this page carried a Sensor Tower estimate that turned out well below the official figure. It stays below only for the PS5 and Xbox split, which Take-Two hasn’t shared.

The estimate at a glance

Metric Sensor Tower estimate
Copies sold, all platforms 24.58 million
Revenue About $2.36 billion
PS5 share 75% of copies
Xbox Series X/S share 25% of copies

If that 75/25 split holds for the whole total, it works out to around 18.4 million copies on PS5 and 6.1 million on Xbox. That math is ours, based on the figures above, not something Sensor Tower published.

Where the number comes from

Sensor Tower tracks how releases perform across the games industry and keeps a live database of what each one earns. GamesRadar+ pulled the figures from that tool and notes that the bulk of the revenue most likely came from pre-orders.

Analyst estimates can shift and can be off. They give a sense of how big the launch was, but the number that counts is the official one at the top of this page.

Why PS5 is so far ahead

GamesRadar+ says the gap was expected well before release:

  • there are simply far more PS5 consoles out there than Xbox Series machines;
  • Sony partnered with Rockstar and leaned hard into marketing;
  • GTA 6 was the PS5’s startup graphic for a while, and it came back for launch.

For how the game actually runs on each console, see GTA 6 FPS and performance on PS5 Pro and Xbox.

What about PC?

PC isn’t part of the count because GTA 6 hasn’t come out on PC, and GamesRadar+ points out that Rockstar hasn’t even confirmed that version yet. More in is GTA 6 on PC, PS4 or Xbox One.

Quick answers

How many copies has GTA 6 sold?

It sold 32.5 million copies before release alone, according to Take-Two's official announcement. A total including sales since launch hasn't been published.

How much money has GTA 6 made?

Take-Two announced $3.79 billion in day one retail sales, based on company estimates through the end of November 19.

Did GTA 6 sell more on PS5 or Xbox?

PS5, by a wide margin. Sensor Tower estimates a 75% share for PS5 and 25% for Xbox Series X|S.
