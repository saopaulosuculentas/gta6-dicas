How to Get Body Armor in GTA 6: Where to Buy It, How to Equip It and Free Armor

In GTA 6 you buy body armor at a gun store and put it on from the weapon wheel. It adds a blue bar over your health, refills if it doesn't break, and you can find it for free.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:43 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

In GTA 6 you get body armor by buying it at a gun store, then you put it on from the weapon wheel. While you wear it, a blue bar sits above your health and soaks up bullets before your health does. You can also find armor for free in police cars and in houses you raid.

Where to buy it

At a gun store. JorRaptor grabbed his at the store nearest Jason’s house, Chuck’s Tackle & Shot, and says you can choose the color. His video doesn’t show the price.

How to put it on

  1. Open the weapon wheel.
  2. Armor has its own slot on the wheel, apart from your guns.
  3. Select it and the blue bar appears on top of your health bar.

JorRaptor only straps it on right before a shootout.

How it works

Here’s how JorRaptor explains it:

  • Incoming fire drains the blue bar first. Your health only takes hits once it’s gone.
  • If the vest survives the fight, the bar refills over time and you can use it again.
  • If the bar hits zero, the vest breaks and you need to buy a new one.
  • One trick: take the vest off just before it breaks. You keep it, but every bullet after that goes straight to your health.

Free armor

  • Police cars: BennyCentral opened the trunk of a cop car at the Hamlet impound and walked off with a rifle, a shotgun and a vest. Save first, since the cops don’t take it well. Full steps in free guns from cop cars.
  • The gun nut’s house: according to BennyCentral, a stilt house in Key Lento that looks a lot like Jason’s holds armor, a machine gun and a rifle, plus the unique Gun Nut SMS01 sniper. The owner is home and comes at you.
  • Mansions: Typical Gamer broke into a random mansion, forced open a safe and came out with armor, a rare rifle and cash.
  • Cheat: there’s a body armor cheat, but it only unlocks after 100 workouts.

Is it worth it early?

JorRaptor puts armor near the top of his early shopping list, along with a suppressor for your pistol. The rest of the arsenal is in the GTA 6 weapons list.

Quick answers

Where do you buy body armor in GTA 6?

At a gun store. JorRaptor bought his at the store closest to Jason's house and picked the color at the counter.

How do you equip body armor in GTA 6?

From the weapon wheel, where armor has its own slot. Once it's on, a blue bar shows up above your health.

Does body armor regenerate in GTA 6?

If it doesn't break, yes: the blue bar fills back up over time. If it breaks, you have to buy a new one, according to JorRaptor.

Can you get free body armor in GTA 6?

Yes. Police car trunks can have a vest, and so can houses you break into. There's also a body armor cheat.
