GTA 6 Legendary and Unique Weapons: All the Locations Found So Far

GTA 6 legendary and unique weapon locations by region: suppressed M306, gold KZ59, Assassin Nipper .38, Smuggler Gorse 425, Dirty Drifter, snipers and more.

Confirmed · 14 sourcesUpdated Nov 22, 10:40 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has unique weapons: special variants with their own wraps and attachments that you couldn’t afford to build early on. BennyCentral says there are 130 spread across Leonida, while WiLLiSGaming talks about “hundreds”. Some are close to the start of the game.

When you pick one up, the game tells you it’s a unique weapon variant and that, if lost, it can be recovered at the weapon locker in Ammu-Nation. WiLLiSGaming and YOUNG DIRK back this up: unique guns survive death and come back from the locker or your car’s trunk. See how to store weapons in your trunk.

Not every unique gun is hidden: some are sold in gun stores (see the section near the end). And if you want an early-game route that grabs guns and cash in one trip, see GTA 6 secret early items.

How to spot a unique weapon

  • Yellow icon on the map. According to WiLLiSGaming, a small yellow gun icon shows up on the map when you’re near a unique weapon. Arekkz Gaming calls it a golden gun icon, and inside gun stores it shows on the minimap.
  • No modding. OutlawGarry thought a blue weapon is yours and can be customized while a yellow one is unique and can’t be, but he wasn’t sure. Arekkz Gaming and BennyCentral now say the same thing: weapon variants can’t be modified. What you find is what you get, which is why the ones that come with a scope and suppressor are so handy early on.
  • Not everything is unique. Grenade launchers and rocket launchers you find lying around are regular weapons: die with them and they’re gone (WiLLiSGaming). The SM-69 sniper on the Key Lento radio tower and the Duke 5.56 rifles in hideouts are regular too, according to Arekkz Gaming, Rodwenn and RageGamingVideos. Keep those in your trunk.

Several spots have now been shown by more than one channel: the Kingfishers W-22, the tiger motel sniper, the swamp cabin guns, the Conspiracist Type 9, the glider pistol, the Crabshack VF5, the M306 rifle and the gold KZ59 at the fishing warehouse. When only one channel has shown a spot, the text says who.

Hunter’s Laird 350 sniper (random event)

KhrazeGaming calls it your first “yellow” (rare) sniper: top-tier accuracy and range with a long-range scope already on it.

  1. Head to the west side of the highway into Hamlet, near Grassrivers, at a junction.
  2. You’ll hear a gunshot nearby. If you can’t place it, press L3 + R3 (focus) to reveal where the sound comes from.
  3. You’ll find two guys (one is named Todd) with the sniper lying on the ground.
  4. Play along: grab the truck down the road and drive them to the hospital.
  5. At the hospital, one of them hands you the rifle for good.

Kingfishers W-22 Small Game Rifle (near silent)

A small game rifle with a scope and a suppressor that barely makes a sound. Headshots drop enemies instantly, which makes it great for stealth. KhrazeGaming and WiLLiSGaming both picked up this rifle.

  1. Early on, Donnie tells you about a robbery opportunity at a Kingfishers compound in Grassrivers.
  2. Bring firepower: WiLLiSGaming counts 20 or more enemies there. KhrazeGaming recommends coming by boat and picking off enemies on the pier from range.
  3. Go into the building closest to the water, one of the gang’s fishing shacks.
  4. A weapons crate on a desk holds the rifle.
  5. To get away, WiLLiSGaming takes one of the boats out back and leaves along the river.

Arekkz Gaming, BennyCentral and RageGamingVideos went after the same rifle. Arekkz and Benny call the spot Wetman Bay: a small island with a little dock and the gang’s fishing shacks. Come in by the dock and the rifle is in a case inside the main cabin; Benny says it sits on the kitchen counter. Arekkz warns that body shots do very little damage, so aim for the head.

There’s a bonus too. According to RageGamingVideos, behind the little red shed on the docks a scoped Duke 5.56 rifle is leaning against the wall. It isn’t unique, but it’s free. Put it in your trunk and it stays with you.

Clear the rest of the compound too: the drug bricks there go for about $350 each at Brian’s lockup.

Black Eagle Import M306 rifle (Black Eagle warehouse)

This is WiLLiSGaming’s favorite find: a marksman rifle with a scope and a suppressor. It sits in a Black Eagle warehouse, part of one of the game’s gun store chains. He doesn’t show where the warehouse is on the map.

  1. Save first. The staff are unarmed, but they will call the cops. Once the police surround the building, the gun is out of reach. He needed the warehouse to himself for roughly 10 to 20 minutes.
  2. Play as Lucia. The rifle is in a room you can only reach through a narrow gap, and Jason is too big for it.
  3. As you walk into the warehouse, head left. The gap is behind a small opening in the wood. He used cover (R1 on PS5) to squeeze through.
  4. The rifle is in a case inside. Leave through the same gap.

It took WiLLiSGaming 5 to 10 minutes to get in, and he says he doesn’t know if there’s another way. The same warehouse has a rocket launcher, and a safe in the office to the right of the entrance holds a Black Eagle Desert Eagle. The code going around online didn’t work for him. Vexies has since opened it, using one code for the door and another for the safe. We’ve only heard those numbers in the voiceover, so they stay off this page until checked on screen.

Another way in: the forklift (Arekkz Gaming)

Arekkz Gaming showed where the warehouse is and a way into the room without the gap. It’s next to the Black Eagle gun store, just down the road from the Griffin Shooting Range, the same range as the Conspiracist Type 9 (see below), so you can grab both on one trip. The warehouse is the big building to the right of the store.

  1. Cover your face (bandana, hat or mask) before going in so the cops can’t ID you.
  2. Hop the fence. There are three workers on the ground floor: aim at them and threaten them until all three are on their knees. Stay close or they’ll run and call the cops. Don’t go upstairs: the people up there only notice you if you go up.
  3. Go past the bars into the back storage room. There’s a forklift there: hotwire it.
  4. Three pallets block the hidden room. Line the forks up under each one, lift with the left stick, move it out of the way and drop it by pushing the stick down. Lining up the forks takes a bit of practice.
  5. With all three gone, the room opens up: the M306 is in the corner, along with a rocket launcher.

According to Arekkz, the M306 is semi-auto, has a zoom scope and a suppressor, about 20 rounds per mag, and drops almost any enemy in one shot.

Vexies also moved the pallets with the forklift and took the rifle, marking the warehouse just below the space between the I and the D in LEONIDA. For the full route with a screenshot, see Black Eagle M306 location.

Swamp Bait revolver and Cruel Huntsman SM69 sniper (swamp cabin)

Two unique guns in one trip, according to WiLLiSGaming. They’re in an abandoned cabin in the middle of the Pelican Valley swamp, near the Keys. Just walk in: the Swamp Bait revolver and the Cruel Huntsman SM69 sniper are both there.

Don’t go at night. If an alligator grabs you in the water, it kills you in one go.

Millzaa visited the same cabin. He places the little house, which has no name on the map, just south of Hamlet, and you have to wade through a bit of river to reach it. Inside you’ll find butchered alligators, a cleaver on the table and a body. The sniper is on the floor with a scope already fitted. The Swamp Bait revolver isn’t out in the open: Millzaa spotted it by zooming in with Focus. There’s also some footage on the floor showing what happened to the cabin’s owner.

Cockroach Extreme carbine and Dvatek GL40 grenade launcher

WiLLiSGaming found both in Vice City, in the Vice-Dale area (he calls it “Dale County”), in the back storeroom of a store that anyone can walk into.

  1. Go into the store and head for the storeroom.
  2. Knock down the wall of toilet paper.
  3. The Cockroach Extreme carbine and the Dvatek GL40 grenade launcher are behind it.

The carbine is unique. The grenade launcher isn’t, so you lose it if you die.

Vexies narrows the location down to a storage unit just above Tequesta in Vice-Dale County, with a door that looks painted on but opens. WiLLiSGaming describes it as a store’s back room, and both found the guns behind a wall of paper rolls. There’s also food, body armor, grenades and a safe with a collectible card.

Conspiracist Type 9 SMG (junkyard)

WiLLiSGaming found the Conspiracist Type 9, a unique SMG, in a junkyard west of Vice City, on SR-120 near Omega Pkwy, with Buckskin Swamp to the west. He says “Mariana County” out loud, but the map in his video puts the junkyard between Kelly County and Vice-Dale County. The weapon’s name shows on screen. The same junkyard has a rocket launcher. He suggests waiting until Chapter 2, when the story takes you to that area anyway.

  1. Jump the junkyard fence.
  2. Look for an RV covered in antennas.
  3. Round the back there’s a bunker door. Inside you’ll find a dead man and a weapons container with the SMG.
  4. There are also grenades and a rocket launcher down there. Save the rockets for when you really need them: the launcher isn’t unique and you lose it when you die.

Millzaa, Rodwenn and BennyCentral found the same SMG and give an easier landmark. The junkyard is a trailer park right across the street from the Griffin Shooting Range, a gun range in Balsero. On the map, Millzaa describes it as sitting “between the D and the A” of the word LEONIDA.

  1. Stop in front of the shooting range, cross the street and climb the fence.
  2. Go into the trailer. WiLLiSGaming mentions a bunker door at the back; Rodwenn and Millzaa show a hatch in the floor that drops into a cellar.
  3. Down there, the Conspiracist Type 9 is in a gun case next to the rocket launcher, grenades and healing items.

According to BennyCentral, it’s a machine pistol and goes into your pistol slot.

Donnie’s H&L 38 revolver (missable)

DPJ warns this one is easy to miss for good.

  1. Near the end of Chapter 1, after the mission Swamp (with Brian), you have to pack Donnie’s things at his trailer.
  2. You load them into his car and drive it somewhere remote to destroy it.
  3. Before you destroy it, open the trunk: the revolver is inside.

Destroy the car first and the weapon goes with it.

Sniper at the tiger motel (Manatee Motel)

Four channels have now shown this motel, with a tiger on the loose and a sniper as the prize.

  • BennyCentral describes a sniper with a custom wrap, taken from the Kingfishers, at an abandoned motel in Pelican Valley. It comes fully kitted out.
  • OutlawGarry calls the place the Manatee Motel and says it’s just up the road from Key Lento, where the game starts. In his video, a group of hunters had a tiger locked up, it got loose and mauled everyone.
  • Millzaa found the motel marked on the map as an event. He puts it just south of Hamlet, about a mile from Jason’s house. On the map in his video, that’s the northern edge of Pelican Valley, across the water.
  • Rodwenn went to the same spot and took the sniper from the next room on the left.

This page used to say the area depended on who you asked (Pelican Valley or near Key Lento). Millzaa’s map settles it: the motel sits in northern Pelican Valley, right below Hamlet, and the road from Key Lento gets you there.

  1. When you get there, the tiger is on the attack. Wait for it and the wounded man to go down, or shoot it yourself.
  2. Loot the rooms and the bodies for cash. OutlawGarry found a safe with $1,000 and left with about $3,300 in total. The safe at the back gave Millzaa $917 and Rodwenn just over $1,000. There’s also a flare gun and some healing items.
  3. Grab the sniper from the weapons case in the room next door. OutlawGarry found two snipers, one of them yellow (unique). Millzaa also picked up $640 worth of drugs in that room.
  4. Leave quickly or be ready to fight: in OutlawGarry’s video someone called the police, and in Millzaa’s an armed group showed up right after.

Earlier, neither video made the weapon’s name clear. Now it’s settled: Millzaa’s screen shows Kingfishers Laird 350 along with the unique variant notice, and Rodwenn and BennyCentral say the same name. Rodwenn adds that it comes with its own wrap, a sight and a stock upgrade. Don’t mix it up with the Hunter’s Laird 350 from the random event.

Sniper on top of the radio tower

Arekkz Gaming found a scoped sniper at the top of a radio tower in the starting area, close to the clothing store.

  1. Take the stairs to the top platform.
  2. Go through the door at the top and climb the ladder: the sniper is there.
  3. Further up there’s a parachute to jump off with.

Now we know which tower it is. It’s the one in Key Lento, about 0.36 miles from Jason’s house, according to Millzaa. Arekkz says it’s near Donnie’s trailer and counts as one of the game’s 31 landmarks, so scan it with your phone before you climb. Millzaa, Arekkz and BennyCentral all call the gun the SM-69.

Heads up: this sniper is a regular gun, not a unique variant. Arekkz and Rodwenn both point out that you lose it if you die. On the same climb you’ll find 2 collectible cards and the parachute at the top, which only works once. See collectible cards and parachutes.

The first video didn’t make clear whether this is the same tower as the radio tower easter egg. Going by the locations, it isn’t: the egg tower is near Hamlet, while this one is in Key Lento.

The crashed glider pistol (Turnt Nipper .38)

Milo found a propeller glider stuck in a tree on a small island in the south. Shoot the strings holding it up and it drops, with a unique pistol inside. The video places the island “just north of Mariana County” but doesn’t show the exact spot.

Arekkz Gaming and BennyCentral went to the same glider and fill in the gaps. On Arekkz’s screen the gun shows up as the Turnt Nipper .38 Pistol, with its own decal and a red dot sight already fitted. Benny describes the wrap as energy drink branding.

  1. Head to Bobcat Bight, at the far end of the sandy strip. The glider is on a small island, so bring a boat or a jet ski.
  2. Walk up from the beach to the pine trees. The wrecked glider is hanging in the branches with a bunch of ropes.
  3. Shoot every rope. Arekkz used a rifle with a big magazine, since the last rope swings around and takes a few tries.
  4. Once the glider drops, the pistol is inside it or on the ground right next to it.

Crabshack VF5 SMG

This is the reward for the giant crab mystery in Mariana County. YOUNG DIRK shows it lying on the floor right inside the boathouse, scope already fitted, and it goes into your weapon locker. RageGamingVideos and Millzaa add that it also has a tactical flashlight and a biohazard wrap. The full solution, with all three codes, is in the giant crab mystery.

Marabunta Grande KZ59 rifle (Nova Auto junkyard, Hamlet)

Only BennyCentral has shown this one so far, and the name comes from his voiceover. It’s a gold assault rifle engraved with M7 gang designs.

  1. Head to the Nova Auto junkyard in Hamlet.
  2. Climb the wall from the dumpster and take out the M7 gang in the yard.
  3. The rifle is in a case on the workbench under the shed.
  4. In the garage, the safe in the back room opens with 777, which is written right above it.
  5. A button on the wall opens the garage shutter. Drive out in the lowrider inside: Benny says it has no tracker.

Gold KZ59 at the Palmer Fishing Warehouse (Bobcat Key)

BennyCentral and Arekkz Gaming have both shown this rifle. It’s another gold KZ59 with fine engravings; the full name isn’t clear in the voiceover, so we’re not printing it. Arekkz doesn’t name the place, but he describes a gang fishing warehouse with the same layout: computer with the code downstairs, rifle and safe upstairs.

  1. Go to the Palmer Fishing Warehouse in Bobcat Key.
  2. Fight your way through the gang and head up to the office: the rifle is in a case.
  3. The computer downstairs gives you the code for the office safe. BennyCentral read out a different number from Arekkz and Vexies, and we haven’t checked on screen which is right: read it off the computer in your own game. The safe gave Arekkz $3,000 to $5,000, and he thinks it refills after a few days.
  4. Arekkz’s route is through the water, by the bridge: climb up the small back dock, which works as a back door into the main building. Under the stairs there’s a grenade launcher (not unique: stash it in your trunk) and upstairs a luxury watch on the table that sells well at the pawn shop.

Vexies ran the same robbery by circling left around the gate, taking the grenade launcher downstairs and finding the gold rifle upstairs near a gold watch. The warehouse on the left has more to steal, and a boat out back makes the getaway easy.

Gun nut’s sniper (Key Lento)

According to BennyCentral, a stilt house in Key Lento that looks a lot like Jason’s belongs to a firearms enthusiast. Climb the back stairs and break the door open. The owner is home and comes at you. The unique sniper, with a patriotic wrap, is in a case on the kitchen counter, and the bedrooms hold body armor, a machine gun and an assault rifle. Benny marks the house on his map but doesn’t give the street name.

Vexies found the same haul in a Key Lento house with a pool nearby, an outside staircase and an armed owner: a unique sniper, armor on the bed and a machine gun in the bedroom. It sounds like the same house. There are boats out back if the police show up.

Marabunta Grand Hobo shotgun (behind the Hamlet pawn shop)

RageGamingVideos found this engraved legendary double-barrel shotgun while visiting the Hamlet pawn shop at night. Behind the shop was a car with its trunk open, blood on the ground and screaming from the building next door. Follow the blood trail through a door into a warehouse: the shotgun sits in a gun case out in the open, with some cash. Whether you deal with what’s in the next room is up to you. He isn’t sure if it’s a random event or if it always happens there.

Black and gold suppressed rifle (guarded manor south of Grassrivers)

Arekkz Gaming showed this spot. It’s a fully automatic black and gold assault rifle, loaded with attachments and a suppressor. The name in his voiceover isn’t clear, so we’re not printing it.

  1. Head to the guarded manor marked as an illegal opportunity on the edge of the swamp, just south of Grassrivers.
  2. Bring a gun and, if you have them, armor and meds. Guards are all over the house, and once one spots you the rest come pouring out. With a suppressor you can try to stay sneaky.
  3. Go in on the left side, along the riverfront, where there’s an easy spot to hop in.
  4. Head to the upper floor. The back corner room has a box of sticky bombs, and the rifle is right behind you.
  5. Through the kitchen there’s a luxury watch, and the computer shows the safe code; the safe gave Arekkz about $3,000. Read the code off the computer in your own game.

Suppressed rifle at the Watson Bay safe house

Vexies found a unique suppressed assault rifle at the Watson Bay illegal operation, which has high security.

  1. Work around the back, keeping left, until you reach a blue tarp you can climb over.
  2. Bring armor and healing: they barely survived the shootout.
  3. The rifle is upstairs. The house also has a watch, about $5,000 in goods and a safe, with the code on the laptop.
  4. Escape in the special vehicle in the garage.

This may be the same manor as the black and gold rifle from Arekkz. We haven’t compared both maps yet, so they’re listed separately.

Shotgun and suppressed pistol in the boat wrecks (near Mile Zero Air Station)

Shown by Arekkz Gaming and later by Vexies. You can grab both right at the start, as Jason.

  • Shotgun: in the cluster of small islands near Mile Zero Air Station and the Southwind port, go to the island with the sandy patch. On the north end, the green side, there’s a small boat wreck, and the shotgun is on the ground next to it. He says it fires almost like a semi-auto.
  • Suppressed Nipper .38 pistol: at the tip of the pointy island at the south end of the same cluster there’s another boat wreck. Climb in and open the hidden compartment with the on-screen prompt: the pistol is inside. Arekkz calls it the Assassin Nipper .38. It only holds about 8 rounds, but it’s silent and free (a suppressor alone runs about $1,000 in the shop).

Two channels now back this up: Vexies took both guns from the same wrecks and uses the same names, Smuggler Gorse 425 and Assassin Nipper .38. WAiNK labels the pistol boat a derelict spy vessel. Full route: Assassin Nipper .38 and Smuggler Gorse 425.

Burst pistol at the Little Cuba shipping yard

Only Arekkz Gaming has shown this one. It’s a silver, gold and ivory burst pistol; in his voiceover he calls it the Guardian ES 18.

  1. Go to the shipping yard illegal activity by the port in the Little Cuba area of Vice City.
  2. To get in quietly, go left of the main gate, climb the trash near the bridge and slip through the gap in the barbed wire.
  3. The building in the middle of the yard has about 5 or 6 enemies. Use a suppressor or non-lethal takedowns, but expect a fight.
  4. At the back of the building, by the door, there’s a weapon case with the pistol. There’s also a safe, but Arekkz couldn’t find the code.
  5. The gate button is to the left of the guard at the entrance. Open it if you want the red muscle car in the yard: he says Moses pays $12,000 for it.

Clunker 556 rifle (Port Gellhorn, no fight)

Only Arekkz Gaming has shown this one. It’s a rifle that already comes loaded with attachments, and you can take it without a fight. It’s easy to get early in Chapter 2, once Lucia joins.

  1. Go to the robbery opportunity next to the port bridge in Port Gellhorn.
  2. Park outside the warehouse by the blue tarp fences, around the corner from the main entrance, so nobody sees you.
  3. Hop the blue tarp fence, run past the warehouse and go through the hole in the next fence.
  4. Take it slow to the marked small hut: the rifle is inside.
  5. If you want, take the orange truck in the yard: it’s great off-road and also sells.

Sea lion house shotgun (Key Lento)

Shown by Vexies, with 7.7 damage, higher than the store shotguns they compared it with.

  1. Find Nifty Threads Islandwear on the map and follow the street to its end.
  2. Look for the sea lion statue and enter the green house to its right. Expect armed men inside.
  3. Go straight, take the left door, then turn left into the bathroom: the shotgun is at the back.
  4. Grab the med kit and adrenaline on your way out. There’s a free boat outside.

Drug Mule KZ-59 rifle (sunken boat)

Also from Vexies. Leave the south of Key Lento by boat or swimming and head right of the two landmasses marked in the video. Dive to the sunken boat, turn on Focus and look under the hull. The same dive paid $3,600 in goods. Scuba gear helps, but Vexies managed without it.

Combat knife (glider island)

On the same island as the Turnt Nipper .38, Vexies keeps going to the far end and, left of a small split in the land, twists open a cover. Inside is a unique combat knife that can also be thrown. The name sounds like Soldier’s Combat Knife in the voiceover.

Stoner K17 pistol (Ambrosia motel)

A fully automatic pistol, according to Vexies.

  1. Go to the motel with the big sign marked just above Ambrosia.
  2. Enter the room where everyone is passed out.
  3. The pistol is on the right-hand nightstand.
  4. The room also has a safe with a collectible card.

Dirty Drifter shotgun (Bonanza, far north)

A sawed-off double barrel shown by Vexies. Go to the lookout camp in Bonanza at the top of the map, take the collectible and scan below with binoculars. Climb down to the car you spot and open the trunk.

Gutterland Moreland 850 shotgun (Kingpin Bowling)

PC Gamer found it on the pool table in the main room of the Port Gellhorn bowling alley. See also the bowling alley safe code.

Gold guns at the Kolt & Associates hideouts (Port Gellhorn)

RaidAway found two, during robbery opportunities you can run with a partner:

  • Kolt & Associates hideout near Dunk’s trailer: a gold assault rifle that shows up with Focus once you’re inside.
  • Brooks Boatworks, northwest Port Gellhorn: another gold weapon, just past the narrow entrance.

Neither gun is named in the voiceover.

Unique weapons you can buy

Every gun store sells one or two exclusive variants, marked with the golden gun icon. Chuck’s Tackle & Shot in Key Lento has the Mahi Nipper .38 for $1,160, and Big Shots in Hamlet has The Silver 1922 for $2,210: Arekkz Gaming and BennyCentral show the same prices. The full list, with bundles, is in GTA 6 gun store unique weapons.

Tip

According to KhrazeGaming, enemy hideouts refresh every few in-game days and restock goods and weapons. For buying and customizing everything else, see the GTA 6 weapons list.

Quick answers

What are unique weapons in GTA 6?

Special weapon variants with their own wraps and attachments, hidden around the map, handed out in missions or sold in gun stores. They can't be modified. BennyCentral counts 130 of them, while WiLLiSGaming talks about hundreds.

Can you get a lost unique weapon back in GTA 6?

Yes. When you pick one up, the game says that if it's lost, all owned weapons can be recovered at the Ammu-Nation locker. Regular grenade and rocket launchers you find don't count: you lose those when you die.

How do you get Donnie's revolver in GTA 6?

At the end of Chapter 1, after the mission Swamp, you drive Donnie's car out to destroy it. Open the trunk first: the H&L 38 revolver is inside. Destroy the car and the gun is gone.

Where can you get a sniper early in GTA 6?

The Hunter's Laird 350 comes from a random event near Grassrivers, west of the highway to Hamlet. The Kingfishers Laird 350 is at the Manatee Motel, just south of Hamlet. There's also a unique sniper in a Pelican Valley swamp cabin and a regular one at the top of the Key Lento radio tower.
