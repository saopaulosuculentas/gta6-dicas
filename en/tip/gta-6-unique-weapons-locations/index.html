GTA 6 Unique Weapons: Where to Find Rare Guns Early

GTA 6 hides unique weapon variants around the map, with wraps and attachments you can't afford early. Here's where to get the Hunter's Laird 350, the Kingfishers W-22, Donnie's revolver and more.

Unverified · 5 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 4:18 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has unique weapons: special variants with their own wraps and attachments that you couldn’t afford to build early on. BennyCentral says there are 130 spread across Leonida, and some are close to the start of the game.

When you pick one up, the game tells you it’s a unique weapon variant and that, if lost, it can be recovered at the weapon locker in Ammu-Nation. See how to store weapons in your trunk.

Each spot below was shown by a single channel, so this page is unverified for now.

Hunter’s Laird 350 sniper (random event)

KhrazeGaming calls it your first “yellow” (rare) sniper: top-tier accuracy and range with a long-range scope already on it.

  1. Head to the west side of the highway into Hamlet, near Grassrivers, at a junction.
  2. You’ll hear a gunshot nearby. If you can’t place it, press L3 + R3 (focus) to reveal where the sound comes from.
  3. You’ll find two guys (one is named Todd) with the sniper lying on the ground.
  4. Play along: grab the truck down the road and drive them to the hospital.
  5. At the hospital, one of them hands you the rifle for good.

Kingfishers W-22 Small Game Rifle (near silent)

A small game rifle that barely makes a sound. Headshots drop enemies instantly, which makes it great for stealth.

  1. Early on, Donnie tells you about a robbery opportunity at a Kingfishers compound in Grassrivers.
  2. KhrazeGaming recommends coming by boat and picking off enemies on the pier from range.
  3. Go into the building closest to the water.
  4. A weapons crate on a desk holds the rifle.

Clear the rest of the compound too: the drug bricks there go for about $350 each at Brian’s lockup.

Donnie’s H&L 38 revolver (missable)

DPJ warns this one is easy to miss for good.

  1. Near the end of Chapter 1, after the mission Swamp (with Brian), you have to pack Donnie’s things at his trailer.
  2. You load them into his car and drive it somewhere remote to destroy it.
  3. Before you destroy it, open the trunk: the revolver is inside.

Destroy the car first and the weapon goes with it.

Kingfishers sniper at the Pelican Valley motel

BennyCentral describes a sniper with a custom wrap, taken from the Kingfishers, at an abandoned motel in Pelican Valley. It comes fully kitted out.

  1. When you get there, a tiger is mauling a man. Shoot the tiger.
  2. Loot the room for some cash.
  3. Grab the sniper from the weapons case in the room next door.

The weapon’s name came out garbled in the video, so we’re not naming it here.

Sniper on top of the radio tower

Arekkz Gaming found a scoped sniper at the top of a radio tower in the starting area, close to the clothing store.

  1. Take the stairs to the top platform.
  2. Go through the door at the top and climb the ladder: the sniper is there.
  3. Further up there’s a parachute to jump off with.

The video doesn’t make clear whether it’s the same tower as the radio tower easter egg.

The crashed glider pistol

Milo found a propeller glider stuck in a tree on a small island in the south. Shoot the strings holding it up and it drops, with a unique pistol inside. The video places the island “just north of Mariana County” but doesn’t show the exact spot.

Crabshack VF5 SMG

This is the reward for the giant crab mystery in Mariana County. The full solution is in the giant crab mystery.

Tip

According to KhrazeGaming, enemy hideouts refresh every few in-game days and restock goods and weapons. For buying and customizing everything else, see the GTA 6 weapons list.

Quick answers

What are unique weapons in GTA 6?

Special weapon variants with their own wraps and attachments, hidden around the map or handed out in missions. BennyCentral counts 130 of them.

Can you get a lost unique weapon back in GTA 6?

Yes. When you pick one up, the game says that if it's lost, all owned weapons can be recovered at the Ammu-Nation locker.

How do you get Donnie's revolver in GTA 6?

At the end of Chapter 1, after the mission Swamp, you drive Donnie's car out to destroy it. Open the trunk first: the H&L 38 revolver is inside. Destroy the car and the gun is gone.

Where can you get a sniper early in GTA 6?

The Hunter's Laird 350 comes from a random event near Grassrivers, west of the highway to Hamlet. There's also a sniper at the top of a radio tower in the starting area.
