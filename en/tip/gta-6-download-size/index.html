GTA 6 Download Size: How Many GB on PS5 and Xbox

GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB of free space, according to Rockstar, and PlayStation says to clear at least 212 GB on PS5. The boxed copy has no disc, only a download code.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 4:55 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB of free space. That’s the figure on Rockstar’s support page, which says the exact amount depends on your console. PlayStation’s official game page tells PS5 owners to clear at least 212 GB before downloading.

Size by console

  • PS5 and PS5 Pro: clear a minimum of 212 GB (PlayStation).
  • Xbox Series X|S: Rockstar’s “up to 212 GB” covers Xbox too. We haven’t found an exact Xbox figure on an official page, but Windows Central’s Series X review called the game roughly a 200 GB package.

For scale, 212 GB is more than a quarter of a standard 825 GB PS5. On the Xbox Series S, which has less storage, it takes an even bigger slice.

The box has no disc

Rockstar confirms that the physical version of GTA 6 only contains a download code. There’s no disc inside. In practice:

  • you download the full game whether you buy digital or boxed;
  • the boxed version only exists for the Standard Edition, and Standard owners can upgrade to Ultimate later in the PlayStation or Microsoft store;
  • PS5 codes are tied to the region of your PlayStation account, while Xbox codes aren’t region-locked.

How to get ready

  1. Free up space first. On PS5, check what you have under Settings > Storage. To see the exact size the game needs, open it in your Game Library, go to “…” > View Product, and the required space shows on the right.
  2. Let it download in Rest Mode. PlayStation recommends turning on Auto-Install in Rest Mode (Settings > Saved Data and Game/App Settings > Automatic Updates) and Stay Connected to the Internet (Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode).
  3. Use a wired connection if you can. At 100 megabits per second, 212 GB takes roughly five hours in ideal conditions. Wi-Fi is usually slower.

Pre-loading opened on November 10 for anyone who pre-ordered digitally, so if you bought early, the game may already be on your console.
