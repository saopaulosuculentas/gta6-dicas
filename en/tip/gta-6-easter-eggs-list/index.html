GTA 6 Easter Eggs: Every Secret Found So Far

Every GTA 6 easter egg found so far: Bigfoot (the Skunk Ape), the radio tower egg, the jet ski dog, the alien lab, Michael's boat, Dexter's apartment, the giant crab and more.

Confirmed · 6 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:23 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 launched packed with secrets, and players found dozens on day one. This list collects every GTA 6 easter egg that has been shown on video, from the most famous to the most hidden. The ones with a full walkthrough have their own page. The list grows every round.

When a secret has been shown by two different channels, it’s marked confirmed. When only one channel has shown it, it’s marked unverified.

The biggest secrets

The Skunk Ape, Grassrivers’ Bigfoot

The Grassrivers swamps are home to the Skunk Ape, the Bigfoot of Florida legend. You see it eating a deer on the road, find a wooden effigy in the woods and black out. That kicks off a hidden quest that ends with a camo sniper rifle. Confirmed: Punish, T5G, ZacCoxTV and JFM. See the Skunk Ape mission.

The Easter egg on the radio tower

At the top of the tallest radio tower in the game, after about six minutes of ladder, there’s a nest with an Easter egg. Throw it off the top and a cutscene plays. Confirmed: T5G, ZacCoxTV, Kotaku and Milo. Milo also ran into an influencer hanging out at the top, and you can chat with her (unverified). See the radio tower easter egg.

The jet ski dog

Stranded in the middle of Gambit Bay with no boat, you get rescued by a dog in a life jacket and goggles on a jet ski. It takes you to Dog Island, where a dog digs up about $1,200. Confirmed: ZoneX, ZacCoxTV, T5G, Kotaku and Milo. See the jet ski dog location.

The alien lab (code 2314)

An abandoned facility near Pelican Valley opens with the code 2314. Below it is a government lab with skeletons, a glass of milk and an alien you can set free. Unverified: ZacCoxTV. See the alien lab.

GTA 5 references

  • Michael’s boat. The boat in Jason’s yard is Michael’s, the one stolen in a famous GTA 5 mission. Climb aboard and check the GPS: the Los Santos map is still on it. Confirmed: T5G and ZacCoxTV.
  • Michael’s ringtone. On your phone, go to settings, ringtones, and pick “Wind Chimes”. It’s Michael’s ringtone from GTA 5. Confirmed: T5G and ZacCoxTV.
  • Fabien, the yoga instructor. A magazine in Jason’s backyard features Fabien from GTA 5, a little older. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.
  • Floyd and Debra. In a car with on-demand radio, a true crime podcast says Wade was arrested for the deaths of his cousins in Los Santos, a crime that was Trevor’s in GTA 5. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.
  • Los Santos souvenirs. A trailer in Grassrivers has Grand Senora Desert and Grapeseed postcards and a newspaper with GTA 5’s alien headline. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.

References to other GTA games

  • GTA 4. A book at Jason’s house, “Lethal by Design”, is the memoir of a Liberty City bodyguard who skipped the day Niko Bellic took out his crew. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.
  • Vice City. In the Keys, an iguana wanders around wearing Tommy Vercetti’s shirt. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.
  • San Andreas. Lil’ Probe Inn bumper stickers on cars and a UFO-obsessed house just like San Andreas’ in Bellville, with a meteorite under the bed you can sell. Milo got into the same house and found a snake in the trunk of the car parked out front. Confirmed: ZacCoxTV and Milo.
  • The Epsilon mystery. A truck with the Epsilon eye and a QR code. For now the code just leads to Rockstar’s website. Unverified: T5G.
  • Race’n’Chase. A post on the in-game social feed complains that “Race and Chase 7” got delayed again. Race’n’Chase was the original name of the first GTA, so the joke is about the wait for GTA 6 itself. Kotaku spotted the post early in the game on Snapmatic, the phone’s social app, where someone grumbles that even a game based on the kids’ cartoon Barnyard Blue came out before “Race and Chase 7”. Kotaku reads the 7 as a wink at GTA 7. Confirmed: T5G and Kotaku.
  • “Little Sam” (Chapter 3 spoiler). Skip this one if you haven’t played the Chapter 3 mission “Boobie Time”. When Jason meets up with Boobie Ike, a quiet guy called Little Sam shows up. Fans think he’s Rockstar co-founder Sam Houser and point back to Sam in GTA 3, a minor character long rumored to be him. Rockstar won’t confirm it. Confirmed: GamesRadar+ and Kotaku.
  • Tony the bird. According to Kotaku, Tony the bird from GTA San Andreas makes a cameo, with the same attitude as before. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • A photo from 2021. A location seen in a photo from GTA San Andreas: The Definitive Edition, released in 2021, has been found on the GTA 6 map by players from a fan mapping project. In other words, Rockstar was quietly showing a piece of Leonida five years early. Unverified: Kotaku.

Pop culture

  • Dexter’s apartment. Dexter Morgan’s building is in Bay Harbor. You can’t go in, but the roof has a knife, rope, gloves, plastic and handcuffs. A boat that looks like his is at the docks nearby. GamesRadar+ found the building too, with Dexter’s tools on the roof, but places the complex on Washington Island. Confirmed: T5G, ZacCoxTV and GamesRadar+.
  • SpongeBob’s house. A pineapple-shaped bus stop. Unverified: T5G.
  • Knockoff X-Men. Comics and trading cards with parodies of Magneto, Cyclops and others. GamesRadar+ highlights an issue of “RX Men” starring Dr. Placebo, a villain who looks a lot like Magneto. Confirmed: T5G and GamesRadar+.
  • Brian’s car from Fast and Furious. Brian O’Conner’s 1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse, Paul Walker’s car from the films, sits in a garage on Vespucci Island. According to GamesRadar+, it’s already movie-accurate, no upgrades needed. See the Paul Walker Eclipse.
  • A Hulk in Leonida. A side quest involving strawberries ends with one character turning into a Hulk-like giant, torn clothes and all, according to GamesRadar+. Kotaku mentions a “Red Hulk” side mission that streamer IShowSpeed played live, which looks like the same one. Confirmed: GamesRadar+ and Kotaku.
  • Die Hard and Heat. Without going into detail, GamesRadar+ says later missions borrow heavily from both films. Heat had already inspired GTA 4’s Three Leaf Clover mission. Unverified: GamesRadar+.
  • Bimyou, the “Japanese” bakery. Vice City has a Japanese restaurant called Bimyou whose sign promises sushi and ramen. Inside, it’s a coffee shop, and “sushi” and “ramen” are the names of its breads. In Japanese, “bimyou” means something like “so-so”. One of the breads is coppepan, a Japanese roll similar to a hot dog bun. Kotaku reports that players in Japan love the jab at restaurants that use Japanese words just to sound fancy. Unverified: Kotaku.

Aliens and strange things

  • Late-night abduction. Near Port Gellhorn, between 2 and 3 a.m., you can stumble on a flying saucer abduction. Get too close and you’re next: the screen goes white and you wake up the next day. Milo points to the north end of Kelly County, the same county as Port Gellhorn, at night and as Lucia, and says it’s rare; he didn’t catch it on his own footage. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.
  • Meteorite on Mount Kalaga. At the top of the mountain there’s a crater with a still-smoking meteorite. You can pick it up and sell it. There’s also a ring of stones up there nobody has figured out yet. Unverified: ZacCoxTV. Milo showed a similar meteorite at the top of the map in Lummox County, where Mount Kalaga is: head east down the hill to a wrecked campsite with a glowing purple rock, and you can grab a piece. He doesn’t name the mountain, so it may or may not be the same spot.
  • The Kraken statue. Deep underwater in the southeast, just below Gloriana Key, there’s a giant statue of an octopus grabbing a boat. A scuba diving boat sits above the spot, and a creepy figure appears nearby and sinks out of sight. Nobody has found treasure there yet. Unverified: Robbin Rams.
  • Giant crab. Inside a building, a monster crab can attack out of nowhere. Confirmed: T5G and Kotaku. The mystery has since been solved, from the password to the reward: see the giant crab mystery.
  • The watchtower shed (code 69185). There’s a fire watchtower on Mount Kalaga. Climb to the top: next to a magnifying glass is a sticky note with the word “shed” and the number 69185. The shed is under the tower’s stairs, with a keypad on the door. Punch in 69185 and walk in. Inside is a UFO hunter’s stash: alien stickers, a camera and the lookout’s logbook, which has some strange drawings, including an abduction. Robbin Rams isn’t sure the number is the same in every game, so check the note in yours. Confirmed: Milo and Robbin Rams.
  • A sealed bunker in Grassrivers. Out in the swamps there’s a huge bunker with thick doors and, far from them, a hatch with a keypad that won’t let you type anything. Focus doesn’t highlight a thing. Nobody has found a way in yet. Unverified: Robbin Rams.
  • The SOS island. On a tiny island far out at sea there are cans everywhere, a giant SOS sign and a phone with a video recorded by the people who got stranded there. The video cuts off suddenly. Milo places it off the coast of Mariana County, in the south, but the name is hard to make out in his audio. Unverified: Milo.
  • UFO news. A story in the phone’s news app talks about a wave of UFO sightings across Leonida. Unverified: T5G.

Small details worth seeing

  • Filming the cops gets you in trouble. Record police during an arrest and they get annoyed and may give you a wanted star. Unverified: T5G.
  • Video games at home. Your safehouse gets a console with full games: a zombie shooter, a top-down shooter and a puzzle game. Unverified: T5G.
  • Forklift. You can steal a forklift and use it to flip cars. Unverified: T5G.
  • Escaped polar bear. In one random event, a polar bear breaks out of the truck taking it to the zoo. If it happens after you’ve met a certain hunter, he’ll buy the dead bear from you. Unverified: Kotaku. See also fishing and hunting.
  • 62 kinds of birds. You can log every one of them, according to Kotaku. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • Openings that change. Small details shift depending on how you play: a Kotaku writer and her husband got slightly different intro scenes depending on where and how Lucia was caught by the police. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • Books you can read. Lots of the books lying around can actually be opened, and plenty of them are funny. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • TV worth watching. The TV works like real TV, and some players have lost hours to it. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • Weird Al on the radio. “Weird Al” Yankovic hosts Vice City FM, the all-’80s station. See GTA 6 radio stations and songs.
  • Max weight Jason. Eat plenty of fast food and skip the gym and Jason hits his heaviest, while Lucia changes much less. See the hair and weight system.

Quick answers

What are the easter eggs in GTA 6?

The best known so far: the Skunk Ape (Bigfoot) in Grassrivers, the Easter egg on the radio tower, the jet ski dog, the alien lab, Michael's boat at Jason's house and Dexter's apartment.

Are there GTA 5 references in GTA 6?

Lots. Michael's boat sits at Jason's house with the Los Santos map still on its GPS, Michael's ringtone is in the phone settings, and Fabien the yoga instructor appears in a magazine.

Is Dexter in GTA 6?

His apartment is, in Bay Harbor. You can't go inside, but the roof has a knife, rope, gloves and plastic, like his kill kit.

Are there aliens in GTA 6?

Yes. There's a secret lab with an alien, a UFO abduction late at night, and meteorites you can sell.
