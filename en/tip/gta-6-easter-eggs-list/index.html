GTA 6 Easter Eggs: Every Secret Found So Far

Every GTA 6 easter egg found so far: the Vice City portal, the Mario block, Bigfoot (the Skunk Ape), the radio tower egg, the jet ski dog, the alien lab, Dexter and more.

Confirmed · 15 sourcesUpdated Nov 23, 8:20 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 launched packed with secrets, and players found dozens on day one. This list collects every GTA 6 easter egg that has been shown on video, from the most famous to the most hidden. The ones with a full walkthrough have their own page. The list grows every round.

When a secret has been shown by two different channels, it’s marked confirmed. When only one channel has shown it, it’s marked unverified.

The biggest secrets

The portal to 2002 Vice City

A tower in the south, 12 electrical substations hit in the order that spells “Life Is Passing Me By” (an old Vice City cheat) and a power plant visit between 7 p.m. and 6 a.m. open a pink portal. It drops Jason into the PS2-era Malibu Club, with Kent Paul upstairs. Back home, you get Tommy Vercetti’s blue shirt and a chopper. Confirmed: Kotaku and Dan Allen Gaming. See the Vice City portal easter egg, with the switch for each substation.

The Skunk Ape, Grassrivers’ Bigfoot

The Grassrivers swamps are home to the Skunk Ape, the Bigfoot of Florida legend. You see it eating a deer on the road, find a wooden effigy in the woods and black out. That kicks off a hidden quest that ends with a camo sniper rifle. Confirmed: Punish, T5G, ZacCoxTV and JFM. See the Skunk Ape mission.

The Easter egg on the radio tower

At the top of the tallest radio tower in the game, after about six minutes of ladder, there’s a nest with an Easter egg. Throw it off the top and a cutscene plays. Confirmed: T5G, ZacCoxTV, Kotaku, Milo and InkTV. Milo and InkTV both ran into an influencer hanging out at the top, who chats with you and snaps a photo. See the radio tower easter egg.

The jet ski dog

Stranded in the middle of Gambit Bay with no boat, you get rescued by a dog in a life jacket and goggles on a jet ski. It takes you to Dog Island, where a dog digs up about $1,200. Confirmed: ZoneX, ZacCoxTV, T5G, Kotaku and Milo. See the jet ski dog location.

The alien lab and the alien dream

After the UFO abduction, the character who was taken dreams of alien eggs and a keypad. The light bars in that dream give you the code to a secret lab at South Point Airfield in Hamlet, near Pelican Valley. The code changes between playthroughs: 2134 and 2314 have both turned up (this list used to give only 2314). Down below there are skeletons, a bottle of milk and an alien you can set free. According to T5G, rescuing the alien gives you a permanent boost to stamina, health and focus (0:14). Confirmed: ZacCoxTV and GameSpot. See the alien dream and the alien lab.

GTA 5 references

  • Michael’s boat. The boat in Jason’s yard is Michael’s, the one stolen in a famous GTA 5 mission. Climb aboard and check the GPS: the Los Santos map is still on it. Confirmed: T5G and ZacCoxTV.
  • Michael’s ringtone. On your phone, go to settings, ringtones, and pick “Wind Chimes”. It’s Michael’s ringtone from GTA 5. Confirmed: T5G and ZacCoxTV.
  • Fabien, the yoga instructor. A magazine in Jason’s backyard features Fabien from GTA 5, a little older. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.
  • Floyd and Debra. In a car with on-demand radio, a true crime podcast says Wade was arrested for the deaths of his cousins in Los Santos, a crime that was Trevor’s in GTA 5. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.
  • Los Santos souvenirs. A trailer in Grassrivers has Grand Senora Desert and Grapeseed postcards and a newspaper with GTA 5’s alien headline. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.

References to other GTA games

  • GTA 4. A book at Jason’s house, “Lethal by Design”, is the memoir of a Liberty City bodyguard who skipped the day Niko Bellic took out his crew. Unverified: ZacCoxTV.
  • Vice City. In the Keys, an iguana wanders around wearing Tommy Vercetti’s shirt. Unverified: ZacCoxTV. To visit 2002 Vice City itself, see the secret portal at the top of this page.
  • San Andreas. Lil’ Probe Inn bumper stickers on cars and a UFO-obsessed house just like San Andreas’ in Bellville, with a meteorite under the bed you can sell. Milo got into the same house and found a snake in the trunk of the car parked out front, while InkTV opened the same trunk and found a revolver. Confirmed: ZacCoxTV, Milo and InkTV.
  • The Epsilon mystery. A truck with the Epsilon eye and a QR code. For now the code just leads to Rockstar’s website. Unverified: T5G.
  • Race’n’Chase. A post on the in-game social feed complains that “Race and Chase 7” got delayed again. Race’n’Chase was the original name of the first GTA, so the joke is about the wait for GTA 6 itself. Kotaku spotted the post early in the game on Snapmatic, the phone’s social app, where someone grumbles that even a game based on the kids’ cartoon Barnyard Blue came out before “Race and Chase 7”. Kotaku reads the 7 as a wink at GTA 7. Confirmed: T5G and Kotaku.
  • “Little Sam” (Chapter 3 spoiler). Skip this one if you haven’t played the Chapter 3 mission “Boobie Time”. When Jason meets up with Boobie Ike, a quiet guy called Little Sam shows up. Fans think he’s Rockstar co-founder Sam Houser and point back to Sam in GTA 3, a minor character long rumored to be him. Rockstar won’t confirm it. Confirmed: GamesRadar+ and Kotaku.
  • Tony the bird. According to Kotaku, Tony the bird from GTA San Andreas makes a cameo, with the same attitude as before. ZacCoxTV shows where: during the mission where neighbor Shirley sends you for rum, a foul-mouthed red parrot in a cage behind the liquor store talks about cash and gives away a hidden stash. Confirmed: Kotaku and ZacCoxTV. See the parrot money.
  • The old cities signpost. Tucked among the trees on a small island in the Keys is a wooden signpost pointing to Liberty City, San Fierro, Las Venturas and Los Santos, the cities from earlier GTA games. GameSpot lists it as a WAiNK curiosity, near an abandoned beach party in the middle island chain of the Permit Keys. The post has several arrows: ZacCoxTV reads Liberty City, San Fierro, Las Venturas and Los Santos, and vAnother Level gets a close look at the Los Santos and Vice City arrows. T5G also reads Vice City and Carcer City, the setting of Rockstar’s Manhunt (20:00). Confirmed: ZacCoxTV, GameSpot, vAnother Level and T5G.
  • A photo from 2021. A location seen in a photo from GTA San Andreas: The Definitive Edition, released in 2021, has been found on the GTA 6 map by players from a fan mapping project. In other words, Rockstar was quietly showing a piece of Leonida five years early. T5G tracked the house in the photo down in Bellville (11:56). Break into the garage and there’s a conspiracy board: the owner suspects he lives inside a video game, that everyone looks the same and that it’s all one big loop. A Rottweiler guards the front door, a nod to Chop from GTA 5. Confirmed: Kotaku and T5G.

Pop culture

  • Dexter’s apartment. Dexter Morgan’s building is in Bay Harbor. You can’t go in, but the roof has a knife, rope, gloves, plastic and handcuffs. A boat that looks like his is at the docks nearby. GamesRadar+ found the building too, with Dexter’s tools on the roof, but places the complex on Washington Island. Confirmed: T5G, ZacCoxTV and GamesRadar+. In its second video, T5G goes further (9:43): in Key Lento, a beach house has a garage door you can break open to find Dexter’s kill room, plastic sheeting and all. And in a Leonard County house, through the back door, a bedroom has a paint workbench and a severed mannequin arm with painted nails, a nod to the Ice Truck Killer. Unverified: T5G.
  • SpongeBob’s house. A pineapple-shaped bus stop. Unverified: T5G.
  • Knockoff X-Men. Comics and trading cards with parodies of Magneto, Cyclops and others. GamesRadar+ highlights an issue of “RX Men” starring Dr. Placebo, a villain who looks a lot like Magneto. GameSpot says RX-Men sits on the magazine rack of the 24/7 store just east of Key Lento’s main road. Confirmed: T5G and GamesRadar+. See magazines, books and comics.
  • Brian’s car from Fast and Furious. Brian O’Conner’s 1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse, Paul Walker’s car from the films, sits in a garage on Vespucci Island. According to GamesRadar+, it’s already movie-accurate, no upgrades needed. See the Paul Walker Eclipse.
  • A Hulk in Leonida. A side quest involving strawberries ends with one character turning into a Hulk-like giant, torn clothes and all, according to GamesRadar+. Kotaku mentions a “Red Hulk” side mission that streamer IShowSpeed played live, which looks like the same one. Confirmed: GamesRadar+ and Kotaku. The giant is called Kyle, and at the end you can hug him or decline, as Aaraz Gaming’s clip shows. See the Red Hulk.
  • Die Hard and Heat. Without going into detail, GamesRadar+ says later missions borrow heavily from both films. Heat had already inspired GTA 4’s Three Leaf Clover mission. Unverified: GamesRadar+.
  • Bimyou, the “Japanese” bakery. Vice City has a Japanese restaurant called Bimyou whose sign promises sushi and ramen. Inside, it’s a coffee shop, and “sushi” and “ramen” are the names of its breads. In Japanese, “bimyou” means something like “so-so”. One of the breads is coppepan, a Japanese roll similar to a hot dog bun. Kotaku reports that players in Japan love the jab at restaurants that use Japanese words just to sound fancy. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • Arthur Morgan’s hat. The Red Dead Redemption 2 hero’s hat is for sale at a yard sale in Port Gellhorn, according to PC Gamer. In game it’s called the Genuine Cowboy Hat. See Arthur Morgan’s hat. T5G found one more Arthur nod (5:18): at the Leonard County mini golf course, there’s a chihuahua on the deck of the Wild West saloon, and when you pet it you’ll spot a tiny Arthur Morgan riding it. Unverified: T5G.
  • The Mario block. In brick ruins in the woods of Moreau, Lummox County, a half-invisible block floats near the top of the walls. Jump into it or shoot it and two caps drop: a J for Jason and an L for Lucia. Confirmed: Kotaku and TheGamingRevolution. See the Mario easter egg.
  • Better Call Saul in a dumpster. Walk past the right dumpster and a guy pops out to chat. He’s a lawyer, and he wants painkillers. T5G reads it as a Better Call Saul reference (17:06). Unverified: T5G.
  • The Kill Bill truck. While stealing cars for Kenton, you may come across a yellow truck painted and decorated like the one in Kill Bill, and you can take it (15:35). Unverified: T5G.
  • MrBeast’s chocolate. Around the map you can pick up a chocolate bar that looks a lot like Feastables, MrBeast’s brand (23:12). Unverified: T5G.

Aliens and strange things

  • Late-night abduction. Near Port Gellhorn, between 2 and 3 a.m., you can stumble on a flying saucer abduction. Get too close and you’re next: the screen goes white and you wake up somewhere else (the next day for ZacCoxTV, about three days later for InkTV). Milo points to the north end of Kelly County, the same county as Port Gellhorn, at night and as Lucia, and says it’s rare; he didn’t catch it on his own footage. GameSpot says it only opens up after Chapter 1, and InkTV pulled it off at around 11:30 p.m., earlier than the others suggest. Confirmed: ZacCoxTV, GameSpot and InkTV. See the UFO abduction and, after it, the alien dream.
  • Meteorite on Mount Kalaga. At the top of the mountain there’s a crater with a still-smoking meteorite. You can pick it up and sell it. There’s also a ring of stones up there nobody has figured out yet. Unverified: ZacCoxTV. Milo showed a similar meteorite at the top of the map in Lummox County, where Mount Kalaga is: head east down the hill to a wrecked campsite with a glowing purple rock, and you can grab a piece. He doesn’t name the mountain, so it may or may not be the same spot. T5G says meteor crash sites are scattered all over the map, and the rock he took to a pawn shop was worth just $50 (23:23).
  • The Kraken statue. Deep underwater in the southeast, just below Gloriana Key, there’s a giant statue of an octopus grabbing a boat. A scuba diving boat sits above the spot, and a creepy figure appears nearby and sinks out of sight. Nobody has found treasure there yet. ZacCoxTV dove down to the statue, and Milo swam to the same spot without diving gear, spotted the statue’s arm on the seabed and drowned before getting close. Confirmed: Robbin Rams, ZacCoxTV and Milo. T5G shows a shipwreck in the middle of the ocean wrapped in a dead kraken, which looks like the same spot (20:28).
  • Giant crab. Inside a building, a monster crab can attack out of nowhere. Confirmed: T5G and Kotaku. The mystery has since been solved, from the password to the reward: see the giant crab mystery.
  • The watchtower shed (code 69185). There’s a fire watchtower on Mount Kalaga. Climb to the top: next to a magnifying glass is a sticky note with the word “shed” and the number 69185. The shed is under the tower’s stairs, with a keypad on the door. Punch in 69185 and walk in. Inside is a UFO hunter’s stash: alien stickers, a camera and the lookout’s logbook, which has some strange drawings, including an abduction. Robbin Rams isn’t sure the number is the same in every game, so check the note in yours. Confirmed: Milo and Robbin Rams.
  • A sealed bunker in Grassrivers. Out in the swamps there’s a huge bunker with thick doors and, far from them, a hatch with a keypad that won’t let you type anything. Focus doesn’t highlight a thing. Nobody has found a way in yet. Unverified: Robbin Rams.
  • The SOS island. On a tiny island far out at sea there are cans everywhere, a giant SOS sign and a phone with a video recorded by the people who got stranded there. The video cuts off suddenly. Milo places it off the coast of Mariana County, in the south, but the name is hard to make out in his audio. InkTV also puts it in the south, right on the Mariana County line, and ZacCoxTV says it’s out in the Keys, far from land. Besides the SOS, the words “SEND BEER” are spelled out in cans on the sand. T5G also places it off Mariana County and reads it as a nod to Lost: castaways, an SOS and something supernatural (17:41). Confirmed: Milo, ZacCoxTV, InkTV and T5G.
  • The desert island piano. On a tiny island south of Cottonmouth Lake and Gambit Bay (in the Shoeless Keys, according to vAnother Level), an old grand piano sits abandoned on the sand among the mangroves. It’s one of the WAiNK app’s curiosities, and GameSpot thinks it may nod to the 1993 film The Piano. Confirmed: ZacCoxTV and GameSpot.
  • The merman of the Keys. On a beach in the Keys, a man in a mermaid tail films himself on a tripod, talking in a high-pitched voice. You can mess with him: walk in front of his camera or knock the tripod over and he drops back into his normal voice. T5G found the same merman on the little islands, filming for his followers (18:29). Confirmed: ZacCoxTV and T5G.
  • Creature carcasses. In Wetman Bay, on an island north of a beach covered in dead sharks, lies the carcass of a half-fish creature. ZacCoxTV compares it to what people claim real mermaids look like, while GameSpot calls it alien remains. GameSpot lists two more carcasses as WAiNK curiosities, on Abismo Key and Goose Key, and each of the three has a meteorite nearby. Confirmed: ZacCoxTV and GameSpot.
  • The lighthouse ghosts. At night, climb the outside stairs of a tall lighthouse and greenish ghostly figures appear at the top. One goes over the railing while the other panics. Right after, Milo got The Truth Is Out There, the same trophy as the abduction. It only happens at night. T5G shows the same scene (14:08): arrive close to midnight and look up at the light with binoculars. A nearby house has the keeper’s blue logbook. Come back at exactly 1:30 a.m., use the binoculars again and a ship appears; dive where it was and you’ll find a wreck on the seafloor. Confirmed: Milo and T5G.
  • The haunted hotel. The Marbelle Hotel, a closed-down building for sale in Vice City, has a door that opens on its own and strange writing on the walls. After that the whole map turns into a foggy night, Silent Hill style, and a notebook on the roof has the safe code. Confirmed: Robbin Rams and T5G. See the haunted hotel.
  • The Port Gellhorn ritual and Satan (confirmed by T5G and MrDalekJD; full guide on its own page). In a Port Gellhorn house, a group surrounded by demonic imagery chats with you and eventually invites you to their ritual. A van picks you up, you wake up as they dance around a campfire, and they ask you to look into the flames. What follows is a Doom-style run through hell, with heavy rock and waves of demons, ending in a boss fight with Satan himself (5:38). Unverified: T5G.
  • The Capri summoning circle. Near Tortoise Lake in Capri, an abandoned office building has its door wide open. Inside are a pentagram on the floor, a book of human sacrifice drawings and writing on the walls. Bring the body of any NPC, place it in the circle and things get very strange (7:14). Unverified: T5G.
  • UFO news. A story in the phone’s news app talks about a wave of UFO sightings across Leonida. Unverified: T5G.

Small details worth seeing

  • Filming the cops gets you in trouble. Record police during an arrest and they get annoyed and may give you a wanted star. Milo tried it: he pulled out his camera during an arrest, the officers turned on him and he had to run. Confirmed: T5G and Milo.
  • Video games at home. Your safehouse gets a console with full games: a zombie shooter, a top-down shooter and a puzzle game. Unverified: T5G.
  • Forklift. You can steal a forklift and use it to flip cars. Unverified: T5G.
  • The Peewees billboards. Four pink Peewees billboards stand by the highways in Mariana, Vice-Dale, Leonard and Lummox counties. Scan all four with WAiNK to win the grand prize, a Peewees T-shirt and cap (the Calorie Maxxing set, per Jun Guides). PowerPyx notes they aren’t needed for any trophy or for 100% completion. Confirmed: PowerPyx and Jun Guides.
  • The question mark box. Inside a storage container you may find a box covered in question marks. Eat what’s inside and you’re dropped into a wild obstacle course of floating objects, a slide, an underwater stretch full of jellyfish and an exit through the flames (16:17). Unverified: T5G.
  • The abandoned Port Gellhorn funfair. None of the rides work, but use binoculars on the stickers on a box and you can read tributes to people who died on the rides. An abandoned dog hangs around waiting for an owner who isn’t coming back (20:42). Unverified: T5G.
  • The guarded Tallaloko estate. In Tallaloko, a gated estate has armed private security. Fight your way in and you’ll find a mansion you can’t enter, a lot full of supercars and, on the lake, a helipad with a helicopter T5G says you can’t find anywhere else (22:19). Unverified: T5G. See also helicopter and plane locations.
  • The angry cyclist. In a random encounter at a gas station, a cyclist chews you out for driving instead of riding a bike (22:46). Unverified: T5G.
  • Escaped polar bear. In one random event, a polar bear breaks out of the truck taking it to the zoo. If it happens after you’ve met a certain hunter, he’ll buy the dead bear from you. Unverified: Kotaku. See also fishing and hunting.
  • 62 kinds of birds. You can log every one of them, according to Kotaku. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • Openings that change. Small details shift depending on how you play: a Kotaku writer and her husband got slightly different intro scenes depending on where and how Lucia was caught by the police. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • Books you can read. Lots of the books lying around can actually be opened, and plenty of them are funny. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • TV worth watching. The TV works like real TV, and some players have lost hours to it. Unverified: Kotaku.
  • Weird Al on the radio. “Weird Al” Yankovic hosts Vice City FM, the all-’80s station. See GTA 6 radio stations and songs.
  • Max weight Jason. Eat plenty of fast food and skip the gym and Jason hits his heaviest, while Lucia changes much less. See the hair and weight system.

Quick answers

What are the easter eggs in GTA 6?

The best known so far: the secret portal to PS2-era Vice City, the Mario block, the Skunk Ape (Bigfoot) in Grassrivers, the Easter egg on the radio tower, the jet ski dog and the alien lab.

Are there GTA 5 references in GTA 6?

Lots. Michael's boat sits at Jason's house with the Los Santos map still on its GPS, Michael's ringtone is in the phone settings, and Fabien the yoga instructor appears in a magazine.

Is Dexter in GTA 6?

His apartment is, in Bay Harbor. You can't go inside, but the roof has a knife, rope, gloves and plastic, like his kill kit.

Are there aliens in GTA 6?

Yes. There's a night-time UFO abduction, a dream full of alien eggs that gives you the code to a secret lab with an alien inside, and meteorites you can sell.
