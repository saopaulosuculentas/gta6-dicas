GTA 6 Giant Crab Mystery: Computer Password, Safe Code and Reward

The giant crab mystery is in a lab in Mariana County. The computer password is CLEAVES, the safe code is 00072 and the boathouse code is 0212. The reward is a stash bag for Brian and the Crabshack VF5 SMG.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 5:01 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s giant crab mystery can be solved. It’s in a hidden lab in Mariana County where someone was experimenting on crabs to make them bigger. The computer password is CLEAVES, the safe code is 00072, the boathouse door code is 0212, and at the end you walk away with a stash bag and the Crabshack VF5 SMG. Gator Keys solved it on stream, and YOUNG DIRK ran through the same codes in his own video.

Where it is

  • Area: Mariana County, in southern Leonida. YOUNG DIRK calls the area Pelican Valley, which may just be another name for the same stretch of swamp.
  • The spot: a cluster of strange buildings near the water, with a boathouse next to them.
  • The clue: a blood trail runs from the main building to the boathouse door, which has a keypad.
  • Getting there: YOUNG DIRK flew in by plane so he wouldn’t need a boat. Try to land right at the site, because the water around it is full of alligators.

The Skunk Ape lives in the same county; see the Skunk Ape mission.

Step by step

  1. Turn on the power. The switch is at the back of the main house. YOUNG DIRK does this before going inside.
  2. Check the back bedroom. There are words carved into the wood. Look at them and use the on-screen zoom: each one becomes a password option on the computer.
  3. Use the computer and pick CLEAVES. YOUNG DIRK cycles through the options with left on the D-pad.
  4. Read the journal. It logs crab experiments. Most crabs died. One was a breakthrough: specimen 00036.
  5. Jump scare. When you back out of the computer, the giant crab attacks. Jason kicks it off and it runs. You can’t chase it.
  6. Find where it came from. The tank it broke out of has more notes: the crabs were being raised to eat and sell.
  7. Go back to the computer. A note says safe code = double success. The success was number 36, so the code is 36 × 2 = 00072.
  8. Open the safe. It holds some cash ($1,000 in YOUNG DIRK’s video) and the emergency protocol note. Zoom in on it (the prompt in YOUNG DIRK’s video is RT on Xbox, R2 on PS5) or press Triangle (Y on Xbox) to show the text: the number is 0212.
  9. Enter the boathouse. Follow the blood trail and punch 0212 into the keypad. The Crabshack VF5 is lying on the floor right by the entrance. Further in, the person behind the experiments made it there and died. On them are a few dollars and a stash bag for Brian.

The boathouse code

Gator Keys didn’t read the emergency protocol number out loud. In YOUNG DIRK’s video the note is on screen with 0212 (2:02), and he types that number into the door keypad (2:40). If the door won’t open in your game, double-check the note from the safe.

Reward

Item Value
Stash bag Between $8,000 and about $10,000, depending on the video. Fence it at Brian’s lockup (fence locations)
Crabshack VF5 Unique SMG with a scope already on it. YOUNG DIRK says it goes into your weapon locker for good
Safe Cash ($1,000 in YOUNG DIRK’s video)

The payout isn’t the same in every video. Gator Keys puts the bag at about $10,000. YOUNG DIRK got $8,000 from Brian for it, plus $1,000 from the safe and $12 off the body, so roughly $9,000 in total.

The reference

Gator Keys points to Island Claws, a 1980s horror movie where a marine biologist uses growth hormones to breed bigger crabs for food and a giant one breaks loose in the Florida Keys. That’s the exact region GTA 6 recreates.

Quick answers

What is the crab computer password in GTA 6?

CLEAVES. The password options appear after you look at the words carved into the wood in the bedroom; Cleaves is the right one.

What is the crab lab safe code in GTA 6?

00072. The note says the code is "double success": the crab that worked was number 36, and 36 times 2 is 72.

What is the boathouse door code in the GTA 6 crab mystery?

0212. It's written on the emergency protocol note you find with the safe. YOUNG DIRK shows the note on screen and punches the same number into the keypad.

What do you get for the giant crab mystery?

The Crabshack VF5 SMG and a stash bag to sell to Brian. The cash varies: Gator Keys puts the bag at about $10,000, while YOUNG DIRK got $8,000 for it plus $1,000 from the safe.
