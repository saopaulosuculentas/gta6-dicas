GTA 6 Giant Crab Mystery: Computer Password, Safe Code and Reward

The giant crab mystery is in a lab in Mariana County. The computer password is CLEAVES and the safe code is 00072. At the end you get about $10,000 and the Crabshack VF5 SMG.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 4:20 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s giant crab mystery can be solved. It’s in a hidden lab in Mariana County where someone was experimenting on crabs to make them bigger. The computer password is CLEAVES, the safe code is 00072, and at the end you walk away with a stash bag worth about $10,000 and the Crabshack VF5 SMG. Gator Keys solved it on stream.

Where it is

  • Area: Mariana County, in southern Leonida.
  • The spot: a cluster of strange buildings near the water, with a boathouse next to them.
  • The clue: a blood trail runs from the main building to the boathouse door, which has a keypad.

The Skunk Ape lives in the same county; see the Skunk Ape mission.

Step by step

  1. Check the bedroom. There are words carved into the wood. Look at them: each one becomes a password option on the computer.
  2. Use the computer and pick CLEAVES.
  3. Read the journal. It logs crab experiments. Most crabs died. One was a breakthrough: specimen 00036.
  4. Jump scare. When you back out of the computer, the giant crab attacks. Jason kicks it off and it runs. You can’t chase it.
  5. Find where it came from. The tank it broke out of has more notes: the crabs were being raised to eat and sell.
  6. Go back to the computer. A note says safe code = double success. The success was number 36, so the code is 36 × 2 = 00072.
  7. Open the safe. It holds some cash and the emergency protocol number, which is the code for the boathouse door.
  8. Enter the boathouse. The person behind the experiments made it there and died. On them are a stash bag for Brian (about $10,000) and the Crabshack VF5 SMG.

Gator Keys didn’t read the emergency protocol number out loud, so we don’t publish it here. Grab it from the safe.

Reward

Item Value
Stash bag About $10,000 once fenced at Brian’s lockup (fence locations)
Crabshack VF5 Unique SMG
Safe Cash

The reference

Gator Keys points to Island Claws, a 1980s horror movie where a marine biologist uses growth hormones to breed bigger crabs for food and a giant one breaks loose in the Florida Keys. That’s the exact region GTA 6 recreates.

Quick answers

What is the crab computer password in GTA 6?

CLEAVES. The password options appear after you look at the words carved into the wood in the bedroom; Cleaves is the right one.

What is the crab lab safe code in GTA 6?

00072. The note says the code is "double success": the crab that worked was number 36, and 36 times 2 is 72.

What do you get for the giant crab mystery?

A stash bag for Brian worth about $10,000 and the Crabshack VF5 SMG, both found on the dead scientist inside the boathouse.
