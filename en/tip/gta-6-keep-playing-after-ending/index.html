Can You Keep Playing GTA 6 After the Ending? How the Post-Game Works

Yes. Once the final mission and credits are done, GTA 6 rewinds to just before the finale and lets you roam free. You can replay the ending, chase 100% and unlock cheats.

Unverified · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:56 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Yes, you can keep playing GTA 6 after the ending. Once the credits finish, the game drops you back at the moment right before the final mission, and from there the whole map is open to you. Kotaku, which has already played through to the end, laid out how it works without spoiling the story.

How the ending works

  1. The game warns you first. When you reach the last mission, GTA 6 makes a big deal of asking whether you really want to start the finale. Kotaku’s reviewer got there after roughly 90 hours.
  2. The finale is several missions long. When it’s over, you see one of the main endings and the credits.
  3. Then the clock rewinds. You’re back at the moment you were asked. This time Jason and Lucia say they aren’t ready yet and head back out into Vice City.
  4. The map is yours. From then on you can clean up whatever you left behind and explore as much as you like.

You can see another ending

The final mission marker stays on the map. Play the finale again and you may land on a different ending.

GTA 6 has more than one ending, and which one you get comes down to your choices. Kotaku saw two of them and says they go in very different directions. We’re keeping the details out of this page.

Rockstar confirmed the multiple endings to GameSpot as well. GameSpot’s own team saw two, and with other players’ reports added in, it counts at least 4. The site says the best ending asks for two things: Jason and Lucia at the top relationship level and the couple’s savings maxed out at $2 million. So before you replay the finale, it’s worth raising the relationship and banking more cash.

For how many endings people have found so far and what drives each one, with no spoilers, see how many endings GTA 6 has.

What unlocks after the finale

Beating the final mission opens up two things that were locked before:

  • 100% completion. You can only finish everything after the ending. That includes the A Long Road trophy from our GTA 6 trophy and achievement list.
  • Cheat codes. The challenges that reveal the game’s cheats only show up once the story is done. See every GTA 6 cheat and how to use cheats.

Do you need to clear the map first?

No. There’s a huge amount to do outside the story: main and side missions, activities, random events and races. Since the map stays open after the credits, you can push through the story and save the rest for later. To see how much story is left, our GTA 6 mission list has all 5 chapters in order.

How it compares to other Rockstar games

Rockstar’s earlier open worlds handled the post-game in different ways:

Game What happens after the ending
Red Dead Redemption 2 You keep going as a different character
GTA IV You keep playing as Niko, like in most GTA games
GTA 6 The game rewinds to just before the finale, with Jason and Lucia

The catch

Kotaku points out one downside: after the ending, the world feels frozen in time. No big new storylines can kick off, since they could clash with how the story wraps up. What’s left is the map, unfinished activities and the cheats.

Quick answers

Does GTA 6 end after the final mission?

No. After the credits, the game takes you back to the moment before the finale and you carry on as Jason and Lucia.

Can you get a different ending in GTA 6?

Yes. The final mission marker stays on the map, and the ending you get depends on the choices you made.

Do you need to beat GTA 6 to get 100%?

Yes. According to Kotaku, 100% completion and the challenges that reveal cheat codes only open up after the final mission.

How long does it take to reach the end of GTA 6?

Kotaku's reviewer needed roughly 90 hours to reach the final mission.
