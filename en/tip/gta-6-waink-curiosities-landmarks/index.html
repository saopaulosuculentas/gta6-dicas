GTA 6 WAiNK Curiosities and Landmarks: Where to Scan Them

GTA 6 has 31 WAiNK landmarks and, per one video guide, 35 curiosities to scan. Here are the 15 already mapped in Mariana County and exactly where to find them.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:55 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

There are 31 WAiNK landmarks to scan in GTA 6, according to GameSpot, and vAnother Level’s video guide counts 35 curiosities on top of that. Both lists count toward the You WAiNKer! trophy, which needs 100 scans. Below are the 15 spots in Mariana County that GameSpot has published so far, all of which also show up in the video.

To scan, hold Up on the D-pad, aim and press R1 (RB on Xbox). The full walkthrough of how scanning works is on the WAiNK sculptures page.

Curiosities in Mariana County

GameSpot’s route starts near Jason’s house, heads south and west, then finishes in the swamps up north.

Curiosity Where it is
Christmas House A house fully decked out for Christmas on the westernmost street in Key Lento
Plunge Masters Deep Sea Chalet Same street, at the Plunge Masters sign. Scan the mural behind the shed
Abismo Key Carcass Take the highway south to Abismo Key and walk through the mangroves to the beach. It’s the skeleton of something that looks alien
Derelict Spy Vessel A ship run aground at the tip of the southeastern Permit Keys island. Bring a boat
Beach Party Site An abandoned beach party on the northern edge of the central Permit Keys island
Fingerpost Right next to the beach party, tucked behind trees and mangroves. A prompt pops up when you’re close
Goose Key Carcass Another carcass, along the northeastern shore of Mile Zero Air Station
Desert Island Piano A piano on a tiny island in the chain south of Cottonmouth Lake and Gambit Bay
Vibrant Pond A warning sign in front of a pool of muck in the swamp to the north
Dead Sharks A beach covered in rotting sharks on the southern islands of Wetman Bay
Wetman Bay Carcass The third carcass, on the island north of the sharks
NoFlusher Homestead Home of a NoFlusher, one of the game’s oddball groups, west of the Kingfisher swamp base

Be careful at the Goose Key Carcass: it’s inside the military base, which is a restricted area, so try not to let the soldiers see you. See how to get in at military base location.

Each of the three carcasses has a meteorite lying nearby. Find out what to do with them in meteorite locations and selling.

Landmarks in Mariana County

Landmark Where it is
Badger R&D Tower The big radio tower on the main road, south of Brian’s marina in Key Lento
Thicc Herbie The giant blimp on Maroon Key, where the mission Hey, Arnold takes you
Riquezas Key Radome The radome at the southern tip of the Riquezas Keys, past Mile Zero Air Station

Thicc Herbie is a trick one. GameSpot couldn’t scan the blimp while standing on Maroon Key; it only worked from far away, at Abismo Key State Park across the water to the south. vAnother Level scanned it from the park’s parking lot off the Sunbelt Hwy.

More from the video guide

vAnother Level went through all 108 scannables in the game. A few examples outside Mariana County:

  • Landmarks: Aqua Stadium (Gloriana Key), Fleeca Field (Rockridge) and the giant Vice City sign (West Vice-Dale County).
  • Curiosities: the Halloween House (central Capri) and a UFO-shaped animal barn in the backyard of a light-blue house on Kingston Rd in Watson Bay.
  • NoFlusher tags: 15 faint teal chalk symbols on the sidewalk, most of them in Ocean Beach. Cal texts you asking if you’ve ever noticed strange symbols on a manhole. Every tag counts as a scan too.

One difference between the sources: the video files the Derelict Spy Vessel under landmarks, while GameSpot puts it with the curiosities. It’s the same spot either way; only the list changes.

Quick answers

How many WAiNK landmarks are in GTA 6?

There are 31, according to both GameSpot and vAnother Level's video guide.

How many WAiNK curiosities are in GTA 6?

vAnother Level counts 35. GameSpot's list isn't finished yet and covers 12 so far, all in Mariana County.

Why can't I scan the Thicc Herbie blimp in GTA 6?

It only scans from a distance. Head to Abismo Key State Park, across the water to the south of Maroon Key, and scan it from there.

Are there dead aliens in GTA 6?

There are three strange creature carcasses that count as WAiNK curiosities: on Abismo Key, Goose Key and in Wetman Bay. Each one has a meteorite nearby.
