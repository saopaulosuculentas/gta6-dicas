How to Refuel and Repair Your Car in GTA 6

Pull up to a gas pump, open the Services prompt and pick Refuel to fill up or Repair to fix the car. The price shows first. Electric cars charge at their own stations.

Confirmed · 4 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 5:01 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

To refuel or repair your car in GTA 6, pull up next to a gas pump, open the Services prompt and choose Refuel or Repair. The cost is shown on screen before you commit.

The step by step comes from PC Gamer, and MrBossFTW, Camodo Gaming and StevieWunderKid show the same system in their gameplay.

Finding a gas station

Gas stations are marked on the map with a fuel pump icon. Open the map and pick the closest one. Camodo Gaming ran low halfway through a trip and pulled into a station before crossing over to an island, so top up before a long drive.

Checking fuel and car condition

While driving, press down on the D-pad. According to MrBossFTW, that brings up the body condition meter, which works like a health bar for the car, with the fuel gauge right next to it.

StevieWunderKid noticed the fuel needle on the dashboard actually drops as the tank empties, and the engine temperature climbs when you sit still. To see the dashboard up close, pull out your phone while driving and the camera moves inside the car.

How to refuel

  1. Drive up close to the pump until the Services prompt appears.
  2. Choose Refuel. The dollar cost is listed next to it.
  3. Press the interact button.
  4. Jason or Lucia grabs the pump handle.
  5. Hold the button shown in the lower right corner until the tank fills.
  6. They hang the handle back up and you’re back in the driver’s seat.

You won’t do it often

Fuel was one of the most debated features before launch, but it barely gets in the way. According to PC Gamer, a full tank lasts a long time, even when you’re driving fast.

The catch is stolen cars. A random driver’s tank might already be running on fumes when you take it.

Electric cars

Electric cars skip the pump. Look for charging stations on the map instead:

  1. Pull up close to one.
  2. Pick Charge Vehicle. The cost appears on screen.
  3. A quick shot of the charger readout shows the battery climbing to 100%.
  4. Once it’s done, drive off.

How to repair your car

Repairs are the real reason to visit a gas station, and they happen at the same pump:

  1. Pull up until Services appears.
  2. Choose Repair instead of Refuel.
  3. The camera swoops up into the sky for a moment. When it comes back down, the car is fixed.

The price scales with the damage. It’s worth paying to protect a car you’ve customized at a car shop, or a nicer car you’ve claimed by pulling its tracker at a Pay ‘n’ Spray once the story unlocks that.

Other places to repair

According to MrBossFTW, the gas pump isn’t your only option: Pay ‘n’ Spray and mod shops fix cars too. All of them charge, so in your first few hours, when cash is tight, he suggests driving carefully to keep the car in one piece. Camodo Gaming limped a wrecked car over to Coastal Auto Care, Joey’s shop, to get it fixed.

For other car tricks, like shoving a car that ran dry, see GTA 6 hidden features. To keep a car you stole, read how to steal cars, and to park your own, check the garage locations and prices.

Quick answers

How do you get gas in GTA 6?

Pull up next to a pump at any gas station, pick Refuel from the Services prompt and hold the on-screen button until the tank is full.

How do you repair your car in GTA 6?

Use the same gas pump and pick Repair from the Services prompt. The price depends on how banged up the car is.

How do you charge an electric car in GTA 6?

Find a charging station on the map, pull up and choose Charge Vehicle. A short scene shows the battery filling to 100%.

Do cars run out of gas fast in GTA 6?

No. A full tank lasts a long time, but a car you take from a stranger may already be close to empty.
