Where to Find All Magazines, Books and Comics in GTA 6

GTA 6 has 27 magazines, books and comics to collect. Five are in Jason's house, and GameSpot says to read them all before Chapter 1 ends. Here's where they are.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:41 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

There are 27 magazines, books and comics to collect in GTA 6, going by the counter that appears on screen when you pick up your first one. The earliest batch is in Jason’s house in Key Lento, and GameSpot’s advice is to read all of them before Chapter 1 wraps up.

Inside Jason’s house (Chapter 1)

Title Where it is
Lethal By Design Coffee table in the living room
Orca: A Whole Different Animal Desk next to a radio and a stack of books
Ashes of Glory Bedside table
Body Copy Table on the back porch, by the workout area
Reel Talk Inside the shed under the house

GameSpot doesn’t say exactly what happens to these after Chapter 1, only that you shouldn’t put them off. Grab all five early to be safe.

In Christian Konopatzki’s video, Ashes of Glory gets picked up during the mission Feng Shui, when Jason heads home to catch up with Cal. The moment he takes it, the corner of the screen reads Magazines, Books & Comics 1 / 27.

Out in Key Lento

Title Where it is
RX-Men Magazine rack in the 24/7 store east of Key Lento’s main road

RX-Men is a spoof of the X-Men. GameSpot is still adding to its list, so these six are the only published locations for now.

How to read one

  1. Walk up and press the Examine prompt.
  2. With the book in hand you get four options: R1 to open it, Triangle to show the text, Square to flip it and Circle to put it back (RB, Y, X and B on Xbox).
  3. Page through to the end before you put it down.

Why bother

  • Map markers. GameSpot says some of these mark points of interest on your map. See what that can unlock in how to increase Focus.
  • Collection. Each one ticks up the 27-item counter.
  • Laughs. Plenty of them poke fun at life in Leonida or spoof real-world media.

To know where you stand in the story while you collect, keep the Chapter 1 walkthrough handy. If you’re hunting collectibles in general, collectible cards work differently and can be sold.

Quick answers

How many magazines, books and comics are in GTA 6?

The in-game counter says 27. It pops up in the corner of the screen when you grab your first one.

Can you miss the magazines in Jason's house in GTA 6?

GameSpot says to read every book and magazine in the house before the end of Chapter 1. Don't leave them for later.

Where is the RX-Men comic in GTA 6?

On the magazine rack of the 24/7 store just east of the main road through Key Lento.

Why read magazines in GTA 6?

Some of them mark points of interest on your map. The rest are jokes or bits of Leonida lore, and they all count toward the collection.
