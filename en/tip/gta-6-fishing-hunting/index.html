GTA 6 Fishing and Hunting: How to Do It and Where to Sell

In GTA 6, fishing starts on the dock behind Jason's house and boosts your focus. Hunting is the one that pays: Bret in Lummox County buys animals straight out of your trunk. Here's how both work.

Confirmed · 7 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 6:16 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

You can fish and hunt in GTA 6. Fishing starts with the rods on the dock behind Jason’s house and it’s one of the best ways to raise your focus. Hunting is where the money is: you sell your kills to Bret in Lummox County, who buys them straight out of your trunk.

Where to fish

  • At home: Arekkz points out there are fishing rods at the back of Jason’s house, so you don’t have to travel anywhere.
  • From a boat: KhrazeGaming fishes off the side of a boat out at sea.
  • More spots: GamesRadar+ says the Activities tab of the map index lists every fishing and hunting spot you’ve discovered.

How to fish, step by step

  1. Grab a rod and walk to the edge of the water.
  2. Press L3 + R3 (focus) to highlight the fish, so you know where to cast. Arekkz uses this every time.
  3. Cast your line toward the fish. DPJ holds L2 first, then holds R2 to cast (LT and RT on Xbox). The longer you hold R2, the farther the bait flies.
  4. Reel in slowly to lure a fish toward the bait. The button for this depends on your reel mode, covered below.
  5. The moment you feel a nibble, press R2 to hook it. DPJ says the cue is the controller rumbling.
  6. Don’t reel yet. Let the fish thrash until it wears itself out. If it pulls one way, pull your rod the opposite way.
  7. Once it calms down, reel in as fast as you can. If it starts fighting again, repeat step 6.
  8. Near the boat, your character lifts the fish out of the water and you choose whether to keep it or throw it back, per DPJ.

Steps 4 to 7 are KhrazeGaming’s method, which he compares to the fishing in Monster Hunter. His footage shows the on-screen prompts: Hook Fish (R2), Lure Fish, Reset Cast and Grip (L2). In DPJ’s video the same Grip prompt shows up on LT, the Xbox left trigger.

Pick your reel mode

There’s a setting just for how you reel in. DPJ found it under Pause > Settings > Gameplay > Fishing, at the bottom of the list. You get 3 options:

  1. Rotate the right stick.
  2. Button: hold or tap Square (X on Xbox).
  3. Pull the right stick.

DPJ recommends option 2. He doesn’t say which one is selected by default.

That’s why guides disagree on how to lure a fish. Some creators hold L2 and gently move the right stick, which matches one of the stick modes, while DPJ is on the button mode and taps Square instead. If the stick does nothing in your game, check this setting.

Reading the fight

DPJ breaks down what to watch once a fish is on the line:

  • Bubbles on the water and the line pulling: the fish is fighting. Tilt the left stick away from the direction it’s pulling. If it’s swimming away from you, pull the stick down.
  • Bubbles gone: that’s your window to reel in, using whatever input your reel mode uses. Stop when the bubbles come back and go back to steering with the left stick.
  • Keep going until the fish is next to the boat. Then you get the keep or release choice from step 8.

What fishing gets you

  • Focus: Arekkz says each fish gives a 5% focus bonus, far more than the 1% per basket from basketball. BennyCentral calls fishing the most effective way to train focus, and GamingBolt lists it among the activities that permanently raise it.
  • Baited My Tackle trophy: beat Cal in a fishing challenge.
  • Fisheye cheat: unlocked by catching 100 fish. Cheats only work after you finish the main story.

What about selling fish? No source has shown fish being sold so far.

How hunting works

You can hunt any animal you find, or take requests from Bret, Jason’s old military buddy. According to PC Gamer:

  • Bret’s hunting missions open up in Chapter 2, in Lummox County, and they’re Jason only.
  • He asks for tougher and tougher animals and pays more each time.
  • There are legendary animals, and they’re worth more.
  • A single kill pays anywhere from hundreds to thousands of dollars.

For gear, the index sorts Ammu-Nation stores by speciality, and one of them is Hunting, per GamesRadar+. Hunting a unique animal for Bret unlocks the Fair Game trophy.

Where to sell animals

Your buyer is Bret:

  • Small animals: park outside his building and walk in. He automatically buys whatever is in your trunk.
  • Big animals: PC Gamer’s writer spotted a large boar in the woods and shot it with the rifle from his truck. Bret paid $2,500 and came to collect the carcass himself.

So haul your kills in your personal vehicle. GamesRadar+ confirms its trunk stores the spoils from hunting trips. Nobody has published a full price list per animal yet. For other ways to earn, see how to make money in GTA 6.

Quick answers

How do you fish in GTA 6?

Grab a rod from the dock behind Jason's house, use focus (L3 + R3) to spot fish, cast, slowly reel to lure one in and press R2 to hook it. Let it tire out before you reel it in.

Can you hunt in GTA 6?

Yes. You can hunt any animal you come across, or take hunting jobs from Bret, Jason's old military buddy, starting in Chapter 2.

Where do you sell animals in GTA 6?

To Bret in Lummox County. Park outside his building and walk in, and he buys whatever is in your trunk. He picks up big kills himself.

Can you sell fish in GTA 6?

No source has shown fish being sold so far. For now, fishing pays off in focus and in your fish collection.
