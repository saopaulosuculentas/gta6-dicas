GTA 6 Parrot Money: How to Rob the "Strange Noises" Liquor Store

The Three Sheets liquor store hides over $2,000 in its Exotic Animals Front ("Strange Noises") operation: a safe, the register and a stash only the parrot gives away, in the vent by the generator.

Confirmed · 6 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 11:10 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The parrot money is at the Three Sheets liquor store, the same one where Jason’s neighbor Shirley sends you to pick up rum. The back room runs an exotic animal smuggling racket with over $2,000 lying around, and the best part sits in a stash only the parrot gives away. Five channels showed the same robbery: TheProfessional, vassillios16, Arekkz Gaming, KultZ and KhrazeGaming. GamesRadar+ has since published a full guide and gives the official name: in game it’s the Exotic Animals Front illegal operation. The store is in Key Lento, just north of the Pay ‘n’ Spray there.

How the robbery shows up

  1. Go to the liquor store. If you took Shirley’s side job, this is where you pick up her bottle. According to GamesRadar+, Shirley lives a few doors down from Jason, in the house with the orange and green parasol, and she wants a bottle of Salty Squid rum.
  2. Inside, you’ll hear animal noises from the back. The clerk claims it’s the animal channel on TV.
  3. When you leave the store, the spot appears on your map as a robbery called “Strange Noises”: the clerk seems distracted by something in the back.
  4. If you’re carrying Shirley’s rum, GamesRadar+ suggests stowing it in your trunk before you head round the back.

On the way: before you leave for the store, give Shirley’s dog Argula a pet. GamesRadar+ says it counts as a thoughtful action and can raise your focus bar, depending on your criminal profile. See how to pet animals.

You don’t need Shirley’s job for this. vassillios16 went straight there after the prologue. If you can’t see the store, look up liquor stores in the map index.

Getting in

  • Park in the alley next to the store.
  • Climb the dumpster (or hop the chain-link gate) into the back area. GamesRadar+ climbed the fence on the left side of the store as you face it.
  • About four guys are waiting. They grab machetes, and one may pull a gun.
  • TheProfessional recommends knocking them all out with your fists. Gunfire brings the cops; without them you can loot at your own pace.

How many guards? This page said “about four”, going by the videos. GamesRadar+ counts five or six armed criminals patrolling the walled-off area, so plan for the bigger number.

Going in quietly

GamesRadar+ suggests a stealthy approach that counts toward a professional criminal profile:

  1. Pick up the bottles scattered around the area.
  2. Throw them to make noise and lure the guards out of the walled section one at a time.
  3. Take each one down quietly.

Going in guns blazing works too, but it pushes your profile toward violent and brings the cops. More on that in GTA 6 criminal profile explained.

The safe has no code: rotate the right stick until the lock gives, like the other early safes.

The parrot and the stash

There’s a parrot in the back that keeps squawking about “cash” and a “stash”.

  1. Walk up to the cage and press LT (L2 on PS5) to talk to it.
  2. Ask where the money is. In GamesRadar+’s guide the options are “Cash?” and then “Where’s the cash?”. It repeats “generator, generator”.
  3. Walk to the generator but don’t touch it. Open the grate right behind it.
  4. The cash is inside.

Facing the parrot, the generator is straight ahead and to the left, in the corner. Next to it is a white vent you can open. GamesRadar+ found exactly $1,263 in it.

Arekkz Gaming describes the same spot: the generator is in the back left corner and the stash is a vent in the wall, worth about $1,200. KultZ pulled about $1,300 from the vent. vassillios16 found the stash without talking to the parrot, since it sits in a corner of the back area. If you can’t spot it, ask the bird. You can even thank it on the way out.

Check the cages. If the cops aren’t right on top of you, GamesRadar+ reminds you to study the birds in the cages to add them to your animal collection. The door nearby leads to the safe and the money counter, with one more guard to deal with.

Can you do it again?

KhrazeGaming points out this exotic animal front is easy to miss if you never check the back. He also says enemy hideouts like this one refresh every few in-game days and restock.

Getting away

Expect the cops. Both creators escaped by car without much trouble. Take the cash home, because dying wipes your wallet. For more early robberies, see how to make money in GTA 6 and the early money warehouse.

Quick answers

Where is the parrot money in GTA 6?

At the Three Sheets liquor store, the same one Shirley sends you to for rum. The parrot in the back keeps saying "generator": the cash is behind the grate right behind the generator.

What is the Strange Noises robbery in GTA 6?

It's the robbery that shows up on your map after you hear animal noises in the liquor store's back room and the clerk plays it off. The back hides an exotic animal smuggling ring and cash.

How much money is in the parrot stash?

Over $2,000 in total: about $1,300 in the stash ($1,263 in GamesRadar+'s guide), about $900 in the safe and a bit more in the cash counter and the register.

How do you talk to the parrot in GTA 6?

Once the guards are down, walk up to the cage, press LT (L2 on PS5) and pick "Cash?" and then "Where's the cash?". It tells you the money is by the generator.
