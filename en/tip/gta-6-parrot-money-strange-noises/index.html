GTA 6 Parrot Money: How to Rob the "Strange Noises" Liquor Store

The Three Sheets liquor store hides over $2,000 behind its "Strange Noises" robbery: a safe, the register and a stash only the parrot gives away. Talk to it and open the grate behind the generator.

Confirmed · 5 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 4:22 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The parrot money is at the Three Sheets liquor store, the same one where Jason’s neighbor Shirley sends you to pick up rum. The back room runs an exotic animal smuggling racket with over $2,000 lying around, and the best part sits in a stash only the parrot gives away. Five channels showed the same robbery: TheProfessional, vassillios16, Arekkz Gaming, KultZ and KhrazeGaming. KultZ places the store in Key Lento.

How the robbery shows up

  1. Go to the liquor store. If you took Shirley’s side job, this is where you pick up her bottle.
  2. Inside, you’ll hear animal noises from the back. The clerk claims it’s the animal channel on TV.
  3. After that the spot appears on your map as a robbery called “Strange Noises”: the clerk seems distracted by something in the back.

You don’t need Shirley’s job for this. vassillios16 went straight there after the prologue. If you can’t see the store, look up liquor stores in the map index.

Getting in

  • Park in the alley next to the store.
  • Climb the dumpster (or hop the chain-link gate) into the back area.
  • About four guys are waiting. They grab machetes, and one may pull a gun.
  • TheProfessional recommends knocking them all out with your fists. Gunfire brings the cops; without them you can loot at your own pace.

What to grab

Where How much
Stash behind the generator About $1,300 to $1,400
Back room safe About $900
Cash counting machine About $200
Register up front About $200
The thugs’ pockets Small change

The safe has no code: rotate the right stick until the lock gives, like the other early safes.

The parrot and the stash

There’s a parrot in the back that keeps squawking about “cash” and a “stash”.

  1. Talk to it.
  2. Ask where the money is. It repeats “generator, generator”.
  3. Walk to the generator but don’t touch it. Open the grate right behind it.
  4. The cash is inside.

Arekkz Gaming describes the same spot: the generator is in the back left corner and the stash is a vent in the wall, worth about $1,200. KultZ pulled about $1,300 from the vent. vassillios16 found the stash without talking to the parrot, since it sits in a corner of the back area. If you can’t spot it, ask the bird. You can even thank it on the way out.

Can you do it again?

KhrazeGaming points out this exotic animal front is easy to miss if you never check the back. He also says enemy hideouts like this one refresh every few in-game days and restock.

Getting away

Expect the cops. Both creators escaped by car without much trouble. Take the cash home, because dying wipes your wallet. For more early robberies, see how to make money in GTA 6 and the early money warehouse.

Quick answers

Where is the parrot money in GTA 6?

At the Three Sheets liquor store, the same one Shirley sends you to for rum. The parrot in the back keeps saying "generator": the cash is behind the grate right behind the generator.

What is the Strange Noises robbery in GTA 6?

It's the robbery that shows up on your map after you hear animal noises in the liquor store's back room and the clerk plays it off. The back hides an exotic animal smuggling ring and cash.

How much money is in the parrot stash?

Over $2,000 in total: about $1,300 in the stash, about $900 in the safe and a bit more in the cash counter and the register.
