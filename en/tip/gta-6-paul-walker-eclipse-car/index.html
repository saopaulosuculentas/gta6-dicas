Is Paul Walker's Car in GTA 6? Where to Find the Fast & Furious Eclipse

Yes: GTA 6 has a nod to Paul Walker's green Eclipse from The Fast and the Furious, in a garage on Vespucci Island in the Rialto Islands. Wait until you unlock Moses so you can register and keep it.

Confirmed · 9 sourcesUpdated Nov 24, 7:40 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Paul Walker’s car is in GTA 6. It’s a nod to the green Eclipse from The Fast and the Furious, decals included, and it sits in a double garage on Vespucci Island, one of the Rialto Islands near Vice Beach. DPJ and Tristan both showed it, and GamesRadar+ has a guide too. GamesRadar+ calls it a Mitsubishi Eclipse, modeled on the bright green car Paul Walker drives as Brian O’Conner in the first movie.

Its in-game name

The game doesn’t call it an Eclipse. When Imack Studios pulled up on Vespucci Island in it and opened the map, the info card read Maibatsu ’97 Penumbra FF, listed as a Specialist: Tuner. That’s the name the map uses, so look for it there when you want to track the car down later.

The original plate reads HAD YOU. Milo and Millzaa both point out that it’s a nod to Brian’s “I almost had you” line from the movie, and both show the plate on screen along with the green underglow.

Where it is

  • Island: Vespucci Island, part of the Rialto Islands between Vice City and Vice Beach. You cross Catalan Island by bridge to get there.
  • The spot: a house in the middle of the island, with the car in its double garage.
  • Distance: Tristan puts it about 5 miles from Jason’s house.

DPJ shows the exact map marker in his video at 1:52. GamesRadar+ backs up the spot: the garage is on Vespucci Island, one of the Rialto Islands west of Vice Beach, which in turn sits east of Vice City.

Wait for Moses first

You can grab it right at the start, but DPJ advises against it:

  • He believes cars like this don’t respawn. If you don’t register it, it may be gone when you reload your save.
  • Registering unlocks with Moses in Port Gellhorn, in Chapter 2.
  • Registering this car cost him about $6,750, so save up first.

Registering it for free. GamesRadar+ has a better plan: steal the car right before your first visit to Moses at the start of Chapter 2 and make it the first car you bring him. According to GamesRadar+, he registers that first one for free, which saves you a few thousand dollars. DPJ paid the $6,750, and his video doesn’t say whether it was his first car for Moses. GamesRadar+ also notes that later on you can register cars at any Pay ‘n’ Spray, for a fee.

For how registering works, see how to steal cars in GTA 6.

How to get it

  1. Head to the garage on the Rialto Islands.
  2. Break into the garage. The alarm goes off, but nobody showed up in the video: DPJ just waited for it to stop.
  3. Before you leave, open the trunk and clear it out. Watch out for the snake, a Toy Story joke (more below).
  4. Open the shutters and drive off. You don’t need the slim jim.
  5. Take it straight to Moses and register it. Registering swaps the plates (the original reads HAD YOU).

The snake in the trunk

Tristan mentioned the snake first, and now two more channels have it on video. KingAlexHD opened the trunk and a big snake came straight out. Millzaa broke into the trunk on two separate runs and got a snake both times. His advice: when it lunges, mash Square (X on Xbox) as fast as you can, because if you’re too slow, it kills you.

GamesRadar+ adds a few tips on the alarms:

  • Garage alarm: switch it off on the wall to your left as you walk in, and open the garage door from there. Move fast.
  • Snap gun: once you have it in Chapter 3, you can get into the garage without triggering the alarm. If you’re quick with the switch, you don’t need it.
  • Slim jim: Moses hands it over at the start of Chapter 2. It makes breaking into the car easier and stops the car alarm, which can keep the police away. It’s optional too. Once inside, you hotwire the car and drive off.

Both tools are covered in slim jim and snap gun.

Dom’s Charger (unverified)

The Eclipse isn’t the only Fast & Furious car in the game. Maka91Productions found Dom’s Charger, a Bravado in game:

  • It’s in the garage of a run-down house in Grayson, in the middle of town in Lummox County, near the top left of the map.
  • It’s locked but has no alarm and no tracker, so you can grab it as early as Chapter 2, register it and keep it.
  • It comes rusty, just like in the movie. Paint it black at a Pay ‘n’ Spray to match Dom’s.
  • The front plate reads DAY TWO, a nod to cars that leave the factory one day and are already modified the next.

Only one channel has shown it so far, so this part is unverified.

Is it worth it?

DPJ says it’s super fast and one of the cars most worth keeping. GamesRadar+ agrees: it handles well and reaches high speeds, which makes it one of the best cars you can get, especially early on, since it costs nothing and doesn’t draw much heat. Quick answers

Where is Brian O'Conner's car in GTA 6?

Brian's green Eclipse from The Fast and the Furious sits in a house garage on Vespucci Island, near Vice Beach. Disable the alarm panel on the wall before opening the garage, then hotwire it: no key cloner needed.

Is Paul Walker's car in GTA 6?

Yes. There's a nod to the green Eclipse from The Fast and the Furious, decals and all, stored in a double garage on the Rialto Islands in Vice Beach.

When should you get Paul Walker's car in GTA 6?

After you unlock Moses in Chapter 2. You need him to register the car, and DPJ believes unregistered cars disappear when you reload your game.

How do you turn off the garage alarm for Paul Walker's car?

According to GamesRadar+, you switch it off on the wall to your left as you walk into the garage. Be quick about it. With the snap gun from Chapter 3, you can open the garage without setting the alarm off at all.

How much does it cost to register Paul Walker's car?

DPJ paid about $6,750 to register it with Moses in Port Gellhorn. According to GamesRadar+, it's free if this is the first car you bring to Moses at the start of Chapter 2.
