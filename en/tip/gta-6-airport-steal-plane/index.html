GTA 6 Airport Location: Where It Is and How to Steal a Jet

The GTA 6 airport is Vice City International, on the west side of Vice-Dale County. Commercial jets sit on its two runways, and you can steal one. There are also ATMs to break into in the terminal.

Unverified · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:48 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The GTA 6 airport is Vice City International, and it’s huge. Commercial jets are parked on both runways at all times, and you can sneak onto one and fly off. PC Gamer mapped what’s there.

Where it is

  • On the west side of Vice-Dale County, southwest of the city.
  • You can’t miss it once you’re in the city: two giant runways in a wedge shape.

How to steal a jet

  1. Get onto the airfield and head for one of the commercial jets parked on the runways.
  2. Climb aboard and take off.
  3. PC Gamer’s tip: throw it in reverse to get clear before the tarmac support vehicles end up under your wheels.

Expect trouble: an airport is a restricted area, and breaking in is a crime. Once you’re airborne, fly low to be seen less, and have a way out. See how to lose the police.

What happens once you’re in the air

Milo tested it. He walked up to a business jet parked next to a hangar, climbed the stairs and took off with a 4-star wanted level. Soon after, blue lights showed up in the sky. Those were fighter jets, and they opened fire with rockets. He couldn’t shake them, and the jet went down. His video doesn’t show which airfield he took off from, but the lesson is the same anywhere: if you see blue lights while flying a stolen plane, get ready to bail out with a parachute.

The central terminal also has a lot of ATMs you can break into, according to PC Gamer. Beyond that there aren’t many marked activities: it’s a big campus to explore.

Easier options

  • No wanted level: seaplanes and a helicopter sit unguarded in Southwind, close to Jason’s house. See where to find your first plane and helicopter.
  • Fighter jet: the military base has the Hauners P-996 Lazer.
  • Before you fly, grab a parachute.

PC Gamer is still the only English source that pins down the airport itself, so the badge says “Unverified”.

Quick answers

Where is the airport in GTA 6?

Vice City International Airport is on the west side of Vice-Dale County, southwest of the city. You can spot it by its two huge runways in a wedge shape.

Can you steal a plane at the GTA 6 airport?

Yes. Commercial jets are always parked on the runways and you can sneak aboard and fly off, according to PC Gamer.

Is there an easier plane to steal in GTA 6?

Yes. Seaplanes and a helicopter sit unguarded in Southwind, close to Jason's house, so you can take them without a wanted level.
