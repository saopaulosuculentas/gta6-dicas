GTA 6 Mission List: Every Chapter and How Many Missions There Are

GTA 6's story has 91 missions across 5 chapters, according to PC Gamer. Here is every mission in order, with a short spoiler-free note on each one.

Unverified · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:55 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s main story is made up of 91 missions spread over 5 chapters, based on the list PC Gamer put together. Chapter 3 is the big one with 26 missions, while Chapter 5 has only 7 and closes out the game.

PC Gamer kept 3 mission names off the list to avoid spoilers: one in Chapter 1, the last one in Chapter 4, and the finale. The notes below are written to give nothing important away either.

Missions per chapter

Chapter Name Missions
1 Just Like Paradise 22
2 Paradise Lost 11
3 T**s Ass Drug Money 26
4 Vice City, Baby 25
5 Top of the World, Ma 7
Total 91

Only story missions count here. Stranger missions, side activities and random events on the map aren’t part of this list.

Missions with a choice

A few missions stop partway through and ask you to decide something. So far the guides have covered three, and in each one the mission ends up in the same place whatever you pick.

Chapter Mission The choice
1 Hey Arnold! (No. 8) Kill or spare Arnold at the end of the chase. GamesRadar+ says it feeds into your criminal profile and the next cutscene is identical. See kill or spare Arnold.
2 The Delights robbery Frankie asks whether Jason can dance, and you get about two seconds to answer. PC Gamer says the robbery happens either way. See the Delights mission.
3 Dubious Provenance (No. 4) Hand the statue to Benedict Halifax or keep it. PC Gamer says refusing only delays things, since you end up driving back to Capri to give it to him anyway. See the Benedict Halifax statue.

On the Delights job: PC Gamer places it in Chapter 2, in Port Gellhorn, but doesn’t name the mission. Going by the list, it’s most likely No. 10, Afternoon Delight, the only Chapter 2 mission where Jason and Lucia visit a nightclub.

Finding the next mission

If the next mission doesn’t pop up right away, open the in-game index and check the missions tab. We walk through it in how to find missions in the index.

Several of these missions also pop a trophy when you finish them, including Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run and The Cartel Heist. The full rundown is in our GTA 6 trophy and achievement list.

Quick answers

How many missions are in GTA 6?

91 story missions, according to PC Gamer's list, split across 5 chapters.

How many chapters does GTA 6 have?

Five: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby and Top of the World, Ma.

Which GTA 6 chapter is the longest?

Chapter 3, with 26 missions. Chapter 5 is the shortest at 7, and it ends with the final mission.

What is the first mission in GTA 6?

Prologue, which starts with Lucia in prison. Jason shows up in the second one, Gone Fishing.
