GTA 6 Mission List: Every Chapter and How Many Missions There Are

GTA 6's story has 91 missions across 5 chapters, according to PC Gamer. Here is every mission in order, with a short spoiler-free note on each one.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 4:11 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s main story is made up of 91 missions spread over 5 chapters, based on the list PC Gamer put together. Chapter 3 is the big one with 26 missions, while Chapter 5 has only 7 and closes out the game.

PC Gamer kept 3 mission names off the list to avoid spoilers: one in Chapter 1, the last one in Chapter 4, and the finale. The notes below are written to give nothing important away either.

Missions per chapter

Chapter Name Missions
1 Just Like Paradise 22
2 Paradise Lost 11
3 T**s Ass Drug Money 26
4 Vice City, Baby 25
5 Top of the World, Ma 7
Total 91

Only story missions count here. Stranger missions, side activities and random events on the map aren’t part of this list.

Quick answers

How many missions are in GTA 6?

91 story missions, according to PC Gamer's list, split across 5 chapters.

How many chapters does GTA 6 have?

Five: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby and Top of the World, Ma.

Which GTA 6 chapter is the longest?

Chapter 3, with 26 missions. Chapter 5 is the shortest at 7, and it ends with the final mission.

What is the first mission in GTA 6?

Prologue, which starts with Lucia in prison. Jason shows up in the second one, Gone Fishing.

Can You Replay Missions in GTA 6?

No. GTA 6 won't let you replay a mission once it's done. The only way back is to load a manual save from before it, though you can restart a mission while you're still in it.
