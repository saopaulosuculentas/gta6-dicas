Do You Lose Money When You Die in GTA 6? How to Keep Your Cash Safe

Yes: in GTA 6, if you die or get busted with cash in your pocket, you lose it. Money stashed at your safehouse or in your account stays safe. Carry only what you'll spend.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 10:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Yes, in GTA 6 you lose the cash you’re carrying if you die or get busted. WiLLiSGaming, JoblessGarrett and DPJ all warn about it: if you get eliminated, wasted or busted with money in your pocket, it’s gone. The money you’ve stashed at your safehouse or put in your account isn’t affected.

Where to keep your money

  • The stash at your safehouse. At the start, Jason keeps everything under the floorboards of his house. Anything you put there is safe.
  • BuckMe. Early in the story Lucia wants to open a bank account, but Jason has bad credit. The bank gives him the BuckMe app to build it up, and you have to put $5,000 in it for the story to move on. That’s the “family savings”.

According to WiLLiSGaming, the game itself tells you to keep any extra cash you’re not going to spend in your stash, your bank account, an ATM or BuckMe.

How much to carry

Don’t walk around with zero: you’ll need cash for shops, repairs, fuel and taxis. DPJ’s advice is to stash often but always keep a little on you. Take only what you’ll spend on that trip.

When the risk is highest

  • Before a dangerous mission or a robbery: stash your money first. WiLLiSGaming did Donnie’s package runs and then hit a gang hideout with all his cash on him and had to fight about 30 enemies.
  • In the water: sharks attack. JoblessGarrett nearly died to one after making $3,200 diving.
  • With the cops on you: getting busted costs you the cash too.

One more thing: chapter 2

According to WiLLiSGaming, your cash resets to zero when chapter 2 starts, no matter where it is. Only the BuckMe family savings carry over. See how to make money fast in GTA 6.

Quick answers

Do you lose money when you die in GTA 6?

Yes. The cash you're carrying is lost if you die or get busted. Money stashed at home or in your account stays safe.

How do you store money in GTA 6?

Put it in the stash under the floorboards at your safehouse. Later in the story you also get the BuckMe account.

Is there a bank in GTA 6?

Early on, Lucia wants to open a bank account, but Jason has bad credit, so the bank gives him the BuckMe app instead. You need $5,000 in it to move the story forward.
