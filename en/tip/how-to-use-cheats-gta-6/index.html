How to Use Cheats in GTA 6 on PS5 and Xbox (and Why They Don't Work)

GTA 6 cheats only work after you beat the story. Each one unlocks with a challenge, and you enter it on the in-game phone while holding L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 10:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has cheats, but they work very differently from older GTA games. No cheat works until you beat the main story. After that, each cheat still has to be unlocked with its own challenge. Only then can you enter the sequence on your character’s phone.

Every cheat, its sequence and its unlock challenge are in the full list of all GTA 6 cheats.

Step 1: beat the story

According to PC Gamer, that’s 91 main missions. They loaded a mid-story save to test it and the cheats didn’t work. Knowing the sequence early does nothing.

Step 2: unlock the cheat

Once the story is done, a new option shows up in the pause menu: go to the Progress tab (press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox to get there) and open Cheats. It lists the cheats you’ve unlocked and the challenge for the rest. What you did during the story counts.

Some examples:

  • Money ($250,000), unlimited focus and attack force: unlocked when you beat the story.
  • Body armor: complete 100 workouts.
  • Max ammo, infinite ammo and no reload: 25 bronze, silver and gold medals at shooting ranges.
  • Cycle weather: reveal the whole map.
  • Raise heat level: lose a 3-star wanted level 25 times.

Step 3: enter it

  1. Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone.
  2. Hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox).
  3. Without letting go, enter the sequence on the D-pad.

Sequences only use Up, Down, Left and Right, so they’re the same on PS5 and Xbox.

What happens when you use one

  • Trophies and achievements are disabled until you quit the game.
  • Saving is disabled too. To save again you have to quit without saving. That’s why PC Gamer warns you can’t use cheats to breeze through side missions or challenges.

Save your game before trying anything.

How many cheats are there?

61, according to GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer:

  • 10 player cheats (money, focus, armor, wanted level and more)
  • 11 combat cheats (ammo, explosives, attack force)
  • 3 environment cheats (weather and time of day)
  • 21 vehicle spawns (cars, bikes, boats, planes and a tank)
  • 16 screen filters (visual only)

Many sequences haven’t been published yet, because outlets are unlocking cheats as they go. The full list is updated every round.

Watch out for fake lists

A viral “GTA 6 cheat list” with buttons like square, circle, L2 and R2 is made of GTA 5 and San Andreas codes. Real GTA 6 cheats only use the D-pad and the in-game phone.

Quick answers

How do you enter cheats in GTA 6?

Press Up on the D-pad to open your phone, hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox) and enter the sequence on the D-pad.

Why are my GTA 6 cheats not working?

Almost always because you haven't finished the story. No cheat works before that. After that, each cheat also has to be unlocked with its own challenge.

Do cheats disable trophies in GTA 6?

Yes. They also disable saving until you quit the game, according to GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer. Save before you use one.

Are GTA 6 cheats the same on PS5 and Xbox?

Yes. The sequences only use the D-pad, so they're identical. Only the shoulder buttons you hold change: L1 and R1 on PS5, LB and RB on Xbox.
