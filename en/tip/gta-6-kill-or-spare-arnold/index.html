GTA 6 Kill or Spare Arnold: What Happens

In the Chapter 1 mission Hey Arnold!, sparing Arnold keeps your criminal profile cleaner, while killing him pushes it toward violent. The next cutscene barely changes. Here's how each choice works.

Confirmed · 5 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 4:49 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Spare Arnold. In the Chapter 1 mission Hey Arnold!, letting him go is easier on your criminal profile, and killing him doesn’t earn you anything. The scene that follows is almost identical either way. What really changes is your profile, plus a possible Arnold cameo later on.

How the mission gets you there

Hey Arnold! comes after Pipework in the mission list. Arnold is Brian’s business partner and he’s gone quiet. According to GamesRadar+, he’s unhappy with how Brian runs the drug side of things.

  1. Jason, Brian and Cal track Arnold down at a radar station near the Aerostat.
  2. After a heated exchange, Arnold and his crew bolt in a pickup truck. A car chase with gunfire follows.
  3. The chase ends at the Key Lento marina. Arnold ditches the truck and runs. Brian tells Jason to shut him up, however he sees fit.
  4. Chase him on foot. Either you tackle him on the way, or he runs out of room at the end of the pier. Then he starts begging.

Every choice and what it does

Choice How Result
Spare Hold L2 (LT) to interact, then press X (A) on Spare Jason threatens Arnold and lets him go. Counts toward a more restrained profile
Kill on the pier Shoot him while he’s begging A warning says indiscriminate violence leads to a violent profile
Kill during the chase Shoot Arnold or his truck before the pier Faster, but counts toward a ruthless profile
Do nothing Wait on the pier Arnold leaves. PC Gamer says this counts the same as sparing him

The guides don’t agree on the labels. GamesRadar+ calls it a Restrained contribution for sparing and Ruthless for the chase kill. PC Gamer says killing him gives you a Hardened rating. None of those match the five tiers in the criminal profile exactly, so the simple read is this: sparing leans clean, killing leans violent.

Does the next scene change?

Barely. GamesRadar+ says the cutscene after your choice is the same. Gamerpillar and Red Dead Family recorded both versions, and only one line is different. At Brian’s party he asks if the job is handled:

  • If you killed him: Jason says it’s taken care of.
  • If you spared him: Jason says Arnold will keep his mouth shut, and Brian likes hearing that.

You also meet Kenton at that party, which opens up the diving jobs. See sunken treasure and shipwrecks.

The long-term consequence

PC Gamer writes that if Arnold lives, he turns up in a later mission. It doesn’t say which mission or what changes there. No guide or video has shown that return yet, so the long-term payoff hasn’t been shown so far. PC Gamer adds that there’s no downside to sparing him and no bonus for killing him.

Still working through the chapter? The Chapter 1 walkthrough covers the other missions.

Quick answers

Should I kill or spare Arnold in GTA 6?

Spare him. It hurts your criminal profile less, and PC Gamer says killing him gives you nothing extra.

How do you spare Arnold in GTA 6?

Catch him on the pier at the Key Lento marina, hold L2 (LT on Xbox) to interact and press X (A on Xbox) on Spare.

What happens if you spare Arnold?

Jason threatens him and lets him swim off. Your profile leans cleaner, and the story carries on the same way.

Does Arnold come back later?

PC Gamer says he shows up in a later mission if he lives, but doesn't say which one. Nobody has shown it yet.
