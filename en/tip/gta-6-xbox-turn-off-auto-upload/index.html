GTA 6 on Xbox: Turn Off Automatic Clip Uploads to Avoid a Suspension

One player says they got a two-day Xbox suspension over a GTA 6 clip that uploaded on its own. Here's how to turn off automatic uploads and what Xbox has said about clip suspensions.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 25, 9:05 AM BRTXbox Series

If you play GTA 6 on Xbox, turn off automatic clip uploads. The console ships with it on: every clip you capture goes straight to the Xbox network. One player said on Reddit that they got a two-day suspension over a 28-second GTA 6 clip, according to GamesRadar+.

It’s a single report, so this page is unverified. But Xbox has suspended accounts over mature clips from other games before, and its own support team’s advice was to turn off auto-upload.

How to turn it off

  1. Press the Xbox button on your controller.
  2. Go to Profile & system > Settings > Preferences > Capture & share.
  3. Under Automatic uploads, pick the Xbox network and set it to Don’t upload.

GamesRadar+ suggests switching off OneDrive uploads too if you don’t want anything going up on its own. Your clips stay saved on the console: you just choose what to share.

What happened

  • According to GamesRadar+, the player got a two-day suspension with no appeal after a 28-second clip uploaded to the Xbox network on its own.
  • They say the clip only showed the characters in beachwear, and that it was flagged by mistake.
  • Xbox’s email didn’t say which rule the clip broke.

What Xbox has said

In January 2024, after a wave of suspensions over Baldur’s Gate 3 clips, Xbox Support told GamesRadar+ that:

  • Suspensions aren’t automatic: a moderator reviews each clip.
  • For a first offense, Xbox may lift the suspension and explain how to avoid it.
  • For sensitive content, the fix is to turn off automatic uploads.

Xbox’s community rules say public content should suit all audiences. GTA 6 is rated for adults, so any clip can end up under review.

Quick answers

Can you get suspended on Xbox for a GTA 6 clip?

One player says they got a two-day suspension over a 28-second GTA 6 clip that uploaded to the Xbox network automatically. It's a single report, but Xbox has suspended accounts over mature clips from other games before.

How do you turn off automatic clip uploads on Xbox?

Press the Xbox button, go to Profile & system > Settings > Preferences > Capture & share, and under Automatic uploads set it to Don't upload.

Are Xbox clip suspensions automatic?

According to Xbox Support, no: each reported clip is reviewed by a moderator. For a first offense, Xbox said it may lift the suspension.
