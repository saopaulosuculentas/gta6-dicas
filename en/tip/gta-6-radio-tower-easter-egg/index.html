GTA 6 Radio Tower Easter Egg: Where the Egg Is and What Happens When You Throw It

At the top of the tallest radio tower in GTA 6 there's a nest with a literal Easter egg. The climb takes about six minutes, and throwing the egg off the top triggers a secret cutscene.

Confirmed · 6 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 11:01 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has a literal Easter egg. At the top of the tallest radio tower in the game there’s a bird’s nest with a colorful egg in it. You can stash the egg in your inventory or throw it off the tower, and throwing it triggers a secret cutscene.

T5G, ZacCoxTV, MrDalekJD, InkTV and Typical Gamer all showed the egg on video, and Kotaku reported a player’s find on launch day.

Where it is

  • The tower: the tallest radio tower on the map, in South Vice-Dale County, right next to Hamlet. InkTV marks it just north of Hamlet, off a side road, and Typical Gamer describes the spot as northeast of Mariana County, just south of Vice-Dale County. It shows up on the map as a 3D object, and you can spot it from far away.
  • Getting in: hop the fence at the base of the tower.
  • The climb: ladders, one after another. T5G took about six minutes; MrDalekJD says 10 to 15 minutes going slow. There are platforms about a third, half and two thirds of the way up where you can catch a break.
  • By helicopter: you can land on top, but MrDalekJD warns it’s risky. The ladders are the safe way.
  • Clouds at the top: the tower pokes through the cloud layer. InkTV reached the top unable to see the ground and waited a couple of minutes for it to clear.

There are several tall radio towers around the map. T5G found a character on another one trying to climb it, a nod to streamers who climb towers in games.

What to do with the egg

  1. Grab the egg from the nest. The nest is near the top but not at the highest point: there’s still more ladder above it.
  2. Choose: stash it in your inventory or throw it off the top.
  3. If you throw it, aim high, over the railing. MrDalekJD’s first, weaker throw didn’t trigger anything. A high throw starts a short cutscene. We won’t spoil it, but it’s worth the climb.

You only get one egg. After throwing his, Typical Gamer tried to pick up another and the game wouldn’t let him.

Base jump at the top

Above the nest, at the highest point of the tower, there’s a base jump challenge. Two cones mark the jump-off spot, and a full parachute sits right there for you to grab, so you don’t even need to bring one.

  • Typical Gamer equipped the parachute, jumped and found that the course wraps around the tower. He clipped the cables and that was the end of the run.
  • ZacCoxTV also climbed to the very top and jumped from there.

The other jump spots are in GTA 6 base jump locations.

Who you meet up there

InkTV grabbed the egg, threw it and got the cutscene. Then, on the last set of ladders, he ran into an influencer who was already at the top. She tells you she got there first and you’ll have to wait, and the two of you trade a few jabs. It isn’t a mission, just one more surprise for whoever makes the climb.

Why it’s an easter egg

ZacCoxTV points out that GTA Vice City had a hidden chocolate Easter egg. The radio tower egg in GTA 6, which is also set in Vice City, looks like a callback to that joke.

Be careful

A fall from the tower kills you, and falling halfway means climbing it all again. Walk, don’t sprint, when you move from one ladder to the next.

Before you start, equip your parachute in your items. It saves you if you slip, and it’s the fastest way down.

Quick answers

Where is the Easter egg in GTA 6?

In a bird's nest at the top of the tallest radio tower in the game, in South Vice-Dale County right next to Hamlet. T5G and MrDalekJD show the same tower.

What happens if you throw the egg off the tower in GTA 6?

A short, unexpected cutscene plays. Throw it high over the railing: MrDalekJD says a weak throw didn't trigger it.

How long does it take to climb the GTA 6 radio tower?

Six to fifteen minutes of ladders. T5G and ZacCoxTV took about six; MrDalekJD says 10 to 15 minutes if you take it slow.

Who is the woman at the top of the GTA 6 radio tower?

An influencer who got there before you. In InkTV's video she says she got there first and you'll have to wait, and they trade a few jabs. There's no mission attached.
