GTA 6 Project Rumba: The Bunker Secret That Permanently Boosts Focus, Step by Step

After President Russo's bunkers, a chair sends you into a looping secret facility. Here are the 09-06-07 and 0612 codes, the order of the choices, the Duke 1922 pistol and the focus boost at the end.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 26, 4:09 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Project Rumba is the follow-up to the President Russo bunker secret. A conditioning chair sends you into a secret facility that loops, and a clock lets you travel between the past (clean and well lit) and the present (in ruins). At the end you get a permanent focus boost and the Brainwash tattoo.

GameSpot and WhizL both show the full route. This page spoils the mystery.

Before you start

Finish both of President Russo’s bunkers: the island one with the Russo’s 1922 pistol and the woods one with the tank. Full steps in free tank in the secret bunker.

Getting in

  1. Go back to the second bunker and press the button on the control room console.
  2. Return to the hallway near the hatch: the conditioning room is now open.
  3. Sit in the chair. The game switches to first person.

Loops 1 and 2: the clock

  1. Follow the exit signs to the reception, which has a clock on the wall. President Russo blocks the red doors.
  2. Go through the other door next to him. You’re back in the first hallway: it’s a loop.
  3. On the second loop, go into the room where you started and answer the red phone.
  4. At reception, look at the clock and hold L2 (LT on Xbox). The screen warps and you’re in the same facility in ruins: the present.

The reception clock is your time machine. Use it whenever you need to switch eras.

Loop 3: the beds and the codes

  1. In the present, check the dormitory: three beds are left (left, middle and right).
  2. In a room with a broken window, answer the phone: it tells you about the three candidates who survived.
  3. Go back to the past via the clock and read the numbers above those three beds: 06, 07 and 09.
  4. At reception, don’t go through the door. Look right for a keypad. Enter 09-06-07 to open the control room.
  5. Answer the phone next to the computer, then log in with President, Russo and 0612.
  6. Pick in this order: Open Your Eyes (turns on the cameras), then Find the Source (opens the lab). Don’t pick Break the Cycle yet.

Loop 4: the hidden pistol

  1. Walk past Russo to start another loop and go into the laboratory. Answer the phone.
  2. Travel to the present via the clock and go back to the lab.
  3. On the right wall is a photo of your character. Look at it with L2 and zoom with R2 until the screen turns red.
  4. Pan down: there’s a case with the Duke 1922 pistol. Take it.

Loop 5: the end

  1. Go back to the control room in the past. If the door won’t open, do one more loop.
  2. On the computer, pick Break the Cycle.
  3. At reception, Russo no longer blocks the exit. Go through the doors and he tries to run.
  4. Use focus mode and hit a critical spot.

You leave the simulation with a permanent focus boost and the Brainwash tattoo unlocked for Jason and Lucia.

Quick answers

How do you start Project Rumba in GTA 6?

Finish both of President Russo's bunkers. Then go back to the second bunker, press the console button and return to the hallway near the hatch: the conditioning room will be open. Sit in the chair.

What is the Project Rumba keypad code?

09-06-07, on the keypad to the right of reception. The numbers come from the beds left standing in the dormitory.

What is the Project Rumba computer password?

President, Russo and 0612. Then pick Open Your Eyes, Find the Source and, only at the end, Break the Cycle.

What do you get for Project Rumba?

A permanent focus boost and the Brainwash tattoo for both Jason and Lucia. Along the way you pick up the Duke 1922 pistol.
