How to Get Every GTA 6 Ultimate Edition and Pre-Order Bonus

GTA 6's Ultimate Edition and pre-order bonuses unlock bit by bit from Chapter 1 to Chapter 5. Here's where to find each car, weapon, shop and outfit, and which ones are Chapter 1 only.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 4:08 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s Ultimate Edition and pre-order bonuses don’t all show up on day one. Each one unlocks in a specific chapter, from 1 to 5, and sits at a specific spot on the map. Below is when and where to grab every item. The pre-order pack applies to both the Standard and Ultimate Editions; everything else is Ultimate only.

Where to check your bonuses

Finish mission 11 in Chapter 1, Coming Home. That unlocks the store in the main menu, where every bonus has an entry telling you which chapter it arrives in and how to collect it.

For the pre-order pack, open the main menu, go to the store and scroll down past GTA+ to the Vintage Vice City Pack.

Almost everything is tracked through the map index. If you haven’t used it yet, see how to use the map index.

Grab these three in Chapter 1

Both sources mark the Ganado Retro Build, the Dinka Enduro and the Crest Kayak as Chapter 1 only. Neither says what happens if you move on without them, so don’t wait.

Chapter 3: the pre-order bonus

The Vintage Vice City Pack goes to everyone who pre-ordered, on Standard or Ultimate. You can only claim it in Chapter 3:

  1. ’55 Vapid Stanier and garage: a task shows up in the Exploration section of the index. The car is parked outside the Ocean View Hotel. Collecting it also unlocks the Bonita Court garage, north of Washington Beach.
  2. 80s outfits and hairstyles: once you have the car, the outfits are in your wardrobe and the hairstyles are in the Special section of any salon.
  3. Palm tree weapon pattern, a nod to Tommy Vercetti’s shirt: once you have the car, head to any Ammu-Nation and apply it under Cosmetics / Wraps in the weapon’s modify menu.

Quick answers

Where are the Ultimate Edition bonuses in GTA 6?

Each bonus unlocks in a set chapter at a set spot on the map. The main menu store, which opens after the mission Coming Home, tells you when and where to grab each one.

How do you claim the GTA 6 pre-order bonus?

In Chapter 3, a task appears in the Exploration section of the map index. Pick up the '55 Vapid Stanier at the Ocean View Hotel and the outfits, hairstyles and weapon pattern unlock with it.

When do you get the '95 Grotti Cheetah in GTA 6?

In Chapter 5. It's parked in the garage under the Pegassi Residences building, and an Exploration task in the map index points you there.

Which Ultimate Edition bonuses are Chapter 1 only?

The Ganado Retro Build, the Dinka Enduro motorcycle and the Crest Kayak. Both sources flag all three as Chapter 1 only.
