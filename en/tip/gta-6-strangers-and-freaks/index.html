GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks: The 8 Mission Strands That Count for the Trophy

The GTA 6 Strangers & Freaks trophy asks for 8 stranger strands. Here are the ones that count (Kirk, Kyle, Alvan, Yago, Pete, Dr. Merle, Freddie and Papa Audacity) and where each starts.

Confirmed · 15 sourcesUpdated Nov 25, 5:00 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Stranger missions in GTA 6 are requests from characters you run into around the map, and the Strangers & Freaks trophy asks you to finish 8 mission strands of them. There’s no complete list yet: neither Rockstar nor the big guide sites have said which eight count. Below is what players have turned up on day one.

The trophy

Trophy Requirement Value
Strangers & Freaks Complete 8 mission strands for strangers across the state of Leonida Silver, 30G

The word to notice is strands. A single stranger can have more than one mission in a row, like Shirley does.

The 8 that count

PowerPyx and Jojo Gaming Guides landed on the same list of 8. Not every favor counts: according to PowerPyx, the questlines for Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy, Billy and Eddie don’t count as strangers. All of them are still available after the story.

Stranger Missions When it opens, per Jojo
Kirk (Skunk Ape) Search for the Skunk Ape, the tree effigies and Mr. Mangrove Chapter 1
Kyle McKittrick Farm, collecting chemicals and checking on him Chapter 2
Alvan Gellhorn’s missing tourists and the trophy room Chapter 2
Yago A street race, mentoring him, racing him and backing him up Chapter 3
Pilot Pete Recovering Pete’s plane and the deliveries You help him in Chapter 1, but the strand only opens after Chapter 4
Dr. Merle (No Flushers) The Final Flush, at Frank’s Waffles Any chapter
Freddie Retesting his bunker defenses Any chapter
Papa Audacity The Unknown Stuntman stunts through to the launch party Chapter 3

Where each one starts:

  • Kirk: scan a branch effigy in the swamp and call the number it saves. See the Skunk Ape mission guide.
  • Yago: join a street race; afterward he asks you to mentor him. The race against him has a time limit.
  • Dr. Merle: scan the QR code in the public restroom on Corsair Key.
  • Freddie: break into his bunker. See how to unlock hurricanes.
  • Papa Audacity: do his stunts; each one unlocks the next. Jojo says few players know this strand counts.

You can see how many you’ve done in the trophy tracker in the trophy list.

Other missions found so far

These aren’t on PowerPyx’s list, but they’re worth doing for the rewards.

Shirley, the neighbor

She lives on the corner by Jason’s house and yells at you as you drive past early in the story.

  1. First favor: she asks for a bottle of Salty Squid rum from the Three Sheets liquor store, as OutlawGarry shows. RageGamingVideos says bringing it back gives you a permanent focus boost.
  2. Second favor: keep visiting and she’ll ask for help involving her friend Peggy and Peggy’s mobility scooter. The reward is being able to borrow Shirley’s car whenever you like, per Arekkz. It’s not a registered personal vehicle, but it drives well.

Kirk and the Skunk Ape

A hidden quest in the Grassrivers swamps. JFM shows it starting when you find an effigy made of branches in the woods, near where the creature is seen eating a deer on the road. The game doesn’t point you anywhere after that. Kirk, a Skunk Ape hunter, joins in, and there’s a choice at the end. The reward is a unique camo sniper rifle. Full steps in the Skunk Ape mission guide.

Kenton, the treasure hunter

Kenton shows up after the story mission Hey Arnold!, at his dive shop on the Key Lento beach, according to KultZ. He teaches you to dive for wrecks, and WiLLiSGaming says he calls again later with more dive spots. There’s even a trophy just for him, This Was No Boat Accident, for finishing Kenton’s final mission. According to PowerPyx, his strand isn’t one of the 8 for Strangers & Freaks. More in sunken treasure and shipwrecks.

Street encounters

These pop up in the open world. It’s not known whether they count toward the trophy.

  • D-Bag, the gym bro: on Shore Drive, across from the Hotel Dixon, he bets you $1,000 you can’t make him tap out of a chokehold. MrBossFTW files him under strangers and random encounters. See the D-Bag challenge.
  • Todd and the sniper: west of the highway into Hamlet, near Grassrivers, you’ll hear a gunshot. It’s two hunters, one of them Todd, with a sniper rifle on the ground. Drive them to the hospital and one of them hands you the rifle, per KhrazeGaming. More in unique weapons.
  • The missing spouse: Kotaku describes someone who needs help finding their spouse after a night of partying. It’s one of the random events you can stumble into while exploring.
  • Jimmy and the spear gun: BennyCentral describes a drunk named Jimmy, wearing a captain’s hat and carrying a Bullhead spear gun, who tries to rob you at a spot he marks on the map (the place name isn’t clear in the audio). Don’t fight him right away. Follow him to the pool, and while he’s distracted, grab the spear gun, which is great for diving. If you want the hat too, knock him out and pick it up off the ground. That’s two free items from one encounter.

Other strands on the trophy list

Besides Kenton, the trophy list has awards for finishing the final mission of four more characters: Andy (Road to Rehabilitation), Billy (The Lone Rider), Randy (Supply & Demand) and Tyrone (Spin On This!). According to PowerPyx, none of these count for Strangers & Freaks: each has its own trophy.

How to find more

  • Map index: GamesRadar+ says the Exploration tab lists optional quests. MrDalekJD shows that favors for your neighbors show up there too.
  • Listen: Kotaku’s advice is to follow shouting and loud noises. Press L3 + R3 (focus) to see where a sound is coming from, then go take a look.

See also the platinum trophy guide.

Quick answers

How many stranger missions are in GTA 6?

The Strangers & Freaks trophy asks for 8 strands. According to PowerPyx and Jojo Gaming Guides, the ones that count are Kirk, Kyle, Alvan, Yago, Pilot Pete, Dr. Merle, Freddie and Papa Audacity.

Where do you find stranger missions in GTA 6?

In the Exploration tab of the map index, which lists optional quests and favors for your neighbors. Shouting or commotion on the street is often someone who needs help, too.

What is the first stranger mission in GTA 6?

The easiest is neighbor Shirley, on the corner by Jason's house. She wants a bottle of rum from the Three Sheets liquor store.
