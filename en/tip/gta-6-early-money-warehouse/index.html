GTA 6 Early Money Warehouse: Where It Is and What to Loot

South of Vice-Dale County, close to Jason's house, an abandoned warehouse holds a cash bag, a rifle, an SMG, contraband and a safe with a collectible card. You can go right after the prologue.

Confirmed · 9 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 11:03 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Early in GTA 6 you can leave Jason’s house and come back with over $10,000, a rifle and an SMG. It’s all in an abandoned warehouse in the south of Vice-Dale County. Whoever was inside is already dead, so there’s no shootout: walk in and take it. GrandTheftIntel, DPJ, LoDj1c, DooDooPoo, YTLukeyy, Arekkz Gaming, FuriousFade, Rodwenn and BennyCentral all showed the same spot.

Where it is

  • Area: south of Vice-Dale County, near Hamlet, a short drive from Jason’s house.
  • The building: a red warehouse on the left side of the road, near a quarry and a power plant with smokestacks. The yard is full of trucks and vans.
  • When to go: any time. GrandTheftIntel went before finishing the first mission. If you haven’t discovered the area yet, you can still come back later.

The map is faded around there at the start. Zoom in and drop a marker, or head south through Vice-Dale until you see the warehouse.

Step by step

  1. Drive there, ideally in something with a big trunk. You’ll need it.
  2. Outside box: open the box by the entrance first for a bit of cash.
  3. Teddy bear van: the teddy bear truck parked out front has drugs in the back. Arekkz and BennyCentral both grab it before heading in.
  4. Break the door open and go in. The bodies are already on the floor.
  5. Stash bag: the big bag is worth about $6,000 (DPJ found one with $4,800). Arekkz, FuriousFade and Rodwenn also found a $6,000 bag on the table.
  6. Weapons: loot the bodies. There’s an SMG, pistols and, further in, a rifle (DPJ got a Duke 5.56). Arekkz points out the Duke 5.56 here isn’t a unique weapon, so you lose it if you die. Rodwenn also found a Desert Eagle pistol in the central room.
  7. Contraband: grab the drugs lying around. They turn into cash at a fence. Each brick is worth $400: the amount pops up on Arekkz’s screen, and Rodwenn and FuriousFade give the same number. There are more bricks in a second truck behind another door and in the side room.
  8. Cash register: in the office, empty the register.
  9. Safe: it’s in the same office. No code needed: turn the right stick until the circle fills quickly and the lock breaks. Inside is cash, more drugs and a collectible card.

The card in the safe

The card is Leroy Rook (Silver), from the Vice City Narcos set. FuriousFade read the name out as soon as he opened the safe, and Arekkz and BennyCentral pulled the same one. Arekkz’s screen shows a “Vice City Narcos 1/24” counter, so the set has 24 cards.

In Rodwenn’s video, the map card for the Hamlet pawn shop already lists $6,000 in collectibles to sell. That’s what the card fetches on the spot. If you’d rather keep it for the set, see collectible cards.

When your bag fills up

Your bag fills up fast. Open the back of your car and stow the full stash bags there. GrandTheftIntel thinks a car holds about six bags. Then keep looting.

Turning it into money

  • Contraband: take it to Brian’s lockup, the first fence, in Key Lento. The door code is 7700. DooDooPoo sold the full drug bag for about $10,000. See all fence locations.
  • Or at Donnie’s: BennyCentral compared both on the map and Donnie’s trailer paid $800 more than Brian for the same bag, which came to about $10,000. FuriousFade took the warehouse bags to Donnie and the prompt showed $11,600. He noticed you can’t split the sale: drugs and marked bills go together. The payment shows up later in your mailbox at home.
  • Marked bills: some of the cash comes marked and has to go through a fence too.
  • The collectible card: GrandTheftIntel sold the Vice City Narcos card for about $6,000 at the store in Hamlet.
  • Stash the rest at home: there’s no ATM early on. Keep your cash under Jason’s floorboard, because dying wipes your wallet.

All in, GrandTheftIntel got close to $20,000 before the first mission, and Rodwenn lands in the same place: just under $20,000 once the card is sold. For more ways to build cash, see how to make money in GTA 6. For more early stashes you can hit on the same trip, see GTA 6 secret early items.

Quick answers

Where is the early money warehouse in GTA 6?

In the south of Vice-Dale County, near Hamlet and a short drive from Jason's house. It's a red warehouse on the side of the road, next to a quarry, with trucks and vans in the yard.

How much money is in the warehouse?

Between the stash bag, the cash register and the safe, it adds up to roughly $9,000 to $11,000. The collectible card from the safe sells for about $6,000 more in Hamlet.

Do you have to finish a mission first?

No. GrandTheftIntel went before finishing the first mission, straight from Jason's house. You can also go later in the story.

How do you open the warehouse safe in GTA 6?

There's no code. Slowly turn the right stick until the circle fills quickly, and the lock breaks.
