Where to Find a Rocket Launcher in GTA 6

A rocket launcher with 4 rockets sits in a junkyard bunker west of Vice City, next to the Conspiracist Type 9. A second one is inside a Black Eagle warehouse.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 6:13 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

According to WiLLiSGaming, the easiest rocket launcher to grab in GTA 6 is in a bunker hidden in a junkyard west of Vice City. Inside you’ll find a launcher with 4 rockets, some grenades and the unique Conspiracist Type 9 machine pistol. He found a second launcher in a Black Eagle warehouse.

Where the junkyard is

WiLLiSGaming pulls up the spot on the in-game map, and that’s the best guide:

  • it’s west of Vice City, in a patch of land boxed in by highways, between Kelly County and the Vice-Dale County line;
  • the character is standing on the SR-120, with Omega Pkwy just to the south;
  • Buckskin Swamp is off to the west.

In the voiceover he says “Mariana County”, but the map on screen points to the Vice City area, so we went with the map. He suggests waiting until Chapter 2, since the story sends you that way anyway.

Step by step

  1. Hop the junkyard fence. The game pops a reminder here that cops will spot you faster if your weapon is out in the open.
  2. Look for an RV covered in antennas.
  3. Once you find it, head to the very back. There’s a bunker door: open it and go in.
  4. There’s a dead man inside and a phone with a View Media option. WiLLiSGaming says the clip on that phone shows what happened to him.
  5. The weapon cases hold the Conspiracist Type 9, Frag Grenade pickups and the rocket launcher.

How many rockets you get

  • 4 rockets. The weapon wheel in the video shows 1 loaded and 3 in reserve.
  • He says rockets can’t be bought, so hang on to these for late in the story or a real emergency.
  • The launcher isn’t a unique weapon. Die with it and it’s gone, so keep it holstered until you need it.

He mentions other launchers around the map but doesn’t say where, apart from the Black Eagle one.

The second one: Black Eagle warehouse

The Black Eagle warehouse, home of the Black Eagle Import M306 TMR rifle, has another launcher. That makes 2 launchers and 8 rockets by his count. WiLLiSGaming doesn’t show where the warehouse is on the map, and getting in is fiddly: only Lucia fits through the gap that leads to the rifle. The details are in unique weapons.

The gun that comes with it

The Conspiracist Type 9 shows its name in yellow, the mark of a unique weapon. WiLLiSGaming says those stay with you even after you die. To carry guns in your trunk, see how to store weapons in your car, and for everything else, the GTA 6 weapons list.

Quick answers

Where is the rocket launcher in GTA 6?

In a bunker at a junkyard west of Vice City, by the SR-120, and in a Black Eagle warehouse. WiLLiSGaming shows both.

How many rockets does the junkyard launcher have?

Four: one loaded and three in reserve, as the weapon wheel in the video shows.

Do you lose the rocket launcher when you die in GTA 6?

Yes. WiLLiSGaming says it isn't a unique weapon, so it's gone if you die.

Can you buy rockets in GTA 6?

Not according to WiLLiSGaming. That's why he says to save them for when you really need them.
