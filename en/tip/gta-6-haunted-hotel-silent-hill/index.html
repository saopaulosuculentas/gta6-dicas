GTA 6 Haunted Hotel: Marbelle Hotel Location and Safe Code (Silent Hill Easter Egg)

The Marbelle Hotel in Vice City is GTA 6's haunted hotel. A door opens on its own, the map turns foggy at night like Silent Hill, and a rooftop notebook has the safe code.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 20, 10:37 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6’s haunted hotel is the Marbelle Hotel, a run-down building in Vice City that’s closed and up for sale. Inside you’ll find mannequins, a door that swings open by itself and glowing writing on the walls. After that the whole game turns into a foggy night, straight out of Silent Hill, and a notebook on the roof gives you the code to the safe downstairs. Robbin Rams is the only creator to show it so far, so it’s marked as unverified for now.

Where it is

  • County: Vice-Dale County, in the city part of Vice City. Robbin Rams shows the map marker at 0:24 in his video.
  • The building: three floors, “Marbelle Hotel” across the top, “For Sale” and “Closed” signs out front and two lit windows on the top floor.
  • Neighborhood: the rooftop notebook is a realtor’s pitch that mentions Crosstown and views of the 404 interstate.

Getting in

  1. Walk around to the back of the building.
  2. One door there opens without breaking in. Just walk through.
  3. You’ll already see messages scrawled on the walls inside.

What’s inside

  • A safe with a keypad in a corner downstairs. You need the code from the roof.
  • A mannequin standing in a corner and another lying in a bed. They don’t move, but Robbin Rams warns about a jump scare.
  • A collectible card. See how the collectible cards work.

The Silent Hill effect

On one of the floors, a door opens by itself and the lights start flickering. Walk through and glowing white text fills the walls. Once it fades, the roof door is open and the game has changed: it’s night and thick fog covers the entire map, not only the hotel. It was daytime when Robbin Rams walked in.

Up on the roof you’ll hear strange noises and spot writing on the floor. There’s a billboard next to the hotel. Robbin Rams climbed it, fell and lost a big chunk of health, and found nothing up there, so skip it.

The safe code

  1. On the roof, find the notebook in a corner.
  2. It’s a sales listing that insists the hotel has no ghosts and tells you to stay off the roof.
  3. One number on the page is circled. That’s the safe code. In Robbin Rams’ game it was 955214, but he says yours could be different.
  4. Head back down to the safe and punch it in.

What’s in the safe

  • Cash: $236 plus another $141 in the video.
  • A luxury watch: the Crowex Ambassador ‘Breakfast’. The counter read “Luxury Watches 2/34”, so there are 34 watches to collect.

As he looted the safe, the “The Truth Is Out There” trophy popped. That’s the same trophy as the UFO abduction, and nobody has explained yet why it unlocked at the hotel.

It only happens once

Robbin Rams went back later. The text didn’t appear, the door didn’t open on its own and the fog stayed gone, though it was still night. There were now two identical notebooks on the roof.

Quick answers

Where is the haunted hotel in GTA 6?

It's the Marbelle Hotel, a closed three-story building up for sale in Vice City, in Vice-Dale County. Go in through the back door.

What is the Marbelle Hotel safe code?

It's the circled number in the notebook on the roof. In Robbin Rams' game it was 955214, but he warns yours may differ, so read your own notebook.

What's in the haunted hotel safe?

Some cash and a luxury watch, the Crowex Ambassador 'Breakfast', which counts toward the game's watch collection.

Can you trigger the Silent Hill fog again?

Not in Robbin Rams' video. On his second visit the wall text never showed up and the fog didn't come back.
