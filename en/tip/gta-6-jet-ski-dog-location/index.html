GTA 6 Jet Ski Dog Location: How to Find Dog Island and Get $1,200

In GTA 6, if you swim with no boat in the middle of Gambit Bay, a dog in a life jacket and goggles shows up on a jet ski. It takes you to Dog Island, where a dog digs up about $1,200.

Confirmed · 4 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 10:05 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

GTA 6 has a dog that drives a jet ski. It wears a life jacket and goggles, shows up when you’re stranded in the water and gives you a ride to an island full of dogs. If you make friends with them, one of the dogs digs up hidden cash: $1,218 in ZoneX’s video.

Three channels showed the encounter (ZoneX, ZacCoxTV and T5G), and Kotaku also reported the dog that rescues players with its jet ski.

How to make the dog show up

  1. Take a boat to Gambit Bay.
  2. In the middle of the bay, jump in the water and leave the boat behind. According to ZoneX, you need to be truly stranded, with no boat nearby.
  3. Keep swimming and wait. After a while the dog arrives on a jet ski and starts barking.
  4. The game lets you praise or scold it. Praise it.
  5. Climb on the back of the jet ski.

Don’t get off early

The dog rides around the bay before heading to the island. Stay on until it stops on its own. ZoneX warns that a lot of people jump off thinking it’s just a joyride and miss the best part.

On Dog Island

The ride ends at an island with a pack of dogs on the beach.

  1. They bring you toys. Pick them up and throw them so the dogs fetch.
  2. If they like you, one of them starts walking around the island. Follow it.
  3. It digs up the cash. ZoneX got +$1,218 on screen.

ZacCoxTV tells the same story with one difference: a dog led him to a hidden wallet filled with money. The reward may vary.

Once you’ve been there, the island is on your map and you can come back by boat. ZoneX shows the exact spot at the end of his video, from 1:10.

Still unknown

  • Whether you can collect the money more than once.
  • Whether the dog shows up for both Jason and Lucia.

For more early-game cash, see how to make money fast in GTA 6. More secrets are in the GTA 6 easter eggs list.

Quick answers

Where is the jet ski dog in GTA 6?

It shows up in Gambit Bay. Head to the middle of the bay, jump in the water, leave your boat behind and keep swimming until it arrives.

How much money do you get from Dog Island in GTA 6?

In ZoneX's video the dog dug up $1,218. ZacCoxTV was led to a hidden wallet with cash instead.

Should you praise or scold the jet ski dog?

Praise it. When it arrives the game gives you both options, and praising it is what gets you the ride.
