How to Get a Tank in GTA 6: Rhino Location and the Challenging Tank Trophy

The Rhino tank in GTA 6 is parked at the Mile Zero Air Station military base. Jump the fence, head to the tank row and press Triangle. Taking one pops the Challenging Tank trophy.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 20, 10:54 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

To get a tank in GTA 6, head to the Mile Zero Air Station military base in the southwest corner of the map, jump the fence using the dirt ramp and drive over to the row of Hauners Rhino tanks. Walk up to one and press Triangle (Y on Xbox) to climb in. A few moments later the Challenging Tank trophy pops. Maka91Productions, Arekkz Gaming and MrDalekJD all ran the full route on video.

Before you go

  • Make a backup save. Arekkz Gaming saved to a separate slot first. After his rampage in the tank, his Jason’s criminal profile had turned to Psycho. The trophy sticks to your account, so you can load the old save afterwards.
  • Armor and healing. Maka91Productions suggests popping adrenaline before you go in and wearing body armor.

Getting into the base

  1. The base is in Mariana County. From Jason’s house, MrDalekJD followed the road through Abismo Key down to Southwind.
  2. Just before the main gate, drive past the yellow blocks in the road and the guards at the checkpoint.
  3. Take the right. There’s a dirt mound against the fence: it’s a stunt jump that launches you into the base.

Arekkz Gaming tried several cars and nearly all of them cleared it. Big trucks can clip the railing and roll. The full route is in military base location.

Where the tanks are

  • Left after the ramp. Maka91Productions and MrDalekJD both turned left after landing. The tanks sit to the left of a huge building just past the fence, with the biggest ones closest to the fence.
  • A whole mix. You’ll also see smaller tanks, APCs, machine gun vehicles and even fixed turrets. The Rhinos are the big ones. Arekkz Gaming found a whole row of them at the back of a corner on the southwest side of the base, past the bunkers and the jets.

Driving the Rhino

  • Get in: press Triangle (Y) next to the tank. Your character climbs in through the hatch automatically, and you can still take fire while that happens.
  • Shoot: the Rhino has one cannon, up front. Maka91Productions fires it with R1 (RB on Xbox).
  • Power: in Arekkz Gaming’s video, a single shell wrecked nearly everything and even knocked out another tank.
  • Blind spot: the cannon can’t aim high enough to hit helicopters.
  • Radio: yes, the tank has a radio, and you can change stations.

What happens next

The moment MrDalekJD climbed into a tank inside the base he had 5 stars. Once he started causing damage he reached 6 stars. From there you get lighter tanks, other Rhinos, cars trying to box you in and helicopters with rockets, which are the real threat. Eventually the engine gives out and the tank explodes.

The tank also has a tracker, so the police know where it is even once you’re off the base. Arekkz Gaming’s suggestion is to sneak in without firing a shot and drive straight out, to leave with fewer stars. For the top wanted level, see six-star wanted level.

Tank cheat

There is a cheat that spawns a Rhino, but it only unlocks after you complete every survival challenge, and the button sequence hasn’t been published yet. Keep an eye on the cheats list.

Quick answers

Where is the tank in GTA 6?

At the Mile Zero Air Station military base in Mariana County, in the southwest corner of the map. The Rhinos are parked in one corner of the base alongside smaller armored vehicles.

How do you get into the tank in GTA 6?

Walk up and press Triangle (Y on Xbox). Your character climbs in through the hatch on their own, but you can still get shot on the way up.

How do you unlock the Challenging Tank trophy?

Get into a Rhino. It popped a few seconds later for Maka91Productions and after a few shots for Arekkz Gaming.

Can you keep the tank in GTA 6?

Not for good. The tank has a tracker, so the police always know where it is, even off the base.
