How to Get a 6 Star Wanted Level in GTA 6

Six stars is the top wanted level in GTA 6. PC Gamer only reached it by stealing a tank from the military base and blowing up other tanks and vehicles. Once you're there, there's no escape.

Unverified · 1 sourceUpdated Nov 19, 4:11 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

To get a 6 star wanted level in GTA 6, you have to cause mass destruction and a huge body count. The only method PC Gamer found was stealing a tank from the military base and blowing up other tanks and vehicles with it.

Six stars is as notorious as you can get under the new wanted system. Getting there means killing a lot of cops and soldiers, and that comes with a price.

How the top star levels work

Some things haven’t changed from earlier games:

  • Breaking into the airport and stealing a passenger jet gets you into the highest star levels. Here’s how to steal a plane from the airport.
  • Hijacking an APC at the military base does the same. The way in is covered in the military base guide.
  • At 5 stars, the police send a tank after you.

The sixth star sits above all of that, and only mass destruction and loss of life will unlock it.

What worked for PC Gamer

  1. Head to the military base.
  2. Steal a tank.
  3. Use it to destroy other tanks and vehicles until the sixth star lights up.

The article doesn’t say how many vehicles you need to wreck or how long it takes. PC Gamer also frames this as the only way it managed to do it, which isn’t the same as the only way that exists.

Know this before you try

  • There’s no coming back from 6 stars. At this level your heat and wanted level no longer go down, and the law keeps hunting you.
  • Dying is the only real exit. That’s PC Gamer’s takeaway, since hiding doesn’t work.
  • Your criminal profile takes the hit. Killing that many cops and soldiers can drag your criminal profile way down. It works a lot like the honor system in Red Dead Redemption 2.

If you just want to shake off an ordinary chase, see how to lose the police.

Quick answers

How do you get 6 stars in GTA 6?

Cause mass destruction. PC Gamer only managed it by stealing a tank from the military base and using it to blow up other tanks and vehicles.

Can you escape a 6 star wanted level in GTA 6?

No. At six stars your heat and wanted level stop dropping, and the police never give up. Dying is effectively the only way out.

How many stars until a tank shows up in GTA 6?

Five. Just like in older GTA games, hitting 5 stars gets a tank sent after you.
