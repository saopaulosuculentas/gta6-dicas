GTA 6 Bowling Alley Safe Code: 0300 (Kingpin Bowling)

The Kingpin Bowling safe code in GTA 6 is 0300. See where it's written down, how to blow the safe open and which unique shotgun to take from the pool table.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 22, 10:40 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

The Kingpin Bowling safe code in GTA 6 is 0300. PC Gamer says it’s a fixed code, and GamesRadar+ lists the same number. The safe holds around $3,400.

When you can rob it

The alley is the target of Hit Kingpin Bowling in Chapter 2, though PC Gamer notes you can hit it earlier as Lucia on her own.

Where the code is written

It isn’t in the bowling alley. The notepad by the safe points you to Brooks Boatworks, another Kolt & Associates hideout in northwest Port Gellhorn.

  1. Get into Brooks Boatworks and expect plenty of enemies.
  2. Head for the raised buildings on the east side. PC Gamer points to the small one in the northeast.
  3. Climb the stairs.
  4. Check the map on the wall next to the whiteboard.

That map also lists the codes for the La Cascara Motel (6969) and for Brooks Boatworks itself (6823), so note all three if you make the trip.

Opening it without the code

The safe takes explosives too. If the plant option is grayed out, pick up the sticky bombs in the next room. GamesRadar+ first thought you had to wait until Chapter 3 to buy them, then corrected that: the ones at the alley are enough.

Don’t leave without the shotgun

According to PC Gamer, the unique Gutterland Moreland 850 shotgun is lying on the pool table in the main room. See more legendary and unique weapons.

For other robberies, read how safe codes work. Any narcotics you take can be sold to Dunk, as covered in fence locations.

Quick answers

What is the GTA 6 bowling alley safe code?

It is 0300. PC Gamer says this code is not randomized.

Where is the bowling alley code written down?

On a wall map in an office at Brooks Boatworks, the gang hideout in northwest Port Gellhorn.

Can you open the safe without the code?

Yes. GamesRadar+ found sticky bombs in the room next to the safe, so you can blow it open.
