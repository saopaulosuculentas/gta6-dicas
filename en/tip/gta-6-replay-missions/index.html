Can You Replay Missions in GTA 6?

No. GTA 6 won't let you replay a mission once it's done. The only way back is to load a manual save from before it, though you can restart a mission while you're still in it.

Confirmed · 3 sourcesUpdated Nov 19, 6:09 PM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

No, GTA 6 has no mission replay. Once you finish a mission, there’s no menu that lets you pick it and play it again. ZoneX notes that this is a change from GTA 5, and GamesRadar+ confirms there’s no way to replay missions. Your only route back is loading a manual save you made before it.

The only way: manual saves

If you saved before a mission, loading that save lets you play it again. The catch is that you’re sent back to that point in the story and lose everything you did afterward. That’s why ZoneX says to save before every mission and before every big story choice.

Here’s how to save, per GamesRadar+:

  1. Open the main menu with Start (Options on PS5).
  2. Go to Game.
  3. Pick a save slot.
  4. Select Save Game.

Loading works from the same menu. The full walkthrough is in how to save in GTA 6.

Restarting the mission you’re in

You can’t save during a mission. GamesRadar+ explains that you can still restart the mission from the menu (Start), either from the beginning or from the last checkpoint. That’s handy for retrying a section that went wrong before you wrap it up. After the mission ends, it’s locked in.

Making 15 slots count

You get 15 manual save slots, and GamesRadar+ puts the mission count at close to 100. You can’t keep a save in front of every single one, so you have to plan:

  • Chapter saves: GamesRadar+ suggests keeping one save at the start and one at the end of each major chapter. That way you can jump back to any part of the story.
  • Mission and choice saves: this is ZoneX’s tip. Use your leftover slots for it and keep overwriting the oldest ones.
  • The 16th slot is reserved for autosaves. It kicks in after missions, when you die or get busted, and after some big open-world encounters. Because it’s a single slot the game overwrites by itself, it can’t hold on to an older point in the story. More in GTA 6 autosave explained.

Mission payouts are one-time

Vice City Dispatch, in a video that pulls together points from several reviews, notes that a lot of GTA 6’s biggest paydays come from story missions, heists included. Since finished missions can’t be replayed, you can’t run one again to collect the reward twice. Each of those payouts lands exactly once.

For money you can earn over and over, see how to make money in GTA 6. To see how many missions are left, check the GTA 6 mission list.

Quick answers

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

No. Once a mission is finished it's gone. The only way to play it again is to load a manual save from before it.

How do you restart a mission in GTA 6?

While you're in the mission, open the menu with Start and restart from the beginning or from the last checkpoint.

How many save slots does GTA 6 have?

There are 15 manual save slots, plus a 16th slot used only for autosaves.

Can you farm mission payouts in GTA 6?

No. Since finished missions can't be replayed, each mission only pays out once.
