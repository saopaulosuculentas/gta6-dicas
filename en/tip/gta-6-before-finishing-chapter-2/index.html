What to Do Before Finishing Chapter 2 in GTA 6: What You Lose When You Move to Vice City

Picking up the case at the Starlet Motel ends Chapter 2 and boards up the motel. Before that, pay Linda, grab the two motel cards, hit the savings goal and save in a separate slot.

Confirmed · 2 sourcesUpdated Nov 25, 8:59 AM BRTPS5 and Xbox Series

Chapter 2 in GTA 6 ends when you pick up the case in room 6 at the Starlet Motel, and there’s no going back: the motel gets boarded up. Before that, pay Linda, grab the two motel cards, hit the savings goal and save in a separate slot.

For now, this list comes from ABTW’s video only.

When Chapter 2 ends

  • The task to move to Vice City shows up once you finish all the main missions in the chapter and have $15,000.
  • For ABTW, it appeared when Vicky’s $10,000 from the Afternoon Delight mission came in. That mission pays out the next day.
  • Going into the room and picking up the case starts the last mission. From then on, the Starlet Motel is boarded up. Port Gellhorn stays open. GameSpot confirms the Chapter 2 safehouse becomes inaccessible, just like Chapter 1’s.

1. Save in a separate slot

GTA 6 doesn’t let you replay missions. Before you pick up the case, make a manual save in a new slot. That way you can go back if you forget something.

2. Pay Linda and get the necklace back

If you bought Lucia’s necklace in Chapter 1 and fell behind on rent, Linda is holding on to it. Pay her before you leave and the necklace comes back. Once the motel is boarded up, you can’t.

3. The two motel cards

Both are missable:

  • Vicky: on the floor of the reception bathroom, next to the toilet.
  • Victor Maravillas, the baseball player: inside the fridge in the motel kitchen.

See the rest in collectible cards.

4. Hit the savings goal

In the BuckMe app, complete the $5,000 family savings goal. The bank deposits about $2,000, the account moves up to silver and the limit goes up to $100,000. Keep in mind that money in savings can’t be taken back out.

5. Make the most of Moses’s free registration

The first car you register with Moses is free. Bring a car you actually want to keep, or one that costs a lot to register. See how to sell and register cars.

6. Empty Dunk’s trunk

Dunk’s trunk takes stolen goods, but the money comes in later through BuckMe. Before you leave, ABTW recommends emptying whatever is still in his trunk.

7. Your money comes with you

Unlike Chapter 1, this time your money and your car come with you to Vice City. ABTW left with about $22,000, sold one last bag and arrived with $23,600.

Other things that are harder to reach later

  • Dark Knight Drifter shotgun: according to ABTW, it’s a unique variant sold only at Gelhorn Outfitters, for $2,400.
  • Gelhorn Raceway race: ABTW shows a practice lap with Beau. Beat his time and you’re in with the racing crew.

Quick answers

When does Chapter 2 end in GTA 6?

When you go into the room at the Starlet Motel and pick up the case. That mission shows up once you've finished all the chapter's main missions and have $15,000.

What do you lose when you finish Chapter 2 in GTA 6?

The Starlet Motel gets boarded up. The two collectible cards there are gone for good and, if you didn't pay Linda, so is Lucia's necklace.

Does your money carry over from Chapter 2 to Chapter 3 in GTA 6?

Yes. Unlike Chapter 1, your money comes with you to Vice City. ABTW left with about $22,000 and arrived with all of it.

Where are the Starlet Motel cards in GTA 6?

Vicky's card is on the floor of the reception bathroom, next to the toilet. Victor Maravillas's card is inside the fridge in the motel kitchen.
